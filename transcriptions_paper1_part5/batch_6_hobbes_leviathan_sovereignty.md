### Historical Milieu of Thomas Hobbes: The English Civil War
- **Archival Reference:** `page_061.png` to `page_063.png` (Hobbes Historical Background & Problematique).
- **Thomas Hobbes (1588–1679):** Born in the year of the Spanish Armada ("My mother gave birth to twins: myself and fear"). Lived through the catastrophic English Civil War (1642–1651), the execution of King Charles I (1649), and the rise of Cromwell's Protectorate.
- **Hobbes's Primary Problematique:** The catastrophic danger of state collapse, civil anarchy, sectarian religious warfare, and the disintegration of political authority. His overriding philosophical goal is establishing the non-negotiable foundations of **political order, absolute obedience, and sovereign peace**.
- **Masterpiece:** *Leviathan: Or the Matter, Forme and Power of a Commonwealth Ecclesiasticall and Civil* (1651).

### Scientific Materialism and the Resolutive-Compositive Method
- **Archival Reference:** `page_063.png` to `page_065.png` (Hobbes's Epistemology & Scientific Method).
- **Scientific Materialism (Mechanical Materialism):** Influenced by Galileo's law of inertia and Euclidean geometry, Hobbes rejects Aristotelian teleology and scholastic metaphysics. The entire universe, including human beings, consists purely of **matter in motion**.
- **Mechanical Psychology:** Human consciousness, thoughts, and emotions are merely internal bodily motions caused by external pressures on sense organs.
- **Appetite and Aversion:** Human motivation is governed by two fundamental physical motions:
  - *Appetite (Desire):* Motion towards objects that enhance internal vital motion (pleasure). What man desires, he calls "Good."
  - *Aversion:* Motion away from objects that impede vital motion (pain). What man avoids, he calls "Evil."
- **Relativism of Values:** There is no objective transcendent "Summum Bonum" (Greatest Good); there is only the subjective pursuit of whatever satisfies fleeting desires. However, there is a universal **"Summum Malum" (Greatest Evil) — violent death**.
- **The Resolutive-Compositive Method:** Borrowed from the School of Padua and Descartes. To understand a complex machine (the State / Commonwealth), one must resolve (disassemble) it into its basic component parts (individual human beings), analyze the mechanics of those parts, and then recompose them into a stable political order.

### The State of Nature: The War of All Against All (Bellum Omnium Contra Omnes)
- **Archival Reference:** `page_065.png` to `page_068.png` (Leviathan, Chapter 13).
- **Hypothetical Mental Construct:** The State of Nature is not necessarily a historical epoch, but an analytical thought experiment: what human life would be like in the complete absence of sovereign political authority and coercive law.
- **Restless Desire for Power:** "I put for a general inclination of all mankind, a perpetual and restless desire of power after power, that ceaseth only in death." Humans seek power not necessarily out of inherent malice, but because they cannot assure the power and means to live well which they have at present, without the acquisition of more.
- **Foundational Equality of Vulnerability:** Men are naturally equal in physical strength and mental faculty. The weakest has sufficient strength to kill the strongest, either by secret machination or confederacy with others. Natural equality produces equal hope of achieving ends, which leads to inevitable conflict.
- **Three Principal Causes of Quarrel in Human Nature:**
  1. *Competition:* Makes men invade for gain (material resources, wealth).
  2. *Diffidence (Mutual Distrust / Fear):* Makes men invade for safety (pre-emptive strikes).
  3. *Glory (Reputation):* Makes men invade for honor and pride.
- **Famous Description of the State of Nature:** In the absence of a common power to keep all in awe, men exist in a condition of war — where every man is enemy to every man.
- **The Classical Dictum:** "In such condition there is no place for industry, because the fruit thereof is uncertain... no arts, no letters, no society; and which is worst of all, continual fear and danger of violent death; and the life of man, **solitary, poor, nasty, brutish, and short**."

### The Laws of Nature: The Prudence of Rational Self-Preservation
- **Archival Reference:** `page_068.png` to `page_070.png` (Leviathan, Chapters 14 & 15).
- **Right of Nature (*Jus Naturale*):** The absolute liberty each man has to preserve his own life by whatever means he judges best, including the right to another person's body.
- **Law of Nature (*Lex Naturalis*):** A precept or general rule discovered by human reason, forbidding a person from doing that which is destructive to their life.
- **Fundamental Laws of Nature:**
  1. *First Law:* To seek peace and follow it; and when peace cannot be obtained, to use all advantages of war.
  2. *Second Law:* That a man be willing, when others are so too, to lay down his natural right to all things, and be contented with so much liberty against other men as he would allow other men against himself.
  3. *Third Law:* That men perform their covenants made (foundational basis of justice).
- **Covenants without Swords:** Reason dictates that men must cooperate, but passions (fear, pride, greed) tempt men to renege. Therefore: "Covenants without the sword are but words, and of no strength to secure a man at all."

### The Social Contract and the Birth of the Sovereign Leviathan
- **Archival Reference:** `page_070.png` to `page_072.png` (Leviathan, Chapter 17).
- **The Contractual Formula:** The Social Contract is not between the ruler and the ruled; it is a **lateral, reciprocal contract between each individual and every other individual**:
  - *"I authorise and give up my right of governing myself to this man, or to this assembly of men, on this condition; that thou give up, thy right to him, and authorise all his actions in like manner."*
- **Creation of the Mortal God (Leviathan):** By this single contract, isolated, atomized individuals simultaneously create both **Civil Society** and the **Absolute Sovereign**. The Sovereign is the product of the contract, but **not a party to the contract**.
- **The Inherent Logic:** Because the sovereign is not a contracting party, the sovereign cannot breach the contract. Subjects cannot claim sovereign forfeiture or breach of trust.

### Active Recall Flashcards (Batch 6)
- **Q1:** What is Hobbes's Resolutive-Compositive method?
  **A1:** An analytical method borrowed from Galilean mechanics: disassembling a complex political whole (the state) into its elemental parts (individual human passions and atomized bodies), understanding their mechanical interactions, and recomposing them into an orderly commonwealth.
- **Q2:** What are the three primary causes of quarrel in human nature according to Hobbes?
  **A2:** Competition (invading for material gain), Diffidence (invading for self-defense and safety out of mutual distrust), and Glory (invading for reputation and honor).
- **Q3:** Why is the Hobbesian Sovereign not a party to the Social Contract?
  **A3:** Because the contract is made laterally between individual subjects; the Sovereign is created by the contract, not a contracting party, meaning the Sovereign cannot commit a breach of covenant or be legitimately deposed.
