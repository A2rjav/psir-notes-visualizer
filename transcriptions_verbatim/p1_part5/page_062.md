$\bullet$ __Evaluation of Aristotle__
$\rightarrow$ __Aristotle as a Father of Political Science.__
If plato is father of Political philosophy, Aristotle is
rightly called as the Father of Political science.
We should not interpret the word Science in the
Strict technical sense, it is only in relative sense.
Aristotle is more practical in comparison to plato
There is a difference in Plato's Theory of Ideas &
Aristotle's Theory of Forms.
For plato Idea is Independent of the material
reality. Idea has an existence beyond the
world of sense.
Aristotles Theory of forms does not undermine
the importance of Matter. He has showed that
idea is inherent in matter not independent of it.
