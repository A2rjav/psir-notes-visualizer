HEADING: __No Law: No Liberty.”__
---
- In above statement the term law refers to the
  Due Process of Law or reason. In the
  state of Nature man was enjoying Rights to
  Life, Liberty, Property because of the presence
  of the reason in man.
- According to locke reason in man taught him
  not to ~~harm the, life~~ not to harm life, health
  property, possession.
- It is because of reason in man, the state of nature
  was the state of peace, goodwill and mutual
  assistance.
- There were certain inconviniences in the state of
  Nature & so man enters into the social contract.
  By 1st contract man creates civil society. gives
  formal recognition to each others right.
  By the 2nd contract man creates govt. even when
  man has created govt. man has not surrendered
  his Liberty. state cannot take away man's right to
  Life, liberty and property. The law made by state
  has to confirm to the law of reason or due process
  of law. Any law made by state which deprives
  man from his right to life, liberty and property
  cannot be considered as Fair, Just and Reasonable.
- Hence, in countries where judiciary recognises the
  Doctrine of due process of law, there is a better
  guarantee of Protection of liberty.
- SC of India has accepted the Doctrine of due
  process of law in Menka Gandhi case 1978.
  This has ensured better protecⁿ for life & per-
  sonal liberty & has strengthened the rule of law.
