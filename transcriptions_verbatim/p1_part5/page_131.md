Q. "In the first place it is mostly considered unjust
to deprive anyone of his personal liberty
his property or any other thing which belongs to
him by law."

- Above statement is from Mills book on Utilitarianism
where Mill also proposes Utility as a principle
of Justice & Ethics.

- According to Mill , it would be unjust if state deprives
a person from his personal liberty. Liberty is a
condition for the growth of a person. In his
book on Liberty he has clarified that only harm-
ing others can be the basis for intervention.
Similarly state cannot deprive a person from his
property except according to the law. If person
is a owner of property by law state have no
right to deprive.

- The views are very similar to those of Locke, Bentham
Hobbes & other Utilitarians.

- Socialists are critics of utilitarians. According to
socialists liberty & property of a person can be
made limited in the wider interest of the society.

- Even positive liberals would have the similar view
with respect to absolute right to liberty & property.
