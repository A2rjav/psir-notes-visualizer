What is sovereignty
- It is the supreme power of the state over its
  subjects and citizens. Sovereignity has two dimesions
(i) External mean no authority from outside the
  state can dictate the state. Neither any external
  association e.g. Roman church nor any association
  within the territory of the state shall have supremacy
  over the state.
