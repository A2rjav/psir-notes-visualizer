- According to Barker, Mill is talking about an
  Abstract man not the real man living in the
  society.
- It is true that Mill is inconsistent thinker,
  inconsistency arises because he belongs to
  the time when people were realising the
  -ve consequences of -ve liberty. But the
  idea of +ve liberty has not yet cristalised.

- There is also an inconsistency in Mill, when
  Mill permits state to intervene in self rega-
  rding actions. If person is taking that actions
  which may harm him because of ignorance.
  He give the example of a person trying to cross
  a bridge which is about to fall. It is the duty
  of the state to make that person aware.
  However if person is already aware self
  harm is not the basis for intervention of
  the state.

- Though there are inconsistencies in Mills ideas yet
  it would not be justified to call him as
  Prophet of Empty Liberty & it is correct to say
  that if there is anyone who is liberal, it is Mill.
