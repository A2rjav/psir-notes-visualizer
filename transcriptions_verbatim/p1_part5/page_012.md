② Heraclitus -
  According to him, change is the characteristic of
  the world in which we live.

③ Parmenides -
  He gave idea of permanence. There is continuity
  as well as change.

④ __Socrates__ -
  Plato considered himself as the desciple of the
  Socrates. He considered Socrates as the wisest
  man on this earth. Plato was influenced by -
  (i) Socrates Theory of Knowledge.
  (ii) Socrates Method of Dialectics.
  (iii) Events in the life of Socrates -
       - Socrates was ordered to drink poison.
         Socrates had opportunity to escape.
         Socrates preferred to die for his principles.
         When Socrates was ordered to drink poison
         at that time it was democracy in Athens.
         Plato hated for democracy is linked to
         life of Socrates.
