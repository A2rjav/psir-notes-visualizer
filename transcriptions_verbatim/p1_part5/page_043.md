Plato's theory of Education is the logical result
of his conception of Justice.
Plato is not only the father of Political philosophy
his influence goes beyond the discipline of Politics.
Rousseau recognise plato's Republic as the best book
in the field of education.
Plato's approach towards the eduⁿ continue to influe-
nce the system of eduⁿ in many western countries
even today. (compulsory Primary education)
He dealt with eduⁿ in a Holistic manner. He talks about
moral, physical, as well as mental developement of
personality. He talks about theoritical as wellas
practical eduⁿ.
The scheme of eduⁿ is devided into diff stages. In each
stage there is a filteration process to identify the
natural aptitude.
Thus it can be said that the system of eduⁿ is the
logical result of his theory of Justice.
According to plato Justice is Proper stationing &
Non-interference. Justice results into excellence &
Harmony. Just state is a state where people are
assigned duties according to their Aptitude.
Each group/class perform its own duties without
interference.
Thus to find out who has what aptitude plato
devise the scheme of eduⁿ.
