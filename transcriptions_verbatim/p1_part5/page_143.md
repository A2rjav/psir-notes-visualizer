Base and super structure conflict of Marx.

In order to understand the society we have to understand
the basic structure of the society. According to Karl Marx,
Economic structure form the basic structure.

How he proves that economic structure is the basic structure?

origin of the society. According to Marx, the origin of the
society is is the need of satisfaction of the appetite of
Man. As he suggest that in order to make History, Man has
to live, in order to live man has to produce. Production is a
basis of society.
