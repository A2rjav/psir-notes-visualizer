Q. Marx treats individual primarily as member
    of a class. Critically examine his views on
    economic man.

— The above discription of Human nature comes from the
   basic principle of Marxism, i.e. Historical materialism
   According to Historical Materialism economic
   structure is the basic structure of the society.
   It is for the task of producⁿ, that man enters into
   social relations. All relations are the relations of
   production. There are two types of relations of
   production. Either person belongs to the class of
   owners & non-owners. Persons real interest are
   the interest of his class. Relations b/w classes are
   nothing but inherently conflictual. Hence the real
   identity of man is economic, real interest is class.
   Hence, he does not want working classes to develop
   the false consciousness of nation or religion. They
   should develop true consciousness i.e the conscious-
   ness of their class. Once they will develope true
   consciousness class in itself will become class
   for itself.
