- Mill is also known as reluctant Democrat because
  he was also fearful about the state of democracy
  even in western countries.
- Mill was fearful that Democracy will get converted
  in tyranny of majority or mobocracy.
- He was critic of Democracy in USA.
- In order to protect the future of Democracy
  Mill has proposed following Reform:
[Margin: ARISTOCRATIC]
(i) The concept of weighted voting.
  Weighted voting - Everyone to be given one vote
  but those who are educated & possess property
  should be given more than one vote.
(ii) Plural voting
  Person should have Right to vote in all such constituencies
  where he have his own property.
(iii) Proportional Representation instead of majoritarial
  Model.
(iv) Greater powers to Upper house / House of lords.
(v) Open Ballot rather than secret Ballot (compatible
  with freedom of speech & Expression). > Freedom of speech & Expression
(vi) Mill has proposed Right to Vote in favour of women
- With the exception of Right to Vote for women
  as well as the recommendation for open Ballet
  all of his reforms are Aristocratic in Nature.
- He wants to secure future of Democracy by
  introducing illitist element. Hence he is also a
  reluctant democrat in above sense.
- In comparison to Mill Indian leaders like Gandhi,
  Nehru emerged as a passionate democrat.
- If for Mill people should be made fit for democracy,
  for Indian leaders people will be made fit
  through Democracy.
