Q. The state is Individual writ large.
                                 larger
Ans-> Above statement means state is the magnified version
      of individual.
      In the words of Plato the only difference is of big
      letter and small letter.

__Context__- The statement is given by plato to logically establish
         his view that Principles of justice found at the level
         of individual can be applied at the level of state.

[Margin: H]
● How he justifies that the state is Indi. writ large -
 -> In the words of plato states do not come out of
    oak or rock, it inheres in the mind of the persons
    living in the state.

__Message__- The hidden message in Plato's view is that the
         state is nothing just an __abstraction in Idea__ . [Audio]
         state is nothing but collection of people who are
         organically related. Only then people are just.
         State will be just. Thus he suggests that the Pri-
         nciples of justice meant for the individual are
         also the Principles of justice at the level of state.
         Hence, there is continuation betⁿ ethics & politics.
