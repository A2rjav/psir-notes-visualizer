__ARISTOTLES THEORY OF PROPERTY__

Aristotle was critic of plato's views on property.
Aristotle did not support the idea of communism
of property.
$\rightarrow$ __what is property according to Aristotle?__
That property is legitimate which is the
product of one's labour.
The property which is earned by force or fraud is
not legitimate.
He even criticised the institution of money lending

(i) __common ownership & common ~~use~~ (communism)__
Aristotle does not support common owner-

property is no one's responsibility.
According to him, it is a difficult ~~f~~ business for
the people to stay together. It is more difficult
when the question of property involves. Common
ownership is not good for the property, person
