Marx understand alienation in terms of objecti-
fication and the loss of real human nature:
In capitalist society every person feels Alienated.
Alienation is not limited to workers. Even capitali-
sts are Alienated. Alienation is a situation where
all freedom of Man is taken up by the market.
According to Marx, in capitalism two humans do not
speak with each other. One is a seller other is a
buyer. E.g. the relation between patient & doctor
is a relation of buyer & seller. Marx has explained
the Alienation faced by the working class.
→ According to Marx the true nature of Man is-
① Man is creative by nature
② Man is social by nature
→ Capitalism gives no scope for fullfillment of basic
human nature. He has explained Alienation faced
at 4 levels.
① Alienation from the product of his labour.
According to Marx man works not just for the satis-
faction of his Appetite. There is a difference in man
and other animals. Man works for satisfaction
of his creative urge. Capitalism undermines labour
~~Wen~~ when a person works he creates his own world.
Capitalism gives no scope for a man to work accord-
ing to his choice. Man has to produce not what he
wants. But what market wants. Hence the work
looses its joy it become burden.
According to Marx, it is wrong to think that man can
work efficiently only under market condition. It is a
false consciousness created by capitalists.

② Alienation from the process of Production-
The process of production has become so much
machanised that man has reduced to the level of
'cog in the wheel'.
