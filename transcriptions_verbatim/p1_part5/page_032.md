"No law or ordinance is mightier than knowledge".

__Theme__ - The justifica$^n$ of absolute powers enjoyed by
the philosopher king.
- plato wanted to make Athen an ideal state &
  according to plato, "until philosophers are the
  rulers......"
- __why philosopher king?__
  Because he is symbol of knowledge & sacrifice.
- Plato has given absolute powers to the philo-
  sopher king with the only exception of the
  constitution which he cannot change.
- In the words of plato no law or ordinance is
  mightier than knowledge.
- philosopher king posses absolute & ultimate
  knowledge which cannot made ordinary by
  public opinion.
- If we limit philosopher king with ordinary
  wisdom (public opinion) it will be as foolish
  to limit to expert practioner of medicine
  with a book of medicine.

__Conclusion__ Aristotle is critic of institution of philosopher
king. Aristotle prefers rule of law over, rule of
person howsoever, knowledgeable he is.
__why?__
(1) It is impossible to find the philosopher king.
(2) philosopher king is also a human being. If he has
reason he also has Appetite. It may happen that
Appetite comes to dominate his reason.
Uptil now the law has served the purpose.
Law represent collective wisdom & wisdom of
ages which is more dependable than wisdom
of one person.
