> How Bentham defines Utility?
Utility is the capacity of any commodity to give
pleasure. Hence its basis is materialistic & its
experience is direct

__Mills view on Utility:__
"I regard Utility as an Ultimate appeal on all
ethical questions but it is Utility in Its widest
sense grounded in the framework of the
Permanent interest of man as a progressing
being."

- How mill redefines Utility?
for both Bentham as well as mill Utility ~~as~~ is
the yardstick of all ethical questions.
However Mill modifies the concept of Utility.
For Mill there is a need to make a difference
between pleasure & Happiness.
Happiness is superior to pleasure.
- Pleasure is materialistic & Happiness is non-materia-
listic. Pleasure is a satisfacⁿ of Appetite, Happi-
ness is the satisfaction of soul,
- If pleasure is gained by experience, Happiness
is gained by sacrifice.
- Once Mill makes a diff. betⁿ pleasure & Happiness
Mill also questions the belief of Bentham that
man is ~~capable of~~ not capable of understanding
pleasure & pain of the others.
- Mill takes the progressive view of man.
Initially man may have been dominated by Appetite
but gradually man became rational.
- According to Mill, the concept of utility has to be
grounded in the permanent interest of Man.
