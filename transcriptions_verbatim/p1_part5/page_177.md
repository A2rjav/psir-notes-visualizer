__Importance of the concept__
She has established that the most imp. action is
Political action. It is essential in the interest of
freedom that man's participation in politics other-
wise we are giving rise to Totalitarianism.

__Hannah Arendt's concept of Political__
Hannah Arendt's concept of Political is based on
her theory of Human Action. According to Her, it
is necessary that people take interest in Public
sphere specially in politics. Her concept of political
is linked with her concept of power.

[Margin: Imp.] What is Hannah Arendt's concept of power?
She makes difference between strength, force,
violence, and power.

__Difference in Strength and Power__
Strength belongs to the person (personal) whereas
power belongs to people (Group) It means person
is either strong or weak. Person is not power-
ful or powerless. It is not the characteristic of
individual. Power is a characteristic of a community.
