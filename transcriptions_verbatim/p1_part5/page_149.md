Marx analysis of capitalism
What type of system of production
is capitalism?
capitalism differs from the previous modes of producⁿ.
How?
capitalism emerge in modern time. Scientific revolu-
tion gave rise to industrial revolution. Industrial
revolution led to the machanised producⁿ or producⁿ

__Earlier modes of Production__ -
Earlier production was through man. Earlier major
source of production was labour. People could
produce for full-fillment for their needs, they could
also produce some amount of surplus. However,
sorplus was not ~~of~~ of such dimension that market
was required for sale.
Once machines started production it started producing
surplus. Now mass production instead of production
by masses. Factory/machine goods were cheaper &
could be produced in large quantity. The system of
machine based production left no option for indi-
vidual production.
Factory based production started, workers neither
had land nor capital. They only had their labour
to sale.
__Nature of Capitalism__ -
capitalism is dehumanising. Labour becomes a
commodity or object to be sold & purchased.
capitalism is exploitative in nature.
Why?
Profit is the engine of the capitalism. In capitalism
the motive of production is not need but profit.
How profit is Earned?
By reducing the cost of production.
