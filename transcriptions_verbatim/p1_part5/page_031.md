$\rightarrow$ __Aristotle on communism of family.__
- Extremely critical of plato's communism of family.
- Remedy is worse than the disease.
- Plato's ideas are not progressive rather take us
  back to the barbaric ages.
- The purpose of marriage & family is not just
  procreation. It is very imp. for emotional stability.
- State is not a family rather family of families.
- Both Property & family have been time tested
  institutions, had there been any other alternative
  it would have been in existence.

$\bullet$ __Institution of philosopher king.__
$\rightarrow$ __why philosopher king?__
  If Athens has to become ideal state it has to be ruled
  by philosophers.
$\rightarrow$ __Qualifications__
  man of reason, symbol of sacrifice, knowledge of
  philosophy, doesn't have family, no right to property.
$\rightarrow$ __Powers of philosopher kings__
  He has absolute powers. In the words of plato
  No ordinance or law can be mightier than knowledge.
  His wisdom cannot be questioned by public opinion or
  by book of law.
- In the words of Plato,
  "It is foolish to limit the expert practioner of medicine
  with a book of medicine."
- There is only one limitation on philosopher king.
  - He cannot change the constitution. (Plato's consti$^{n}$)
