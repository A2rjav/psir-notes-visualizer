What is Historical materialism

laws of History.
What is Marx view on History ?
Marx contradicts Hegel's view of History.
According to Hegel History is the result of the
dialectical movement of ideas.
What Marx tells about Hegel's view of History ?
According to Marx Hegel is standing on his Head.
It is the dialectical movement in matter that shapes
History & not ideas.
