*(v) __Socialism__
This socialism should not be confused with socialism
in general. It is a very specific use of the word
socialism by Marx. This word has been popularised
by soviet Marxists as ~~ded~~ dictatorship of proletariat
Socialism will be the first stage after prolitarian
revolution. In this stage there were two classes i.e.
workers and capitalists. Now workers will capture the
state. Now state is an instrument of working class.
capitalist democracy was false consciousness. It was
democracy of minority and dictatorship over majority.
It was dictatorship of capitalists over workers. Now
(socialism) it will be dictatorship of proletariats over
capitalists. It will be democracy for majority &
dictatorship of majority over minority.
In Marx this stage was to be the temporary stage or
transitional or inter mediate stage.
> What work is to be done in this stage?
This stage is needed to make all necessary preparations
before communism. As a result of communist revolu-
tion power is captured by the workers but lots
remains to be done.
