● What is the purpose of Theory of Education in Plato ?
- Eduⁿ is the method by which aptitude of a person
  is known and ideal state ~~can~~ ~~be~~ is established.
- Plato has dealt with the issue of eduⁿ in a very
  exhaustive sense that Rousseau called Republic as
  the best book in the field of Education.

● __Influence on plato__ -
- He has combined the elements of the education system
  of Athens and Sparta.

● Good features in __Spartan system__-
- State sponsered education.
- compulsory military education.
- Equality of opportunity to women.

● Elements of Athenian system which plato dislikes -
- Education in private hands.

- No vigilence by state over the curriculum.
- No equality of opportunity for women.
- Neglect of physical education.

● Merit of Athenian System-
- In Athens the focus was on developing the logical
  and critical faculties of mind.
  More focus was on study of literature, maths etc.
