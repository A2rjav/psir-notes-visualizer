● __On Origins of Totalitarianism-__
In her books on origins of totalitarianism she has
analysed the totalitarians regime of 20th century.
This includes Nasism and Stalinism.
> How Hannah Arendt describes Totalitarianism?
she describes totalitarianism as the rule of terror
and ideology. In the words of H. Arendt totalita-
rianism is Bureaucratically centralised violence
made possible by 20th century technology in the
service of 19th century ideology. In order to clarify
the difference betn earlier autocratic regimes &
20th century Totalitarianism she suggests that it is
completely a new form of Govt. with no examples
in history. In all autocratic regimes so far violence
has been used as a means but in Totalitarianism
violence becomes an end in itself. It is a use of
violence without strategic rationality. In earlier
autocratic regimes, regimes tried to control the
speech, these regimes tried to control thought also.
It was not just the control of body, it was con-
trolling souls.
