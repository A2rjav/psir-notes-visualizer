What is structuralism?
Structuralism is suppose to be scientific approach
in social sciences. It is based on the assumptions
that there are some structures or elemental, funda-
mental aspects of reality.
What are the structures which will vary from
scholar to scholar.

What is Gramsci's analysis?
Gramsci felt that Marx has taken elements of
superstructure lightly they are not just the reflection
of the base but they are structure in themself.
We cannot take it for granted that if material
structure change, superstructure will automatically
change . we cannot take it for granted that if mode
of production becomes socialists all other institutions
will change on its own. Hence he suggest the need for
more deeper analysis of the institutions of the civil
society.


Gramsci suggested that working classes need to learn how
Bourgeoise have established their domination: (control)
Bourgeoise class established their domination not just
by controlling the means of producⁿ but also controll-
ing the ideology & culture i.e. means of producⁿ of
ideology & culture.
Gramsci talks about the concept of Hegemony.
Hegemony implies leadership. leadership is based on
the consent of the governed. workers need to learn
how to develop leadership. Bourgeoise class deve-
loped leadership by developing nexus with other
classes. other classes may not have identical aims
but they have common aims. Bourgeoise class has
built the coalition of classes. They convince other
classes that they are serving their interest also.
