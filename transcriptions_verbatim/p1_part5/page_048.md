State should try to seek unity not through
uniformity but through unity in diversity.
Aristotle proposes, 'Polity' as the best practicable
form of govt. where citizens representing diff.
interest & families come together to deliberate
and formulate laws. The laws made by state should
take care of multiple interest, should try to achieve
balance among competing interest & the principles
of family cannot be applied in case of state.
Thus, Aristotle was the $1^{\text{st}}$ person to segregate
Personal from Political.
