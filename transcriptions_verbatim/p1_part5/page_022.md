④ __Glaucon -__

  His theory is called contractual theory.

  According to Glaucon, the justice is the interest of the weak.
  According to him those who were weak who could not
  protect themselves on their own came together &
  form the state.
  Hence is the state is for the protection of weak.

  __Plato's Arguement -__

  It is wrong to think that the state is meant
  only for weak. State is in the interest of all.

- On the basis of above discussion we get following
  ideas of Justice.
  (i) Justice is a duty.
  (ii) Justice is a virtue, the objective of justice cannot
      be harming anyone.
  (iii) The laws of the state are for the welfare of all.
  (iv) It is better to be just rather than unjust.
  (v) Justice cannot be based on the principle of
      'Might is Right' because it will lead to conflicts
      and disorder.
  (vi) Justice leads to peace harmony & excellence.

  virtue - Behaviour showing high moral standards.
