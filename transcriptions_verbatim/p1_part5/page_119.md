• __Introduction to Utilitarianism.__
  Utilitarianism has been recognised as the philosophy
  of common man.
  It appeals to the common man & that is the
  reason that Utilitarianism became the dominant
  philosophy.
- The term utilitarianism comes from the term Utility.
  Utility is the capacity to give pleasure or satisfaction
- According to Utilitarianism pleasure & pains are
  the only guides.
- According to David Hume, Ethics is nothing but a
  moral sentiment. Even such ethics will not work
  which overlook pleasure & pain.
- According to Bentham, "Nature has placed mankind
  under the governance of two sovereign masters
  pleasure & pain. It is for them alone to point out
  what we ought to do as well as to determine
  what we shall do."
- According to Bentham only utility can be the basis of
  ethics & should be the basis of ethics. because man
  cannot act otherwise. There is no point advocating
  such ethics which man is unable to act.
