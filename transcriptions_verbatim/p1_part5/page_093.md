Now state emerged absolute. When State exists
man has no liberty. Man is Free only to the
extent state has preferred to keep silent & not
make law. In case if state makes law man has
no liberty but to act as per the law. State also
has power to punish those who do not act as
per law.
- Hobbes is not the supporter of Liberty. He is
rather fearful of liberty. Hobbes prefers order &
security over liberty.
"Liberty or Freedom signifies properly the
absence of opposition in external impediments
to motion."
- Since beginning scholars have been trying to make
a difference betⁿ liberty and capacity.
- Hobbes also does not believe that capacity can be
linked to liberty.
- According to Hobbes, A patient in the bed lacks
capacity to walk rather than liberty to walk. lack of
capacity is internal impediment to motion.
- He gives the example of Liquid in the bottle -
The walls of the bottle acts as External impediments
to motion. If External impediments are removed
the liquid in the bottle is free to move. Thus he
defines liberty as Absence of interference or
obstacles. However Hobbes is not supporter of the
liberty.
- According to Hobbes, man has liberty only to the extent
State has permitted it by not making law.
