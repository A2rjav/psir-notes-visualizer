Q. Explain how machiavelli's application of imperical
method of human affairs makes an important
stage in the evolution of political science?
-> Pol. science is one of the oldest disciplines & is
also a dynamic discipline.
There are different stages in the evolution of the
discipline.
we can trace the origin of discipline to Ancient
Greece in the works of plato who is known as
father of Political philosophy & in the works of
Aristotle who is known as father of Pol. science
& who called ~~it~~ Pol. science a master science.
During ancient Greece politics was not indipendant
branch of learning it was part of ethics.
For both plato & Aristotle good life & good political Life
were not seperated from each other.
In medieval times politics became the branch of
religious studies. with Machiavelli Pol. Sci. as a
discipline got its autonomous character.
He was the 1st person to seperate politics from
Ethics & Religion.
Machiavelli also contributed in the growth of
methodology of pol. science
