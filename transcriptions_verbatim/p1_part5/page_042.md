Just man in who reason dominate.
Thus 2 ultimate principles of Justice are proper
stationing, Non-interfering. Same principle of
Justice to be applied at the level of state.
State is Individual writ large.
__Just state__- devided into 3 classes - each classes
performing its assigned duties & they are not inter-
fering in each others task.
State is inter-dependence. Each class has to sacri-
fice its immidiate narrow self interest for the
wider interest of the society.
Ideal state is where philosophers are the rulers who
understand the meaning of good life, who are
symbol of knowledge & sacrifice:
Philosopher will sacrifice his life for pursuit of
knowledge for the sake of the state.
- Plato proposes two machineries to create the ideal
state. Primary machinery is Education. to know
the Aptitude & to find out who is a man of
knowledge.
- Plato also proposes communism. Communism is a
supplementary machinery & additional safe guard
to check even the remotist possibility of the
philosopher king becoming corrupt.
- According to Plato, Ignorance, Property & Nepotism
together are the causes of corruption and thus
he proposes communism along with education.
Thus, this statement is correct.
