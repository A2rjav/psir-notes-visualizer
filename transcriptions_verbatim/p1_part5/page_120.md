__characteristics of pleasure according__
__to Bentham.__
(i) Pleasure is purely quantitative term. There is no
    concept/issue of quality.
    Either there is more pleasure/less pleasure. There is
    nothing like inferior or superior pleasure.
    In the words of Bentham "Pushpin is as good
    as poetry".
    [Diagram: ↓
    Game of small children]
(ii) Since pleasures do not differ in quality persons also
    do not differ in quality. He does not believe in the
    theories like some are men of reason & some are
    men of Appetite. In this sense Bentham is more
    Democratic.
(iii) ~~Que~~ Pleasure has materialistic origin.
(iv) Pleasure is always an Individualistic experience.
(v) According to Bentham, state should follow such policy
    which gives maximum Happiness to Maximum no. of people.
- __In what ways state can provide maximum Happiness.__
  Only way the state can bring maximum Happiness
  is min. interference in the eco. Because this
  will create more wealth.
  Bentham has also deviced felicific calculus to calculate
  total pleasure and total pain.
- __short note on Greatest Happiness of Greatest Number.__
  It is one of the most deceptive principles. Socialists
  criticise the principle because it give primary to
  Happiness over number. Whereas for socialist
  we should give primacy to no. over Happiness.
  According to Bentham Society is Aggregate of
  Individuals. He rejects the organic conception
  of the society.
