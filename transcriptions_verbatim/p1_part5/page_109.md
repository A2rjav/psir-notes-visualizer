• Locke on Toleration -
In his essay titled , ' A letter concerning Toleration'
Locke has given his views on toleration.
Locke suggests that state should follow the policy of
toleration towards the persons following different
religions. Unlike Hobbes who brings even religious
matters under the state , Locke wants to keep religious
matters outside the scope of the state.
According to him state should leave free man in
religious matters( freedom of religion). As far as
Religion is concerned man should have complete
freedom because in matters of Soul. God does not
appoint magistrate. There is no point using force,
because force can compel people to obey the state.
But it cannot force people to change their beliefs.
Though locke suggest state to be tolerant towards
people having faith in diff religions he does not
suggest ~~tolerance~~ toleration towards ~~At~~ Athiest.
Because Athiest could not be trusted.
In the words of locke, Promises, Covenants and .
~~et~~ oaths which are bonds of human society can
have no hold upon an Athiest.
• __Locke's Theory of Revolution-__
Revolution in locke is just peaceful change.
Since man is Rational there is no need of
violence. The conditions for revolutions are -
(i) When govt. does not act as per the wishes of
trustees.
(ii) When executive does not allow legislature to function.
(iii) When executive does not rule according to the laws
made by the legislature.
(iv) When govt. puts people under foreign control
