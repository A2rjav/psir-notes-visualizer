__Assessment of Hobbes as a Thinker -__
① __Hobbes as a 1ˢᵗ modern Thinker -__
"While Jean Bodin was standing at the gate
of modernity it was Hobbes who jumped inside."
- The most imp concept of Modern Political Theory
is the concept of sovereignty.
- Machiavelli gave the concept of Nation State But
not sovereignty.
- It was Jean Bodin who was the 1ˢᵗ person to
give the theory of sovereignty. He defined
sovereignty as supreme power of state over
citizens and subjects. However, he could not
give the complete theory. He could not come out
of medieval mentality completely. He held that
state is sovereign only in secular sphere of life.
As far as religion is concerned he held that church
will be supreme.
- It was Hobbes who has given the complete theory
of sovereignty & have clearly established that in a
particular territory there cannot be more than one
sovereign. & Even in religious matters state is
supreme.
