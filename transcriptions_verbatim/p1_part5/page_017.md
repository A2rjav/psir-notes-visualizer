Why to know reality?
It is necessary to ~~live~~ lead/ a good life.
Hence, scholars belonging to diff. traditions have
been trying to understand reality.

In ancient Greece we 1^st see physicist explaining
natural phenomena focusing on the physical world.
Later on we see the emergence of philosophers,
who were not satisfied with the understanding
with the physical world & who believed that there
is another world beyond physical world/ beyond the
world of senses.

|Role of Socrates
We trace the origin of ethics in the ideas of Socrates.
Socrates believed that it is more imp. to know how we
ought to live rather than just knowing how we live.
It is more imp. to understand what is good life
rather than just knowing what is life.
In this context Socrates has given the theory of knowledge.

Socrates has suggested to look for the things which may
not be visible to eyes, which requires __reason__.*

Plato has developed his theory of Ideas on the basis
of Socrates theory of knowledge.
(i) The knowledge about physical world
(world of appearences) is not the true knowledge.
True knowledge is of permanent nature. It does
not change. Physical world or the world of appearences
is a world of change. We cannot have permanent
knowledge about the things which are changing.

reason - the power of mind to think.
