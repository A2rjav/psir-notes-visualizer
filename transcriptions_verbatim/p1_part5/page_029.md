__THEORY OF COMMUNISM__

- What is communism?
- Communism is a way of life which rejects pvt.
  ownership and supports common ownership.
- Which communism is plato talking about?
- Communism of property,
- Communism of family.
  He proposes communism only for the guardian
  class means only for the ruling & soldier class.
- Why no communism for producer class?
- Not required. Because they are not in power.
- Not possible because they are men of Apetite &
  cannot control their lust for worldly pleasures.

● Communism of property.
- Ruling class will not be allowed to own property.

● Communism of family-

  Because ~~it is~~ family promotes both corrupⁿ & nepotism.
