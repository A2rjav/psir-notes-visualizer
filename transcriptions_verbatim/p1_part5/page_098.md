(I) __Content of the 1st Treatise__
- In his 1st book he has criticised Filmer's book
  'Patriarcha.'
- In the 2nd book he has given his theory of govt.
  why Locke criticised Patriarcha?
- In Patriarcha Filmer has justified the ~~abs~~ absolute
  authority of the state.
- According to Filmer, state is a big family of God.
  God has created Adam. God has given absolute
  powers to Adam. Adam is the 1st king.
  Hence, He should enjoy absolute authority.
  Filmer did not accept contract as the basis of
  Political Authority. According to him, the basis of
  Authority should be heridatory. From god the
  power has been transferred to Adam & ~~from~~
  Adam to his sons & their successors. It means
  all kings are successors of Adam & should enjoy
  absolute rights.
  Filmer criticised social contract on two grounds-
(i) Social contract assumed that ~~the~~ man is born free.
  However, man is not born free. man is born
  in the society. Hence man does not have free-
  dom to choose society.
