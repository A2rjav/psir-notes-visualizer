Q. Hegemony means something more specific ~~than~~ than
   power and domination.

Power is a central concept of political Theory.
However, like all concepts of politics power
remain a contested concept. Different scholars
define power different. Diff. scholars also bring
forward diff. dimensions of power. The purpose
of power is to get things done by others. We have
been familiar with the coersive dimension of
power. When Marx uses the term power or power
of the state in the interest of Bourgeoise class He
points towards the coersive form of power.
- Gramsci has brought another dimension of power
which he preferably calls as Hegemony. Hegemony is
intellectual cultural domination. Hegemony is soft
power it is a invisible power. If p coersive power of
society is located in the state, Hegemony is located
in civil society. When Hegemony is manufacturing
consent, intellectuals play major role in manufacturing
Hegemony. Hegemony is constitute the values of the
dominant class. It is because of Hegemony that
these values appear as common sense.
- Thus Gramsci highlight the ideological power & ideo-
logical domination. He suggest the need to build
counter Hegemony. He suggests the two stage
revolution ie. war of ideas & war of manoeuvre.
