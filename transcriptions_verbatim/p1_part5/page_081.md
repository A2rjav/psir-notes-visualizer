__Hobbes Methodology-__
- If Machiavelli is treated as Galileo of Pol. science,
  Hobbes can be treated as Newton.
- Hobbes was a 1st person to apply a scientific
  method.
[Margin:

- Hobbes belongs to Social Contract tradition-
  As economic system changes other spheres of our
  life also changes.
  In medieval times the economic system was
  feudalism.
  feudalism is based on custom whereas capitalism
  is based on contract.
  Philosophy of contract has also been applied in case
  of political system.
  In modern western societies constitution is
  considered as the contract document.
  In modern societies state is considered as the
  creation of man. This view of state is called as
  Mechanistic view. which means state is a
  machine created by man to serve him. It also
  means that man is prior to state.
- In ancient times state was considered as prior to
  man. State was considered as natural. such view is
  called as organic view of the state.
- In medieval times the theory of Divine origin of
  the state was prevalent. It was believed that state
  was creation of gods, king was considered as repre-
  sentative of god, as a representative of God king
  had absolute powers (Divine rights of a king).
