  __Introduction__
- Rousseau is a thinker of Paradoxes.
  Not only his language is Paradoxical but his ideas
  have been a source of inspiration for many
  contradictory schools of thoughts.
- liberals, socialists, idealists, totalitarians, democrats
  all take inspiraⁿ from Rousseau.
- Rousseau was the 1ˢᵗ person to highlight the
  negative impact of Science & Reason.
- As a thinker he was the 1ˢᵗ person to suggest that
  man is not bad rather society is bad. Hence
  what is to be changed is society. Man will auto-
  matically become good.
- He is known as Father of French Revolution &
  Father of concept of Popular Sovereignty as he
  have given the Theory of General will.
- The central concern of his political philosophy was
  reconciliation between Liberty & authority.
- In the opening statement of his book Social contract
  he suggests that, "Man is born free, but he is
  everywhere in chains."
What is Freedom?
Freedom is to act according to one's choice or will.
It also means to act according to ones true nature.
What is the True/original Nature of Man?
According to him, man is has self love as well as
sympathy for others. Man is essentially good.
