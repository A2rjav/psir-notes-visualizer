Hence it is said that there is not much diff.
except morphological difference.
- However, it would be unfair if we compare
  Leviathan & Gen. will & believe that they are same.
- Rousseau was actually the greatest exponent of
  Direct democracy. Rousseau was impressed by
  the system of Direct Democracy which was
  prevailing in Switzerland even at that time.
- We should not overlook the fact that according
  to Rousseau the only way General will emerges
  is when people go for direct participation in
  law making.
- Thus Rousseau is a champion of not only parti-
  cipatory democracy but also deliberative demo-
  cracy.
- Though Rousseau's ideas are subjected to the
  manipulation by the totalitarians justifying
  state totalitarianism yet we should not forget
  that Rousseau is one of the greatest
  champion of Democracy.
- With Rousseau also starts the idea of positive liberty
  when he says that man is free only when he
  obeys the state.
- Another term for positive liberty is moral freedom.
