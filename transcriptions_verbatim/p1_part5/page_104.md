Locke proposes seperation of Powers as in the state
of Nature there was no seperation of powers.
> what powers govt. has?
People have not transferred all of their rights to
the govt. They have transferred only 3 natural rights-
(i) Right to Make Law
(ii) Right to Execute Law
(iii) Right to Adjudicate Law

People have unlimited rights & govt. have limited rights.
> Nature of the Power of Govt.
Govt. has no original power. It only have deligated
power. Original powers lies with the people. Govt.
is a trust, people are trustees. Trust cannot run
against the wishes of trustees. Hence, govt. cannot
make any law for which people have not given
their consent. Since, people have not transferred
Right to life, Liberty & Property. Govt. cannot make
any law which will limit these rights.
> what if govt. makes such laws-
People have right to revolt against the govt.
__Meaning of Revolution__
Revolution in locke does not mean violent overthrow
of the system. It simply means change in the
person running the govt. by constitutional method
ie. through elections.
How govt. is elected?
- In the first contract all people enter into the contract
with all
- In the second contract where govt is formed, few persons
will be elected among the people to represent them.
- Govt. is elected by majority. Whichever group gets ~~ma~~
majority will get right to form govt. Thus govt. repre-
sent the consent of the people.
