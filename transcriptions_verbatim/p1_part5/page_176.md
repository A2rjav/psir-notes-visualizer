__Hannah Arendt's Theory of Action OR__
__Concept of Politics__
- Hannah Arendt's theory of Action: is given in her
book 'Human conditions'.

[Diagram:
Action
├── Thinking (vita-contempletiva)
└── Action (vita-Activa)
]

Human beings performs two types of Action ie.
Thinking and Action.
According to Hannah Arendt, Action is important.
Plato is wrong when he says thinking is important
and Marx is correct when he says Action is Imp.

[Diagram:
[Margin: zoon-animal]
Action (vita-Activa)
├── Labour (Animal laboran) ──>
├── Work (Homo Faber) ──>
└── Action (zoon Politikon)
]

Most imp. action is Action.
__Labour__
Labour is an Action which all living beings perform.
Even animals perform this action. Man has no
freedom in this sphere.
Marx is wrong when he thinks that labour is most
important.
__Work__
In Homo Faber man is more than Animal but less
than Human. Works like that of Craftsman, Artists,
Professionals comes under this category. Here, man
is relatively free but not completely free.
__Action__
Man is Political Animal. Hence, the most important
action of all human actions is Political action.
This is the only action which Humans perform.
This is human condition. Only when we participate in
political life we act as Humans This is an action
which gives complete freedom.
