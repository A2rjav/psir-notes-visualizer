HEADING: Plato as Father of Totalitarian-

$\rightarrow$ __what is totalitarianism-__
eg. china $\rightarrow$ Totalitarianism denotes the [total control of
the state].
The examples of Totalitarian society are
communist china, Iran, North korea.
$\rightarrow$ __why plato is called Totalitarian?__
Western liberal scholars like Karl Popper,
Isaiah Berlin calls plato as totalitarian.
Because we can see the traces of two
varieties of totalitarianism i.e. Communism
and Fascism in works of Plato.
Plato has given absolute powers to the state.
He does not give any rights to the citizens.
Even family & marriage is controlled by the
state. He creates a regimentive society.
state will determine to aptitude & profession.
There is no freedom of choice.

Still it will not be appropriate to call plato as
totalitarian. We have to go into the essence of
the totalitarianism. rather than the form of
Totalitarianism. whatever totalitarian society
that had come into existence whether in germany
or Former USSR or Present china, all such
societies have been extremely exploitative of
their people. Plato's republic remains an Utopia.
Hence we cannot say that Republic would have
been an exploitative state. It seems plato's
intention was to not to exploit the citizens but
to give them the life of virtue & excellence.
