__GRAMSCI__
__(1891-1937)__

● Introduction
— Gramsci is Italian Marxist [Margin: Marxist]
— Gramsci is founder of communist party of Italy.
  contemporary of Mousoulini.
— The second greatest personality in the History of
  Marxism.
— Gramsci has saved Marxism from the criticism
  of crude economic determinism.
— Since Gramsci was put behind the bar, Gramsci
  had no access with respect to the changes
  happening outside
— Gramsci wrote on pieces of paper which were
  later published as 'prison notebook'
  It is said that the advisers of Mousoulini advised
  him that Gramsci should not allowed to think.

● __Prominent contributions of Gramsci__
① concept of Hegemony.
② concept of civil Society.
③ Concept of Integral state. [Margin: अखंड / अविभाज्य]
④ Gramsci's theory of revolution. [Margin: नवक्रांती]
⑤ Gramsci's Analysis of Intellectual class.

Concern of Gramsci
Gramsci was active in communist movement.
He was disappointed because communist revolu-
tion as predicted by K. Marx didn't happened.
Hence, Gramsci revisited Marx theory of History.
He felt that there are some defects of Marx
understanding of History.

Determinism — the doctrine that all events, including human
action, are ultimately determined by causes external to the will

Hegemony — leadership/dominance especially by one country
or social group over the others.

Revolution — a forcible overthrow of a govt. or social order in favour
(नवक्रांती), of a new system.
