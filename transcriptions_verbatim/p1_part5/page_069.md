“Men are by Nature ungreatful, deciet, coward,
fickle minded and avaracious.(extremely greedy)”

“Man easily forgets the loss of ~~fath~~ his father
than loss of his patrimony. Hence, wise prince
executes rather than confiscate.”

Machiavelli represent the psychology of emerging
Bourgeoise class. Machiavelli wants prince to
understand the basic nature of man and make
its policies accordingly:
According to him man is ungreatful by nature
Hence man will forget the favour done by
the prince. Hence it is better for a prince to be
‘feared than to be loved.’
Man is decietful by nature. Whenever their self-
interest demand they will betray the prince.
Hence, prince should not trust anyone or should
not take anyone for granted.
Man is coward by nature. which is man is fear-
ful by nature. Man fears the loss of the things
which gives him happiness. Prince can exploide the
weakness of man. Prince should convince them
that they are secured only when prince is ruling.
- Man is fickle minded which means man can easily
change the sides. This weakness can also be e
exploited because prince can bring the person
who is favouring other to his side.
- Man is avaracious means extremely greedy.
Hence, prince should give opportunity for
fulfillment of the materialistic nature of man.
In this way prince can ensure the continution
of his rule.
