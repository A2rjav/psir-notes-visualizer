Q It is not our consciousness which determines our
  existence on the other hand it is existence
  which determine our consciousness.
  Above statement comes from marx concept of
  Historical materialism which marx consider as
  the scientific explaination History. The theory is
  in reference to Hegel. Hegel held that idea is
  real. In the words of Hegel real is rational &
  rational is real. K. Marx held that Hegel is
  standing on his head. According to Marx it is
  matter & not idea which forms ultimate reality.
  Through his base & superstructure model Marx show
  that economic structure forms the basic structure.
  The class which controls the basic structure also con-
  trols the elements of superstructure. In the words of
  Karl Marx in every epoch the ideas of the ruling class
  have been the ruling ideas. Ideas are not independent
  of material reality. They are derivative of the material
  reality. Hence it is not our consciousness that determines
  our existence. On the other hand our existence shapes
  our consciousness.
  Above statement tells us about the Epistemology of
  K. Marx. as well as ontology of K. Marx. Marx wanted
  that working classes should not live with false con-
  sciousness they should rather true consciousness.
  ~~Text~~ True consciousness means class consciousness.
  Once they develope true consciousness they will rise
  in revolt against capitalists.
