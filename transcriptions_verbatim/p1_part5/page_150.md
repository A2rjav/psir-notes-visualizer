How to reduce cost of production?
① __Reduce Wages__ -
what is capitalist law of wages?
wages should be sufficient for him to survive; it
should not be enough that he does not come
back the next day. It means no surplus to be
left with the worker. They get wages which is not
enough, to ~~en~~ increase profit further. Capitalists
squeeze wages which increase the hardship.
② __Displace the labour & replace it with machine__
__This will create more hardships for the workers.__
when one capitalist brings machine other is bound
to bring resulting into further laying off of workers
and further reduction of wages. The law of
Demand & Supply determine wages.
As capitalism will grow it will become more & moore
exploitative. Capitalism starts developing its own
contradictions. On one hand they are increasing
the profit but on the other hand they are depri-
ving large no. of masses from their purchasing
power. Hence, capitalism show the contradiction
where there is surplus in the market but people
lack purchasing power. Hence, Cycles of depression
keep on coming. To some extent capitalists are
able to manage the crisis but after which it
goes beyond their control. Capitalism automati-
cally leads towards monopolisation. It is a cut-
throat competition. Only few big players will survive.
Even the small capitalist, petty Bourgeoise class
will start loosing the ownership. They will also
become proletariats. Middle class will start join-
ing the Proletariats.
