→ There are two dimensions of Liberty -
  ① __Negative Liberty__ - which means freedom in the
     absence of state.
  ② __Positive Liberty__ - which means freedom in the
     presence of state.
  1st person to give the concept of Positive Liberty in
  political philosophy is T.H. Green.

" Liberty is where law is silent"
- Liberty is the concept which explains the status of
  relationship betn Individual & the state.
  In the state of Nature man had absolute Liberty
  to do whatever man wanted to do.
  In the state of Nature there was no state or
  common authority.
  In the state of Nature man was not able to enjoy
  liberty as there was lack of security of even life.
  Absolute ~~security~~ Liberty has resulted into the
  situation of Anarchy. Since man is utilitarian
  by nature man wanted to come out of state
  of Anarchy. Man has transferred all of his
  rights to the state.
