- • __What are the options?__
  ① Either go back to the state of Nature.
     However it is not feasible.
  ② To enter into the contract & to create a society
     where we can live according to our real nature.
     and we can get true happiness.

• __Concept of General Will__ -
  __General Will/Popular Sovereignty__ -
  __Terms & conditions of the contract__ -
  - All people agree to act to with their real will.
  - The sum of real wills of all create the General Will.
  - General will is the sum of real wills of the all.
  - General will is essentially good because it is the
    sum of good wills.
  - General will is represented in the state & in Rousseau
    people coming together form the state.
  - General will is represented by the state & people
    are state
  - The idea comes to near the view that state is
    individual writ large.
  - What does state symbolise?
    when people combine together they form body
    politic. (Body politic - state
    Eg. It is assumed that the members of the
    constituent assembly were working with their
    real will & constituent assembly represented the
    General will or will of the people of India.
