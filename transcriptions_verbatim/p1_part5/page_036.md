-> Levinson wrote, 'In defence of Plato'.
Levinson does not agree with Poppers view on
Plato that 'Plato is fascist'.
According to Levinson there is huge difference
betⁿ time & space betⁿ Plato & emergence of
Fascism. Comparing Plato with Fascism is
'Comparing the Uncomparables'.
The Fascist state was reality, Fascist have
established very exploitative system.
Plato's Republic is utopia.
We do not know what form Plato's republic
would have taken if ever implemented.
It seems that Plato's motive was not the
exploitation of the people.
There was genuine desire in Plato to give life
of excellence & virtue through all citizens in
the state.
-> __Conclusion 1__
According to Karl Popper, if we do not acknow-
ledge Plato as Fascist, it is only because of
spell of Plato and the tradition of admiring
Plato.
Though Levinson disagree that Plato was fascist
but he acknowledges, that Plato's critics were
not entirely wrong.
-> __Conclusion 2__
According to Gilbert Ryle, whether Poppers
view on Plato is correct or not, but it is
certain that after Poppers work Plato will
not be seen in the way it used to be.
