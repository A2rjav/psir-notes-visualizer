$\rightarrow$ According to Locke consent is of 2 types-
(i) Explicit consent
(ii) Tacit consent.

[Margin: Lecture 14 | 30/12/2016]

$\circledast$ __Explicit consent / Tacit consent__ -
Majority gives explicit consent. It is assumed
that minority has given implicit/ tacit consent.
The purpose is to suggest that govt. is assumed
to be based on consent and everyone ~~sh~~ should
follow the laws made by govt.

$\rangle$ __Scope of the power of the Govt.__
Govt. has limited powers.
Govt. enjoys only deligated powers & no original
powers. Govt. can make law only to the extent
permitted by the people.
$\rangle$ Can govt. make law which limit right to life,
liberty and property?
According to Locke, govt. cannot because man has
not transferred these rights.
