Gramsci has looked into the works of other
scholars. He got attracted towards the explainaⁿ
of History given by Benedetto Croce. He has
analysed the role of cultural factors in shaping
History. Gramsci felt that Marx has overempha-
sised on the economic factor & over looked the role
of other factors in shaping History. Gramsci has
modified Marx model of History.
__Marx Model of History-__
Economic structure is the Basic structure & other
structures are reflection of the base. They have no
idependent autonomy of their own. If we change
economic structure. elements of superstructure auto-
matically change.
Hence, in K. Marx only one revolution is needed. i.e.
against the economic structure.

The Gramsci has given 3 layered structure.
According to Gramsci elements of superstructure
are not just the reflection of base but structures
in themselves. He devided superstructure into
2 levels.
① civil Society.
② State.

Gramsci has given more serious thoughts on the
elements of superstructure & hence Gramsci is also
known as the Theoritician of superstructure. In
this way Gramsci rescue Marxism from being crude
economic determinism.
