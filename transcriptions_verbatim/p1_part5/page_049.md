__ARISTOTLES THEORY OF CITIZENSHIP__

● What is citizenship?
It's a legal status of a person with respect
to state.
Aristotle also recognise the principle of Birth
and ~~blood~~ Blood. However, Aristotles basis of
citizenship is not limited to just these two
criterias.
Aristotle excludes women, children, old people and
slaves from the definition of the citizens.
Why?
For Aristotle citizenship is not just a formal
status. He focus on the substantive aspect of
citizenship. [Margin: [Hindi word] / having a seperate & independent existence]
__What is the substantive idea behind citizenship?__
According to Aristotle citizenship is a duty or
responsibility. It is duty to participate in the
affairs of the states.
Which affairs?
(i) Legislative functions- the functions of law making
(ii) Judicial functions-

→ Hence, he excludes those groups which are not
capable of performing these roles.
(i) __Women__ - He believes women are inferior men.
He confines women within the & four walls of
family
(ii) __old people__ - Because of their health conditions they
may not be able to play the required role.
(iii) __children__ - children are immature.
(iv) __Slaves__ - They lack reason, which is imp to make laws

Thus only natural born adult male possessing
property qualifies to be a citizen.
