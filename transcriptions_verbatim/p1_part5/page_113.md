• __How was the state of Nature?__
  State of Nature was state of Bliss.

> What does he imply when he uses the term state
  of Nature?
  It is a description of human life before the advent
  of modern age, science & reason.

> What is the condition of Happiness?
  When person is free to act according to his nature.

> How Happiness got spoilt?

  when man started settled life gradually he started
  loosing Happiness.
