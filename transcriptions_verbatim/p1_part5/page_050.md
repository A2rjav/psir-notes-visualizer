__Criticism__
Aristotle's theory of citizenship is too narrow &
cannot be supported in the present age of
democracy. Howevers Aristotle's therory of
Citizenship was practically followed in all
western countries till 19th century. Only in 20th
century property qualificaⁿ was removed,
working class was allowed to vote as well as
women. Though his theory cannot be accepted
yet we cannot overlook the substantive dimen-
sion of citizenship as given by Aristotle.
Aristotle is a source of inspiration for many
contemporary scholars like Hannah Arendt
who also talks about active citizenship.
