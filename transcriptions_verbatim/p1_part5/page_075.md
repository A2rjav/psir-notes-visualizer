⑤ MACHIAVELLI ON FORTUNE (LUCK) -
   Machiavelli is realist to suggest that we should
   not overlook the factor of fortune (bad luck)
   Even when prince has all the qualities to be
   success-ful. Still there can be few things which
   are beyond control. Prince can still fail. when
   Bad times comes, It comes like torential rain or
   roaring river. It devestates man completely.
   Wise prince will always make preparations like
   embankments to control the flood. Yet he may
   be devastated.
   Machiavelli also suggests that the nature of fortune is
   like women. Women embrace brave men. If prince
   will face circumstance with courage he can turn
   things in his favour.
