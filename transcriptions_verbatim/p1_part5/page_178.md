__Hannah Arendt's concept of Political -__
In conventional sense political belongs to the state
but in Hannah Arendt it belongs to civil society. In
conventional sense power is exercised by elits
but in H. Arendt' power is with the people.
In conventional sense power is for domination
but in H. Arendt. Power is acting in concert. It
results into mutual empowerment.

__Hannah Arendt's concept of Revolution.__
Hannah Arendt is critic of marx because marx
favours violent revolution. Marx looked revolution
as a means for resolving social & economic questions.
According to H. Arendt, the reals revolution is secu-
larisation. It is freedom from fear, It is freedom
from myths. The real revolution means the funda-
mental change in the manner we think. She compa-
red French Revon & American Revolution. She appre-
ciates American Revon because it gives political freedom.
She criticises French Revon because in the name of
social & economic justice state inhance its power.
Though American Revon is better yet it is incomplete.
It provided constitutional checks on the arbitrary exer-
cise of power but has not created enough space for
peoples participation in public sphere.
