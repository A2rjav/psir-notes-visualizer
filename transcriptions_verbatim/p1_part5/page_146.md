__The Concept of Class & Class Struggle -__
"The History of All Hitherto Existing Societies Has
Been The History of class struggle."
— Marx
> what is class?
Class is a economic status which depends on
persons relationship with means of production
According to Karl Marx there are only two classes
Owner & Non-owners, Haves & Have nots, Exploiter
& Exploited, Dominated & Subjugated class.
Relation betⁿ classes are always of struggle.
There has always been class struggle & class struggle
have been moving force of History.
Exploiter class will be a reactionary class & exploited
class will be the revolutionary or progressive class.

— Marx has given following stages of History:
He devides the History of the world into two groups -
(I) The History of the West
(II) The History of the East.

__Stages in the History of West ·__
(I) __Pre- Historic Age -__
Before history. This stage he called Primitive commu-
nism. Initially property was held in common. All were
owners hence, society was not devided into classes.
Since classes were absent State was also absent.
> why he mentions primitive communism?
He wants to show that there was a time when man
was living without private property. It means man
can live without private property.
