Locke's Theory of Natural Rights -
- Man enjoyed Natural Rights in the state of Nature
Out of all Natural Rights the most imp. rights are
Right to Life, liberty and property..
At time locke has used the term Right to Property
in an inclusive sense to include Life & Liberty.
It gives two meanings-
(i) Without property life & Liberty carries no meaning.
(ii) It also means that they are man's property and
not states property. So state cannot deprive man
from his Natural Rights.
Life, Liberty & Property are the parts of Man's
Personality. It is unreasonable to think that man
can elinate himself from his Life, Liberty & property.
Locke has given justification for absolute Right to
Property.
According to locke what is the purpose of Life?
The Purpose of life is pursuit of Happiness.
God has created Apple, Milk, Meat to eat
and drink to satisfy the Appetite. God has not
created man to sacrifice.
Like Hobbes, Locke is also utilitarian. Property
is a source of Good Life.
__Origin of Property -__
Initially property was held in common.
However, it is commonsense to understand that
property cannot remain in common.
Those who were hard working industrious
became owners of property & became rich &
those who were fancyful, lazy, quarrelsome,
containtious remained poor.
