- __What is Utilitarianism?__
  Utilitarianism has been the dominant philosophy
  of the Britain.
  We can trace the origin of Utilitarianism to
  sophism, Epicureanism.
  One of the earliest exponent of Utilitarianism
  were David Hume, Hobbes, Priest ley. (Priestley)
  It was Bentham who made Utilitarianism as a
  school of thought.
  Hence Bentham is called as Father of Utilitarianism
  as he made it a systematic philosophy.

  __Why Mill thoughts of revising Utilitarianism?__
  Mill used to consider himself as the disciple of
  Bentham and by that time Utilitarianism came
  to be criticised as pigs philosophy.

  __How Mill revises Utilitarianism.__
  Utilitarianism is a extreme materialism, in order
  to revise Utilitarianism Mill brings ideas from
  Greek scholar like Socrates & plato. Since he mixes
  idealism with materialism that it is said that
  Mill has destroyed Utilitarianism.
