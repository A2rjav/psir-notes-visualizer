Aristotle has studied 158 constitutions. On the
basis of his study he have given 6 basic
forms of constitution. He have classified
constitution on two parameters:
ⓘ Objective of Governance
ⓘ No. of persons ruling

In his classification monarchy or the rule of
philosopher king is the best. but not the best
practicable. The practicle form of monarchy
will be tyranny. Tyranny is the worst form
of govt. And hence it will not be stable.
Polity is the rule of middle class in the interest
of the people. According to Aristotle, it is the best
practicable form of govt. It represent the Golden
mean of oligarchy and Democracy.

Polity is a rule of many but not too many.
- Democracy is extreme because it is a rule of
  too many which means rule of ignorance &
  poor. Democracy is the second worst form
  of govt. Democracy will get replaced by Tyranny.
- Oligarchy is a rule of rich but it will also not be
  stable because poors will be conspiring against
  the rich. Similarly in Democracy the rich will
  be conspiring against poor.
- Aristotle gives following arguements to support
  that the polity is the best practicable form
  of govt.
ⓘ Rich do not trust poor, Poor do not trust rich.
  But both will trust the middle class.
ⓘ Poors suffers from ignorance & rich from
  Arrogance.
