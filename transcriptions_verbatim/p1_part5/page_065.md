What was Renaissance
The Renaissance is revival of reason. It is a revival
of Greek philosophy.
What was the Salient feature of Greek philosophy?
Humanism. It should not be confused with Huma-
nitarianism. Humanism is a school of philosophy
which keeps Human Being at the centre. It is not
that Greeks didn't believe in Religion or mythology
yet for the secular sphere social, political, Eco.
sphere they preferred reasons or logic.
Thus Humanism and Rationalism were the 2 core
feautures of Greek thinking revived by Renaissance.

After Ancient time medieval Europe saw the centrality
of religion. Age of reason came under eclipse.
It became age of religion. This ages are called
as Dark Ages of European History.
During medieval time there was interference of
religion in politics which had bad consequences
for both politics as well as religion.
