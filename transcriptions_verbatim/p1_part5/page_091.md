● __Hobbes Theory of Rights__ -
- In the state of Nature man have all rights.
- Man had absolute right to do whatever he wanted.
- However, in the state of Nature nobody was in
  opposition to enjoy rights.
- In the state of Nature, 'Might was Right'.
- Only those who have power could enjoy rights.
  Hence he suggests that ~~man~~ what man posses in
  the state of Nature was not rights but powers.
- He belongs to the theory of Legal Rights.
  Man can enjoy only those rights which are granted
  to him by the state.

● Hobbes Theory on Right to Self Preservation -
  (Right to Life).
  According to Hobbes Right to Life is supreme right
  given to man by God/Nature.
  Man has transferred all of his rights to the state
  including Right to Life but in case of Right to Life
  the transfer is conditional.
