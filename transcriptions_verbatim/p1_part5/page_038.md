$\rightarrow$ __what is Feminism?__
Feminism is an umbrella ideology. There are
different schools of feminism.
The connecting link between all schools is
concern toward violence & discrimination
against women. The objective is to end
exploitation & to provide equality of a
status & opportunity.
$\rightarrow$ __Grounds for considering Plato as Feminist__
(i) Equality of opportunity to women in field
of education
(ii) Equality of opportunity to women to become
philosopher Queens.
(iii) He is the first person to suggest that women
do not suffer any natural disability with
respect to politics / statecraft.
(iv) His communism has liberating influence for
women as state takes up responsibility of
child rearing so that women can partici-
pate in the affairs of the state.

Plato as a feminist is debatable. Those who
do not consider plato as feminist argue
that plato's ~~was~~ main objective was not
upliftment of women, he provides equality
of opportunity so that the talent of a women
can be used in the service of the state.

To conclude, we can say that plato was much
ahead of his times. plato's suggestions with
respect to the education of women & women
joining in politics would have certainly resur-
lted into the improvement of women in the
society.
