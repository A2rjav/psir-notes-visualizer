② __Normative Individualists__·
Hobbes is individualist even in normative sense.
He does not believe that individualism is bad.
He was the 1st person to suggest that being
individualist is the nature of man & it is not a
sin. It is as natural fact as earth revolves around
the sun.

It may appear contradictory that Hobbes is Individual
st & Absolutist at the same time. Howerever there is
no contradiction in his individualism & Absolutism.
It is because of extremely individualistic nature of
man that man has no option but to live under
the absolute authority of the state. It is for the
protection of individual that absolute state has
been created. He does not make effort to change
individual. He only creates the system so that the
individualistic nature of man can be managed.
Hobbes has given right to self preservation & puts
restriction on a state to take away man's right
to life in an arbitrary manner.
