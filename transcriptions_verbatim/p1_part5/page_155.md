[Margin: ③] __Alienation from his society__
Man does not have time for the society. Man does not
have time for realisation of his social nature. Situa-
tion of worker is worse. He has to work for long
hours. He does not have surplus money that he can
realise his social nature.

[Margin: ④] __Alienation from Himself.__
The compound effect of all alienations is that he gets
alienated from himself.

According to Marx, so called freedom in the capitalists
societies is not freedom but Alienation It is an
isolation of Man. The only way to end Alienation is
by revolution. Only by violent overthrow of capitalism &
establishment of communism that man will achieve
the state of perfect freedom & his Alienation will end.

> __why communism is a state of perfect freedom?__
In communism each will work according to his
capacity & each will ~~work~~ ^get according to his need.
Society will take care of the need of the man.
Man will work not for fullfillment of his basic
needs but for his satisfaction of creative urge.
True freedom is freedom from necessities. We are
free to do what we want only when we dont have
to worry about our basic needs. Marx makes a
difference between freedom & liberty.
→ __Liberty.__
Liberty is a Bourgeoise concept. Liberty in a capitali-
st society is a false consciousness. Liberty is a right
which man has to protect himself from the society.
→ __Freedom__
Freedom is a condition. Freedom is a situation where
society guarantees the fullfilment of basic needs
of Man. Freedom does not alienate man from man.
Freedom ends the Alienation.
