Working classes should also develope their own
coalition. Gramsci talks about coalition of
depressed classes or subaltern class.
Gramsci suggest working class should form the coalition &
should provide leadership. Working class will get leader-
ship only when they also take care of the interest
of the others.
According to Gramsci, dominant class does not rule only
by coersion. He brings the concept of Hegemony.
Hegemony is also a type of power. Hegemony is
a soft power. Hegemony is invisible power. Hege-
mony is controlling the mind. Bourgeoise class rules
by controlling the mind. They rule by developing the
power of attraction. Hegemony implies manufacturing
consent. When any idea or value is starts appealing to
common sense it becomes Hegemony.
Who creates Hegemony?
What is the site of Hegemony?
Civil society is the site of Hegemony. It is a location
where Hegemony is manufactured. Often we overlook
the role of civil society in maintaining the domination
of Bourgeoise class. Civil society acts silently. Civil
society is much nearer to base than state. Civil society
acts as a cushion or a shock absorber. The states
where strong civil society exists, where media has
freedom, where educational institutions are autonomous
it is more difficult to bring revolution. Gramsci categorises
states into two types:
① Transparent state.
② Opaque state.
- In transparent state civil society is absent. So the exploi-
  tative nature of state is visible & revolution is easy.
  China is transparent / USA is not transparent
- In opaque state where civil society exists it is able to
  hide the real nature of the state & so revolution is
  difficult. India is opaque state.
