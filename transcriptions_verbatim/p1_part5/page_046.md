HEADING: Aristotle's Theory of state -
---
- “Man is by nature a Political Animal.”
- “state comes into existence for the sake of life &
  continues for the sake of good life.”
- “The man who can live without state is either
  a beast or god But he cannot be a man.”
- “State is highest of all associations.”
- State is a union of families and villages.”
- “The authority of a statesman is different from
  the authority of the master.”

-> “Man is by Nature a Political Animal.”
- Above statement tells the importance of state for man
  and why man should be obliged towards the state.
- In above statement Aristotle shows the limitation of
  the sophist view of the state.
- Sophists gave primacy to individual over the state.
  For sophists state is Artificial, a product of contract.
- Aristotle belongs to the tradition of socrates and plato.
- According to Socratic tradition state is natural and
  prior to man.
- Aristotle establish that a state is natural by suggest -
  ing the origin of a state lies in the natural needs of
  man.
- According to Aristotle god has not made man self
  sufficient. Man cannot fullfill even his basic needs
  on his own & hence man creates family. Family
  can full-fill some of the needs but not all. So man
  creates village. Village can fullfill some of the needs
  but not all. Hence man creates state. Only state is
  such institution which can fullfill all the requirement
  of man Hence state is highest of all associations.
