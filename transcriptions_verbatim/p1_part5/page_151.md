> What happens in the society?
complete imbalance. The concentration of entire
wealth of the society is in the hands of few.
And extreme poverty & misery on the other hand.
Masses in the state of poverty and few have wealth.
This create revolutionary situations.
> What is the law of Nature?
Equilibrium. is the law of Nature. Revolution will
become inevitable. Thus marx suggest that capitali-
sts dig their own graves.
> At what point revolution takes place?
Revolution will take place when masses develop
revolutionary consciousness.
$\rightarrow$ There are two types of consciousness according to Marx
① __False Consciousness__
False consciousness is generated by the intellectual
class working in the interest of dominant class.
E.g. Religion and Nation have been used to gene-
rate false consciousness.
② what is True consciousness?
class consciousness is true consciousness. When
workers become aware of their exploitation They
they start developing their class consciousness.
$\rightarrow$ class consciousness also has two levels:
ⓐ __Class in itself__
lower level of consciousness when person realise
that he belongs to the different class.
ⓑ Class for itself
It is a high level of consciousness. It happens
because of the growing understanding of exploi-
tation. Eg. In capitalism workers will initially
develope their class consciousness. As capitalism will
grow exploitation is going to increase. Not only
exploitation increase but the no. of persons being
exploited will also increase.
