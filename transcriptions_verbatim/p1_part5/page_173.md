HANNAH ARENDT
(1906 - 1975)

- Hannah Arendt is a Heterodox Thinker.
- She describes her thinking as , 'Thinking without
  Barriers.
- She gives her own meanings to the conventional
  concepts. Though, she cannot be linked with any
  specific school of thought yet the common idea
  connecting her work is the concern for parti-
  cipation in civic affairs.
  Hence, some scholars categorise her as belonging
  to civic Republicanism.
- she adopted the methodology of phenomenology.
  phenomenology is a method where theories are
  given on the basis of experiences as they are lived
  rather than on the basis of reason or logic.
- she was influenced by scholars like Aristotle, St.
  Augustine, Martin Heideggar and Karl Jaspers.
