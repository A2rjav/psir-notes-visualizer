• __Nature of Revolution__
__Revolution is only when mode of production changes.__
In Karl Marx we see only one revolution when eco-
nomic structure changes all other structures auto-
matically change.
__why revolution happen__
Law of dialectics explain the cause of revolution.
According to Karl Marx, Every contradiction contains
the seed of its own distruction. Change does not
happen overnight it only appears so. Initially quanti-
tative changes happen & then quantity changes
into quality. New courses of production, means of
production develope, ~~even~~ ^when relation of prodⁿ develope.
For some time old & new co-exist. However time
comes when co-existence is not possible. Old
becomes fatter on the growth of new. Old has
to be destroyed for new to move further.
__when communist revolution will take place?__
According to Marx, communist revolution will
take place only in countries where capitalism is
fully developed.
