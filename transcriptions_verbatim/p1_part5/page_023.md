__Plato's Idea of Justice-__

__Plato's Ambition -__
To know god's idea of justice, the perfect ultimate idea of Justice.
To understand god's idea of justice we require reason.

● From where we can get God's idea of Justice?
- From Soul. Because soul is the part of God.

● Which soul is a just soul? or who is a just man?
- Just man does not create problem for others rather live in peace with other (& harmony) as well as with himself.
- Person in ~~whom~~ reason dominates over courage & Apetite is a just man.

● What is God's idea of Justice?
- Each element at its specific place.
- Non interference in each others domain.

◎ __Idea of Justice At The Level of State.__
- __State is Individual writ large.__
- It means state is bigger form of individual.

Why Plato makes this statement & what is the context?
- He wanted to show that the principles of Justice applicable at level of individual can be applied in case of state.

Application of Principle of Justice found at the individual level of individual case of state.
Just like the three elements are organised in a specific manner in the just soul similarly state should be organised into 3 classes.
