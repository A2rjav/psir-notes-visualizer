In the                  of distributive justice, Aristotle proposes
the principle of Merit.

what is difference between Desert & Merit?
- Desert means what one deserves (person's talent)
- merit is a social concept. It is the value society gives
to the particular work e.g. it depends on society
whether it gives better importance to the work of
the teacher or domestic servant.

Aristotle gives the principle of merit. Reward should be
in accordance to the importance of the work of the
person for the society.

Aristotle does not believe in absolute equality he believes
in proportionate equality.
