“ In politics ends justify the means
- The seperation betⁿ ends & means is the most
  defining characteristic of so called machiavellianis
  It is for this reason Machiavelli has been criticised
  as immoral.
- Machiavelli was the 1st person who could dare to
  suggest that Politics and Ethics are two different
  domains. He was the 1st person to establish the
  Autonomy of politics from Ethics or Religion.
- Political actions are to be judged not from the
  yardstick of religion or ethics. The yard sticks of
  politics is National Interest.
- What may be ethically wrong may be politically
  correct.
- The action of the prince will be judged by the
  ends he secured.
- Action is correct if he is able to protect the NI
  Machiavelli is treated as the greatest patriot
  among political scholars.
- Machiavelli suggested that nothing should come in the
  path of NI not even religion.
- Machiavelli has invented the concept of Dual Morality
- The morality of the prince is diff. from the
  morality of ordinary person.
- Ordinary person may sacrifice his life for the sake
  of his principles but prince cannot put the life
  of his people & security of his state in danger for
  the sake of his principles.
- Machiavelli is not immoral nor he is teaching
  immorality. He is permitting prince to use
  whatever means prince think are in the best
  interest of Nation.
