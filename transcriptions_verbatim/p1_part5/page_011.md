● Plato's Theory of Soul -
  Universe has two parts -
  (i) Physical or Material
  (ii) Spiritual
  Human beings are also part of universe. Human
  beings also have two aspects -
  ⊛ Physical and spiritual.
  Every person has a soul, every soul has 3 elements -
  (i) Reason
  (ii) courage
  (iii) Appetite (passion)

  Every soul is not same, some are Superior Souls and
  some are inferior souls.
  He creates "Myth" of Metals. He classifies people
  into 3 categories on the basis of qualities of soul.
  (i) Persons in whom Reasons Dominate he called
      them men of gold.
  (ii) Persons in whom courage dominate is men
      of Silver.
  (iii) Person in whom Ap petite dominate is men
      of Copper.

      Reason - तर्कशक्ती / Rationality
      Appetite- natural desire / passion - भूख, इच्छा,
