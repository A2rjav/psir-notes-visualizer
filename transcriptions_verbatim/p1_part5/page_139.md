Dialectics in Hegel / Concept of Dialectics
Idea of dialectics was 1^st given by Socrates as a
Means to understand the truth. It is a process
through which our beliefs get converted into true
knowledge. It is a process to arrive at the truth.
Hegel has expanded the concept of dialectics.
According to Hegel, Dialectics is a process of change
in History. History moves in the dialectical manner
what does dialectics indicate?
Dialectics indicate contradiction.
Hegel has given dialectical idealism. It means
History moves in the form of dialectics of ideas.
Hegel has given 3 laws of dialectics.
(1) unity of opposites
In every creation of God in universe exists its oppo-
sition. For every idea there is a opposite idea.
It is the presence of contradictions which is a moving
force of History.
(2) Negation of Negation
The direction of change is such that negativity gets
negated. It is a progressive view of History. Every
next stage is a better stage.
(3) Quantity changes into Quality
changes keep on happening.
A point comes when change become so much that
it appears as if it is a qualitative change.
