● __Social Contract__      :.
① __Human Nature -__
"Life of a man is a continuous search of Power,
After power which ceases only with his death...
The object of man is not to enjoy once but to
be assured forever & that is why the life will
be continuous search for power."
By using Resolutive compositive method ~~Hobbes has~~
shown that man is utilitarian by nature. All
human actions are shaped by the movement of
particles in Brain.
man is not in a position to comprehend the move-
ment of particles in the brain of other person.
Hence man is individualistic by nature. Since
man is utilitarian, man will seek those things
which give him pleasure.
Hobbes brings forward the dilemma of Limited
resources & unlimited wants. Till man is alive his
Apetite for pleasure does not end. The moment
he satisfies one Apetite the search for the other
starts. People may get pleasure from different
objects. However, the means to get pleasure is
same. The means to get pleasure is power.
Hence there will be a competition for power.
Hence the life of man is a continuous search of
power after power which ends only with his
death. Death symbolise end of the movement
of particle, end of Apetite. It is because of
utilitarian nature of man that man is mate-
rialistic possessive & individualistic. Since we
cannot understand the movement of particles
in the mind of other person, other person is
a source of insecurity and discomfort :
