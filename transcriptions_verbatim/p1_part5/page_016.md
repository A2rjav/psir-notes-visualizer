① Theory of Ideas.
"Reality is the shadow of Ideas."
__Context of Theory.__
Plato wanted to make Athens an ideal state.
In order to make Athens ideal state
we should have an idea of ideal state.
Idea can be understood only through
the use of reason. philosophers have reason.
So, only philosophers can establish the ideal
state. Thus theory of idea also justifies the
institution of philosopher king.

__Bases of Developing Theory of Idea -__
(i) On the Basis of theory of knowledge
(ii) On the Basis of Theory of Soul.
