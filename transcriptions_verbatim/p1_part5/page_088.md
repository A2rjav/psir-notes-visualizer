__Hobbes Theory of Sovereignty__ -

__What is sovereignty__ -
- Sovereignty is supreme power. Hence, in or a
  particular territory & the people living on that
  territory there can be only one sovereign & i.e.
  state.
  __Bases of state sovereignty__
  Man has transferred all of his natural rights except
  the Right to life.
  __What are the characteristics of sovereignty__
① __Permanence__ - It's a permanent transfer of power
  from people to the state.
② __Indivisible__ - sovereignty cannot be devided.
  There is only one sovereign.
③ Sovereign has ultimate power to make laws.
  According to Hobbes, law is command of sovereign.
  In medieval times it was not clear as to which
  law will prevail. There were multiple sources of
  law. Eg. Customs, church & multiple associations.
  Hobbes has established that the law made by
  state will be supreme. Thus, even the religious
  laws & church is also under the state.
  Nobody is allowed to disobey the law. State
  has power punish those who do not follow the
  law made by state.
  __what does it implies if person does not follow__
  __the law of the state.__
  The person is trying to claim the sovereignty
  back. Not following the law means having its
  own law. This is not permissible. There cannot
  be more than one sovereign. State will have
  police power to punish that person. If one
  person does not follow the law & goes unpunished.
  others will also do the same.
  Man will follow law so long it fullfills ~~it~~ his interest,
  man will defy law if law is against his interest.
  Hence, state has a police power. Life of everyone
  cannot be pushed back into the state of Anarchy
  because of few persons.
