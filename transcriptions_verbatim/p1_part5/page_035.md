• __Should Plato be called as fascist__
Karl Popper called Plato as 1st Fascist.
According to Popper, Plato's political programme
was far from being morally superior through
fascism, it was identical to fascism.
In his book 'open society and its enemies'
he called Plato, Hegel & Karl Marx as the
enemies of open society. In the 1st volume of
his book title, 'the spell of Plato' he proves
that the Plato was Fascist; Plato was enemy
of democracy & supporter of totalitarianism.
-> Popper criticises Plato on three grounds:-
@ (i) Plato's hatred towards __democracy__.
(ii) Plato projected democracy as worse form of
state. He called democracy as the rule of

(iii) Plato held that anyone can become ruler in
