Q "The History of all Hitherto existing society lies
  has been history of class struggle."
  In Marx materialistic Interpretation of History,
  Economic structure forms the basic structure. The
  most imp. component of the basic structure is the
  relations of producⁿ or concept of class.
  According to Marx, in all stages of History there
  have been two classes - Haves & Have not,
  Exploiter & Exploited. Eg. there were two classes in
  slave society, master & slave. Similarly lords &
  serfs in fuedalism & workers & capitalists in
  capitalism. ~~The relations bet~~
  The relation between classes are of the class
  struggle. Reconciliation of the interest is not possible
  between the exploiter & the exploited. Class struggle
  has been the moving force of the history. Class
  struggle betⁿ capitalists & workers will result into
  the communist revolution. Communist revolution will
  ultimately result into the establishment of commu-
  nism. In communism classes end, class struggle ends.
  common ownership of means of producⁿ is established
  contradictⁿ betⁿ man & society ends. Contradicⁿ
  betⁿ liberty & equality end. Since contradiction
  ends History ends. Thus Karl Marx suggest that
  class struggle is inevitable in a class devided so-
  ciety.
  The above discription of History though claimed by
  Marx as scientific can be considered as deductive
  rather than scientific. Real purpose of Marx was
  to inspire working classes for revolution & hence
  he constructed a particular view of history. Marx
  above view of history is influenced by Darvin's
  Theory of struggle for survival/existence. Karl
  popper in his book open society & its enemies
  charges Marx for committing the guilt of Historicism
