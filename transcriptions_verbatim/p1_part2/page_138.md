SC is a guardian of FRs. At times it become diffi-

U/A 122 there is a restriction of judiciary w.r.t the

⑤ Hate Speech.
uptill now there is no law defining what would consti-
tute a hate speech. Hate speeches are very common
during elections there is no concrete parameter to deter-
mine hate speech. SC in Pravasi Bhalai sanghatan Vs
Union of India case has directed law commission to come
up with the definition of Hate speech & to prescribe
punishment. Law commission has recently submitted its
report where it has defined hate speech. It has also
suggested certain amend ments in IPC prescribing puni-
shment on Hate speech.

⑥ Freedom of Press.
Unlike USA freedom of Press is not explicitly mentioned
In India it is a part of Freedom of speech & Expression.
In Brijbhushan case, SC explained following features
of the right:
① there is no censorship on media except on movies.
② freedom of press include freedom to collect information,
publish & circulate.
③ In involves freedom to decide the volume of publication.
