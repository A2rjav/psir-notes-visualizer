Ramchandra Guha Advocates empowerment of
people, proper valuation of Natural Resources &
knowledge dissemination. In their another book,
'Ecology & Equity' they have highlighted the use
& abuse of nature & in contemporary India &
have offered the critic of developemental model
followed since independence.
Ramchandra Guha has identified 3 strands in
Indian environmental movement.
(i) Crusading Gandhians-
They challenged the developemental model & suggest
alternative approach based on Indian tradition.
(ii) Appropriate Technology -
It represent those sections which focus on techno-
logical solution to ecological issues.
(iii) Ecological Marxists -
They are working at the grass root level & support
radical democracy.
—
We can say that it is unfortunate that today it is
believed that technology is a solution to human pro-
blems but technology cannot be a substitute to the
political view & social action. What is required is not
change in technology but change in value system.
