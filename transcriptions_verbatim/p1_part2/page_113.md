③ Golaknath case, 1967
Significance of year 1967.
Down of the congress system -

17th Amendment Act was challenged in Golaknath case
Significance -
1st case where Judiciary has applied the doctrine of
prospective overruling. It is also considered as the 1st
case of Judicial Activism.
opinion of Judiciary -
Judiciary held that the term law used in Art 13 (2)
will include amendment also.
What is the implication?
Even by amendment FRs cannot be abridged.
Analysis of SCs decision.
Judiciary was accused of judicial activism on beha
lf of which & destroyed the harmony which is
present b/w Part III & Part IV.
Response of the Govt?
