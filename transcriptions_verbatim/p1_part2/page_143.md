-> Whether death penalty should be abolished.
There should be some principle of punishment.
There are 3 theories of punishment.
(i) Retributive Theory ie. Revenge.
Revenge should not be basis in civilised society.
Feeling of revenge is understandable from the perspe
ctive of the family members of the person who
has been killed.
However there are examples where family members
themselve requested to commute death sentence.
Sentiment goes/ends with time.

(ii) Deterrence Theory
- there is no correlation b/w deterrence & death penalty
& deterrence will not work for those who are ready
to sacrifice their life. Deterrence does not work in
India for two reasons:
(i) India is a soft state.
(ii) when sc has given rarest of rare doctrine there is a
little scope for deterrence.
