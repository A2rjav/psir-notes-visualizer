② Article 141
As per Article 141 the __law declared by SC__ shall be
binding on all courts within the territory of India.
It means constitution empowers Judiciary to declare
the law. Above provision emplies that so long legis
lature does not make the law, the law declared
by SC will be treated as law. Eg. Till parliament
has not brought the law on sexual Harassment ie
of women in workplaces, Vishakha guidelines
issued by SC were to be treated as law.

● Powers of Supreme Court.
① __Article 129.__
As per Art. 129, SC is a court of record. As a court of
record the orders of SC are to treated ^as law in all courts.
& as a court of record it has power to punish
for its contempt.
