Pratap Bhanu Mehta mentions about 'Post Demo-
cracy'. Post Democracy means strengthening of the
power of non-elected institutions like SC, Electi-
on commission & CAG.

The scholars like Pratap Bhanu Mehta take extremely
critical view about the SC in his article 'India's
Judiciary the promise of uncertainty'. He calls
Judiciary as self perpetuating paradoxical & highly
politicised institution.

Lavanya Rajmani, is also critical of Judiciary as she
suggest that judiciary in India prefers to play the
role for which it is ill equipped & neglects the role
which is required to play.
