FUNDAMENTAL RIGHTS

-> concept of Fundamental Rights:
The idea of Fundamental Rights is based on the recogni-
sing the sphere of sovereignty of the Individual & limi-
ting the sphere of state's sovereignty.. thus Fundamental
rights check the arbitrary exercise of the state's power
& ensures the rule of law. The Fundamental Rights
are based on the the philosophy of Natural rights.
According to which rights are not confered by the
state & they are rather confirmed by the state

Indian scheme deviates from the original idea in
some context.
(i) In Part III it is not just prohibition against state
are mentioned but also contains ~~clauses for~~
clauses for the intervention
(ii) Unlike other countries Fundamental Rights are protec-
tions available not only against the state but also
against the society.
(iii) In India certain Fundamental Rights are also made
available to non-citizens.

-> Scheme of Fundamental rights.
(i) Article 12 - Definition of state
(ii) Article 13- Judicial review.
(iii) Article 14-18- Right to Equality.
(iv) Article 19-22- Right to Freedom
(v) Article 23&24- Freedom from exploitation
(vi) Article 25-28 - Freedom of Religion
(vii) Article 29-30 - Cultural & Educational rights of minorities
(viii) Article 31- repealed - Right to Property
