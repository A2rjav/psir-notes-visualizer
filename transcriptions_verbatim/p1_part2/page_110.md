What is there in Part III
Part III guarantees 6 FRs. They are primarily
civil rights based on the philosophy of Liberalism.
FRs are given a priviledged position because there
also right for enforcement of FRs. Article 32
Art. 13 gives explicit powers to Judiciary to declare
any law null & void if it abridges or take away
any of the rights given in part III.

What is there in part IV
Part IV also contains many rights of social & econo-
mic nature which aims to establish India an ega-
litarian society. They are inspired by socialist vision.
As per Art. 37 they are not enforceable in the court
of law, yet they are 'Fundamental' in the gover-
nance & it is a constitutional obligation on the
govt. to give effect to these directives in the poli-
cies & laws. Unlike FRs there is no right to consti-
tutional remedies for enforcement of rights given
in Part ~~IV~~. These rights have no automatic enforce-
ment.
