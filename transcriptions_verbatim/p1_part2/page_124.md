Prof. Andre Beteille & Pratapbhanu mehta are critical
of Reservation Policy. They have resigned in protest of
Govt. policy of introducing reservation in higher
educational institutions. They resigned from National
Knowledge commission. On the other hand scholars
like Yogendra Yadav & satish Deshpande are not
against Reservation. Prof. Yogendra Yadav recogni-
sed that it was not a wasteful journey. However
he believes that Reservation policy needs to
be revised. According to Prof. satish Deshpande there
~~Pro~~ is a lack of understanding w.r.t the purpose
of Reservation policy. It is often understood as an
employment guarantee scheme or poverty alliviation
scheme.
According to Prof. Amit Thorat, Reservation should not
be understood as an Employment Guarantee scheme
or Poverty Alliviation scheme. The ~~basic~~ basic purpose
of Reservation is that it is policy tool to end preju-
dices against certain communities which have been excluded.
The purpose of reservation in India is to give repre-
sentation to those sections who have not been
present in Administrative or political systructure
because of caste system. In previous centuries
there was reservation in favour of upper
caste in Education & employment.
