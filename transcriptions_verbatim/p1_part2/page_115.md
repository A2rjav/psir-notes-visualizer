What is Doctrine of Basic Structure?
Parliament has power to Amend the constitution
to whatever extent but such amendment shoul
not destroy the basic structure of the constitu-
tion.
What is the logic?
According to Judiciary there should be difference
b/w Amendment of constitution & re-writting the
constitution. Amendment cannot be done in such
a way that original constitution is not found
anywhere.
