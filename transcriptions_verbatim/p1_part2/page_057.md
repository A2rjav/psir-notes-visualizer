-> Short Note - Role of Opposition.
Opposition is an integral component of the Parlia
mentary form of Govt. In parliamentary system opposi-
tion enjoys certain rights & priviledges like Right to
ask questions, bring motions, opposition acts as a
check & balance. Recognising the importance of
opposition, opposition in Britain called as His majesty,
opposition. In India also there is a statutory recogni-
tion to the leader of opposition in both the houses.
He enjoys the status of the minister of cabinet rank
Leader of opposition is included in the screening co-
mmittees in the appointment of important posts
like Lokpal, NHRC chair person. Role of opposition
has not been satisfactory. Initially opposition lacked
strength. In the congress system opposition was actin
outside the margins. With the breakdown of congress
system the strength of opposition started increasing
in numbers.
