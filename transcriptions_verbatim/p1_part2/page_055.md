World Bank has given 6 parameters to evaluate
the working of legislature:

(i) Financial
Parliament is a supreme institute of accountability
of the Govt. Hence, parliaments financial accounta-
bility or cost to the country has to be evaluated.

(ii) Compliance
Parliament is a supreme institution of law making in
the country. There is need to assess how much parlia-
mentarians comply with the rules of procedure which
they make themselves for the conduct of Parliament.

(iii) Efficiency:
We have to evaluate the efficiency of parliament in
terms of quality of work, time invested & cost
involved.

(iv) Effectiveness.
How much presence of the institution felt in the country

(v) Relevance
How much qualitative improvement the institution is
bringing in the life of the people.

(vi) Sustainability.
Is institution is ^in rise or decline?
