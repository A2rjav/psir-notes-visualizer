Western scholars were apprehensive about the possibi-
lity of success of parliamentary Democracy in India
from the very beginning.
Why they were apprehensive?
The type of social & economic conditions required for
the success were absent. one of the earliest work
on Indian Parliament & Parliamentary Democracy is
of Morris Jones. Morris Jones in his book 'parlia-
ment in India' give following reasons for his appre-
hensions against working of parliamentary Democracy:
(i) Western in origin
(ii) Lack of enough experience.
(iii) scale of diversity
(iv) social cleavages which automatically result into
adverserial democracy rather than consensual democracy.
(v) In traditional society like India power becomes very
important for survival so there will be an extreme
competition in politics.
Morris Jones has highlighted the following weaknesses
of the parliamentary Democracy during the time of
Pandit Nehru.
(i) Lack of opposition in the country,
(ii) Lack of independent thinking among back benchers.
(iii) close relationship b/w Govt. & Party.
(iv) Concealed Dictatorship of Nehru.
(v) Social environment in the country.
