Q. Discuss the appropriateness of parliamentary Democracy.
should we shift towards Presidential System or Direct
Democracy.

Q. What do you understand by decline of parliament.
what are the causes of decline of parliament & the
consequences.

Constituent Assembly has considered all the 3 options.
for the form of Govt to be adopted in India.
Gandhian's were proposing Partyless democracy.
Gandhi was critic of political parties because they
persue unethical politics. They persue party interest
in the name of National Interest. Parties results into
concentration of power.
However, Modernists like Nehru & Ambedkar, rejected
the idea of Direct Democracy. They also rejected the
Proposal for Presidential system & prefered represen-
tative democracy & parliamentary form of Governance
for following reasons:
(i) Experience with the working of parliamentary system.
(ii) Parliamentary system is more accountable.
Govt is accountable to legislature on day to day basic.
(iii) Parliamentary system is more democratic because
Parliamentary system results into cabinet form of
Govt. or collegiate executive or plural executive
(iv) Parliamentary form of Govt better considering India's
diversity because different groups can be represented.
