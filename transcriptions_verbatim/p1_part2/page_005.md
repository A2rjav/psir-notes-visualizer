__What is Grassroot Democracy?__
Grassroot Democracy is the essence of Democracy.
It is an empowerment of people at the grassroot
level. Grassroot democracy aims at empowering
the powerless & giving voice to voiceless.

The idea of Swaraj given by Gandhi, radical democra
cy given by M. N. Roy & Antodaya given by Dindayc
upadyaya are Indian versions of Grassroot Democracy

__Why Grassroot Democracy?__
Advantages:
(i) __Good Governance:__ It is not possible without
community participation. Good Governance require
transparency & accountability.
(ii) __Sustainable Developement:__
Agenda-21 adopted at Rio summit 1992 recognise
the necessity of community participation.
(iii) __Political Education & Trainning:__
Democracy depends upon active citizenship. Hence
institutions have to be created for the participa-
tion of people they have to be given capacity to
make meaningful participation.
