Application -
1st case where Judiciary has applied the doctrine of
Basic Structure was Minerva Mills case, 1980.
What was challenge?
42^nd Amendment Act was challenge.
What 42^nd Amendment did?
It was considered as a mini-constitution.
SC has declared following Articles Null & Void.
(i) Art. 368 (4).-
Any Amendment made under Article 368 shall not
be challenged on __any ground in any court__
(ii) Art. 368 (5).
For clearing all doubts, there will be no limitation what-
soever on the amending power of parliament.
Judiciary has declared above two as null & void.
(iii) 42^nd Amendment Act made amendment in Art. 31(c)
now, protection was extended to all directives &
not just 39 B & C. Judiciary restored the situation as
it was existing under 25^th Amendment Act.
