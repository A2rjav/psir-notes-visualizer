Sunita Narayan of centre of Science & Environment
have also analysed Indian environmental movement
She categorises Indian Movement as 'Utilitarian
Conservatism' & Western movement as 'Protectionist
Conservatism! She gives the concept of 'New Environ-
mentalism'. It is wrong to think 'Environmentalism
is Anti-developement'. New Environmentalism req-
uires that we look at developement & people differe-
ntly. According to her, Environment is not about prote-
cting Tigers or planting trees it is about Democracy.
we have to have the model of developement which
make people as custodians of the Natural resources
New Environmentalism calls for ideas without dog-
mas & idealism which is practical. She has highlighted
the following weaknesses of Environment movement
The biggest weakness is 'Fire-fighting Approach'. They
have not been able to strategiese themselve. Environ-
mental activists may have been sucessful in closing
few plants or industrial units but they have not been
sucessful in changing the developement policy in India
Till environment does not become the issue of electoral
democracy, its impact is going to be limited. She also
mentions the challenges faced by the environmental
movement in India.
(i) lack of resources
(ii) lack of expertise
(iii) Bureaucratic Apathy
(iv) Corruption
(v) Crony capitalism (mining barons are MLAs & MPs)
(vi) Global Environmental rules are now been shaped
    by countries of north rather than south.
