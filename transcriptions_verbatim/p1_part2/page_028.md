What is the role of Judiciary ?

In a Democratic country like India Judiciary is consi-
dered as the Institution which ensures that Demo-
cracy does not get converted into tyranny of
Majority Hence, Judiciary is given the power of
Judicial Review which ensures the rule of law
in the country.

Nature of Judiciary under the Indian constitution:

According to Graville Austin once political Revolution
was over in 1947. India started with the 2nd
revolution ie. social & economic revolution. According
to Pratap Bhanu Mehta, constitution of India is itself
a revolutionary document. constitution became the
basis for transformation of traditional society into
a modern society. Indian revolution was supposed
to be led by the state. According to Pandit Nehru
constitution of India does not envisage a passive
Judiciary. It actually looks at __Judiciary as a active__
__participant in India's social revolution.__

What are the grounds to suggest that Indian
constitution does not create passive Judiciary:

① Article 36.
In Art. 36 Judiciary also comes within the definition
of the state.

__Note__
Passive- accepting or allowing what happens or what others
do, without active response or resistance
