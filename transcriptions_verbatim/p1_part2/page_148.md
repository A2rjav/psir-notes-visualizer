According to Sanjiv Baruah Regionalism is natural for the
subcontinental size polity like India.
According to Paul Brass, Regionalism has been the long term
trend of Indian politics.

India has faced regional movements of various intensities:
ranging from the demand for autonomy from the seperate
state to a Union. In contemporary times the major challenge
is to deal with the regionalism in Kashmir as well as in north
eastern states.
Different scholars have given diff. interpretaⁿ, one of the major
cause has been uneven developement, culture of scarcity,
Modernisation in traditional society & politics of opposition.

Atul Kohli in his article 'can democracies accomodate ethnic
movements' suggest that ~~ethnicity~~ democracy has been the major factor
for ethnic mobilisaⁿ but believes that only democracy has a
solution.

two contrasting examples. In case of Tamil sub nationalism
there has been a resolution of the conflict on a permanant
basis because of democratic politics of accomodaⁿ. In case of
J&K resoluⁿ has not been that easy because the situaⁿ of
Kashmir is extremely complicated. Kashmir issue is a multi-
dimensional issue & there has been too much involvement of
foreign powers. In case of Kashmir as well as Punjab govt.
have to rely on excessive use of force. ~~Excessive~~ use of force
in turn has created greater crisis of legitimacy. In case of
