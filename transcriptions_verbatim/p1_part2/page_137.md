④ Priviledges of Parliamentarians
Parliamentarians are given certain priviledges so that
they can work without fear or obstruction. constitutio-
mention Freedom of Speech & Expression as a privile-
dge of Parliamentarians & also suggest that parliament &
state legislature should codify priviledges.
Problem:
No attempt has been made to codify priviledges so far
what is the consequence?
At times priviledges has been invoked to curb the
freedom of press. Parliament has power to punish
for its contempt as well as for breach of priviledge.
Solution:
codification of Priviledges so that boundaries are well
defined. It would be easier for judiciary.
