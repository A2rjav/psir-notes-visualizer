• DOCTRINE OF BASIC STRUCTURE-

What is doctrine of Basic structure?

It is a judicial innovation of SC of India which makes
SC of India as the strongest court in the world.

Why?
No other court including USA SC has power to challen-
ge the amendment.
Why?
Judicial Review implies that Judiciary struck down any
legislative & executive act which is not as per the
constitution. However, in case of basic structure judiciary
can challenge the provisions of constitution added by
amendment. ~~Amen~~ Amended provisions are also part
of constitution. It is like Judiciary challenging constitu-
tion itself. Judiciary assuming the role of constituent
assembly. Whereas nowhere Judiciary has given such
role as judiciary is least representative & least accoun-
table. Art. 368 gives ~~poa~~ constituent power to the
parliament parliament can make amendments in
the constitution by adopting procedure as given in
Art. 368.
