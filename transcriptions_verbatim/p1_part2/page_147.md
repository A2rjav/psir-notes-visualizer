Q. Keeping in view the political developements in India
Explain how the need for National Integration & urge for
regional autonomy can be reconciled.

ANS- Need for National Integration.
Nation is a contested concept.
According to Liberals nation is evoluⁿ linked with modernisation,
whereas for Marxist Nationalism is an invented tradition & for
cultural nationalists Nation is natural & spiritual.

Nationalism is a Western concept which became the basis of
achieving solidarity among the different groups following
different religions. Post colonial societies Nationalism in general
and India in particular is considered as one of the consequence
of the colonial rule. Nationalism still remains a challenging
concept in post colonial societies. In case of India Nehru
believed that India is Nation in making. Secularism & fede-
ralism, centralised planning has been adopted as a method
of Nation Building.

The need for National Integration in general & for India in
particular- ① India has suffered years of slavery or
because of lack of internal unity which made India vulnerable
to external threats. National Integraⁿ is a precondiⁿ for deve-
lopement & good governance so long we donot adopt the idec-
logy of Nation first we will never be able to realise our potential.
India has a potential to emerge as a major power. Hence, Nat. Inte-
graⁿ is not just a developemental concern but also a strategic
concern. However since beginning the movement of India towar-
ds the path of Nat. Inteⁿ has not been smooth, castism, commu-
nism, regionalism, linguism, has always challenged the emergence
of India as a nation.

Regionalism is a major challenge because it is expressed in terri-
torial terms. Regionalism not only challenges the idea of India but
it also challenge the existence of India in concrete sense.
