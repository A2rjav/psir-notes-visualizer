(2) Defamation.
Defamation is a ground mentioned in Art. 19 (2).
section 499 & section 500 deals with defamation.
Defamation is both a civil wrong as well as cri-
minal offence. However in most of the countries
it is not a criminal offence. In civil wrongs defa-
mation is decided on the basis of tort law or
case to case basis.

Damages can be awarded on probabilities.
Section 500 mention the penalties in case of
criminal offence. It prescribes for minimum impri-
sonment of 2 yrs or more with or without fine.
However, damage has to be established beyond rea-
sonable doubt. It is an offence of compoundable,
bailable & non- congnicible nature. mere filling of
FIR will not result into arrest.
SC in 2016 in Subra Manyam swami case has upheld
the validity of section 500 of Ipc.
