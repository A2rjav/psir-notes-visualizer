According to Economic Survey, 2016-17 there has been
a remarkable transition from closed & listless eco-
nomy to open & thriving economy. Progress is not
only qualitative it is measurable. Survey has evalua-
ted liberalisation on the basis of 4 standard measure
(i) Openess to the trade,
In comparison to size, India is trading with interna-
tional economy more than expected.
(ii) Openness to foreign capital.
Despite capital control the net inflow of foreign capital
is as normal as any other emerging economy.
India has recieved FDI at annual rate of $ 75 billion
which is not far short of what china recieving at the
