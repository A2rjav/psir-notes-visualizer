(ii) __Tackling corruption__
corruption of any branch of Govt. is unacceptable,
corruption of Judiciary is unthinkable. It is unfortu-
nate that corruption in Indian Judicial system has
increased many times it is not just limited to
subordinate judiciary but it is not reaching to
higher Judiciary. Eg. In Gaziabad Plovidend fund scam
many Judges of HC & even SC have been accused.

(iii) __Institutional Reforms__
There is a need to reform the system of appointment
and removal of judges. There is a need to increase
the no. of courts. There is need to strengthen the
capacity building programme of Judges.

-> __Whose responsibility of Judicial Reforms?__
(i) Union Govt.
(ii) Judiciary.
(iii) Bar Associations
(iv) Civil society.

(i) __Responsibility of Union Govt.__
The biggest responsibility is of Union Govt. to create
infrastructure, provisions for capacity building of judges
review outdated laws, reform criminal justice system,
re police reforms, reform code of civil procedure &
criminal procedure, effective Judicial impact assess-
ment, recruitment of judges, judges to population
ratio in India is lowest. It is around 10.5 judges per
million population.
