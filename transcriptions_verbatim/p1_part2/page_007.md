What are Panchayats?
There is a lack of clarity wrt the Panchayati
Raj Institutions & the purpose of these institu-
tions.

Since beginning Panchayats are seen as the agencies
of rural developement. Though Art. 40 projects
Panchayats as an institution of self govt.
__73^rd Amendment Act, defines panchayats as the__
__institutions of self govt. for rural areas.__
State act explain power, functions & responsibilities
of Panchayats The prime responsibility of pancha-
yats is:
ⓐ Prepare plans for social & economic developement of
rural areas.
ⓑ Implementation of schemes for social & economic
developement of rural areas.

Thus, Panchayats role has been made limited only
as developement agencies they have not been given
any police functions or regulatory powers.
