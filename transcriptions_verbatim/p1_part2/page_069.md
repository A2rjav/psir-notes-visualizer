Q What are Civil Rights ?
Rights which are considered necessary for civilised
existence are called as civil Rights.
Eg. Right to Life, liberty, property, Equality before
law, Right against arbitrary arrest.
Civil rights are based on philosophy of liberalism.
Basic purpose of the Civil Rights is to check
arbitrary exercise of power by executive & to main-
tain rule of law.

Indian freedom Movement was not just a freedom
movement or a national movement it was one
of the largest civil Rights movement.
Early nationalists demanded basic freedoms like
freedom of Press, better representation in assemb-
lies. Agitation against Rowlatt bill was also an example
of civil rights movement. Pandit Nehru was one of
the prominent civil rights activist. On 7th November, 1936
Indian civil liberties union was established at the
initiative of Pandit Nehru.
