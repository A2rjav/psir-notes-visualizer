Situation in India-
- Paradoxical
India is ranked even lower than Afghanistan in terms
of freedom of journalists. there is a loss of editorial
freedom. there are incidents of use of force & violence
against reporters & journalists..
- on the other hand media in India have become 'self
perpetuating entity'. commercialisation in media has
created a nexus among politicians civil servants, corpo-
rate groups & criminals. Mainstream Media has become
'eco-chamber of elites' ie. why there has been enormo
us growth of social media. In order to curb the free
dom of social media govt. has brought IT Act section 6
66 A which is now declared null & void in Shreya Singhal
Case. was considered as having chilling effect on freedom
of speech & expression.

Levenson Report of British House of Lords mention
many dis-functional features of Mainstream Media
which is even applicable for India. TRAI (Telecom Regula
tory Autority of Ind.) have also brought a report on media
highlighting the misuse of powers by media

It is to be understood that media enjoys power because
media had revolutionary role in the struggles of com-
mon man. media has rights not for itself but to serve
the people. It is for this reason media is called as
Fourth estate (4th pillar of democracy).

J Pullitzer has held that corrupt & self perpetuating
Media is a dangerous entity. Hence, media should understand
its social responsibility.
