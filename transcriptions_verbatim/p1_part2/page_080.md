According to Sunita Narayan, Environment is not just
about ecology it is about social ecology.

Vandana Shiva and Jayant Bandopadhyaya in their
Article 'Political economy of ecology movements' suggest
that developement activity in India proceeded on
'resource intensive path it has seriously disrupted
ecological stability & life support system. which have
been maintained through centuries. It is in this
context that environmental movements have emerged
in India.
