- Reservations in Promotions-
In Indira sawhani case, SC held that Reservation
should be only at entry level & not in promotions.
Govt. has brought 77th Amendment Act to give Res-
ervation in promotions for members of SC, ST
community. 77th Amendment Act was challenged in
Ajit Singh case, SC held that Reservation in Promo-
tions is permissible but govt. should introduce
catch up Rule. Govt. Brought 85th Amendment Act
to nullify catch up rule & provided for reserva-
tion with consequential seniority. 85th Amendment Act
was challenged in Nagraj case, SC accepts the validi-
ty but held that reservation in promotions with
consequential seniority can be permitted if Govt.
provides quantifiable data of under representation.
Govt. has to take into consideration the guidelines
mentioned in Art. 325 related to "Administrative
Efficiency".
Previous Govt. has proposed 117 constitution Amend
ment bill to nullify SCs judgement in Nagraj case
It would have ended the requirement of collect-
ing data & the riders present in Article 335.
