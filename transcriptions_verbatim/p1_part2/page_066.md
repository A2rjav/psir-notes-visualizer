Role of Prime Minister in coalition Government

Evolution of Institution of PM in India-

Nature of the Institution:

In a Cabinet form of Govt. PM is considered as the
captain of the ship of the state. He is considered as
keystone of the Cabinet Architecture. He is appointed
by the President but other ministers are appointed
on his recommendation. Other Ministers are on the
pleasure of President which means pleasure of PM.

In practice we find two models of the relationship
b/w PM & COM.
(i) Primus inter pares - (1st among equals)
In case of India when Pandit Nehru was PM &
sardar Patel was Home Minister the situation of
Pandit Nehru was of 1st among equals.

(ii) Inter stellas Luna Minores - (moon among stars)
After the death of Sardar Patel the position of Pandit
Nehru was like moon among stars because there was
no other minister who had the charisma at par
with Nehru.

The concentration of power in the office of PM kept
on increasing even after Pandit Nehru. As position
of PM kept on becoming stronger the position of
COM kept on becoming weak. It was during L.B. Shastri,
the power started ~~to~~ shifting to PMO from cabinet
secretariat.
