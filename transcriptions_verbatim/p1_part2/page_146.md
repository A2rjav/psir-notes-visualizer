the success of democracy in India is a matter
of great curiosity considering matters of
obstacles like diversity, poverty, backwardness &
literacy. Credit for the success goes to constitution.
We have to accept the exceptional wisdom of the
Constitutional assembly. If we see the case of other
countries in South Asia, constitutions have changed at
a short period of time. Indian constitution survived
because it is a consensus document. It has reconciled
alternative perspective. Though the primary philosophy
shaping govt institutions is liberalism yet other per-
spectives are also present. The best place to locate
alternative perspective is part IV of the constitution
It contains the combinations of ~~the~~ view points like socia-
lism, Gandhism, multi culturalism, Environmentalism &
international peace. Versatality of the constitution
& flexibility which it provides made constitution a
'corner stone of a nation'.
