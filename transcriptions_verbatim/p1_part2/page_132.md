● RIGHT TO FREEDOM

→ Freedom of Speech & Expression. (Art. 19)
Art. 19 contains six freedoms. None of the freedom is
absolute there is a reasonable restriction on all. The
sub clauses determine the conditions for putting restric-
tions. Only legislature & not executive can impose
restriction. Court has power to challenge the reaso-
nability of law. thus the doctrine of due process
of law is inherent in Article 19.
