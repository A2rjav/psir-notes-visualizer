__Article 32__
- According to Ambedkar Art. 32 is soul of part III.
- Art. 32 is a part of Basic Structure.
- Art. 32 makes SC sentinel qui vive ie soldier with the sword.
- SC has introduced -
@ PIL, epistolary jurisdiction (post card), suo moto
- SC has defined PIL as Public Interest Litigation rather than private or political interest. Person should approa-
ch not only with clean hands but with clean mind & clear
heart. PIL is an important Judicial innovation to pro-
vide access to justice to the poor misuse will create
problem in the objective.
- While SC grant writs under Art. 32 it applies the
Doctrine of Res judicarta, Doctrine of Laches(unnece-
ssary delay) It means that court will protect the
right of those who are vigilant about their rights &
so person should approach to the court without delay.
