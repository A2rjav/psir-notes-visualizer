● Human Rights Movement in the country.
From 1980s around the world there has been growth
of the consciousness of Human Rights. In case of
India also a new phase of activism started. Civil
Society working with Judiciary has led to the beginning
of new phase of Human Rights Activism. Activist
judges like P.N. Bhagwati institutionalise PIL. PIL
proved a revolutionary step in providing access to
justice to the marginalised section.
SC which has been ~~eal~~ earlier reluctant w.r.t. Directive
Principles became the champion of social & economic
rights. It has given wide & substantive interpretation
to right to life to include rights like Right to Edu-
cation, Health, clean Environment & Right to life not
just animal existence but to live with dignity.
Globalisation has increased the no. of advocacy
groups in India. Govt. of India have also established
institutional mechanisms like National Human Rights
Commission, RTI.
