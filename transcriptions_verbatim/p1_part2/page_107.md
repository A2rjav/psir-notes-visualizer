→ Doctrines of Judicial Review : -
  ⓘ Presumption in favour of the constitutionality of
     the law.
  ⓘ No Suo Moto application of Judicial Review except
     in case of enforcement of FRs.
  ⓘ Doctrine of severability - entire law not to be declared
     invalid Eg IT Act , Anti- defection law.
  ⓘ Doctrine of eclipse - coming under shadow.
     Whatever law when you look under light of constitution
     looks dark → null & void.
  ⓥ Doctrine of Liberal Interpretation. Menaka Gandhi
  ⓥ Doctrine of Literal Interpretation. AK Gopalan
  ⓥ Doctrine of Procedue established by law.
  ⓥ Doctrine of Due process of law.
     ⓐ substantive due process
     ⓑ Procedural due process.
