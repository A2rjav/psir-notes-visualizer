Why stagnation?
Internal as well as external reasons.

(i) Internal Reasons:
As soon as panchayats came into existence its
positive outcome started appearing. This has made
state leadership & bureaucracy insecure.
Why state leadership was insecure?
(i) New grassroot leadership started emerging, traditi-
onal elites became apprehensive.
(ii) In India federation is tilted towards union, States
are often called as "Glorified Municipalities",
Since states are expected to transfer funds, functions &
functionaries, if they transfer, they will be loosing
the significance. Hence, till the time union does not
devolve powers, states will be reluctant.

Why Bureaucracy became apprehensive?
Bureaucracy has not been just non-supportive, it has
been "Hostile" towards the panchayats. It amounts
the shifting of power & resources from the hands of
Bureaucracy into the hands of people's representatives.
One of the reason for failure of panchayat is from
non-co-operation from Bureaucracy.
