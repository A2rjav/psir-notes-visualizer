RESERVATION POLICY

According to Ramchandra Guha, Reservation Policy
generates more heat than light.

According to Pratapbhanu Mehta, Reservation Policy
diminishes our sense of self. Because our opi-
nion in public is very different than our opinion
in private.

According to prof. Yogendra Yadav Reservation Policy
has reached dead end & it raises more question than
answers.

Above statements show that reservation policy is
a matter of 'high politics'.

• Constitutional scheme w.r.t. reservation-
① Reservation in Parliament & state Legislative Assembly
② Reservation for the members of SC, ST community
as well as for women in Panchayats & Municipalities.
③ Reservation for members of SC, STs communities in
educational institutions (including private institutions
excluding minority institutions).
④ Reservation for SC, ST community in public sector
employment
⑤ Reservation in public sector employment with
consequential seniority in promotions.
⑥ Reservations for OBCs in educational institutions
as well as public sector employment.
