● Types of Land Reforms -

(i) Zamindari Abolition:
This the only successful aspect of land reforms.
However success is formal because most of the
zamindars got heavy compensation become tenants &
utilised self cultivators & invested capital in setting rural
industries like Rice Mills.

(ii) Tenancy Reforms:
Idea was to give security to tenants. Tenancy reforms
are also comparatively successful. The most successful
effort has been in kerala & operation BARGA in west
Bengal. Tenancy reforms benefited intermediate caste.

(iii) Land ceiling:
land ceiling was very crucial for land distribution but
land ceiling remained one of the most weakest aspect.
Laws with loopholes came into existence. People pro-
tected their land by dividing joint families, benami
transfers & even gave formal divorce to their wife
to protect land.

(iv) Distribution of surplus land:
Distribution of surplus land was the most important
aspect of land reform to address poverty. One of
the vision of land reform was land to tillers.
