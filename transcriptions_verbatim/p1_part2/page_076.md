Phase I- 1970s
In his book 'The unquiet Woods' he traces the origin
of Modern environmental movement to chipko
movement in 1970s in central Himalaya
where women played the prominent role.
Environmental movement have added a new dimen-
sion to India's democracy. It also opposes ideo-
logical challenge to the dominiente motions of
meaning, contents & patterns of developement
in India. In 70s Environmental movement was
seen as Interloper. At that time India was aspi-
ring to catch with the western countries. Conserva-
tion of nature was considered as the luxury of
rich nations. Guha also calls 70s as an age of
Environmental innocence. During 70s Environmentalists
like Sundarlal Bahuguna, Chandiprasad bhatt were
seen as CIA agents.
Phase II- 1980s
80s is marked by the growth of Environmental
consciousness. Enviromental issues started getting
media attention. prominent Environmental journalists
like Anil Agarwal, Nagesh Hegde & Shekhar Pathak
could bring the attention of the Govt. In 1980 the
Govt. of India has set up ' Dept. of Environment'
which was later on upgraded into full fledged ministry.
