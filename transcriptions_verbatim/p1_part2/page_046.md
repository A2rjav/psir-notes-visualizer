Ambedkar rejected the proposal as he suggested that
there cannot be Imperium in Imperio which means
state within state. There has to be a checks &

Which system was accepted?
Appointment by executive. But executive was not given
free hand to check politicisation & for the sake of
Professionalism. Hence, appointment only after the
consultation with the members of Higher Judiciary.
Thus constitution maintain a fragile balance though
it seems tilted towards Judiciary. Balance would depe-
nd primarily on the intentions of the executive.

system in Practice?
In Practice the system where Judges appoints judges
have come into existence & the role of executive
has been reduced to the rubber stamp.

Evolution of Collegium system -
(i) Immidately after Keshvanand Bharti judgement,
Justice A.N. Ray was appointed as CJI supercee-
ding 3 senior most judges.

(ii) In 1977 Justice Baig was appointed as CJI superceeding
Justice Khanna.

(iii) Above two actions of the executive were seen as an attempt
of an executive overreach. though there was no prohibition
on executive & even law commissions had suggested that
seniority alone should not be the basis for appointment
of CJI rather merit should be the main criteria
