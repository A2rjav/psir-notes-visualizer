● Nature of the Institution in India -
Nature of the Institution in India differs from the
nature of the institution of British Monarch for
following reasons:
(i) Britain is Monarchy & India is Republic-
(ii) Britain is Unitary Form of Govt. & India is Federation.

Hence, in case of India because institution is elected
we cannot say that it will be completely neutral
as in case of Britain. Similarly, president is also the
representative of states at union level. Indian insti-
tution deviates from British institution in following
ways:
(i) Elected
(ii) In Britain king enjoys absolute autonomy however in
case of India President can be impeached for the vio-
lation of the constitution U/A 61.
