→ What was the consequences?
Dispute emerged b/w the 1st President & the 1st PM.
over Hindu Code Bill. President Dr. Rajendra Prasad
was not in favour of Hindu Code Bill, he asserted
that Indian Constitution does not create a rubber stamp
President as in case of Britain. He asserted that
President has discretionary powers & President has
the responsibility to defend the constitution as he
can be impeached. Ultimately controversy was resolved
with the intervention of 1st Attorney General M.C.
Setalvad who held that in India President do not ha-
ve any real power because we have adopted parlia-
mentary form of Govt.
Later on SC in its judgement in Samsher Singh Case & in
Ram Jawaya Case held that President & Governors are
mere constitutional heads.
