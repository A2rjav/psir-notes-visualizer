SC is reluctant to bring the office of Chief Justice
of India within the scope of Right to Information
Judiciary has also not been supportive of Govt.
Proposal like "Judicial Standards & Accountability Bill"
in the name of Judicial Independence. Judiciary has
to enforce discipline among the judges & take correct
ive steps.

Role of Bar Associations -
one major reason for excessive delays is the malpra-
ctices by the lawyers. There is a need that Judiciary &
Bar Association together formulate model code of con-
duct for lawyers. There is a responsibility of civil soci
ety to promote legal awareness, strengthen alternati
dispute redressal system ie lok adalats. Civil Society
should provide legal aid.

Other suggestions include the introduction of more no
of Fastrack courts, E Governance & capacity building
of judges.

Reform in system of Appointment of Judges in
Higher Judiciary -

Constituent assembly debates -
Constituent assembly considered 3 options.

(i) Appointment by executive, which is most common
practice around the world.

(ii) Appointment by executive & ratification by legislature
as found in USA. It was rejected because of the
possibility of politicisation.
