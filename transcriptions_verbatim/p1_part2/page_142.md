[Margin: (ii)] In Machhi sing case, SC has suggested in what
situation rarest of the rare to be applied. Death
Penalty to be given only in a situation when it
appears that Life Imprisonment would not be appro-
priate considering the crime from the perspective of
Justice. However above doctrine creates the scope for
discretion of the judge & will vary from judge to judge.

[Margin: (iii)] SC is trying to humanise death penalty, In 2014 there
are 3 important judgements.

[Margin: a] Shatrughan Chauhan case by which SC held that in case
of unnecessary delay in mercy petion SC can commute
death sentence into life imprisonment. There should be
a gap of 14 days between rejection of mercy petition
& execution. Person should be informed & legal aid
has to be provided for his defence. He should not be
kept in solitary confinement & family members should
be informed.

[Margin: b] Mahammad Arif case Vs Registrar SC of India.
SC has tried to bring transparency in review petition.
Now review petition will be considered in open court
& defence lawyer can put a defence arguement.

[Margin: c] PUCL VS Govt of MH case. SC has issued guidelines
w.r.t. encounters.
- In case of encounter if the accused is alive he should
be given immidiate medical aid.
- case has to be registered
- The sample of Gun powder, weapon, of both policemen
& accused will be sent for forensic analysis.
- Investigation by CID or any other police station.
- No award or promotion to policemen till the
final disposal of appeal.
