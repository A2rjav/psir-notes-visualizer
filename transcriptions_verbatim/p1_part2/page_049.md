Global Trend-
In most of the countries there is a shift towards
an independent appointment system. Eg. Australia,
canada, SA; UK.
Govt. of India has also brought 99th amendment Act
which has been struck down by Judiciary.

• 99th Amendment Act:
→ Purpose of NJAC-
(i) Appoint ment of CJI
(ii) Appointment of other judges of SC
(iii) Appointment of Justices of HC
(iv) Transfer of Justices of HC.
→ Functions -
NJAC will prepare memorandum of procedure which
will determine the parameters for the appointment
w.r.t. merit & integrity.
→ Composition
(i) CJI as a chairperson.
(ii) Two seniormost judges of SC.
(iii) Law Minister
(iv) Two eminent persons
→ Process of selection of eminent persons-
- selected by 3 member committee comprising of PM,
leader of opposition & CJI
