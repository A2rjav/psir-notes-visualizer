Q What is Judicial Activism & How Judicial Activism
differs from Judicial Overreach.
Discuss the desirability of Judicial Activism in
Indian situation.

→ __What is Activism?__
The concept of Activism can be understood by applying
the concepts of chemistry. Electron moving in its
orbits gets extra energy & jumps into orbit of the
other. Similarly in case of institutions when they
get extra energy & go beyond where constitutionally
mandated limits & interfere into the orbit of the
other is called as Activism by that branch. Thus
Judicial Activism is when judiciary goes beyond the
constitutional mandate & intervenes in sphere of
legislature & executive. Judicial Activism is not
unique to India it is a universal phenomena. Simi-
larly Activism is not unique to Judiciary. In India
we have seen Activism by executive, legislature, EC,
CAG at different times.
