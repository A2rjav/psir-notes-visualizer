-> __Judicial Review -__
It is the ordinary function of Judiciary. Otherwise it
will be called as Passive Judiciary. However, when
Judiciary does not limit itself to the task of
Judicial Review & also suggest what should be the
law & starts taking the responsibility of implemen-
tation it can be called as Activism. One of the
extreme example of Judicial Activism has been the
Doctrine of Basic structure given in Keshvand Bharti
case. In KB case, Judiciary has gone beyond the
constitution, Judiciary has invented the concept of
Basic structure & has assumed not just the role
of legislature but the role of constituent assembly.
Similarly the way judiciary has interpreted the term
consultation in context of appointment of judges &
has declared 99th constitution Amendment as invalid.

There is a difference b/w Judicial Activism & Judicial
overreach. However, the difference is subjective
Today Judicial Activism has developed consent
in its favour. Those actions of Judiciary for which
there is consent are called as activism whereas those
actions where people feel that judiciary should not
have overstepped can be considered as overreach.
It will vary from person to person. Eg. w.r.t the
Judiciary's Decision on Inter-state river linking
