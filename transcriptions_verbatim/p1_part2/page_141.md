[Margin: [bullet]] Article 21.
Related to Right to Life.

[Margin: Q.] Do you support the view that capital punishment or
Death Punishment should be abolished in India or it
should be retained to be used only in case of heinous
crimes like Terrorism. substantiate your view.

[Margin: Q] Discuss the approach of SC w.r.t Death Penalty.

__Global Trend__:
Global Trend is in favour of abolishing death penalty.
Recently more than 140 countries have voted in favour
of ressolution putting moratorium on death penalty.
Govt of India voted against the resoution.

UN Declaration of HR- directs countries to ~~punish~~
abolish inhuman punishment.

International criminal court which have been established
for the trial of crimes like Genocide & crimes
against Humanity does not prescribe death penalty.
The Maximum punishment is life imprisonment.

Verma committee set up to give suggestions for ame
ndments in criminal laws did not support Death
Penalty for the crime of rape.

SC-
SC is also against death penalty & has been ~~to~~
humanised capital punishment.

[Margin: (i)] In Bachan singh case SC has given the doctrine of
rarest of the rare. It means award of death penalty
is an exception rather than rule.
