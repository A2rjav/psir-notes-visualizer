Evolution of Panchayats:
phase I - 1960s - phase of Enthusiasm

phase II - 1970s & 80s - phase of stagnation.

phase III - 1990s - phase of Revival

phase IV - 21^st century - phase of disillusionment

Manishankar Ayyar committee 2012 has suggested
that disillusionment is prevailing with the working
of panchayats. Committee also suggest that the outco-
me of 73^rd Amendment Act has been the decentra-
lisation of corruption. rather than Democratic Dece-
ntralisation It suggest that we have introduced Bad
Panchayati Raj which is worse than no phanchayati
Raj.
