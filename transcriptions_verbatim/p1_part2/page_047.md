-> Cases related to the appointment of Judges -
(1) Sankalchand case, 1977
In this case SC was to interpret the meaning of the
term consultation used in Article 217 dealing with
transfer of Judges of HC.
In this case SC held that consultation does not mean
concurrence.

(ii) S.P. Gupta case, 1981
It is known as 1st judges case. SC again held that
consultation is not concurrence.

(iii) SC advocates on records vs UOI case, 1993 this is
known as 2nd judges case. SC overruled its view &
held that consultation means concurrence.
It gave primacy to the opinion of CJI. CJI was to
give opinion after consultation with 2 seniormost
judges.

(iv) 3rd Judges case - Presidential Reference to the SC, 1998
U/A. 143 neither SC is bound to give nor Govt is
bound to accept but SC took the undertaking as
a pre-condition to give opinion whatever will be the
opinion it will be binding on the executive.
Features of Judgement:
(a) consultation mean concurrence.
(b) CJI could consult 4 seniormost judges.
(c) For appointment concurrence of atleast 3 judges is
needed which must include CJI. to create the balan-
ce it was mentioned that if 3 judges disagree &
CJI & one judge __agree__ then also name will not be
recommended.
