many posts remain vacant in HC & SC, Because of lack
of consensus b/w Judiciary & Executive. Govt. has to
increase physical infrastructure as well as manpower
Govt. in India has been an Active litigant It is good
that govt. has brought new litigation policy which
restrict govt. being active litigant. Govt. need to
take steps to create Gram Ngayalaya's. Though Gram
Nyayalaya Act has been in existence since 2005
yet only handful of Gram Nyalaya's have been
created. Govt. should actively promote alternative
dispute redressal system. Govt. should also promote
legal literacy & strengthen legal aid. There is need to
introduce plea bargaining as suggested by Malimat
committee. There are many areas where Govt. & Judiciary
need to work together Eg. reducing no. of vacations for
judges, system of appointment & accountability.

-> __Role of Judiciary__ -
Judiciary should also take steps to reduce the no. of cases
which are pending. Judiciary can restrict itself from
accepting large no. of appeals & specially under special
leave petitions. At times Judiciary has been accused
of doing activism at the cost of its normal business.
Higher Judiciary should take steps to check the cases
of corruption. On one hand Judiciary takes initiative to
bring transparency & accountability in the functioning
of Govt. but on the other hand Judiciary is relucla-
nt w.r.t its own accountability.
