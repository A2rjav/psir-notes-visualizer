(3) Contempt of Court
U/A 19 (2) ~~Contempt~~ Contempt of court is a ground restrict
freedom of speech & Expression. Both SC & Hc are court
of records & have power to punish for the contempt.
In 1971 parliament has passed contempt of court Act
defining the scope of contemp:
(1) Civil contempt
It means willfull disobedience to the order.
(2) criminal contempt
It means interfering in the course of justice, geopardising
the dignity of the court.
Problem:
(i) Contempt of Court power has been used to check any
criticism of courts judgement & to protect erring judges.
(ii) It has been used against media.
(iii) In 2006 contempt of court Act was amended & it is
suggested that truth in the speech is a self defence.
In Mugaonkar case, sc has issued guidelines w.r.t.
the use of contempt of court. It will be done only
when there is a malafide intention to jeopardize the
dignity of the court. It will not be used to protect
erring judges.
