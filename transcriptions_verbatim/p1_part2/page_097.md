● Land Reforms And Green Revolution.

Land Reforms
Purpose:
① Eradication of rural poverty & hunger.
Philosophy of land reforms:
Based on socialist idea of redistributive justice.
History of Land Reforms:
There was a huge participation of peasants in national
movement & land reform was one of the promises
of congress.
According to Daniel Thorner, India is the most intere-
sting case study of land reforms considering the largest
body of land reform legislation & extremely large no. of
poors & the magnitude of hunger.
What does land reforms indicate in India.
In case of India land reforms meant institutional
reforms (state led) aiming at addressing the concentra-
tion of wealth & giving land to landless.
Constitutional provision.
Directive Principles Art 39 (B&C) makes it a constitu-
tional obligation on Indian state to observe concentra-
tion of wealth & economic resources.
