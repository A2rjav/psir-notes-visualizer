• Present status-
Bill has been lapsed, thence the ruling in Nagraj
case is applied. Recently SC has struck down a
action of Karnataka Govt. providing reservation in
promotions without having quantifiable data.

• Reservations in super speciality jobs & research
institutions like ISRO, DRDO.
In Indira Sawhani case, SC held that no reserva-
tion in above situation. But in PGIMER chandigarh
case, SC has permitted reservation in superspeciality
Again AIIMS case, SC has stayed the reservation
in superspeciality.

• Quantum of Reservation:-
In Indira Sawhani case, SC held that Reservation
beyond 50% will go against equality of opportunity
But where there are compelling reasons some rela-
xations can be granted. Presently TN Govt. policy of
giving 69% reservation is subjudice.

• Reservation in Private sector-
National commission for Backward classes has recently
recommended union Govt. to introduce reservation
in private sector.
Why?
(i) Rolling back of the Public Sector.
(ii) Private sector also takes lot of benefits of Govt.
services like tax Holidays.
