24th Amendment Act, 1971 has made it mandatory
for the president to give assent to the amendment
bill.
42nd Amendment Act further settled the controversy
by Amending Article 74 which made it mandatory
for the President to act as per the advice of COM.
thus, clearly establishing that in a parliamentary form
of Govt. is a figure head & he is supposed to be a
rubber stamp.
According to constitutional experts 42nd Amendment
act has done the right amendment w.r.t the office
of President. According to James Manor, Constituent
Assembly lacked the understanding w.r.t the nature
of the institution they are creating & they have left
on time to settle the controversy & expected that
healthy conventions would develope. 42nd Amendment
Act had put conventions in Black & white.
42nd Amendment Act, also brings clarity as to what
does it mean when constitution mentions impeach-
ment of President for violation of Constitution. In
case of President violation of constitution means
when President does not act according to the advice
given by COM headed by PM. It is to be noted that
as per SC parliamentary form of Govt is the basic
structure of the constitution.
