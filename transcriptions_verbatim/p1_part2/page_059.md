-> Role of President according to constitution of India -
According to Art. 54 President is the Head of union
Executive. All executive actions of the union are taken
in the name of President. President shall take actions
in accordance to the constitution.

President According to the Theory of Parliamentary
form of Govt:

In a parliamentary form of Govt. there are two head
(i) The Nominal head/ Titular Head
(ii) Real Head.
-> Why two heads?
(i) There is a uncertainty w.r.t. the tenure of the Govt.
hence for the continuity of the Administration
President is required.
(ii) Since Parliamentary system is based on party politics
it is necessary for symbolic porposes to have a head
who can be treated as neutral like Queen in Britain.
