Judicial Activism has acted as 'safety valve'.
we can imagine a situation where parliament is
in decline. Executive is either not fullfilling its
duties or acting in a arbitrary way, If judiciary
has also closed its doors the only option left with
the people was revolution. Judicial Activism in
India has protected:
(i) Sanctity of the constitution.
(ii) Strengthened Human Rights.
(iii) Tried to adress corruption.
(iv) Filled the gaps when there was policy paralysis.
(v) Tried to bring electoral reforms
(vi) Has strengthened substantive justice in context of
Right to education, Right to food.

According to Bhikhu Parekh, India emerges as a case
of 'Judicial co-governance' He also suggest that th
India is an example of Judicial sovereignty.
P.B. Mehta, suggest that India is an example of
post democracy where there is a greater consent
for non-elected institutions.

To conclude we can say that Judicial Activism had
benefited the country but it does not mean that
country should be run by judges. Judicial Activism
can be a medicine but it cannot become a daily.
bread.
