__Phase III__
In 1990s there was growth of professionalism
in Environmental Movement. The positive aspect was
Professional scientists & social scientists started taking
~~as an~~ Environment as an area of research. Indian
Institute for Science in Bangalore started centre
for ecological science. On the other hand there has
been the growth of NGOs including foreign NGOs.
Environmentalism got more complicated. Environmen-
tal degradation has increased many times with
the introduction of New Economic Policy. Reforms
have been neither pro poor nor pro environment
& it is the major reason for rise of left wing
extremism. Environmentlists where earlier attacked
as CIA agents but now dismissed as 'fold socia-
lists Guys'. 1
It is unfortunate that ministry of Environment made
project clearances of formality resulting into high
level of air pollution & depleted Ground water.
According to Ramchandra Guha, 'Polluted skies,
dead rivers, disappearing forests, displaced pea-
sants & tribals is what we see around us.
40 years after chipko Movement. India today is
a Basket case of Environmental tragedies. In his
book the Fissured Land' & Ecological History of India'
co-authored with Madhav Gadgil.
