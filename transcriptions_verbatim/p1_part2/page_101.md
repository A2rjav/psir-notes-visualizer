__GREEN REVOLUTION:__

$\rightarrow$ __Purpose:__
Purpose was to make India self sufficient in
the production of food grains.

$\rightarrow$ __philosophy:__
Production centered approach based on the philoso
phy of capitalism. Which results into the use of
high yeilding variety seeds, fertilisers, pesticides,
implements thus, it is capital intensive. It also
cause intensive use of land.

Green Revolution started in 1961 as intensive agri-
cultural Developement Programme (IADP) ~~later oc~~
Initially 14 districts were selected on experiemental
basis & lateron extended to 114 districts in the
form of Intensive Agricultural Area Programme in
1965.
