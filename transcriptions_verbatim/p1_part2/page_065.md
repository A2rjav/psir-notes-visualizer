A.P.J. Abdul kalam was not just an activist president
he was populist president. It is not expected for the
President to have such a high level of engagement
with the public. It may result into the develope-
ment of alternative centre of power.

- Constitution of India has created a very delicate
balance b/w President & PM. PM is the real centre
of power & President's position is the position of
dignity. According to Bage Hot there are two exe-
cutives in British system:
(i) Real Executive
(ii) Dignified Executive

According to wage Hot, the role of queen in British system
is to act as Friend, Philosopher & Guide of the Govt.

Article 78 of the Indian Constitution established the
fine balance. It mentions the duties PM towards
the PM.
