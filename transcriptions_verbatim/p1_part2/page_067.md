With Indira Gandhi the Institution of PM started
developing the features of the Presidential form of
Govt. She has undermined the com & had greater
reliance on personal advisers. It was one of the
factor for weakening of the congress party. Trend
continued under Rajiv Gandhi. Congress Party
suffered largest no. of defections. Since 1989, we see
the beginning of coalition Govt at the union level
as a regular feature. Coalition govt is a fragmented
Govt. Coalition is a bargain. Coalition govt. weakens
the position of PM. In coalition Govt. even the small-
est fraction can punch beyond ~~way~~ weight. Accordi-
ng to M.P. Singh, ~~in~~ in the coalition politics there is
no one PM but many PMs.
The position of the former PM becomes weak he is
reduced to the manager of the coalition.
Institution is shaped by no. of factors like personality
factor, Political environment & culture. Though coalition
govt weakens the position of PM but it is not nece-
ssary. Lot depends on the personality & the political
skills. We can give the example of Vajpayee who mana
ged large no. of parties & has provided the 1st exam-
ple where coalition Govt. completed full term. Even
PM Manmohan singh could provide great leadership
and could run the Govt. for complete term in UPA-1.
