HEADING: What are the conditions which give rise to Judicial Activism?

-> What are the conditions which give rise to
Judicial Activism?
(i) Written constitution:
(ii) If a constitution contains the scheme of FRs.
(iii) If a country is a part of Global HRs Regime.

-> What are the unique factors in India?
(i) overreach by executive.
(ii) Decline of Parliament.
(iii) Coalition Politics
(iv) Passivity on the part of other two branches of Govt.
(v) Globalisation
(vi) Growth in corruption
(vii) Civil society Activism.
(viii) Emergency & violation of Rights.
(ix) Constitution of India itself provides for Activist Judiciary
(x) Political culture where judges enjoy lot of respect.
(xi) Personal preference of Judges.
(xii) Indian public romanticising Judicial Activism.

-> Idea of Judicial Activism.
Judicial Activism should be differentiated with similar
concepts like Judicial Review.
