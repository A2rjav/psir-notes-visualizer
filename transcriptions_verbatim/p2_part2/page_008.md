● Features of Traditional comparative politics:

[Diagram:
                      As a discipline

      before    ←          II WW          →    after
         ↓                                       ↓
scope- constiⁿ of west countries             New states emerged
         ↓                                       ↓
Method- traditional                           Modern- Behavioural]

The traditional comparative politics concentrated its
focus on the study of western countries. Because
non-western world was colony of western countries.
They lacked independent political system.

Traditional comparative Politics was studied in tradi-
tional manner the major approach applied was
‘legal institutional approach.’

● Features of Modern Comparative Politics:
Focus shifted from the study of western countries
to the study of non-western countries or develo-
ping areas. Not only the scope of subject changed
even methodology changed under the influence of
Behaviouralism. Maximum utilisation of Behavioural
method has been in the discipline of comparative
politics.
