1. Pressure Groups are called invisible empires.
- PG perform the role of interest articulation.
- comparison of PG politics is one of the prominent
area of investigating in modern comparative politics.
- Gabriel Almond has given following classification:
Institutional, Associational, Non-Associational, Anomic.
- Associational PG are interest based PG they have
greater presence in the developed countries.
- Anomic PG are crowd Behaviours & they are more
prominent in developing countries because Democracy
is not well institutionalise.
- According to Samel P. Huntington, Developing countries
suffer from Political Decay as participation of people
increases coherence & adaptability of political insti-

decay we see more Anomic responses.
