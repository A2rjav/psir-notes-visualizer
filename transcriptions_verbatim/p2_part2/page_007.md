Q. What is comparative Govt. & Politics or what is
comparative Method. Discuss the evolution of the

● What is comparative politics ?
                                            science
comparative Politics is as old as Political ~~philosophy~~.
Aristotle is known as Father of comparative politics.
Aristotle was influenced by his father who was a
physician. Aristotle has applied the method of classifi-
cation found in Biology, w.r.t. the study of constitu-
tion. Aristotle has studied 158 constitutions & have
classified them on the basis of number & purpose.
Aristotle's theory of constitutions is one of the earliest
work in comparative method.

In comparative politics scholars make comparison b/w
Domestic Political system.

Evolution of comparative politics ?
It evolved in two phases:
① Uptill 2nd World War (Traditional comparative Politics)
② Since the end of 2nd World War (Modern comparative
                                   Politics).
