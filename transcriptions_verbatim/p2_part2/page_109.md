(3) Critical School or Emancipatory vision.
scholar- Andrew Linkater.
Source of Influence- Habermas
concept- Radical Democracy or Grassroot Democracy.

According to Linkater, Problems of wars are because of
the concept of `territorial nation states. Hence, the solu-
tion is to make territorial boundaries irrelevant. He suggest
to create new moral boundaries. He also believes that only
by empowerment of masses at the Grassroot level one can
find solutions for peace.
