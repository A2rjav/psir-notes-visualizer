● Political Culture Approach -
Scholar- Gabriel Almond, Sydney Verba.
Book - Civic Culture

• What is Political Culture ?
• What is culture?
People's values, norms, orientation is culture
Since culture is a long term trend we can use it as a
variable for comparison.

• Political culture is a sub set of culture. Political culture
denotes people's values, norms, orientation w.r.t. the
political system.

• Almond & verba have used Easton's Input output mode
& Max Webers ideal type to develope the models
of political culture. They have given following models:

(i) Parochial Political culture:
• Here people are indifferent towards the political
system. Eg. Political culture of Tribes living in the
Andaman can be considered as Parochial towards
Indian Political culture.

(ii) Subject Political culture.
• People behave like a subject rather than citizen.
they feel they are not significant as far as input
is concerned but they are influenced by output or
policies. Political culture of most of the Third world
Democracies have been subject political culture.

(iii) Participant Political culture.
People play active role not only as a subject but also as
a citizen. Political culture of western countries used
to be participant political culture. At present times
there is a growth of participant political culture in
India.
