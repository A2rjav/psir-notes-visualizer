② __Deterrence :__

__What is deterrence?__
Realist Approach to Peace.
Deterrence comes from the latin world "de terrere"
which ~~means~~ to frighten.
Deterrence is a policy to frighten potential adversary
so that he does not attack. It is a psychological concept
based on game theory. It is assumed that actors are
rational. It depends on convincing the adversary-
(i) No chances of winning.
(ii) In case adversary wins then the cost of the victory
is not worth paying.

Deterrence automatically leads to arms race. Deterren-
ce is a very old concept. In recent times, Nuclear de-
terrence has assumed new relevance.
