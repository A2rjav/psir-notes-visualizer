-> Role of NGOs
AS old as MNCs. Still there is a dominance of
NGOs of western countries.
Art. 71 of UN charter recognise the role of NGOs as
consultative bodies
Transnational NGOs like Aministy NGO, Redcross have
presence in more than 130 countries. there role have
been recognised in International Peace as they have been
Awarded Nobel Peace Award

MNCs NGOs & Govt of western countries work in close
co-operation. China , Russia & at time India has accused
Western NGOs working against the interest of these
countries. Interference in Domestic Political Affairs.
MNCs channelise their funds through NGOs rather than
Govts of developing countries.

-> Role of terrorists -
Role of terrorist organisations is also very old. Insurgents
have been used by the state in Europe to destabalise
empire. Communist govt. have also used left wing guerillas
for expansion of communism. After 2nd World War poli-
tics of terrorism started in West Asia since the creation
of Israel. Once Arab Nations failed to defeat Israel in
open war they started using non-state actors Eg. PLO.
(Palestenian Liberation Org.) (earlier) for proxy wars.
USA has been termed by Noam Chomsky as the first
terrorist state in the world. USA has used Non-state acto-
rs to destabalise communist govt. in Afghanistan. Proxy
war was adopted by USA against USSR. Almost all major
powers have used non state actors as a means of
foreign policy. Realist scholars consider them as proxies
