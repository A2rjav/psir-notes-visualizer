Contribution by Robert Jervis -
Robert Jervis has given 4 situations to explain
the intensity of security dilemma.
Situation is shaped by the nature of environ-
ment prevailing ie. Geography, Technology, cal-
culations by policy makers (foreign).
[Margin: Lecture 7th
| 09 | 04 | 2017 |]

Jervis gives 4 scenarios in context of intensity of
security Dilemma -

① When offensive & defensive behaviours are not distingui-
shable, offense seems to be advantageous . security Dile-
mma is very intense , Environment is doubly dangerous,
~~Env~~ Possibility of Arms races is high , co-operation is low ,
Status Quoist states will behave in agressive manner.

② When offensive & defensive behaviours are not distingui-
shable, defence appear to be advantageous. Security
Dilemma is intense but not as in the first case.
Possibility of Arms races is not that high, state can
increase its security, without threatening the security
of others.

③ When offensive & defensive behaviours are distinguisha-
ble, offense seems to be advantageous , security dile-
mma is not very intense. However security issues
exist , Environment is safe, agression is possible.

④ Offensive & defensive behaviours are distinguishable,
defence is advantageous, security dilemma is not
very intense, environment is safe, little danger of
offensive option, defense budget can be reduced.
