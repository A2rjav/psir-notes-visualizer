• Views of Joseph Stiglitz-

Stiglitz has written two books 'Democracy
and its discontent' & 'Making Globalisation Work'.
In above two books Stiglitz have highlighted how
Globalisation have impacted Inter state & Intra state
inequalities. He has also highlighted the Financial
crisis in SEast Asia, S. East Asia Russia, Mexico. He depi-
cted the Democratic Deficit in the institutions of Glo-
bal Governance. He suggested to adress democratic
deficit to make Globalisation work. In his recent article
Globalisation & its new discontents he has highlighted
that how Globalisation has started impacting people in
developed countries. According to Stiglitz Globalisation
has benefited 1% of Plutocrats in developed countries
It has adversely impacted the middle classes & working
classes. According to him, median income of full time
male worker in USA is actually lower in real terms
than what it was 42 years ago. At the bottom level
the present wages in real terms are comparable to
the level existing 60 years ago. Not just income is
moving in wrong direction every thing else is movi-
ng in wrong direction including heath & educational
standards. Globalisation has benefited rich section
in developing country. It has benefited middle classes
in emerging economies. It has adversely impacted
the poors.
