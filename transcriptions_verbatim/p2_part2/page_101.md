Exponents -
Based on the ideas of Immanuel Kant.
1st formally presented in 14 points speech of
Woodrow Wilson.
It has been USA's vision to build liberal world order
since USA involvement in world Affairs.
Evolution of Liberal World order -
John Ikenberry has given the evolution of Liberal
world order in Three phases.
(i) liberalism 1.0 -> When after 1st World war League
of Nations was established & for the 1st time the
idea of collective security came into existence.
However, It could not continue for long as USA had
to go back from world Affairs & European Nati-
ons reverted to old politics.
(ii) Liberalism 2.0 -> After 2nd World War
Eg. Establishment of UN, IMF, WB, UNDHR
-> spread of Liberal world order-
It remained limited to the western world. It was
challenged by the 2nd World and 3rd World
(Communist & ~~NAM~~ NAM resp.)
(iii) Liberalism 3.0 -> emerged in its strongest form
after end of cold war. Eg. Establishment of WTO,
UNHRC, ICC (International Criminal court) Region-
al organisations
