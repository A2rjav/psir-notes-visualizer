● Modern Comparative Politics.

Modern comparative politics started after 2^nd World
war. As many countries of non-western world
got independence & they established their own
political systems.

As new number / large no. of countries came into
existence. It was realised that traditional method
will not work. We have to go beyond the study of
constitution.

Why?
In western countries we do not found the gap b/w
theory & practice but in non-western world there
is a big gap b/w theory & practice. In most of the
countries constitution exist but constitutionalism does
not exist. Hence, it become essential to understand extra
constitutional factors like Nature of society, culture,
stage of Economic developement, level of moderni-
sation.

Thus, Modern methods like systems Approach, stru-
ctural functional Approach, political culture, political
sociology, political economy have come into existence.

Today comparative politics is a vibrant discipline.
And large no. of researchs are going on.
