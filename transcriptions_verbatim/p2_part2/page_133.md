__collective Security in Practice-__
(i) collective security was one of the main objective of
UN But it remained paralysed because of East-West
conflict.
(ii) During cold war the only example of collective security
is korean crisis of June 1950.
(iii) However USSR vetoed the collective security operation
in case of Korea which stopped the action.
-> Uniting for peace ressolution; or Acheson Plan.
If Security council is in deadlock 2/3^rd of the members
of General Assembly can authorise collective security
action. However, USSR never accepted the legality of
uniting for peace ressolution. Later on it became
even more difficult to operationalise collective security
even through uniting for peace ressolution.
-> UN Peace Keeping.
Peace keeping is not a part of UN charter. It was a
brain child of the then UN Security General Dag Hamme
rskjold.
