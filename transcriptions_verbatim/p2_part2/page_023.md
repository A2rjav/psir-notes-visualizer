• Origin & Evolution of the discipline-
  Adam Smith is considered as the father of modern
  Political Economy. His book 'the Wealth of Nations'
  is considered as the first textbook of Political
  Economy. Adam Smith defined political Economy as '
  science of a statesman ~~dea~~ willing to bring prospe-
  rity to the nation. Adam Smith was a critic of
  Merchantilism & supporter of laissez faire state.
  Against the school of Adam Smith emerged the
  Marxist school of political economy. Karl Marx has
  offered critique of Political economy of the school
  of Adam Smith & suggested that Adam Smith's
  policies will bring only wealth to few & not wea-
  lth to the nation as a whole.
  Thus there emerged two major schools of political
  economy:
  (i) Liberal school.
  (ii) Marxist school.

  In Liberal school there are two schools:
  (a) Classical & Neo Liberal
  (b) school of welfare economics of social liberalism
