• Nature of State in Advanced capitalist societies.

-> Introduction - 
Political science begins & ends with the state.
Though Political science begins & ends with the state
syet state remains a contested concept.
Nature of state in advanced capitalist societies can
be analysed from different perspectives:
(i) Liberal Perspective
(ii) Marxist Perspective

Analysis of state from Liberal perspective:
For Liberals the state is neutral arbiter of conflict
ing interest. State is the product of will of the
man. In advanced capitalist countries initially the
State was minimal / police state But since 1930s
onwards we see welfare state.

Analysis of state from Marxist Perspective:
Marxist challenge the neutrality of the state & suggest
that state is a class institution. Within marxist
perspective exists two perspectives.
(i) Instrumentalists
(ii) Relative Autonomy / structuralists.
