• __Pressure Groups:__                    [Diagram: lecture 4 | 04/04/2017]

Pressure Groups are called 'invisible empires' by Finer.

Pressure Groups perform the role of 'interest articulation'

Pressure Groups are found in both Democratic & non-
Democratic society, but nature of pressure groups
may vary.

• __Types of Pressure Groups:__

① Gabriel Almond's classification.

ⓐ Institutional - Civil servants, Army Professionals
ⓑ Associational - FICCI, ABVP
ⓒ Non-Associational - Kshatriya Mahasabha
ⓓ Anomic - Jalikattu Protest

ⓐ Institutional Pressure Groups.
They are most powerful in developing countries. Because'
most of the states are overdeveloped states. In developed
countries the role of Govt. is minimal.

ⓑ Associational Pressure Groups.
They are purely interest based like Students/Trade union.
They will be more prominent in the western countries.

ⓒ Non Associational Pressure Groups.
They will be more prominent in western countries

ⓓ Anomic Pressure Groups.
They will be more prominent in non-western countries.
