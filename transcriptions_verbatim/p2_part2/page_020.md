(ii) __Weberian__
Max Weber was critic of Karl Marx & supporter of
capitalism. He has challeged following assumptions of
Karl Marx.
(a) Economic structure is a determinant of all that happens
in superstructure. He calls Marxism as mono causal
explanation. In his book 'Protestant Ethics' & 'Rice of
Capitalism' he has shown how protestant ethics led
to the rise of capitalism.

(b) He does not support Marx view that as capitalism
will advance society will get polarised into 2 classes.
On the other hand he held that there will be proli-
feration of classes (growth of middle classes).

(c) Max Weber was admirer of capitalism & considered
it as most rational.

Thus a new school of Political sociology starts with
Max Weber. Contribution of Max Weber include
the ~~the~~ method of ideal Types, The concept of Au-
thority, Theory of Bureaucracy, The concept of
Political Parties.
