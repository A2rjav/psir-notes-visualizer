Is it good if there is Democracy in Pakistan ?
There are two schools of thought :
(i) When army is in power they will feel more secu-
re & relations will be more stable.

(ii) If Democracy comes in Pakistan it is good for India
because civil society of Pakistan is the only hope
for India . However, it should be Democracy in
real sense.
On its part India should not make any gesture
about strengthening of Democracy there because
it will make army insecure & there can be a roll-
