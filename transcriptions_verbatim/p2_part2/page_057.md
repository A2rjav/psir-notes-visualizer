What was balance of power?
States have to ensure that the power of one stat
does not grow to such an extent that it threatens
sovereignty & Integrity of other.
How States will maintain Balance of Power-
(i) Internal Balancing- Increasing its own capability
    Military , Economy
(ii) External Balancing- Joining/making alliances with
others.
