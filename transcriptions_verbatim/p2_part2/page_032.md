- according to Instrumentalist Perspective State is a
executive committee of Bourgeoise class(Karl Marx).
Ralph Miliband has analysed the nature of state in
post capitalist societies. According to him even
welfare state remains the instrument of the
capitalist class. Economic power remains concen-
trated & the class which controls economic power
also controls political power.

Habermas has predicted that welfare state will
suffer from, " legitimation crisis".

- Structuralists Approach, Relative Autonomy Approach,
suggest that in certain situations state ceases to
be a instrument of the dominant class.
In context of welfare state pulantzas has adopted
relative autonomy approach, According to Pulantzas
because of the competitive party system state cea-
ses to be the instrument of particular class. However
in the time of crisis state will always work in
favour of the rich.

- Elitists Perspective
C Wright Mill has analysed Democracy in USA &
held that power lies with the 3 (~~classes~~/sections).
Groups ie. the corporate groups, federal politicians
& the top leaders of military.
