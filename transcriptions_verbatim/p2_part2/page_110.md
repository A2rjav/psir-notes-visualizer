⦿ Scientific Approach -

① Systems Approach/ Behavioural Approach/
   positivism

→ What is System's Approach in International Politics-
  System's Approach International Politics is primarily
  associated with the work of Morton Kaplan. It is influ-
  enced by General Systems Theory & the concept of
  political system given by David Easton.

→ What is the idea?
  Idea is to study International politics as a system
  rather than analysing independent foreign policy.

→ Why systems Approach will be relevant?
  (i) According to Morton Kaplan systems Approach is rele-
      vant for study of International Politics because the
      nature of International Politics is inter-disciplinary &
      systems approach provides a Framework for inter-
      disciplinary study.

  (ii) Systems Approach is free from ideological or nor-
       mative pre-occupation.

→ What was the objective of Morton Kaplan?
  He wanted to Build the Grand theory of International
  politics. Theory which can explain politics in a Holistic
  manner & politics of past, present & future.
