Scholar- Kenneth Waltz
Book- Theory of International Politics.
Year of Publication- 1979

Kenneth waltz is known as the Detente Realist.
Purpose of his work:
Between 1962 to 1975 there were many co-operative
actions b/w superpowers Eg. Partial Test Band
Treaty, 1964; NPT, 1969; Helsinki Accord , 1975
These co-operative actions questioned the relevance
of realism. Hence Kenneth Waltz has given the
defense of realism as "timeless wisdom". Realism
will always remain relevant, this was the purpose
of his work.
on what basis he proved that realism is a time
less wisdom-
He proved that realism is a timeless wisdom on
the basis of his discretion of structure of
International Politics. He described his methodo-
logy as Structuralism.
