: From the perspective of Marxist scholars :
(i) Dependency school
State in Africa & Latin America were considered
as puppet states or clientlist regime. These states
have been the instruments of the Bourgeoise cla-
sses of core countries.

(ii) Structuralist school.
Humza Alavi has analysed the nature of state in Pakis-
tan. The theory can be applied to other South Asian
states including India. In these countries we find
overdeveloped state as a colonial legay. Thus states in
these countries are different from their counterparts
in Western countries. In non-Western world modern
state is an important institution & there is a mis-
match b/w stage of economic developement & poli-
tical developement.
