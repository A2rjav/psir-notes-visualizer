-> Introduction:
The term cold war was coined by Bernard Baruch
to explain the state of relations b/w USA & USSR
after 2nd World War. It was popularised with the
publication of Walter Lippman's Book titled 'Cold War'
Winston churchill's Fulton's speech in 1948 confirmed
the existence of cold war. churchill held that from
Stettin in Baltic to Trieste Adriatic has descended
Iron curtain speech. In 1961 Berlin wall which devided
east & west germany became the concrete manifesta-
tion of cold war known as East West conflict.

-> Defining cold war:
cold war denote all war like situation but it did not
result into direct hot war because of Nuclear weapons
It was believed that had nuclear weapon not present
world would have seen the third world war. Nuclear
deterrence was the defining feature of cold war.
