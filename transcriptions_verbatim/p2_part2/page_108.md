② __Gramscian Theory__
In orthodox Marxism role of superstructural factors
or ideological factor has been neglected.
In International Politics Robert [illegible]cox has applied Gram-
scian tradition in his article the social forces state
and world order; Beyond International Relations
theory published in 1981 he has shown the role of
ideological factors in general & in USA's Foreign
Policy which provides basis for domination by USA
In his famous statement, "theory is always for
someone & it always has some purpose." He
has shown that mainstream theories like Realism
or liberalism do not explain International Politics
in an objective manner. They are written from the per-
spective from those who are benefited ~~for~~ by these
theories. He has shown how ideological power opera-
tes behind material power with the example of
US Hegemony.
ⓘ US Hegemony is based on the economic power. USA
has strengthened the economic power by promoting
the idea of free trade as if it is in the interest
of all & USA is a protector of free trade.
