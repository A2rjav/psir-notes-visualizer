HEADING: Structural Functional Approach:::
---
Scholar- Gabrial Almond & Powell.
Purpose-
(i) To develope a model which can be utilised for the
study of developing areas.
(ii) To address the defects of Easton's model.
Which defects?
(i) Easton's model is too general.
(ii) Easton's model does not give detailed view of vari-
ous structures & functions within the political system.

Source of Influence -
(i) David Easton:
Easton's model provide the basic framework.
(ii) The concept of political developement given by
Lucian Pye.
(iii) Functionalism associated with Anthropologists like
Malinowski;

According to Anthropologists like Malinowski, every
society has some key functions for its survival, though
the structure performing these functions may vary.
(iv) They have also taken influence from legal institu-
tional approach.

According to Almond & Powell structural functional
approach is a good approach for comparative politics
because it takes into account the fact that different
political systems are at different stage of political
developement.
