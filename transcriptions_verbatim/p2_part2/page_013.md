How political system function?
According to David Easton, Political system is like a
machine. Like other machines political system also
work on __input output mechanism.__

[Diagram:
Demand ---> \
             ---> Input ---> [Pol. System / Conversion] ---> output ---> Decisions
Support ---> /                                                       ---> Policies
      ^                                                                   |
      <----------------------- Feedback loop <----------------------------]

Political system functions on input output mechanism.
Demand comes from the Environment, demand can be
for making a particular policy. David Easton has give
4 types of demand:
(i) Demand for allocation of Goods & services:
(ii) Demand for regulation of Behaviour.
(iii) Demand for participation.
(iv) ~~[illegible]~~ Demand for Communication & Participation.

Support is of 4 types:
(i) Material Support.
(ii) obidience to the law
(iii) Support by participation.
(iv) support by paying attention to Govt. communication.
