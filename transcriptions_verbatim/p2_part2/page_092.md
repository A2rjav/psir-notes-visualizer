③ Functionalism/ Peace by Pieces.

Functionalism is based on the experience of Euro-
pean union. It is influenced by ideas of Richard
Cobden who suggested to keep the politicians out.

Functionalism as an approach is based on following
concepts:

① Peace by Pieces. Which means look for different
issues. categories this issues into the issues of
low politics & high politics. First take up the issues
of low politics. Give leading ~~to~~ role to non-politi-
cal groups like Technicians, scientists.

⑪ Spill over effect- once nation will realise the benefits
of cooperation in one field they will go for co-ope-
ration in other field. It will develope 'functional
linkages'. It will create an environment which is
conducive even for resolution of conflict on the
issues of high politics.

(iii) Functionalism supports the idea of functional soverei-
gnty. Instead of monistic sovereignity where state
has ultimate power in all sectors, shift decision
making power to the expert bodies.

Above approach was applied in European union which
has ultimately resulted into qualitative transforma-
tion in the ~~relations~~ ^nature of inter state relations.
