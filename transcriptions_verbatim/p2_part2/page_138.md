collective security & collective defence.
Eg. NATO
There is a difference of point of view b/w India &
USA over collective defence.
India's Point of view.
India opposes collective defence pacts like NATO
According to India collective defence pacts under-
mine collective security. It show that country has
no faith in UN system.
USA's Point of view-
(i) No contradiction.
(ii) UN charter recognise the right of countries for self
defence (Art 51.)
(iii) According to USA collective defence is a practical way
for operationalisation of collective security.
collective Defence is only for member countries.
whereas collective security is universal concept.
