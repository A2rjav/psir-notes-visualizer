• __Responses towards Globalisation.__

In both developed & developing countries we see
mix responses there are sections who consider
Globalisation has benefited them, on the other
hand there are sections who consider it
as anti-growth. Major critics of Globalisation
are:
① Marxists -
Marxists are not against Globalisation but they
are against the present model of G.
From the perspective of marxist present Globalisation
is nothing but the expansion of capitalism on global
scale.
② the most violent response against Globalisation
has come from Islamic societies. They interpret
Globalisation as an attempt to spread western
values. Many politicians in the non-western world
empasise on Asian values. Scholars like Bhikhu Parekh
recommend the cosmopolitan idea of Globalisation
based on dialogue b/w civilisations. It is not that
only western model of Globalisation exist there are
many other models of Globalisation. Eg. Indian
model of Globalisation based on the ideal of
"Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam" & "Sarve Bhavantu
Sukhinah". Even Islamic societies have the concept
of Brotherhood.
