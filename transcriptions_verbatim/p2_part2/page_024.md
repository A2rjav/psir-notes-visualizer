  In Marxists we see two schools:
  (a) Instrumentalist & dependency school.
  (b) Structuralists school.

  Political Economy Approach has been found useful
  in context of the study of developing countries.
  The Advantage of Political Economy approach is that
  it is a combination of scientific as well as norma-
  tive approach. Political economy is a policy science.
  Political economists not only use quantitative data
  but also suggest that what should be the econo-
  mic policy of the state.
- Prof. Amartya sen has analysed the developement
  strategy of India & China. He has appreciated the
  China's investment in the areas of Human Deve-
  lopement. He has highlighted the neglect of Hu-
  man developement in India.
- Humza Alavi has applied structural marxists approa-
  ch to understand the political economy of the state
  in Pakistan.
- Gunnar Myrdal (Non-Marxist scholar) in his book 'Asian
  Drama' has analysed the nature of state in India
  & Indonesia & has coined the term soft state for
  India.
- Atul Kohli (Non-Marxist scholar) has analysed the
  process of land reforms in India & has highlighted
  the poor capacity, crisis of Governability of Indian
  state.
