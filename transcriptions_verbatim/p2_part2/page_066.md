• 6 Principles :
① On Human Nature & Relationship b/w politics
and Human Nature.
Like all politics International Politics is also based
on Human Nature.
What is Human Nature?
Man is power seeking animal. (Animus dominandi).
Hence International politics is an arena where
states tries to increase their power.
② On National Interest.
Just like self interest is the prime motivation
for human action. National interest is the prime
motivation for the foreign policies of the state.
③ on the nature of national interest.
National Interest is a Dynamic concept & the content
of Dynamic Interest keeps on changing.
④ Relevance of Ethics
there is no relevance of ethics in politics . there are
no universal standards to judge the actions of the
nation. Pragmatism is the ethics of politics. We
have to see what is correct in a given situation
& ~~also~~ not what is universally correct.
⑤ On Role of Ideology:
Nations do have ideology but ideology does not
have relevance. Nation should not try to impose
its ideology or moral aspiration on the others.
Because it may create unnecessary problems.
