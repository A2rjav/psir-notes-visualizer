• English School/ International Society
  scholar - Hedley Bull
  Book - Anarchical Society

Hedley Bull has criticised the concept of Anarchy as
given by Kenneth Waltz . According to Hedley Bull,
it is not the correct discription of Inter-state relations
International Politics is not a complete anarchy rather
it is anarchical society. It is neither complete anarchy
not complete society but it is somewhere in the middle
It is anarchical society because some features of society
are also found eg. there is growth of International
organisations , Treaties, Norms, Conventions, Anarchical
Society takes the progressive view of both. Human Being
as well as International Politics. As Human Being evolved
there is a qualitative change in Human reason & Behaviour

• Feminist Perspective

Feminists are critical of Realists.
Why?
The consequence of Realist approach is such is that not
only the theory of International Politics is masculenist.
the entire field of International Politics is masculenist.
Cynthia Enloe in her book 'Bananas, Beaches & Bases'
raises the question - 'Where are the women?' . She
also suggest that personal is International & Interna-
tional is person. This show the impact of Patriarchy in
the way women are seen in International Politics.
