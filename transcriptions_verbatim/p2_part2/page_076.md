Q. Write a short note on the difference b/w
Offensive & Defensive Realism.
-> Both Kenneth Waltz & Mearscheimer are structu-
ral realists. Both believe in the timeless wisdom
of realism. Both have similar assumptions w.r.t.
International Politics. They both believe that po-
wer struggle is a permanent feature of inter-
State relations. However, the difference is on -
[Margin: ①] Purpose for which state require power.
[Margin: ⑪] How much power states require?

-> views of Kenneth Waltz-
He believes that purpose of power is defence
& not offense. According to him, defence is a
better option.
Why defense is a better option?
Nation can insure its security & would avoid
unnecessary power struggle. If nation will persue
gaining power mindlessly it will make others
insecure & nation may have to face the com-
bined mind of other states.
