__collective security after Cold War:__
(i) Gulf War, 1989-
significance- so far the best example. More than
30 countries participated under the leadership of
USA. USSR did not used veto despite Iraq had
close relations with USSR. It was thought that
now collective security will be able to work.
Present status:
very soon conflicts b/w US & Russia re-appeared.
UNSC again went into deadlock. Presently, UNSC
remains polarised b/w P2 & P3. In post cold war
situation a new concept has come up known as
Responsibility to Protect because collective security
was meant for inter state conflict whereas present
conflicts are intra-state.
In present times even peace keeping is becoming a
challenging task.
Why?
Because conflicts are intra-state, non-state actors are
not as responsible, there have been instances where
Peace keepers have been attacked.

Current debate in UN Peace Keeping:
(i) Peace keeping is becoming more dangerous.
Developed countries proposed "resolute peace keep-
ing" it means well equipped peace keepers.
(ii) Developed countries do not send their troops.
(iii) Developing countries are true contributors.
(iv) Developed countries contribute fund. From developed
countries Point of view:
- There is a mis-appropriation of funds by developing countries.
- poor quality of services.
