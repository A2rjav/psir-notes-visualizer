cold war was not entirely cold it had its warm

-> Phases of cold war-
① From 1949 to 1969
② From 1969 to 1979 (Detente phase)
③ From 1979 to 1989
- 1989 - fall of Berlin Wall.
- Malta summit b/w USA & USSR - In Malta summit
the two countries declared the formal end of cold war.
the then US president H.W. Bush declared the begi-
nning of New world order.

-> Was cold war Inevitable?
① Determinists perspective
It was inevitable because of fundamental conflicts b/w
the National Interest of the two super powers.

② Social constructivists perspective
It was not inevitable but it was the result of mis-
understandings, mis·calculations. Cold war is a tail
of missed opportunities.
