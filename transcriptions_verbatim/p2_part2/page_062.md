● Theories of International Politics:

(1) Realism:

What are the salient features of Realist view
of international politics.

- Oldest school.
- Hegemonic Discourse
- Basic assumptions are based on west phalian
world order. Which can be explained on the basis
of Billiards Ball model.

(i) state centric or statism means states are only
actors. even when international organisations or
non-state actors exist they are expression of state only.

(ii) Anarchy - structure of International politics is anar-
chical. Anarchy does not mean disorder. Anarchy
means absence of Global Governance. ~~that~~
States are rational actors. (Assumption).
It means states are concerned with their own
interest & the core interest is survival.

(iii) Security Dilemma: vicious cycle of insecurity.
International politics is continuous struggle for power.
