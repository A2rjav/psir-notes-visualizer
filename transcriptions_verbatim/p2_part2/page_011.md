__Q.__ Discuss the salient features of systems Approach.
Do you think systems Approach is status quoist.

$\rightarrow$ __Introduction -__
Type of Approach -
Behavioural Approach. (Scientific Approach).

Systems Approach in Political Science was given by
[Margin: David Easton
$\downarrow$
Behaviouralism] David Easton.
Systems Approach is influenced by General System Theory.
General Systems Theory is based on Biology. It is not
enough to study the organ. It is important to under-
stand the environment in which organ is placed. It is
not enough its features, it is necessary to understand
the processes.

Systems Approach is a result of movement in poli-
tical Science which called for the adoption of interdi-
sciplinary approach. Systems Approach was first use
by Talcott Parsons in sociology. Inspired by parsons
about the possibility of the use of systems approa-
ch in social sciences. David Easton has developed the
concept of political system.

__Features of Systems Approach -__
Systems Approach is based on the concept of system.
__What is system?__
System = set of elements in the state of interaction
Interaction - pattern or specific.
Eg. Circulatory System.
Veins will not do work of Heart
