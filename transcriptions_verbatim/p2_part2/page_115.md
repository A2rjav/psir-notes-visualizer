(ii) __Prisoner's Dilemma.__
Theme situation of states is like prisoners. They
have no way to completely understand the inten
sions of the other. Huge trust deficit exist. They
are not able to take ^action, what is best for them rather
would like to avoid the worst outcome.
Eg. It was used to predict whether India & Pakistan
could go for ~~B~~ building nuclear weapons.
Why there was a doubt?
- India's advocacy for comprehensive nuclear disarmament
- Both India & Pakistan are poor countries. They should
use their resources wisely.
- If both of them do not develope, it will be a win-
win situation as nuclear weapons are not going
to use.
- Since there is a huge trust deficit they will opt for
nuclear weapon.
- International Politics is such where co-operation is not
as beneficial as defection.
(a) Both co-operate & do not make.
(b) India defects & makes Nuclear weapon it will be
detrimental for Pakistan not to have nuclear weapon
and vice-versa.
