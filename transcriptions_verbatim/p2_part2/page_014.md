-> Output -
Either Single decision or set of decisions. David
Easton gives 4 types of output.
(i) Extraction - Eg. Payment of Taxes., fees etc.
(ii) Regulation of Behaviour.
(iii) Allocation of Goods & services.
(iv) Symbolic Eg. Respect National Flag, Anthem etc.

• Critical Evaluation-
(I) Traditionalists Criticism-
(i) Nothing but cosmetic change - Traditionalists have
explained the functioning of the State in a similar
way even without such model.
(ii) Introduced unnecessary complicated terminology.
(iii) Lack analytical depth.
(iv) It is just a conceptual framework.
(v) It can be relevant only at the very initial level
of research for organisation of data.

(II) Criticism by Marxists-
(i) Marxist consider systems Approach is biased towards
the western Liberal Democratic system. Hence, it is
not a value free approach. Despite the fact it
claims to be scientific.
(ii) It present western political system as an ideal type.
Hence, it expect other systems to move in the direc-
tion of western systems or otherwise it may give
the impression that other systems are imperfect.
(iii) Model is status quoist because it show as if system is
running in a smooth manner. It shows as if feed-
back loop communicate people's aspiration & political
system responds accordingly.
