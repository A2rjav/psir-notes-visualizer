HEADING: Why soft power is better?
---
-> Why soft power is better?
(i) Cheaper
(ii) If soft power may not work there is no guarantee
that Hard power will work.

(iii) The country using hard power force others to build coali-
tion against it & sympathy develops for the adversary

According to him, the biggest reason for the decline
of ~~US~~ Hegemony in 21st century has been excessive
reliance on Hard Power.

-> Smart power-
Joseph Nye, modified his views & acknowledged that
soft power alone will not work. Hence, he suggest
combination of Hard & soft power. Joseph Nye,
defines smart Power as an approach which under-
scores the necessity of Military power But invest
heavily in alliances, partnerships & institutions.

Former US secretory of state Hillary Clinton, describe
soft power as skillful use of diplomatic, military,
political, legal, cultural.

Obama Administration made 3 Ds as the pillar
of Foreign Policy.
(i) Defence (ii) Diplomacy (iii) Developement.
