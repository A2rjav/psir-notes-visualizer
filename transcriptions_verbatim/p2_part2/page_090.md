→ Contribution of Karl Deutsch.
ⓘ He has given the concept of security community.
(ii) Security Community is alternative to Security Dilemma.
Examples of Security Community:
Western Europe, North America, Western Europe &
North America (Trans Atlantic Community)
In 2015 ~~ASIAS~~ ASEAN declared itself as a community.
The members of Security community do not feel
that they are threat to each other rather they
feel they have common threats existing outside.
How Security Community emerges?
According to Karl Deutsch, It emerges through
communication. He has actually measured the
communications by measuring the no. of inter-
national travels, letters exchanged, phone calls.
Higher is the communication greater is the feeling
of Community
What is the suggestion?
Increase communication b/w societies. Eg. Visa
liberalisation. Promote people to people contacts,
Relevance in South Asia -
It is an option worth trying in South Asia. It is
believed that only by promoting people to people
contacts b/w India & Pakistan we can remove
apprehensions against each other.
