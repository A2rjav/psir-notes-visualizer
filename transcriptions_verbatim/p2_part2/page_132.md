What is the limitation of collective security?
It is a utopian concept. It could not be enforced
in practice. It is based on wrong assumptions as if
nations are concerned about peace. If states are
guided by National Interest rather than peace, it
would be foolished thing that Nations will forget their
long term interest for the sake of peace.

Experience with the working of collective security?
(i) First experiement was under League of Nations.
Since League of Nations was the 1st experiement
there were conceptual flaws in the way collective
security was concieved under League of Nations.
(a) Under League of Nations all countries have veto
power, any action could be done only with the
consent of all states which was impracticle.
(b) Charter had no clarity w.r.t the determination
of the act of agression or agression.
(c) USA, USSR were out of League.
(d) Britain & France considered USSR as a bigger enemy
than Fascist powers.
(e) League took no action when approached to the League
of Nation against Japan's invasion in Manchuria in
1931.
(f) League took no action when Ithopia approached against
Italy's Invasion in 1936.
