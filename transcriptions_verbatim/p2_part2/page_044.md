➔ Difference in Old & New :
  ⓘ In terms of objectives :
     Old social movements were concerned with Basic
     issues of life. Whereas new social movements are
     concerned with quality of life Eg. Protection of
     Environment, Animal Rights, Human Rights.
  (ii) In terms of social base :
     Social base of old social movements was from
     working classes.
     Social base of new social movements in from
     middle classes.
  (iii) In terms of technique or strategy :
     Old social movements prefer direct action, coercive
     actions like strike, mass protest. Whereas new
     social movements goes for innovative societies
     like use of theatres, Literary mode of expression.
  (iv) In terms of Ideological orientation :
     Old social movements were materialistic in orienta-
     tion & whereas new social movements were post
     materialistic in orientation
● Difference b/w Developed & Developing
  In developing world we see the co-existence of
  old & new. Eg. If we take environmental movement
  in India it has a feature of both old & new.
  Eg. They include both type of objectives :
  ⓘ concerned with basic issues as well as protection
     of Environment.
  (ii) Similarly in terms of social composition main base
     comes from tribals but leadership comes from
     middle classes
