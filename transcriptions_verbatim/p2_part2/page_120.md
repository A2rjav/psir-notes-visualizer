● Comparison b/w Hard Power & Soft Power : lecture 11
[18/04/2017]
(i) Basis of Hard power is materialistic.
However, for soft power is Idealistic.
(ii) Hard power is in the control of the state so it can be
operationalise at the will of state. whereas soft power
is diffused throughout the society & is not at the
will of state.
(iii) Hard power has compelling - Nature , soft power is voulntary.
It may not compel others to do what states wants.
(iv) Results of Hard power may be immidiate , whereas
results of soft power may be realised in long term.
(v) Hard power is resource based.

-> Which power is more relevant in 21st century?
According to Joseph Nye, Soft power.
Why?
World order has changed in the age of complex inter-
dependence the scope of soft power has increased
considerably & scope of use of Hard power has
reduced.
According to Joseph Nye, world today looks like 3
dimensional chess Board.
(i) World is unipolar - basis- Military
(ii) World is Multi polar- basis- Economy
(iii) Social power diffused - basis - Democracy, IT, Public
diplomacy.
