• __What is Globalisation?__
Globalisation is explained by concepts like 'Global
village' given by Marshall McLuhan or 'Borderless
world' by Kenichi Ohame, World is flat by Thomas
Friedman, compression in terms of time & space
by Anthony Giddens.
Thomas Friedman in his book 'the lexus & the olive
tree' mentions globalisation as a process by which
nation state, corporations, civil societies & individuals
are interacting with each other at a level which has
been unprecedented. He defines Globalisation as the
process by which our interaction have become
farther, faster, deeper & cheaper.
Today it will be wrong to say that the Globalisation
is a myth or mere buzz word. Globalisation has
impacted people around the globe. We can feel
globalisation in the ~~clothe~~ clothes we wear, food we
eat & the ideas we hold.
