What is the structure of International Politics:
Anarchy. It means absence of central authority or
world govt. If anarchy is the structure nations
will suffer from security dilemma. Hence, inter-
national politics will be struggle for power.

other elements of Kenneth Waltz analysis about
International Politics.
① Anarchy & Hierarchy:-
Anarchy is the structure of International Politics.
& Hierarchy is the structure of Domestic Politics.

② Functional Differentiation:
Functional Differentiation exists in Domestic politics
but it is absent in International Politics. Because all
~~the~~ states perform same roles.

③ Capabilities
Capability determine potential power. States differ
in terms of capability. International Politics is
primarily shaped by Great Powers.
