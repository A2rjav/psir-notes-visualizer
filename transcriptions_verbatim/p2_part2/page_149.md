Whether Cold War was driven by ideological factors or for geopolitical factors?
Primarily geopolitical but ideology was used in a big way.

→ __Beginning of Cold Wars-__
Actually there is no beginning & no end. Still we can trace the origin to Bolshevik Revolution in Russia.
__Timeline-__
① Bolshevik Revolution in Russia in 1917.
USA supported Reactionary forces.
② __Lenin's Foreign Policy__
Lenin was of the view that communism is not secured if surrounded by capitalist countries. In 1919 he formed Communist International for expansion of communism.
③ __Stalin's Foreign Policy.__
Stalin was more concerned about security of Russia in ~~in~~order to avoid confrontation with the west Stalin adopted policy of socialism in one country indicating no plan for expansion. However west did not trust Stalin. West continued to intervene in domestic affairs.
Ultimately Stalin also started plan for expansion. He formed new body Cominform. Stalin's policy in the neighbourhood is known as Salami tactics. which means cut the opposition one by one. Stalin wanted to avoid involvement in political developement in west europe. He entered into non-agression pact with Hitler in 1939 known as Molotov Ribbentrop. Stalin also signed neutrality pact with Japan in 1941.
