- __Political Parties :__
The systematic study of political parties is known as
statistiogogy. Political parties perform essential input
function in a Representative [illegible]
It performs the function of interest aggregation.
Political parties have been studied by traditional as
well as Behavioural perspective.
Admand Burke has given the definition of political
parties from traditional perspective. According to
him, Group of people having shared ideology, come
together for persuit of common interest.

On the other hand Otto von Kircheimer gives the
concept of 'catch all parties' For catch all parties
ideology does not matter. Their only aim is to
come to power.
