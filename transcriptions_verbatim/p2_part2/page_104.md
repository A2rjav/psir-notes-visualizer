Marxist Theory of International Politics -

In Marxism politics is the element of superstru-
cture. Hence, they focus on the analysis of eco-
nomic structure. Hence, it is better to call Marxist
theory of International politics as Marxist theory
of International Political Economy.
What is the core concern of Marxist scholars?
Their focus is on analysing the working of capitalism
in International context.

Role of Karl Marx -
He has not given any detailed view over the work-
ing of capitalism in International context yet he
believe that capitalism is inherently expansionist.
In the words of Karl Marx, Imparatives of capi-
talism force Bourgeoise to nestle every where &
settle everywhere. Marx did talk about Prolitarian
Internationalism.

Contribution of Lenin -
The real credit for developing Marxist perspective
of International politics goes to Lenin's theory
of Imperialism. Lenin has established imperialism
as highest stage of capitalism. Lenin has shown
that 1st world war was actually capitalist war
for colonies.

Rosa Luxemberg
In her theory of imperialism has shown the interde-
pendence b/w capitalism, militarism, Nationalism
& imperialism.
