Limitations of ~~to~~ Traditional comparative Politics.

(i) Parochial in nature (Narrow in scope).
Why?
Focused on western countries.

(ii) Ethnocentric:
Western scholars did not recognise the existence of
non-western political institutions. They believe that
people outside Europe were Barbaric & Imperialism
was a civilising mission.

(iii) Static in Nature:
Since, Method adopted was the study of constituti-
ons, it remained static or textual. They have not
tried to understand the social & cultural factors
shaping the political system. They focused on the
study of institutions & neglected the study of poli-
tical processes.

(iv) Traditional comparative politics is actually the study
of comparative Govt. rather than comparative politics.

(v) Traditional comparative Politics was essentially
'Non-comparative'. It was non-comparative because
western world is a one world. There is not much
difference in their culture, level of political develope-
ment, economic developement. Hence, we do not get
much contrasting features.
