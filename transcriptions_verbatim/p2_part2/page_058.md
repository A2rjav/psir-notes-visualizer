Vision of new world order-
Since the time of Woodrow Wilson all American
Presidents whether Republicans or Democrats have

Woodrow Wilson's vision of World order.
Woodrow Wilson's vision of New world order is seen
in his 14th points speech in US congress. The promi-
nent ideas are:
(i) Making world safe for Democracy.
(ii) Promotion of free trade.
(iii) Freedom of Navigation.
(iv) Establishment of League of Nations.
(v) Replacing Balance of Power with collective security.
Above vision used to be known as the vision of
liberal world order. Attempts were made after 1st
world war to implement these visions. League of
Nations came into existence. However, nations did
not adhered to the liberal vision. League of Nations
collapsed & within a span of 20 years 2nd world
war started.
