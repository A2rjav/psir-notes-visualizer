Implication :

If more no. of countries are democratic it is
better for world peace .

__Criticism :__

It is most criticised theory.
__Why__
(i) Based on insufficient logic - Democracies have not gone
for war with each other, it may have been just a coin-
cidence.

(ii) Only Democracy is not the reason other factors like
free trade, common External threat may have also
played the role.

$\rightarrow$ __Worst aspect/ part of the theory__
(i) It provides legitimacy to US attempts to promote Demo
cracy at Gun point (Bush Doctrine). Eg. US attempts
to introduce Democracy in Iraq has destabilised the
entire middle east.

(ii) Countries like China & Russia suggest theory providing
justification for US attempts at regime change & to
install puppet govt. US paradox is evident as it has
been supporting the dictators & Monarchies in the
middle east. US could never reconcile with Democracy
in Iran.
