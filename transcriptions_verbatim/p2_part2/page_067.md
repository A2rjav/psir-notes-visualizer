(6) on the Field on International Politics
International Politics is neither ethics nor law
nor economics. It is an autonomous field. It is
uninspiring but that is how it is.

-> Morgenthau has classified states into 3 types:
(1) Status Quoist. Eg. USA
(2) Revisionist Eg. China
(3) Imperialist. Eg. Germany under Hitler

Morgenthau does not have faith in International
organisations & believes that peace is possible
only by diplomacy & by balance of power.

Morgenthau has held that International Politics is
nothing but struggle ^for power.

* critics of Morgenthau:
(*) Morgenthau has been criticised by following scholars
(1) Stanley Hoffman.
He accuses Morgenthau on power monism. Thus
Morgenthau's view is not imperically or theoritically
sustainable because in International politics we
see conflict as well as co-operation.
(2) Kenneth Waltz.
He objects to Morgenthau's method because Morgen-
thau has made Human Nature as the basis of his
analysis. Scientific theory cannot be built on the
analysis of Human Nature.
