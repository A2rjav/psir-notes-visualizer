During his address to a constituent Assembly in
December 1946. Ambedkar held that, "i know today
__we are devided politically, economically & socially.__
__We are group of warring camps. I myself is a leader__
__of one such camps. However iam convinced that a__
__day will come when we will forget these differences__
__and become one nation."__

Ambedkar belong to community of untouchables in Maha-
rashtra. He got an opportunity to get education in the
prominent universities in the west. He earned degree
in law and started practicing in Bombay. Because of
his community people did not approach him for his
services as a lawyer. He realised that till untouchabili-
ty is practice there is no scope for members of his
community to lead a life of dignity. He became active
in politics. He started organising Dalits. He tried to raise
consciousness among the members of his community
He brought magazines like 'Mukh Nayak' (leader of
dumb - who cannot speak) he also formed 'Bahishkrit
Hitkarni Sabha'. He organised Mahad Satyagraha in 1927.
He was disappointed because he could not get the suppor
of Gandhi for his Satyagraha. Gandhi held that Satyagra
ha's use should be limited against the foreign rule.
Ambedkar also founded Indian Labour Party, later on
Scheduled caste Federation and renamed it as Republi-
can Party of India.
