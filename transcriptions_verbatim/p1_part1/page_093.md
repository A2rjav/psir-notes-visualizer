• Legacy of the British Raj -

What does legacy means?
Legacy show the continuity of 'the Raj' even after the
independence.
Why legacy?
Unlike chinese Revolution, Indian Revolution is not a
revolution in true sense. It is actually the transfer of
power. We see the continuity of the raj in almost all
aspects of Independent India.
Constitution of India itself is based on Govt. of India Act
1935.
Rabindranath Tagore was correct when he ~~said that~~ Briti-
shers are going to leave India one day, that is sure.
However, when they will leave India they will leave so
much dirt & filth that generations of Indians will not b
able to clean the mess. one of the worst legacy of the
British Raj is the partition of the subcontinent. The part
ition of subcontinent is still considered as the worst hu-
man tragedy i.e in terms of loss of the life & violence
on the subcontinent. It is the British legacy that South
Asia & Middle East remained the least integrated of all
regions. We can analyse the legacy of the raj as following

    In political sphere, parliamentary
democracy , centralised federation also the
extremely powerful, Bureaucracy & weak institutions
of local self govt. It is the legacy of the british rule &
the preference of the elites that India also remains
‘overdeveloped state’.
