② Asymmetrical Federalism-
Constitution provides scope for different states to pursue
different policies in accordance to customs & traditions.
It has provided 6th schedule, Art. 370 as well as special
provisions w.r.t. various states.
Introduction of 73rd & 74th C.A. Act is also guided by the
motive to guide the demand for seperate/smaller states.
According to Atul Kohli, most of the regional movement in
India have followed the lifecycle which can be presented
[Margin: [Diagram: ∩] ←] through 'inverted U curve'
According to Atul Kohli, there is no formal policy of GOI
in dealing with regional challenges. However we can discern
following trend:
(i) Govt. is ready to negotiate but negotiation has to be withi
the framework of the constitution not outside.
(ii) No compromise with territorial integrity.
(iii) Govt. is more sensitive if demand has religious overtones.
Govt. will not offer any concession. If demand is expressed
in linguistic & not in religious term greater scope for
accomodation.
(iv) Govt. is ready to talk subject to the condition other party
leaves the use of force. If the other party will use force Govt.
will retaliate with much greater force.
(v) Govt. is ready for political devolution & economic packages.
