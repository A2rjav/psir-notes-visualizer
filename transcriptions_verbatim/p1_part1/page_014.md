__Introduction of Arthashastra :-__
Arthashastra primarily deals with state craft. Hence,
it is categorised as the book of Public Administration
rather than political philosophy. Arthashastra was
1st discovered by Shama Shastry. The 1st publication
of Arthashastra of Kautilya in English wrote in 1909.

What is the most important goal of the state according
to Kautilya?
The most important goal is to achieve the material
well being ie. Artha. In the words of Kautilya,
Material well being is supreme, spiritual good and
sensual pleasure-Dharma & Kama all depends upon
material well being.
