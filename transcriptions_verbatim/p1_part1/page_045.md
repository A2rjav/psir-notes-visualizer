[Margin: (ii)] Trusteeship
Gandhi did not support Marxist approach with respect to
the relationship between labour and capital. Gandhi did
not believe in the theory of class struggle. Gandhi believe
in the possibility of Harmony between classes. Like early
socialists Gandhi believed in appealing the conscience of
the capitalists. According to Gandhi capital is social produc
tion, capitalists should not consider himself as the owner
of the capital. Capitalists should consider himself as the
trustee of the capital. According to him, capitalists can keep
whatever amount he thinks is necessary for running his
enterprise & the rest he should donate for the welfare of
the society.

Gandhi was not favouring Mechanised production in India
because it would result into the displacement of labour.
Gandhi favoured the revival of cottage industries.
Gandhi ~~fav~~ preferred charkha economy. Gandhi also
supported land reforms. According to Gandhi land should
belong to the tiller.
