Types of Regional Movements in India-
There has been a varieties of Regional movements.
(i) Successionists movement - Eg. khalistan, kashmir, ULFA,
NAGAS movement, MIZOS
(ii) Movement for seperate state within India.
Eg. Gurkhaland, Bodoland, Telangana movement.
(iii) Movements for greater Autonomy.
(iv) Bhumiputra Movement. Eg.
movement by shivsena, MNS, Assam Gana Parishad.

Explaination or causes of Regional movements in India.
