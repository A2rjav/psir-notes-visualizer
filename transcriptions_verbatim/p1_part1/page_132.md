It is also wrong to think that Indian caste system was
entirely rigid. He gives the concept of Sanskritisation.
He considers Dumont's view as textual. He ^M.N has coined
the concept of 'Dominant caste' to explain the reality of
the caste in India. In different regions caste system is
not uniform It is not that in all regions Brahmins
are the most priviledged. In different regions different
castes are dominant Eg. Yadav's in Bihar, Jats in
Haryana or Maratha's in Maharashtra. Dominant caste
has two features -
① Numerical Majority
② ownership of land.

Origin of caste politics can also be traced during to the
British time. Throughout National Movement congress
was considered as party of upper caste & specially
Brahmin's. Congress was opposed by Ambedkar who
has farmed Republican party of India. representing
Bahujan Samaj. In South Ramaswami Naikar has
built the opposition to congress & the party of OBC's
challenging the domination of Brahmins. One of the
major issue of caste politics in India has been -
① Reservations
② Land Reforms & Agrarian politics.
After Independence-
Though pandit Nehru had a vision for India which is
free from caste & communalism, it is surprising that
Indian constitution only abolish untouchability and
does not abolish caste system.
