• Origin and Evolution of Communalism in India -

(1) Pre-British Time-
Unlike colonial historians who gave essentialists argue-
ments scholars like Romila Thapar has shown that
communal violence in India was the phenomena asso-
associated with the British Raj. There are some
examples of secterian conflicts before British but it
was of local nature. However, with the British it becam
Pan- Indian phenomena.

(2) During British Time-
(i) Britishers started prefertial policy towards Hindu's &
considering Muslims responsible for the revolt of 1857.
(ii) Sir Sayad A. Khan expressed the loyalty of Muslims
to secure their aid for educational empowerment of
Muslims.
(iii) Britishers always favoured minorities because majority
will always challenge the foreign rule & insecurity of
minorities can be manipulated. Eg: they favoured
Muslims in India & Tamils in Srilanka.
(iv) In 1906 feudal elements among Muslim communities
formed Muslim League Muslim League started getting
active support from British.
(v) In 1909 ~~gave~~ statutory recognition that Muslims are
seperate community
(vi) In 1915 Savarkar formed Hindu Maha Sabha in response
to the policies of Muslim League.
(vii) In 1919 seperate electorate was extended for other
religious communities.
