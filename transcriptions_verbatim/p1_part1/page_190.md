__Arguments against smaller states__
(Prof. M.P. Singh, Sudha Pai)

1) There is no relation between the size and good governance
e.g. TN is a bigger state yet governed in a better way
whereas Jharkhand is a smaller state, but poorly governed

2) No relation between size and development. Growth story
of Punjab is over.

3) New ^State means new ministerial post, new bureaucracy
and unnecessary expenditure.

4) It will open the Pandora Box. The demands for smaller
state will continue till each village becomes a state.

5) It will result into balkanisation of country and may
threaten security.

6) Many new states are not economically viable, these state
will be like UTs.

7) We have to seen consequences on the mother states

8) New state means new water disputes

9) Smaller states are politically unstable because
defections are easy. Goa & Jharkhand has suffered
from political stability.
