② Phase II- from 1967 till 1990s.
This zone is known as the transitional zone of
Indian politics. Green Revolution & state re-organisation
has resulted into first democratic upsurge.
Now OBCs came to dominate Indian Parliament,
largest no. of members came from Agrarian Back-
ground & the 2ⁿᵈ largest no. came from. social work

During this phase we see the trend of more youth
joining politics because of JP Movement & Sanjay Gandhi
who started youth congress. Since, youth joined politics
at the time of carrier making, politics became full
time career.
Nothing changed wrt the representation of women
which has stagnated around 8% & representation of
minorities. This show that if constitution has not
provided reservation for members of SC & ST
communities it would not have been possible to
increase their no.

③ Phase III- 1990s onwards
This phase is known as the phase of plebianisation
of Indian Democracy as a result of 1st & 2ⁿᵈ Demo-
cratic upsurge. In 1990s also the domination of OBCs
remained intact, majority of the parliamentarians
come from Agrarian & social service background.
Profile has not changed much in terms of the mem-
bers of minorities as well as wrt women.
