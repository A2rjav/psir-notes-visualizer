Factors which gave rise to regional parties
E shreedharan has given following reasons for the
rise of regional parties:
(i) De-linking of the elections of Parliament & assemblies
provided more space for local issues, local parties as
well as greater scope of bargaining by Regional Parties.
(ii) Growing politicisation of social cleavages
(iii) The rise of intermediate caste because of state reo-
rganisation, land reform & green revolution.
(iv) Suspension of democracy during the time of In. Gandhi.
(v) India's Federal structure where state govts. deal with
those issues which are related to the day to day life
of the people.
(vi) Growing use of religion.
(vii) Social Heterogenety getting reflected more & more.

According to Yogendra Yadav, Explained the rise of
regional parties & the decline of congress through
the concept of 'Democratic Upsurge'. Thus it is a natu-
ral process of the deepening of Democracy in India.
