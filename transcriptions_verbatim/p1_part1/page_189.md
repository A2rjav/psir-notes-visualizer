__Arguments in favour of smaller states:__

Mayawati, Advani, Ambedkar
Ramchandra Guha, Bibek Debroy

1) The most basic argument is good governance.
2) It will address the problem of lack of balanced
regional development.
3) It will meet aspirations of people.
4) It will establish federal balance.

Ambedkar held that north Indian state need to be
organise in smaller state to address federal imbalance.

state and size of population India should have at least
50 state. If we ~~stat~~ take example of UP in term of pop^n
