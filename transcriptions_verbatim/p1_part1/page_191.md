Q. Discuss the constitutional provisions with respect to resolution
__of water disputes.__

    Water is given in 1) __State list entry 17__: It deals with the
    use and consumption of water • Creation of system of
    irrigation, dams etc.

2) Water is also given in __union list entry no 56__. Union has
    powers w.r.t. regulation and development of interstate
    river and river valleys.

    Most of the major rivers are interstate. Rivers are
mobile resources unlike land, forest, minerals which are
static.

__Analysis of role of union:__
    Union has not played its expected role. It has preferred
to treat water as if entirely a state subject. Union government
has lot of scope to regulate interstate river and river valleys
in the national interest. The purpose of keeping particular
entry in union list is that issue must be looked from national
perspective. So far union has gone for balancing competing
interest of states and parties rather than treating water
as strategic issue. In case of Australia union govt/federal
govt. has adopted interstate river.

[Constitutional mechanism to deal with water dispute.]

    Water is the only interstate dispute for which there is a
special provision in constitution.

and water is to fight".
