__MODERN INDIAN POLITICAL THOUGHT__

SIR SYED AHMED KHAN

Time- 18   - 1898

Q. Short Note- The Role of Sir Syed Ahmed Khan as a
    social reformer or as a moderniser.
Q. Why Sir Syed Ahmed Khan has been a controversial
    figure?
Q. Discuss the transition of Sir Syed Ahmed Khan from
    the Prophet of Hindu muslim unity to the person in
    whose thought we trace the origin of two nation
    theory.

Sir Syed Ahmed Khan has the similar position among Musli-
ms like Raja Rammohan Roy among Hindus. Sir Syed
Ahmed Khan has seen the down-fall of Mughal Power in
India. He could understand the state of frustation through
which his community was going through. He understood
that Britishers are emerging force in India. He realised
that there is no point challenging British because Briti-
shers are going to stay. He was convinced that the ony
way muslims can improve their situation is through
Education. He was the critic of outdated customs and
rituals. He aimed at Modernisation of Muslim community
through his Magazine Tehzib-ul-Akhlaq. He has attacked
outdated customs. He founded scientific society at
Gazipur in UP which was later on shifted to Aligarh
He also brought a journal titled 'Indian Institute & Gazette'
for promotion of scientific research. He believed that Islam
can easily adopt science & scientific way of life. His
message to the Muslim youth was to carry Quran in
one hand and the book of science in the other hand.
