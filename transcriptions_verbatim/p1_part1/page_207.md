__SC on discretionary power of governor.__

1) In Shamsher Singh vs union of India case, SC held that
both governor as well as president are constitutional heads.

2) Hargovind Pant vs Raghukul Tilak 1979
Significance of the case:
It was the constitutional bench which judgement
w.r.t the satus of the governor under the constitution
as well as the nature of his discretionary powers.
(i) Governor is appt. by the union govt. and can be
removed by union govt. This does not mean that
governor is an employee of union.
(ii) Governor is an independent const. office of dignity
(iii) Even when governor holds office at the pleasure of
president, he is not subordinate to the union govt
nor he is bound to act on their discretion.
(iv) Governor's discretion is governor's discretion. It is
not the discretion of the union govt. Governor is not
accountable to the union for the manner in which
he exercises his discretion.

3) Nebam Rabia vs. union of India case; 2016
The case it inquires into the nature of the discretionary
power enjoyed by the governor. SC has strucked down the
action of governor summoning the assembly without aid
and advise of council of ministers. SC in a harsh language
held that NDA /union govt action is 'Thrashing' to the
constitution! If such action is allowed democracy will
not survive in the country. SC has also taken into account
the C.A. debates. They have given the reference of view
of Sardar Patel. Sardar Patel has very clearly mentioned
in his reply over the question of special power of governor
that ministerial responsibility is a fundamental principle,
governor can exercise his discretion only in few matters.
Governors special powers can't be allowed to envade
the principle of ministerial responsibility. According to
SC the provision (Art 163) [illegible] clause 2 does not tell
what it seems to say.
