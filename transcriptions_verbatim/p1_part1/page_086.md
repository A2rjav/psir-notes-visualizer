Trade Union Movement.

Trade union movement even in contemporary India
remains a weak movement. From the very beginning
trade unions have been junior partners allied with one
or the other political party. Neither before the indepe-
ndence nor after autonomous trade movement could
develope. Even workers remained devided on lines of
caste & religion. There has been lack of leadership.

Chronology of Trade Union Movement.
(i) Modern workers started emerging in the 2nd half of
the 19th century. With the introduction of railway
post & telegraph. Initial protest were some esporedic
unorganised events.
(ii) Organised labour movement was started by philan-
throphists like S.S. Bengoli & Shashipada Banerjee.
(iii) The 1st labour organisation was Bombay Mill hands
association formed by N.N. Lokhande
(iv) Lala Lajpatray & Gandhi took special interest in the
workers issues Gandhi led the protest of Ahmedabad
textile mill workers.
(v) In 1920 All India Trade Union Congress came into existence
with Lala Lajpatray as the 1st president. AITUC was infil-
trated by communists.
(vi) Later on communists came out of AITUC & formed
red flag trade union.
(vii) Communists again joined AITUC.
(viii) Again left during Quit India Movement
(ix) Again joined but this time Congress left & formed
its seperate Union Indian National Trade Union
Congress (INTUC).
