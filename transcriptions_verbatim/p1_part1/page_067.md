• Compare thoughts of Ambedkar with that of Marx
and Marxism-

Ambedkar was influenced by Liberalism as well as
Marxism. However in case of India he felt that Marxist
Method of struggle will not work. Ambedkar accepted
Marx concept of class struggle. He even accepted the
exploitative nature of private property. Ambedkar also
Stood for the rights of working classes. However, Ambed
kar believed that Marxist principle of Historical materi
alism is not relevant in Indian situation. The basic
structure of Indian society is not economic but ideo-
logical. It is the ideology of Brahmanism which forms
the basic structure of the society. Hence even with
change in the material structure there will be no chang
in the status of dalits without changing the ideology
of Brahmanism or Manuwad.

Ambedkar did not support Marx view of state. Marx
held state as an instrument ~~of~~ ^as evil. According to Ambed-
kar society is bigger evil than state. Society can be
more exploitative. He viewed state as an agency, through
which we can address social injustices. He was support-
er of affirmative action policies by the state.

Ambedkar did not support Marx view on religion. According
to him religion is not just the opium of masses. Religion
is a necessity of Human beings. Marx has not read all
religions Buddhism is not opium of masses. Buddhism
has lot for nourishment of Human soul.
