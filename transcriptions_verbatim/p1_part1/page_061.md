[Margin: ①] __Ambedkar on Caste System :-__

Ambedkar wanted to know the origin of caste System.
He wanted to understand How caste system but specifi-
cally, more urgently untouchability can be eradicated.
Ambedkar was not satisfied with the theory of caste
system as given in Purushsukta of Rigveda. He went for
scientific - Anthropological account of origin of caste
system. According to Ambedkar, initially there were only
3 varna's. ie. Brahmin's, Kshatriya's & Vaishya's. He links
caste system with the settled life. Till society was migra-
tory/mobile there was no caste system.
According to Ambedkar, Shudra's were Kshatriyas.
However those kshatriya's who developed differences
with Brahmin. Brahmin's ~~have~~ stopped upnayan
Sanskar for this group. Upnayan sanskar symbolise
the purification. Thus shudra's were the kshatriya's
who were not purified.

Regarding untouchables Ambedkar suggest that they were
not the part of varna system. Hindu texts consider
them as avarna's, Chandals, Antyaj. He calls untoucha-
bles as 'Broken Men'. They were seperated from Hindu
community. They were living outside the main village.
According to Ambedkar, they were followers of Buddhism.
Those Buddhists who accepted the Hegemony of Brahmin
were brought back in the caste system. However, those
who preferred to continue as Buddhist were segregated,
subjected to exploitation & untouchability was enforced
on them.
