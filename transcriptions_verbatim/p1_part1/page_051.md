When M.N. Roy was marxist he became critic of Gandhi
and Indian National Congress. According to M.N. Roy
Gandhi was the leader of Bourgeoise class Gandhi
was fooling masses. He was extremely critical of Gan-
dhi's decision to call off Non-cooperation Movement after
chauri chaura incidence. According to Gandhi called of
movement because Gandhi was fearful that power will go
to the hands of masses. According to him, Gandhi's Non-
~~co~~violence was actually violence on masses. He had no
faith in Gandhi's programme of charkha. He called Gandhi
a medieval man, a reactionary, a conservative person.

M.N. Roy was invited to attend communist International.
(2^nd communist International by Lenin) Lenin wanted to
decide the strategy of communist international with
respect to the National Movements going on in colonies.
Lenin suggested that we should be supporting National
Movements & Nationalist parties & after achievement of
freedom we can help masses to establish communism.
M.N. Roy did not support to stage revolution. M.N. Roy
was against supporting the Nationalist parties. Accord-
ing to him, Nationalists will join hands with emperia-
lists. He held that masses in India are ready for
revolution. He held that a parallel mass movement is
going on indepently of the Nationalists. Hence Communist
international should support communist parties, workers &
peasant parties & people's struggle.
