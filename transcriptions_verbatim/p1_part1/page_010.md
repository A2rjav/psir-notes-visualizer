Q. What are the salient ideas present in Manusmriti?
Who is Manu?
Manu is known as Manas Putra of Brahma.
Who is Brahma?
Brahma is a creator of Universe.

Manu is known as the 1st king and the law giver.
It is believed that Arajakta or Anarchy was prevail-
ing.
What is Anarchy?
Arajakta means Matsyanyaya which means Big fish
eating the small fish. Hence, people requested God
or Brahma and Brahma has created Manu.
Why?
Manu will establish Dharma. Manu will tell what
Dharma is.
What is Dharma?
According to Rigveda 'Prithvim Dharmam Dhritam'.
Dharma is what holds the earth. Because of Dharma
Earth sustains, Adharma will lead to the Arajakta
or Pralaya.
What is the source of Dharma?
Source of Dharma is Rta (Riti)..
What is Rta (Riti)?
Rta (Riti) means cosmic or Natural law ie. law
given by Brahma means law of Universe.
Rta governs Universe, Dharma governs human society..
