Q. What are the major traditions in Ancient Indian thought
do they reflect continuity or change, or continuity with
change?

[Diagram:

Dharmashastra Dandashastra]

According to Bhikhu Parekh India reflect more of a conti-
nuity than change. The 2 traditions Hindu & Buddhist
tradition do not differ in a big way. Buddhism shares
some basic assumptions with Hinduism. Eg. the concept
of Soul, Karma, Rebirth. It is better to describe Buddhism
as Rebel child of Hinduism or the protestantism of the
east.

Hindu Tradition:-
According to Bhikhu Parekh, the 2 core concepts of Hindu
traditions are Danda and Dharma. Both the ideas are
present in Both texts. The difference in Dharmashastra
and Dandashastra is only in degree rather than kind.
