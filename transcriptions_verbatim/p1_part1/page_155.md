Phase II of Indian Party system-
Started from 1989 onwards. It is known as phase
of multi party system or phase of coalition govt.

What do you understand by the regionalisation or
fragmentation of Indian party system?
What are the causes & consequences of regionalisation &
fragmentation?
What do you understand by coalition politics? What are
the unique features of India's coalition culture? Is
coalition desirable for India?

What is fragmentation?
Fragmentation denotes national parties facing defection
& formation of new parties. |
Regionalisation denotes the presence of regional
parties at National level govt. & politics.
In case of India fragmentation & regionalisation are
inter linked concepts.
Decline of congress has been due to the deinstitutiona-
lisation of Democracy within the party. As power got
concentrated in the hands of central leadership within
the party ~~left~~ many regional leaders (satraps), which
have been maintained within congress by Nehruvian
consensus left the party & formed their regional
parties. the process continued during the time of Rajiv
Gandhi.
