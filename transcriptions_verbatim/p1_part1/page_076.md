1 Why two stage revolution?
British have established their image in the minds of
Indians that they have established rule of law
Better law & order, social reforms, introduction of
modern education. There was a perception that Briti-
sh rule was better than the rule by native princes.
Hence, Gandhi needed time to show the real chara-
cter of the British rule.

Gandhi was also a great psychologist who knew that
Masses have limited capacity Hence Gandhi used to
call off the movement before the weakness of the
movement come to surface.

According to Bipin chandra, Gandhi was also a great
strategist. Gandhi knew when to start the movement
and when to call off. Gandhi never left the ball in
the court of opponent. The place & time was always
of Gandhi's choice.

__Gandhi's movements were not reactive but proacti-__
__ve.__
Gandhi's movements had active & passive phases. Which
Bipin chandra calls the strategy of struggle, Truce &
struggle. During passive phases Gandhi continued with
constructive programmes.
