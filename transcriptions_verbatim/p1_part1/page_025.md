__BUDDHIST POLITICAL THOUGHT__

Buddhism is other worldly religion whereas Politics
belongs to the material ~~world.~~ The status of Buddha
as a political philosopher remains contested. Buddha left his
own kingdom. However, many kings at that time used to
visit Buddha to take advice on good Governance. Scholars
like Professor Gopal Guru and Gail Omvedt considered
Buddha can be considered as Political Philosopher

What are the Salient ideas of Buddhism.
-> There are 3 basic principles -
(i) Anicca     (ii) Anatta     (iii) Dukha
- Anicca means nothing is permanent
- Anatta means deniel of self interest
- Dukha means world is full of sorrow.

• There are 4 noble truths.
(i) World is full of sorrow.
(ii) Ignorance greed & hatred is the cause of sorrow.
(iii) Liberation from sorrow means liberating oneself from
    ignorance.
(iv) To liberate oneself from ignorance follow 8 fold path.
- Right thinking, Right Aspiration, Right Speech, Right conduct,
  Right beliefs, Right efforts, Right source of livelihood,
  Right contemplation and meditation.
