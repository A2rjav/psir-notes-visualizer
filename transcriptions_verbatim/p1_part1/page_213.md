6) 279 A: It will introduce GST council. GST council shall consists
    of following member
        1) Union finance minister as a chairperson
        2) State minister of finance or revenue or any other
            minister
        3) Vice chairman to be selected from among state representative
        4) Union minister of State in charge of finance and
            revenue if exist will also be a member.

* __Role of GST council:__
        Recommendation to the union govt and state govt on
    1) Rate of taxes
    2) Goods to be exempted
    3) Model GST laws
    4) Any special rate for a specified period to raise additional
        resources during natural calamity or disaster
    5) Special provisions w.r.t for N-E state, J&K, HP & Uttarakhand
    6) Any other matter as council may decide.
    7) It will recommend the date from which GST will be
        levied on crude petroleum, high speed diesel, petrol or
        motor, spirit, natural gas, aviation fuel.

* __2nd role__
        GST shall establish a mechanism for adjudication of any
    dispute bet^n govt of India and govt at one or more states
    or any combination.

* __Working procedure:__
        Internal working shall be determined by council. However
    act mention qt
        1) Quorum of council:
            Half of total members will constitute the to quorum

        2) Decision:
            Concept of weighted voting has been introduced
        The weight of union is 1/3rd and, weight of state
        is 1/3rd. Decision will be taken by not less than 3/4th
        of the weighted vote.

        No proceeding shall be called in question because
    of any vacancy, any procedural irregularity if it
    / does not affect merit of case.
