(III) __Whether preamble is aid in the interpretation of the constitution__
In 1950 in A.K. Gopalan case, SC rejected the view that preamble ^should^ be "The Guiding star in the interpretation of the constitution". SC clarified that Preamble can be used as an aid in the interpretation of the constitution only where there is a lack of clarity.

In Berubari case 1960, SC again clarified that preamble will not be used if the language of the provision in the constitution is clear. Only when terms are ambiguous & capable of two meanings, preamble to be used
Preamble shall not be the source of any power if it is not explicitly given in the constitution nor preamble is a source of prohibition if any power is explicitly given in constitution.

$\rightarrow$ Keshvanand Bharti case.
SC held that Preamble furnish valuable guidance in the matter of Interpretation of the constitution & constitutio should be read & interpreted in the light of the grand & noble vision expressed in the preamble.
