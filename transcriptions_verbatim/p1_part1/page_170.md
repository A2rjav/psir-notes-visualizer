Prof. Anand Chakravarth has suggested that gout. of
India has not given enough space to the Pressure
Groups. Many such associations which have dis agree-
ment with the Gout. have been banned under
ULPA (Unlawful Activities Preven^n Act). If govt had
given more space & has shown more accomodation
it would have been much easier to deal with
the Alienation & insurgencies.
Myron Weinor in his book 'Politics of scarcity',
Rudolph & Rudolph in their book 'Pursuit of Lakshmi'
have analysed the role of community based political
associations (PG). Christophe Jeffrelot has analysed
the role of RSS in Indian Politics.

The nature of Pressure Group politics is changing.
Robert Hardgrave & Stanley Kochanek have analysed
pressure group politics since 1990s.
(i) Pressure Group Politics has become more prominent
since 1990s.
(ii) There has been entries in the influence of corporate
groups.
(iii) There has been increased influence or presence
of foreign lobbies & advocacy groups in India.
(iv) Govt. of India has incorporated many such associations
into public policy making institutions like NAC
(National Advisory council) under UPA & NITI Aayog
at present.
