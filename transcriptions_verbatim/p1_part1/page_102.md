Keshvanand Bharti case.

In KB case SC accepted that it has overlooked that it
has overlooked fact associated with, preamble.
what fact?
Preamble was adopted by Constituent assembly by the
motion which suggest that it is being adopted as the
part of the constitution.

(ii) Whether Preamble can be amended or not?
In KB case SC has clarified that though preamble is
not the provision of the constitution yet it is not out-
side the purview of the amending power of the par-
liament.
Why? According to SC Preamble of Indian constitution
does not walk before the constitution as in case of USA.
It walks with the constitution. SC has given special
emphasis on the fact that Preamble was adopted by
the constituent assembly in the last.
Why?
to ensure the harmony b/w the main text & ideals
present in the preamble. they should not walk on sepa-
rate tracks.
Judiciary wanted to avoid the situation where preamble
comes in conflict with the main body. It may happen
that because of amendment the conflict comes b/w
the ideals of preamble & the amended constitution.
