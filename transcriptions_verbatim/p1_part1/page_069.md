Que. Compare Arthashastra & Dharmashastra with reference
     to state power.
  - Explaination of Dharmashastra & Arthashastra.
  - Two traditions of Ancient Indian thoughts (Hinduism)
  - According to Bhiku Parekh, there is more of continuity than
    change. The difference is only of ~~eg~~ degree rather than kind.
  - Dharma & Dand are two issues which are discussed.
  - W.r.t. the state power there is a remarkable similarity
    b/w these 2 texts. Both believe in - quasi contractual theory
    of origin of state, the system of hereditory monarchy.
    The objective of king is to help people in achievement of
    Yogkshem. (4 purushartha's).
  - With reference to the state power both believe that the
    most important role of the state is to ensure all castes
    of observed their Dharma. Hence king has danda, though
    king has power to exercise Dand but king is not above
    Dharma. He has to exercise his power according to the Dharma
    as given in Shruti's & Smriti's. Manu has given an elaborate
    view over the duties of king. Principles of punishment.

Que. Significance of Dharma in Ancient Political thought
     Dharma is Archictonic principle governing Hindu way of life.
     The concept of Dharma is - Prithvim Dharmam Dhritam,
     one who holds the earth. Source of Dharma is <s>*</s> Rita (Rte)
     Rta is a cosmic law. In Hindu texts every varna has his Dhar-
     ma. In Hindu texts there is interdependence b/w Dharma &
     Danda. Even king is under Dharma. If Dharma is not followed
     it will lead to Arajkta.
