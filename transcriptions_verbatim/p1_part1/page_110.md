Freedom of religion is available only in essential
practices not in non-essential practices. Eg. SC held
that Tandav Nrutya is not a essential practice of
Hinduism. What is essential practice is determined
not by any priest but by Judiciary.

which bans conversions. According to the christian
missionaries it restricts freedom to propagate religi-
on. Freedom to propagate. religion ultimately means
freedom to convert.
