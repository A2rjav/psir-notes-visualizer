Making of the Indian Constitution.

Q. To what extent it is appropriate to say that consti-
tuent assembly did not represent the general will
of the people in India. Give arguement to substantiate
your view.

-> Context of the Debate.
There has always been a question mark on the repre-
sentativeness of the constituent assembly as well as
the legitimacy of the constitution. Those who challenge
the representativeness & the legitimacy give following
arguements. on
(i) Constituent assembly was not directly elected by the
people.
(ii) Constituent assembly had two categories of members.
(a) Those who were represeoting princly states.
they were nominated by the princess.
(b) Those who representing British provinces they were
indirectly elected by the members of provincial
legislative assemblies by proportional representation
It is to be noted that at that time only 5% of
Indians had right to vote.
(iii) Even after constituent assembly framed the constitution
it has not been subjected to the referendum to know the
will of the people as happened in France. (Constitution
of 5th republic).
