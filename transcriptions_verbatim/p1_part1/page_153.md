  __Phase III- Phase of survival. (since 1989)__

In 1984, congress got absolute majority but in 1989
congress could not get even simple majority to form
the govt. By this time its organisational weaknesses
became more apparent & competition became more
pronounced. In 1990s, second Democratic upsurge
took place by which majority of Dalits have also
left congress.

2014 elections are considered as Demise of congress
because congress even lost the so called congress
states. like MH, HR, DL. Not only congress social base
is shrinking its territorial space is also shrinking.
From the state of congress system, congress has rea-
ched to a situation where majority of the states
have become non-congress states.
