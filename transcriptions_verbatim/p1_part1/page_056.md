Influences on M.N.Roy
He was influenced by Humanism of Protagoras,
Materialism of Hobbes, secularism of Locke, Utilitaria-
nism of Bentham & Ethics of Spinoza.

Compare Gandhi with M.N. Roy
(i) Both were contemporary
(ii) Gandhi was politician & M.N. Roy was philosopher.
(iii) Gandhi was most successful during freedom struggle.
M.N. Roy was remarkable failure.
(iv) Gandhi was more influenced by Indian religion & philosophy.
M.N. Roy was influenced by western philosophy.
(v) Gandhi was spiritualist & M.N. Roy was Humanist.
(vi) There is a evolution in the ideas of both but there is a big
change in basic ideas of M.N. Roy. In Gandhi, we see not
much change in ideas, only refinement in techniques.
(vii) M.N. Roy was Materialist Gandhi rejected it.
(viii) There are some similarities as both supported partly less
democracy, grass root democracy, still M.N. Roy had a
difference with Gandhi w.r.t. Industrialisation.
