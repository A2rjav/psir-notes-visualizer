(v) __Revolution__
Marx suggested changing the mode of production.
M.N. Roy suggested changing the mode of thinking.
True revolution is not the revolution in the material structure
True revolution is mental revolution.

M.N. Roy tried to give humanist interpretation to Marxism.
__Phase III__
-> __M.N. Roy as a Radical Humanist__ -
Goal of Radical Humanism -
Freedom in Absolute sense
Freedom is not just political independence, Freedom is also not
just ending the social and economic deprivations. Freedom
means freedom in complete sense. Freedom even from supe
rstitions & the fear of supernatural powers. We can achieve
freedom through scientific rationality. Science can liberate
man.
Why it is called as Humanism & what is the meaning of
Humanism?
Humanism is a philosophy which keeps Human Being at the
centre. Greek philosophy was Humanist. Humanism came
under decline during medieval time when religion became
the centre of universe. Humanism reemerged at the time of
Renaissance. ~~Marx~~
M.N. Roy called his Humanism earlier as new humanism.
What is new Humanism?
New Humanism is more refined Humanism than the Huma
nism at the time of Renaissance. It is the humanism based
on the experience of centuries.
