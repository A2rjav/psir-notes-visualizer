• Aam Aadmi Party (AAP).

AAP is considered as By product of Globalisation.
AAP phenomena is not unique for Indian Politics,
similar type of movements have been found in other
Countries also like Syriza Party in Greece, Workers Party
in Brazil, Tehree-i-Insaf in Pakistan, Peoples movement
in Hongkong.
What is the linkage with Globalisation?
ⓘ Globalisation has increased social & Economic Inequali-
  ties.
(ii) Globalisation has increased urbanisation.
(iii) Globalisation has also increased the magnitude of
    Corruption.
(iv) It has increased democratic consciousness.
(v) There is assertion by Urban middle classes as well as
    poors to have ~~more~~ transparent & equitable growth.

AAP emerged out of Anti-corruption movement.
AAP came into existence in 2012. AAP has been able to
form the govt in National capital Territory. AAP ideolo-
gy is Anti-politics & anti-ideology, anti-caste, anti-religion.
The aim of AAP is not to gain power but to gain power
to correct the system.
