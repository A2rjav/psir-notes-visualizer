● __Influences on Gandhi -__
(i) He was influenced by his mother. Gandhi's inclina-
tion towards religion was because of his mother.
(ii) Gandhi was influenced by Vaishnavism, Jainism,
Christianity as well as other religions.
(iii) Gandhi was also influenced by Leo Tolstoy, Plato,
Thoreau, Socrates, John Ruskin.

What is Ahimsa?
Ahimsa is the foundational principle of Gandhism.
According to Gandhi, the objective of life is to under-
stand truth or God. Gandhi never claimed to know the
truth. Gandhi suggested that he can tell the path on
the basis of which man can reach to the [illegible] truth.
Ahimsa is the path.
Why Ahimsa is the correct path?
Gandhi believes in the continuity of ends and means.
If the end is to attain the God. means have to be those
implied by God. God is a symbol of love & sacrifice.
Hence, for Gandhi Ahimsa is not just not using violence,
Ahimsa is much deeper. It is based on the feeling of
love & fraternity.
