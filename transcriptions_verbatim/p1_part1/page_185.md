Q. Discuss dynamic nature of Indian federalism and roles of various
 factor shaping Indian federalism.

 Political system do not operate in vacuum they operate in an
environment. They influence environment and get influenced. The
functioning of federal system has been impacted by social
economic, environment. There has also been role of external factors

__What change is observed?__

 There has been the flow of power from centre towards the
state govt. as well as flow of power from state to union govt.
The dynamics is not operating in a single direction.
Factors bringing change
1) The most imp. factor has been changing the nature of party
 system. Coalition politics increase the bargaining power of
 state govt
2) Globalisation. Globalisation has impacted dynamics in
 diffn ways
 i. Globalisation has resulted into change in economic policies based on
 LPG model. It has resulted into rolling back of state
 and intro. of public-private partnership. This reduces
 dependence of state govt on ~~[illegible]~~ union.
 ii. State govt can attract foreign investment which can
 also promote growth and infra. development
 programme,
 iii) Structural adjustment ~~programme~~ require implementation of
 good governance. Good governance is not possible
 without administrative and financial devolution. Since
 state financial commission alternative devolution scheme
 have been introduced. There is continuous increase in
 , share of state govt. in union taxes.
 iv) Expert bodies have consistently recommended a
 drastic reduction in centrally sponsored scheme &
 gives greater space for state government.
 v) For long, planning commission has been criticised for
 anti-federal and non-democratic character. Planning
 commission replaced with Niti Aayog. Niti Aayog
 incorporate state in proactive role in policymaking
