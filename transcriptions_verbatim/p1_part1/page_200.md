__Manners of use__:
There are numerous occasion when exercise of power
under art 356 can be considered as arbitrary and in the
interest of ruling party at union.
e.g. In 1977, when Janata Party came to power at union
it has dissolved the legislative assemblies of 9 state
ruled by congress in single row on the ground that
congress has lost mandate.
When Indira Gandhi came to power in 1980 she has
repeated act dissolving assembly in 9 states on similar
ground.
__What have been consequence of misuse of art 356?__
It has not only undermined federal spirit of cons
It has also undermined & threated national interest It
was one of the reason for growth of insurgency in
J&K as well as in Punjab.
__What is the current status?__
As a result of judicial activism, presidential activism
& coalition politics there has been reduction in misuse of
art 356. But misuse has not been completely eliminated as
we can see in case of Arunachal Pradesh & Uttarakhand
in 2016.
__What is the position of SC w.r.t art 356?__
In S.R.Bommai case in 1994 SC has declared federalism
as integral component of the basic structure of constitution &
not just an administrative convenience. It has led down the
stringent condition for valid exercise of power under art 365.
1) Court may not inquire what advice was given by
the council of minister to president; but court may
is required the prod^n of material evidence on the basis
of which decision taken.
2) The material evidence should be speaking document
~~which will force~~ any rational person to take decision.
3) Assembly will not be dissolved immediately it will be
kept suspended till the resl^n passed by both house.
