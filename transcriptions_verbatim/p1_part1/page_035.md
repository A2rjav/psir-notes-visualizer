Aurobindo Ghosh has based the theory of Indian Nation
alism on the ideals of Neo-Vedantism. (Sanatan Dharma)
Neo-Vedantism was propounded by Vivekananda.
Vivekananda attacked Brahmanism and caste system. Bra
hmanism is a reason for lack of unity in India. Vivekana-
nda held that amidst the diversity there exists a funda-
mental unity in India. This fundamental unity is the basis
of Indian Nationalism. The fundamental unity comes from
the ideal of Sanatana Dharma about the unity of man-
kind. We all children of one God. We all are part of the
universal spirit. Aurobindo Ghosh has also built the theory
of Nationalism and Patriotism on the basis of the idea of
Mother India given by Bankim Chandra Chatterji. India is a
Nation because all of us are the children of the mother India.
He appealed for patriotism, He appealed youth to free moth
er India from the bondages & chains of slavery.
He presented Mother India in Bengali tradition of Goddess
Shakti. He challenged British that mother India or Goddess
Shakti is representing the Shakti of millions of awakened
Indians. It would not be possible to suppress spirit of
Nationalism now.
Thus Aurobindo Ghosh has spiritualised the concept of
Nationalism. He projected Indian Nationalism as a divine
mission. According to him, God has given the special miss-
ion to India. The mission is to protect the Humanity.
Humanity can be protected only through India. No other
culture or civilisation has such values which can protect
the Humanity. India cannot accomplish the mission without
political freedom. Rise of India as Nation is inevitable.
