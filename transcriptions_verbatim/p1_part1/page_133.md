**② Rajni Kothari has appreciated the role of caste in
Indian Politics. He links the role of caste with the succ-
ess of Democracy in India. Caste was a factor for
mobilisation of people & bringing them to the ele-
ctoral system. He suggest that caste has not only
influenced politics but has also been influence by-
politics. In the words of Rajni Kothari not only politi-
cs has become caste ridden, but caste has become politi-
cised.
