No serious attempts has been ever made for the
scientific Historiography of the assembly & the
constitution.
In the words of Pratapbhanu Mehta, despite the centra-
lity of our social & political life the constitution has
been ill served by our Historical imagination. There has
been no serious & deep Historiography associated with
our constitution, which can put it in proper Historical &
Philosophical perspective.

- Aditya Nigam in his article 'A text without Author:
locating constituent assembly as an event.' He has argued
that there was no 'particular set of values' that guided
the constitution, no single author & no percieved notion
of a nation. The Hegemonic notion of the Nation glossed
over the differences that were prevailing in the assembly

● Different social and Political Perspectives :
Constituent assembly was deliberative platform & the
various provisions of the constitution have been ado-
pted after long debates.
Some of the prominent debates were do-
(i) Between Gandhian's & Modernists.
Gandhians wanted panchayati Raj whereas Nehru &
Ambedkar ~~favited~~ favoured representative democracy.
(ii) there was debate on nature of ~~de~~ federalism. whether
Indias should go for model given in cabinet mission plan
or centralised federation.
