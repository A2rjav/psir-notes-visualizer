Perspectives on National movement

Context of the debate-
Imperialists Historians & administrators like Lord
Curzon, Duffrin always challenged the existence of
India as a Nation & claims of congress as a repre-
sentative of Nation. According to them India is nothing
more than Geographical expression. Imperialists histo-
rians (Cambridge school Eg. Percival spear), Louis Namier
and Anil seal suggest that the political activities happen
ing on the subcontinent b/w 1857 to 1947 cannot be
called as National movement.
Why?
Nation has to exist before National movement.
How do they describe these activities?
They call it as communal movement they call the leaders
as power brokers. They were interested in getting Briti-
sh favours for the members of their community. There
was no grade idea behind political activities, activities
were guided by Animal extincts. It was a communal
politics & formation of Pakistan is the logical outcome
of the communal politics. The nature of politics in conte
mporary India is also not changed.
-> Nationalists Interpretation-
Nationalist leaders & Historians reject the imperialists
discourse. Early Nationalists like Surendranath Banerjee
accepted that India was not a Nation but he also held
that India is 'Nation in Making'
