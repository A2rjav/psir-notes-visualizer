* __Satlaj-Yamuna link canal__
    The problem started with state reorganisation in 1966 when Himachal Pradesh and Haryana were seperated. Haryana has been demanding share in water agreeing at the time of creation of state.
    Tribunal have been constituted which has given its award. Haryana has also given advance payment to state of Punjab to construct link on its side.
    In 2004, Panjab legislative assembly took an unpresident action terminating all agreement with all state. The matter has been refered to advisary jurisdiction of SC under art 143. In 2014, SC held that act is unconstitutional
    Present govt of Punjab has brought another act denatifying the land acquired for construction of canal. It has started returning land to original owners. It has also returned check to Haryana govt. SC has asked Punjab govt to maintain the status quo. Neither the Punjab nor Karnataka is adhering to SC directives.
    There are some examples where tribunal system has worked. It was possible because of +ve role played by union govt.
    Narmada: It could be solved because parties have first reached to solⁿ then constituted tribunal.
    Krishna: Tribunal has to be constituted twice. The award of 1ˢᵗ was not accepted by any party. but the new problem has emerged because of creation of Telangana. Parties are MH, KR, AP, Telangana.
