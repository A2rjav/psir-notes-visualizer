According to Bipin chandra, Indian National Move-
ment is not the only example of Mass movement.
Other examples include French, Russian & chinese
revolution. However, it was unique mass movement
The largest mass movement reaching to its logical end
He calls it as most spectacular movement that even toda
we can feel the warmth of National movement.

→ Mass Movements can be categorised as:
(i) Controlled & Uncontrolled.
French revolution was spontaneous uncontrolled out -
burst whereas Indian, Russian & chinese revolutions
have been controlled mass movements.
(ii) Protracted or overnight Mass Movement.
Russian & chinese movements were overnight whereas
Gandhian movement was protracted fight. Gandhi
knew that Indians have to fight at two levels:
(a) War of Position
(b) War of Maneuver.
