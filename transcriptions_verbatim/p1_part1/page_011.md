• __Source of cosmic Law :__
Source of cosmic law is shruti.
Shruti means vedas & vedas are revealed knowledge.
Smriti's are based on shruti.
Hence, Manusmriti is a part of vedic culture.
Manusmriti deals with Dharma. 1st in General then in
specific context. Some of the main ideas present in
Manusmriti can be enumerated as:
__Concept of Purushartha__
Purushartha means four goals of life.
ⓐ Dharma ⓑ Artha ⓒ Kama ⓓ Moksha.
Hindu way of Life has been a balanced view of Life
which includes material and spiritualistic goals.
(ii) __Concept of Ashrama__
There are 4 stages in life & there is a specific goals
in all phases.
ⓐ Brahmacharya ⓑ Grahastha ⓒ Vanaprastha ⓓ Samnyas
(iii) __Concept of Sanskara__
Sanskara means rituals. There are 16 Sanskara's.
From Garbhadhan to Antyeshti (cremation).
(iv) __Concept of State & Discription of Raj Dharma-__
chapter 7 of Manusmriti deals with state & Raj Dharma.
