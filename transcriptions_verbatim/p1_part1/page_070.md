What is constitutional method.
Constitutionalism is based on the ideology of liberalism.
According to liberalism Human Beings are rational. Hence
conflicts can be resolved through dialogue. There is no need
of any street politics or violent method.
Gokhale in his address to Allahabad session of Indian
National congress in 1907 described, constitutional methods
as method adopted to bring desired changes through the
action of constituted authorities. It includes bringing
