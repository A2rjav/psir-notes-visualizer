*Concerns of state government
1) The taxes which come under state list are rigid and
low yielding.
2) There are limitations on borrowing power of state govt
e.g. State governments can borrow only from domestic
market.
If they have borrowed any loan from union government
or they have taken any loan from market for which
union has given counter guarantee. They can't see
fresh loan even without permission of union.
3) The present constitutional limit on profession tax is
too low i.e. 2500/- per annum.
4) Union govt. get exemption from payment of ~~sales tax~~
w.r.t. consumption of electricity.
5) There is a vertical and horizontal imbalance. State
govt. social expenditure is more whereas their income is
less.
6) Though finance commissions recommendation have been
favourable to state govt yet state govt. have no
representation in finance commission. It is entirely union body.
Uptill now there have been concern w.r.t to role of
planning commission. Planning commission has undermined
not only federalism, but also parliamentary democracy.
Planning commission became super cabinet.
Present govt has replaced planning commission with
Niti Aayog.
States have been given share in the union taxes
14th finance commission has made the sharable
revenue to 42%. Originally all union taxes were
not sharable, but with the recommendation of 10th
FC and ~~an~~ amendment has been made ~~88th~~ 80th CA
which has made all taxes sharable. Though
amendment has made all taxes sharable, but it
does not extend to the cess and surcharge.

At present state want share in cess and surcharge
also. States are also authorised to get grants.
There are 2 types of grant
1) Statutory grant ~~(275)~~ (art 275)
2) discretionary grant (art 282)
