M.N. ROY

Time- 1887 - 1954
M.N. Roy is known as the contemporary critic of Gandhi.
If Gandhi was a politician, M.N. Roy was a philosopher.
Sudipto Kaviraj calls M.N. Roy as Remarkable failure.
There is a transition in the ideas of M.N. Roy.

● Ideational Journey of M.N. Roy-
Phase I
M.N. Roy as a Revolutionary.
M.N. Roy was influenced by Jatin Das. He was a member
of Anushilan Samiti. He was sent abroad to procure
arms for revolutionaries. He visited USA. He got chance to
attend the meeting which was addressed by Lala lajpat Roy.
He asked the question as to what strategy Indian national
Congress has for the upliftment of poors. He was not con-
vinced with the answer. He started his journey to search
for a satisfactory answer. He came to know about Karl Marx.
He became admirer of Marx & critical of revolutionaries.
According to him, Revolutionaries are not going to be su-
ccessful because they lack the scientific programme of
action. Revolutionaries are relying on individual acts of
revolution. Revolutionaries lacked support and base amo-
ng masses. He was convinced that India requires mass
revolution. M.N. Roy became the founding member of
Communist party of Mexico. Very soon he was recogni-
sed an Important personality in International commu-
nist movement because of his understanding of Marxism.
