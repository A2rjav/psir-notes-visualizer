[Margin: ●] __Duties of the King -__
(i) Rakshan and Palan
(ii) King should help his subjects to achieve Yogshem.
Yogshem means helping his people to achieve the 4 purushartha's.
(iii) Manusmriti give a detailed view on the principles of
taxation. 1/6^th of the produce of the fertile land belong
to the king. 1/8^th of the less fertile and 1/10^th of the
least fertile should go to the king.
King also has the right to possess the 1/6^th of other
produces like milk, Honey, Meat, Butter and other
commodities.
Manu also gives a detailed view of a system of punish-
ment punishment varies according to varna. Lower is
the varna higher is the punishment.
Manusmriti remains a controversial work. In modern
India one of the greatest admirer of Manusmriti
was Dr. S. Radhakrishnan. whereas greatest critic of
Manusmriti was Ambedkar, Ramaswami Naikar, Dr.
Ambedkar suggested to put Dynamite on Veda's and
Manusmriti. Manusmriti is also criticised by Feminist
scholars for subordinate status of women given in
Manusmriti. It is to be noted that there are multiple
interpretations of Manusmriti, Manusmriti remains a
highly contested work.
