Moderates were for the gradual reforms & extre-
mists were getting impatient and want it immi-
diately. There was difference in their methods as
Moderates believed in constitutional method and
extremists were for the direct action. Tilak rejected
the method of petitions he asserted swaraj as a
Birth right ~~as a of~~ Indians. He even held that Indians
have right to resist or Praja Droh. Because Britishers
have established exploitative governance.
causes of rise of extremists-
① Foundation was laid by the moderates.
② No concrete achievement of moderates.
③ British govt. started rolling back the reforms and
representations of Indians. Eg. Calcutta Corporation
Act 1899 & Indian University Act, 1904.
④ Political Atmosphere was charged with Anti-British
feeling Because of the British Attitude towards
famines & epidemics. Britishers have actually with-
drawn the relief from affected areas. Partition of
Bengal was the final blow to moderates. Indian
National Congress officially adopted the programme
of swadeshi
Evaluation of Extremists-
It was the next logical step once moderates failed to
make any achievements. It was necessary to maintain
the credibility of congress. Extremists created the
foundation for the launch of Gandhi's Mass Movement.
