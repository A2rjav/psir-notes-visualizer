(1) Preamble.
Though there were proposals in the constituent assem-
bly to introduce the term secularism but it was not
adopted. However, secularism has been introduced in
the preamble by 42^nd amend ment act 1976. The act
of introducing secularism in the preamble itself
remains controversial.
Why controversial?
(11) It was introduced when emergency was imposed &
opposition was behind the bars.
(ii) It is redundant because it is present in the preamble
or not it does not alter the status as india as a
secular state.
