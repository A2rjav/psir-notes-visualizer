③ __Durga (Fort)__
Durga is a symbol of offensive as well as Defensive
power of the state. Kautilya has given detailed ideas
with respect to different types of forts like Hill Forts,
Desert forts, water forts.

④ __Janapada (Village/ Rural Areas)__
Janapada is where the subjects live. King has to take
care of Janapada as Janapada is a source of strength.
It is a source of revenue and other necessary commodities

⑤ __Bala (Force)__
Kautilya suggest the Heridetary ~~also~~ army of Kshatriya's.
other Varna's can be considered in some exceptional
situations.

⑥ __Kosh (Treasury)__
Kosh should be sufficient so that King can take care of
the welfare functions. Kosh should be sufficient to meet
any emergency or Natural calamity.

⑦ __Mitra (Allie)__
Mitra's have symbolic importance. It is the symbol of
the strength of the King. More no. of Mitra's means more
powerful the King because everyone wants to be the friend
of the powerful.
