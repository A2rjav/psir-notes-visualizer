Q What is the significance of Preamble to
the constitution? Bring out the philosophy
of Indian constition, as enshrined in the preamble?

Q Preamble embody the fundamental values & philosophy
and which constitution is based.

① Preamble is like intro. to constiⁿ to understand the main text.
In case of Indian constiⁿ preamble has a special position.
Pandit Thakurdas Bhargav in constituent assembly He called
'Preamble as Jewel set (Precious).
K.M. Munshi called the preamble as the Horoscope.
Ernst Barker one of the prominent political scholar has
held that Preamble of India sums up of its entire
philosophy for which he has written a book of 300 pages.
Unlike other constitutions preamble of Indian constitution
is a sacred document considered by SC as a integral part
of constiⁿ containing elements of Basic structure.
SC held that preamble express the noble vision which
inspired us during the National Movement & should be
considered as fundamental in interpretation of constitution

Preamble tells the philosophy of Indian Political system.
Granville Austin in his book Indian Constiⁿ cornerstone of
nation considered Preamble along with Part III & IV of the
constiⁿ forming the conscience of the constitution.
It tells the philosophy of Indian politics. Preamble suggest
that the aim of constitution is to establish a society
where unity & integrity & human dignity are to be treated
as supreme goals it establishes a social order based on
very substantive & comprehensive idea of Justice ie.
social, economic & political.
