Dalit perspective is represented in the works of
many Dalit leaders & scholars. We can also call it as
Subaltern perspective . One of the earliest expression
about the British Raj was given by Jyotiba Phule
Jyotiba Phule was critic of Brahmanism . He held
that Peshwa Raj was worse than British Raj.
He appreciated British for establishing the rule of
law for the 1st time.

~~Ambed~~ Ambedkar was influenced by the ideas of
Jyotiba Phule. Ambedkar rejected the idea of India
as a Nation. He rejected congress led movement as
a National movement. His idea of Nationalism was
influenced by French Revolution. According to him,
nation is built on the feeling of fraternity. He never
joined INC never participated in congress led mo-
vements, supported Jinna's demand, opposed Quit
India movement & wanted that British should stay.

We can also bring the perspective of E.V. Ramaswa-
mi Naikar, popularly known as Periyar. Naikar ini-
tially joined INC but left congress in 1925 on the
presumption that congress is the party of Brahmins.
There is no scope for the members of other comm-
unity. He joined justice party . He initiated self
respect movement against Brahmanism. He also
supported Jinna's demand for Pakistan. He formed
a political Party Dravid khadgam (DK). He put for-
ward demand for seperate state for Dravidians.
