⑦ Gandhi on Ends and Means-
Gandhi was extremely critical of Machiavellinism
Machiavelli has seperated politics and ethics. Gandhi
was influenced by Gokhale. Gokhale was advocating
for bringing ethics into politics. According to Gandhi,
Politics without Ethics/ Religion is like a '" Death Trap'
Gandhi rejected the seperation of ends & means..
Gandhi rejected the theory that ends justify the
Means. Gandhi emphasised on purity of means. He
emphasised on primacy of the purity of means. He
believed that we cannot reach to the right ends
through wrong means. In his book Hind swaraj he
suggest that it is illogical to expect rose flowers
~~by~~ by sowing the seed of Babul.

Thus Gandhi empasised on Spiritualisation & purification
of Politics. we can see continuity between Gandhi's
thought & the thoughts of ancient Greek thinkers like
Plato & socrates, who never seperated Good Life & Good poli-
tical life. Gandhi has applied his ends & means theory
in choosing the methods in freedom struggle. If freedom
struggle is to be Satyagraha, means employed has to be
non-violent
