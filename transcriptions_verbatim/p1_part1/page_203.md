__Why governors position is controversial ?__

    Governor was expected to act as lynchpin of co-op.
federalism; but governor became an institution of bargaining
federalism. The institution of governor has misused by union
party at union level. There has been huge resentment against
misuse in states like J&K, TN, WB, Punjab

__Where is the problem?__

    The problem is because of discretionary power enjoyed
by governor under constitution.

The discretionary power: President does not enjoy much
discretionary power. The only discretionary power of presidents
are sending the bill and advice for reconsideration of
executive and parliament.

President enjoys some situational discretion in case of
hung parliament. Art 163 There shall be council of minister
headed by CM to aid and advice the governor except in
those situation under the constitution where governor
is required to act as his discretion.

__Nature of discretionary power:__

    Art 163(2) : If any question arises whether any matter is
matter where governor should have exercised discretionary
power or should not have the decision of governor is final and
it can't challenge in court of law. __Is governors discretionary
power is subjected to J.R?__

    Yes, governors discretionary power is subjected to Judicial
Review.

On what ground of malified intention.

__What are the situations under constitution where governor
is required to use discretion?__

    Art 200 : It is related to assent and reserving the
    Art 201 : Reserving the bill for presidents consideration
    Art 174 : Power to ~~send~~ call the session, prorogue the session
                 & dissolve the session.
    Art 175 : Power to send messages to the house.
    Art 356 : SC has already so settled that the use of
                 discretionary power under art 356 (S R Bommai Case)
                 and art 174 (Arunachal pradesh case) is
