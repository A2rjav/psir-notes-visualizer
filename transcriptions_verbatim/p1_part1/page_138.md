According to Sanjiv Baruah, the identity of our existen-
ce as a nation is very new in comparison to our identity
of the member of a particular cultural religious or
linguistic group. It is natural for sub continental size
polity and a new nation like India.

What is a region?
Region is a Geographical concept by which different
regions are demarcated on the basis of tropographical
features , climate etc.
However, in politics region is not just a territorial unit
It gets combined with other expressions like language
culture, relgion . Eg. Regionalism in Kashmir & Punjab
has been based on religion, Regionalism in Tamilnadu
has been based on language. Regional movements can be
considered as sub-national movements. Hence, there is
always a potential that sub-national movement gets trans-
formed into the movement for national self determination
or movement for seperate statehood , there is a greater
fear of successionists challenges in peripheral regions.
whether movement will sustain for long & whether it will
grow into a successionist challenge depends upon the leader-
ship, availability of resources, role of external powers.
In a situation where a particular ethnic group is concen-
trated in a particular territorial space there is a grea-
ter possibility of demand for seperate statehood as
happened in case of Sri lanka.
