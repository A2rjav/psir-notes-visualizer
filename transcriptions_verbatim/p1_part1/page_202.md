Institution of governor.

Q. According to Sarkaria commission governor act as lynchpin
in co-operative federalism.

governor and give suggestion to reform it.

__What is constitutional position of governor?__
Governor is the head of state executive. In the similar
manner president is head of union executive. Like in case of
union where all executive action are done in the name of
president, in case of state all executive action done in the
name of governor.

__What is the need of governor?__
We have adopted parliamentary form of govt even at
state level. Parliamentary form of govt has 2 heads.
1) Tituler head or nominal head (President & governor)
2) The real head (PM / CM)

Peculiar situation in India:
India is not only parliamentary form of govt India is
also federation.

Which type of federation?
Co-operative federalism.

__What is the uniqueness of federalism?__
interlocking
According to ~~Sarkaria~~ SC governor wears 2 hats on
a single head. According to Sarkaria commission governor is
a lynchpin of India's co-operative federalism connecting
link betn union and state.

__What is the envisaged role of governor?__
1) Governor has to act as a channel of commission betn union
and state. It has to communicate the expectation of
state govt to union & It has to bring national
perspective at the state level.
2) According to Paul Brass Indian const. is not product
of hopes and optimism it is a product of fear & insecurity
At the time of independence there were secessionist
challenges. Hence governor was to act as eye and ear of
the union at the state level.
