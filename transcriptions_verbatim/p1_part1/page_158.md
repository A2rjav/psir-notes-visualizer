At National level the formal beginning of coalition
politics started in 1989 when congress which had
absolute majority in 1984 election could not get
even the simple majority require to form the govt.
on its own. Between 1989 to 1999 India has seen
the phase of political instability. From 1999 onwa-
rds coalition politics started becoming mature.
Vajpayee lead NDA govt. was the 1st experiement of
the successful completion of coalition. followed by
UPA I & UPA II. Present govt. is also a coalition govt.
though the core/main party has enough seats to form
the govt. on its own. though some scholars predict
the era of coalition politics is over but it is too
early to say considering 2014 elections not a normal
elections but wave election. Coalition is inevitable in a
democracy with such a huge diversity.

Unique Features of Indias coalition culture
According to Prof. Vidyut chakravarty, coalition cultu-
re in India if compared with western countries
show many contrasting trends. Eg. coalition in we-
stern countries can be called as 'Coalition by design'
where as coalition in India is coalition by political
calculation still in search of design.

In western countries parties go for ideological cohere-
nce but in India there is a little regard for ideolo-
gical coherence.
