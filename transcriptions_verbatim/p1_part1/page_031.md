AUROBINDO GHOSH

Time-1870-1950

Aurobindo Ghosh is known as Prophet of Indian Nationalism
R. Tagore held that the world will get the message of
India through Aurobindo Ghosh. He is known as a great
Synthesizer. He has synthesized the philosophy of east
with west. He held that East needs west & west needs
east. East has neglected material developement where-
as west as neglected spiritual developement. Aurobind
Ghosh wanted to establish India as 'spiritual guide
to Humanity. He held that independence of India is no
just for the benefits of Indians it is for the benefit
of Humanity. However India can give the message only
if India is Free. He was influenced by Neo-vedantism o
Vivekanand and the ideal of Bharat Mata. Proposed by
Bamkimchandra Chaterjee in his book 'Anandmath'.

• Prominent ideas of Aurobindo Ghosh-
① __Theory of Nationality.__
② Ideal of Human unity-
③ Concept of Freedom-
④ Concept of Integral Yoga.
