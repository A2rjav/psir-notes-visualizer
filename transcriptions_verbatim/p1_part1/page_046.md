[Margin: ⑤] : Gandhi's Critic of Modern Civilisation.
Gandhi in his book Hind Swaraj has criticised Modern
Civilisation. Gandhi called it Satanic Civilisation.
Gandhi was influenced by Edward Carpenter. Edward
Carpenter has shown the negative consequences of
Modern medicine on human health. Gandhi is not the
only person nor the 1st person to criticise modernity.
Gandhi has criticised modernity not just because it is
western in origin. He has criticised modernity because
modernity is based on the values of the materialism,
Utilitarianism, consumerism, Militarism, and. individualism.
According to Gandhi, imperialism and fascism are not
different rather two manifestations of Modernity.
Modernity undermines human dignity. It's values redu-
ces Human Being to the level of animal who is just
concerned with the satisfaction of Appetite.
According to Gandhi, modernity made us animals or
Saitans. Our soul is sleeping when we exploit & use
force against fellow human being!
Gandhi was critical of the manner in which the western
world has gone for developement in last 100 years.
According to Gandhi the path of developement which
was adopted by the west in the last 100 years, if
others also adopt the same path of developement atlea-
st 9 more earth's will be required.
