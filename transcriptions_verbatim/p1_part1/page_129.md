HEADING: Why UCC is required? (In case of Muslims).
---
Why UCC is required? (In case of Muslims).
(i) To improve the status of women among Muslims. It is
argued that Non-implementation of UCC violates the
fundamental principle of Right to Equality.

(ii) According to certain sections Ucc has linkage with the
National Unity & Integration though it is difficult to
know how?

(iii) Flavia Agnes has highlighted the following problematic
aspects w.r.t. UCC.

(a) UCC is a directive.
(b) U/A 29 Minorities enjoys Fundamental Rights w.r.t the
protection of their culture & way of life. The problem-
atic issue is how to reconcile the Art. 14, 44 & 29.
(c) It also goes against the principles of federalism.
Because personal laws come under concurrent list.
(d) Ethnic communities in North East also enjoy special
protection w.r.t their customary rights.
(e) There is a lack of clarity whether muslim personal
laws will be treated as law under article 13(2).
In state of Bombay Vs Narasu Appu Mali judiciary
has held that personal laws ~~are~~ treated as laws within
the definition of Art ~~A~~ 13(2) which mentions 'about
laws in force'. Recently SC has decided to constitute
a constitutional bench to determine the legality of
Triple Talaq, Bench will also give its opinion on the
status of the personal laws w.r.t. Art. 13(2).
