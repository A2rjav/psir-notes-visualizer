__M.N. Roy's criticism of Marxism -__
M.N. Roy has criticised the basic principles of Marxism as
given in communist manifesto. Even when he was critici-
sing Marxism he continued to believe himself as Marxist.
He continued to believe that marx was greatest liberator
of Humanity.
__Marxist Principle and M.N. Roy's criticism__
① __Historical materialism -__
   Marx has underestimated the role of ideas shaping history.
⑪ __Dilectical materialism -__
   He held that dilectical materialism is not the real materia-
   lism it is idealism.
[illegible] __Class struggle -__
   Unlike K.Marx who believes that history is the product of
   class struggle. M.N. Roy held that History is a product of
   class co-operation.
ⅳ __Surplus value -__
   Marx considered surplus value as a source of exploitation
   whereas M.N. Roy held that surplus value in itself is not
   the source of exploitation rather lack of equitable distri-
   bution is a source of exploitation.
