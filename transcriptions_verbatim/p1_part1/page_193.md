__Q. Critically evaluate the working of tribunal system w.r.t__
__interstate water dispute.__

Salient features of interstate water dispute act
i) It gives list of dispute to be treated as water dispute
under act. Many varieties of disputes are excluded.
e.g. In case Mulla-Periyar dam parties can directly
approach to the SC. The act excludes the jurisdiction
of SC and provides for the creation of tribunal.

__Analysis of tribunal system:__
1) In case there is a dispute, parties or party will approach
to union government.
2) Initialy there was no time limit within which union govt
will complete political negotiation and constitute tribunal
Union govt. took extraordinary time to constitute tribunal
e.g. It took 20 years to constitute tribunal for Kavery
water and that to after intervention of SC. Hence since 2002
it has been made mandatory to constitute a tribunal within
a year.
Initialy there was no time limit within which tribunal
was to give award. e.g. Kavery tribunal took 17 years to
give award. Its final award ~~cam~~ gave in 2007.
