(v) __At political level.__
Gandhi proposed the concept of oceanic circles of
power. Swaraj in political term does not mean
transfer of power from one set of elites to other
set of elites. Swaraj means empowerment of masses.
Gandhi proposed Panchayati Raj based on democratic

④ __Theory of Sarvodaya__
Gandhi's concept of sarvodaya is influenced by John
Ruskin's book 'Unto this last'. Sarvodaya is Gandhi's
approach towards socialism. Sarvodaya has following
components:
(i) Bread Labour.
(ii) Trusteeship
(iii)

(i) __Bread labour:__
Purpose is to understand the dignity of labour.
How?
According to Gandhi everyone should take Bread only after
performing some amount of physical work.
Why?
Only when we will perform the physical work we can
understand Hardships of those who are involved in
Manual labour. Only in this way we can develope respect
towards those who perform physical labour.

According to Gandhi' if we cannot make people equal in
terms of income we can definitely can make people
equal in terms of dignity. According to Gandhi' work of
a Barber is as important as the work of lawyer.
