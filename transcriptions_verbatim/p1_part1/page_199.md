* __Centre and state administrative relations__:
More conflictual than legislative relations. The major
areas of disputes are
1) Union's power to issue direction
2) Misuse of art 356
3) Institution of governor
4) All India Services
5) Deployment of union armed forces

* __Problems with the power to issue direction__:
Not only unions have power to issue dir^n w.r.t exercise
the executive power of state govt Union also have power to
punish state govt. in situation of ~~power~~ non-compliance
It will result into dissolution of not only govt; but also
assembly.
Above provisions exists in constitution to manage
extreme situation. It is expected that exercise of these power
shall be in accordance to the spirit of federalism and parliamentary
democracy.

* __Controversy related to art 356__
__What is art 356?__
Presidents rule
__Nature of art 356__
1) It is unique feature of Indian federation
2) It is colonial legacy
__The position of constituent assembly__
Members have raised observation over inclusion of
art 356. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar has assured const assembly that
above provision is introduced only as precaution and he
expected that it will remain 'dead letter'.
__Actual practice__:
It has not remained dead letter in less than 87
year of existence of republic it has been used for more
than 100 times.
