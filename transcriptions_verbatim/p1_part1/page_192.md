    Art 131 the original jurisdiction of SC. SC is a federal
court and its role is the resolution of dispute. It was the
main body for the resolution of interstate water dispute
till 1956.

__What happened in 1956?__

    Parliament has passed 2 acts
1) Interstate water disputes act, 1956
2) River boards act, 1956

__On what basis parliament enacted the law?__

    Under art 262 it is mentioned that parliament may by
law make provision for the resolution of dispute. It also
suggests that if parliament desire it can exclude jurisdiction
of court including SC.
