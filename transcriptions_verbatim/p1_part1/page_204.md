__Why discretionary power has been the cause of conflict ?__

    The manner in which power has been exercised has
become an extremely controversial aspect in India's federal
structure. It has been exercised in arbitrary manner in the
interest of ~~union~~ ruling party at union. Misuse of institution has been
one of factor for rise of militancy in J&K. Soli Sorabjee in
his book '__the governor Sage or Saboteur__' has highlighted the
arbitrary exercise of power which has converted institution from
inst. of co-op. federalism to the inst. of bargaining fed.
Misuse of inst. started during the time of Pandit Nehru
itself. In 1952 governor of Madras has used all possible ways
to check communist coming to power. In 1957 inst was misused
against the duly elected communist govt. in Kerala. The case
of Andhra Pradesh in 1984 is classical example of arbitrary
exe. of power. The then CM N.T. Ramarao was abroad for
medical treatment back home governor has dissolved the
assembly. Similarly governor Ramesh Bhandari of U.P. sacked
govt. of Kalyan Singh and appt. Jagdambika Pal as a chief
minister which has brought SC into the picture & SC prescribed
composite floor testing. In 2006 governor of Bihar Buta Singh
dissolved the assembly even before the 1st meeting of assembly
on the apprehension of horse trading. Similarly the governor of
Arunachal Pradesh has preponed the session of assembly assuming
that governor has discretionary power summon prorogue &
dissolve the assembly.

Consequences of the discretionary exercise of power:
    According to SC such exercise of power will result into
the breakdown of entire edifice of democracy in India.

__What should be done?__
    1) Should constitution be amended to end the discretionary
powers of governor.
    2) Should the position of governor be abolished?
