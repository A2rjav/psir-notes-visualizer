According to Rajiv Bhargav . Indian model is neither
the model of religious neutrality nor equal treatment
of all religions. (Equidistance). He calls Indian model as
'__Principled Distance__' or in the words of Pratap Bhanu
Mehta it is __asymmetrical__ intervention of state in
different religions. Principled distance means a contextua
model. depending on the context state will exclude
or include religion. There is a simultaneous existence
of engagement & disengagement. Principle distance
allows differential treatments.
-> Rajiv Bhargav has given seven essential features
of Indian model -
(i) State is not completely identified with particular religion
yet state is not averse with the use of religious symbo
(ii) No strict boundaries rather porus boundaries.
(State to religion ; religion to state)
(iii) No active hostility towards religion & no passive
indifference.
(iv) Different religions are the foundations of the state.
(v) No overarching idea.
(vi) Secularism is dependent on the ideas of Liberty,
equality, fraternity & is considered as integral componen
of democracy.
(vii) Politically negotiated contextual model.
