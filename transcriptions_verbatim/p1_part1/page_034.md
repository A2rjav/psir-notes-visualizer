Nationalism is not a political programme. Nation is a
religion. He was inspired by Vivekananda who held that
Nationalism should be the supreme goal of youth in India.
Vivekanand appealed to arise, awake & don't stop till the
goal is achieved. Aurobindo Ghosh also held that Nationalism
should be the religion of youth in India. The type of commit-
ment we have towards the religion same commitment &
the spirit of sacrifice is required for Nationalism. Ghosh
was against the constitutional methods he called it as
Mendicancy (Beggary). Constitutional methods have no rele-
vance because British rule is not responsible towards
Indians. Instead of Prayers we require resistance. Either
resistance or revolution. He was not against the use of
violence. However He suggested to use the methods acc-
ording to circumstances., He himself had links with revo-
lutionary societies like Yugantar. Aurobindo Ghosh held
that we need those methods which strike at the root of
colonial state. So long Britishers will be able to maintain bene-
fit from India they will not leave it. He also asked modera-
tes to stop calling British rule as a blessing in disguise.
Foreign rule is never a Blessing. It only leads to the exploita-
tion. If moderates will keep on repeating foreign rule as a
blessing it will weaken the morale of the people fighting against
the foreign rule. He suggested to adopt direct methods like
boycott and swadeshi. Not only Boycott british goods but also
Boycott british administration, Judicial System & Education
System. Boycott of British administrative machinery is
needed to show that Indian can live without British. Indians
can manage their own affairs. He was against British sch-
ools. British schools only teach how to become loyalist.
British school kills Nationalist sentiments. Hence, he called
for National education.
