There have been suggestions that to avoid political
instability. India should go for Presidential form of
gout.
The real question is not whether coalition is desirable
or not rather 'Is coalition inevitable'. In case of
country like India coalition is ~~not~~ inevitable. With
the growth of democratic consciousness there are gre-
ater chances that coalition becomes a permanent
feature. Hence, we have to think how to manage
coalition & to minimise the evil consequences & to
maximise the benefits. Coalition in itself is not bad
coalition actually makes democracy representative
deliberative & consociational. Coalition is the regular
feature of the countries in the Western Europe, Problem
emerges when country lacks healthy parliamentary
culture. We have to think how to make coalition work
following suggestionse can be given:
(i) Bringing ethics in politics
(ii) Reform anti-defection law
(iii) Electoral reform
(iv) Check the use of money & muscle power.
(v) Impose intra party democracy.
(vi) Encourage Transparency in functioning of gout to reduce
a scope of bargaining.
(vii) Institutionalise the practice of consultation with
coalition partners.
(viii) Introduce constructive vote of no confidence as found
in Germany.
Introduction of Presidential system is ^neither^ ~~not~~ possible
nor desirable Because parliamentary system is declared
as Basic structure & nowhere except in USA presidential
system could maintain its democratic character.
