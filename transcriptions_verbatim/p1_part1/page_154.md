Congress does not have support from upper caste,
OBCs, SCs. Congress also does not have support from
the younger generation. However congress support
base has not changed w.r.t. support from minori-
ties & STs. Congress is identified as the party of
minorities. & BJP has become the party of majority.
Still 36% of ~~to~~ the votes of minorities have gone
to congress.

-> the major challenge of congress is:
(i) Ideological stagnation, organisational weakness, lack
of leadership.
(ii) Congress lack committed cadres. which is available to
other parties like BJP & regional parties.

come out of majority- minority complex. Politics is about
imagination, crafting coalitions, making alliances.
Congress need new social & economic agenda, new
social coalition, congress should not remain as party
of defense, it should be party of transformation.
