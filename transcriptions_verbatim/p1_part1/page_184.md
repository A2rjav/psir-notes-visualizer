__Q. Discuss how party system influence nature of federalism.__
Conceptual framework: Ideally federalism works well with
presidential form of govt and parliamentary form of govt
stop functioning of federalism.
Parliamentary system is based on party politics, whereas
presidential system ~~found~~ was found as an alternative system
of parliamentary system/party politics. (No party politics in
Presidential system)
Party system shapes the working of federation
- Situation 1: Same party act at union level and at the
state level. Federation will work as co-op federalism.
- Situation 2: Different parties at union and state level.
Federalism transform into bargaining federalism.
- Thus co-operative and bargaining federalism may exist
simultaneously.
In case of India, there is actually no centre & state dispute, it
is all party to party dispute. Hence none of the commission
has ever suggested any fundamental change in basic
design of centre & state relation.
Evolution of federalism with evolution of party system:
__Stage-I : Party system = One party dominant system (till 1967)__
co-op federalism was working smoothly.
