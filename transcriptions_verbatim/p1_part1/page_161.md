History of BJP
BJP is a successor of Jana Sangh which was formed
in 1951 by Shyama Prasad Mukherjee. other prominen
leader was Dindayal Upadyay.

objective of Jana Sangh.
The founders of Janasangh knew that the cannot
come to power hence the basic objective of Jana
sangh was to oppose Nehruvian consensus. According
to them Opposition is decessary in Democracy.

Bhartiy Jana Sangh could get only 3.1% vote share in
1st General Elections in comparison to Jana Sangh, left
parties got 7% vote share. Jan Sangh's vote share
jumped to 9.4% in 4th General Election after death
of Pandit Nehru. Jana Sangh formed the part of
Janata Party govt. There was objections from the mem
bers of Janata Party over Jana sangh's relations with
RSS. Hence Jana Sangh came out of Janata Party
took rebirth in the form of Bhartiya Janata Party.
