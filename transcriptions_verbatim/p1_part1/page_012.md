• __Qualities of a king-__
The Manusmriti talks about Hereditory Monarchy or
kingship. King has to be from Kshatriya.
Manusmriti talks about four varna's ie.
ⓐ Brahmin ⓑ Kshatriya ⓒ Vaishya ⓓ Shudras.
• __What is the Most Important role of the state-__
King has to ensure that all varna's follow their
Varna Dharma that is why state has power of
Danda. King represent divine personality.
Different God's gave the part of their personality
to the king. King combines Indra, Agni, Vayu,
Varun, Chandra, Surya, Yama, and Kuber.
People should obey the king. Though king had divine
personality but king did not had divine rights.
King was not above dharma. King has to follow
his Raj Dharma. According to Manu, the king who
does not follow Dharma goes to hell. Manu also
tell the qualities of the king and the Routine of
the King. King should learn veda's, King should
respect Brahmins, King should get up early in the
Morning, King should worship Brahmin, King should
not sleep during the day time, King should not indu-
lge too much in Hunting, Gambling, Drinking, Dancing.
King should not also indulge much with women.
