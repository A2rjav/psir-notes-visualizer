[Margin: ①] __King (Nabhi)__
  King is Nabhi, or centre. King plays the most import-
  ant role. If King is weak & other elements of
  sovereignty are strong they cannot act as determinant
  of power. However, if other elements are
  weak & King is strong, wise King will convert even
  the weak elements into the elements of strength.
  According to Kautilya King is important but King should
  understand the importance of other elements because-
  - Single wheel cannot turn the chariot. Kautilya also
  suggest that King should treat his subjects as his
  children and in the happiness of the subject lies the
  happiness of the King. ‘Praja Sukham Sukhe Rajya’

[Margin: ②] __Amatya’s__
  Senior ministers are known as Amatya’s.
  Amatya’s should be Brahmin. Amatya’s should have the
  knowledge of Veda’s King should consult Amatya’s
  but King should keep on testing the integrity of
  Amatya’s towards King from time to time. The most
  important person after King is Ambassador. Ambassa-
  dor or Envoy’s are the mouth piece of King. Hence,
  Ambassador should be choosen with great care.
  Ambassador should be handsome, man of integrity.
  If ~~Amatya~~ Ambassador is mouth piece of King spies
  are the eyes and ears. Of the King. King can use the
  spies to check the integrity of Amatya’s from time to
  time. Kautilya also suggest that King should keep 3
  Amatya’s Because 2 can conspire easily against the
  King.
