According to SC governor is not all pervading super
constitutional authority. SC mentions the doctrine of
harmonious construction. Art 154 which gives executive power
of State to governor also mention that governor will exercise
of power acco. to provisions of constitution. Discretionary
power of governor should have been use in manner that
democracy survives. Governor is ~~neither~~ not an elected representative
He is only an executive nominee. He is neither a ombudsman
of legislature nor mentor of the speaker. Governor can't
require speaker to act in a specific manner. What happens
within 4 walls of political party is none of governors concern.
He has to keep himself away from political horse trading.
Governor is a titular head. Constitution never envisage
governor wielding any significant power.

__What is the solution?__
Soli Sorabjee The governor: sage and Saboteur. If governor
is good he can do lot of good, if governor is bad he can
do lot of far harm.
Manmohan Singh: gave four mantras to the governors in
the governor's conference in New Delhi
1> You are the sole judge of what is right & wrong.
2) It is your judgement to understand the mandate of the
constitution.
3) You have to see what is in the best interest of county
4) You have to see what people expect from you.
