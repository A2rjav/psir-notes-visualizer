* Changing trend in centre and state financial relations:

Since coalition government and new economic policy and
the situation of state have got strengthened. Since, 10ᵗʰ
finance commission states share in union tax revenue has been
consistently increasing. Since, present govt. has come to power, govt
has tried to strengthen co-op. federalism. One of the major
initiative has been the abolishment of planning commission and
estb. of niti aayog. The philosophy of present PM is competitiv coop
federalism based on the idea of 'sabka sath, sabka vikas'.
The approach of 14ᵗʰ F.C. has been in favour of greater
autonomy for the state. There has been redⁿ in centrally
sponsored scheme. Govt had accepted all the recommendations
of 14ᵗʰ F.C. Another major change that is going to happen
introduction of GST which aims at establishment of India as a
single market 'one nation, one tax'. The argument of the govt
is that it will increase GDP by approx. 2%. It will make
goods cheaper by addressing the cascading effect of taxes
there will be better administration of taxes. Indian good will
become more competitive in international market. It will be
win-win situation for govt., industry, consumer, state as well
as union govt.

However there has been opposition by some states like
GJ and, MH & TN as GST is a destination based tax which
will benefit the states where consumption takes place at the
cost of manu. states. One of the strongest opp. has come from
state of TN. According to the govt. of TN increase in the
revenue, ease of doing buisness and better tax administration
can't be the basis of undermining federalism in India. The
concerns of state govt have been
1) Loss of revenue. However this concern can be managed.
2) Loss of fiscal autonomy. There is limited debate in the country
over the matter. The basic purpose of choosing federalism
is not uniformity; but unity in diversity. One of the major
source of revenue of the state govt has been sales tax.
The present institutional mechanism of GST council takes
away the autonomy to determine the tax. According to
the state of TN it defeats the purpose of federalism.
States should have autonomy to determine its priority.
