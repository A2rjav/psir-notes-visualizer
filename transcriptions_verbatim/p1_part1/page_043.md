③ __Theory of Swaraj__
Swaraj was the goal of freedom movement. Content of
Swaraj kept on evolving. Till Aurobindo Ghosh Swaraj was
understood as Political freedom. Aurobindo Ghosh has
added the dimension of freedom of an individual.
liberation of a person in spiritual sense. There was a
continuity between Ghosh's & Ideal of Gandhi. Gandhi's
ideal was also not limited to freedom in political
sense only. Gandhi was influenced by ideal of Swaraj
given in Mundak Upanishad. In Mundak Upanishad
Swaraj is controlled on oneself ie. control on one's desir
Swaraj at the level of individual was necessary for the
Holistic attainment of Swaraj. Gandhi described swaraj at
different levels.

(i) __At Individual Level.__
It means self control

(ii) __At the level of society__ Swaraj will come only when we can
abolish evil practices like untouchability, communal violence

(iii) __At cultural level__
According to Gandhi - keep the windows of your house
opened but door closed. Let the winds from all sides
come to you but you should not get uprooted. One should
respect cultures of others but sh one should not forget
own roots. One should have respect in one's culture.

(iv) __Swaraj in Economic sense__ -
Gandhi has proposed the revival of cottage industries.
Production by masses rather than mass production, dignity
of labour and trusteeship.
