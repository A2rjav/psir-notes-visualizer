HEADING: What are the concerns of state govt. in legislative sphere?
---
1) System is tilted in favour of centre.

2) Even on entries in the state list union can make laws even in normal situation.
(i) __Art 249__ (Rajya Sabha passes resolⁿ. 2/3ʳᵈ present & voting)
Though there is a safeguard that it can be done only with resolution R.S. and validity of law will be for a limited yet the concerns of state governments is R.S. is hardly a federal chamber in real sense.

[Margin: Permanent transfer -T]
(ii) __Art 252__ (2 or more states passed resolution)
In this case the transfer of power w.r.t. these states is of permanent nature. Even states can not bring any amendment which can be done only by union. Hence if same party want to pursue another law or policy they can't do so.

(iii) __Art 253__: shall
Distribution of power & function will not apply in case of international treaties and conventions. Earlier it was not major concern because international treaties were limited to the strategic issues. Now they have expanded even to the issues which are core issues coming under state list. A problem arises when union govt. does not go for prior consultation before making such commitments.

(iv) __Art 201__:
Power of governor to reserve bill for consideration of president. problem arises because such actions have been done in the interest of ruling party at the centre. There has been an arbitrary exercise of power. Similar bill of one state is reserve and other state is allowed There is no time limit also within which presi has to give his assent and deny it.

* __Observations by Sarkaria commission and Punchhi commission:__
According to Punchhi & Sarkaria commission, there is no problem in provision of const. Hence, there is no need of amendment. Problem lies in the manner power is exercised. In democracy there should not be any arbitrary exercised power.
