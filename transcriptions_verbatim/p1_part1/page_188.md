Reorganisation on ethnic ground. In this wave reorganisation took place in N-E region. Even national security was factor shaping decision.

__3ʳᵈ Wave:__
The states in hindi heartland were reorganised on the ground of good governance and development. In the year 2000 Uttarakhand, Jharkhand and Chhattisgardh were formed
In 21ˢᵗ century main focus was on development. Even Telangana was created on the grounds of development.

Louis Tillin has summarised following perspective on state formation.
1) Sociological perspective: Sociological perspective is given by prof. Yogendra Yadav and Ramchandra Guha. They project state formation as the a reaction of a marginalised section of society who have been deprived of developmental gains wanted to create their own state. Poli

2) Political economy perspective: Atul Kohli. According to him creation of Chattisgarh and Jharkhand is guided by policy of liberalisation of and globalisation.

3) Administrative perspective; Former Home minister LK Advani and BSP leader Mayawati gives the logic of good governance.

4) Political perspective: Christophee Jaffrelot and Louis Tillin Both believe that only political calculations works. Even the timing of creation of state will depend on political calculation of ruling party
