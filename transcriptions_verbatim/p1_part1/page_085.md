Phase II
Peasant Movement of 1930s.
Congress started organising peasantry. Kisan Sabha's
started emerging. In 1936 All India Kisan Sabha
was formed under the leadership of Swami Sahaja-
nanand. The main demand of peasants was for land
reforms. Peasant movement also developed autonomous
character. Though peasants participated in National
movement in large no's yet they had no concrete
success land reforms remained unfinished goal even
today.

Phase III
Peasant Movement of 1940s.
[Margin: Lecture 57
| 24/02/2017 |]

Towards 40s independent peasant movement started
developing & it was more dominated by the communist or
with leftist ideology. The two prominent movement were
Tebhaga & Telangana Movement.

Overall assessment of peasants movement.
Peasants movement have not resulted into any concrete
gains for the peasants. Despite constitutional commitment
for land reforms it has remained an unfinished agenda
for long peasant movement remained devided on the
lines of caste & religion. Dissatisfaction with Indian
State has given rise to left wing extremism based on
the ideology of maoism.
