Time- 1891-1956

According to dhananjay keer, Ambedkar is tallest leader among
dalits. No one before Ambedkar or after Ambedkar could
achieve what he could do for members of his community
Ambedkar is a controversial figure. He is often criticised
as the leader of his community rather than a leader of
nation. He was criticised because of his demand for
his seperate communal electorate. He was critic of Gandhi
He adviced Dalits not to participate in congress led freedo
m movement. He opposed quit India movement. He su-
pported Jinna's demand for Pakistan. He wanted Bri-
tishers to stay in India.
Arun shourie in his book 'worshipping false Gods' criticised
Ambedkar for his anti- national stand. He calls Ambedkar
anti- national because of his attitude towards the freedom
movement. Ambedkar openly opposed Purn Swaraj
ressolution in 1929. On 8th August 1930 during his adress at
the conference of all India depressed classes in Nagpur
he openly held that he opposes the project of independe-
nce. Like Jotiba fule who held that British Raj was
better than Peshwa Raj. Ambedkar also held that depre-
ssed classes should appreciate the efforts of British as
they delievered them from the long age of tyranny
by Orthodox Hindus. In 1939 Ambedkar made his stand
clear that whenever there will be a conflict of intere-
st b/w country & untouchables he will give preference
to untouchables.
