① __Theory of Nationalism__ :-

Aurobindo Ghosh is known as the Prophet of Indian Nation-
alism. He is the only person who gave theory of Indian
Nationalism. Bold theory of Nationalism.

→ __Context of the Theory__
The Idea of India as a Nation has remained contested.
Colonial administrators & Historians have always challenged
the legitimacy of Indian National Movement.
According to them, India is nothing but a Geographical
expression. The response of early Nationalists to the
British challenge was too mild. They accepted that India
is not a Nation but as Surendranath Banerji held that
India is Nation in making. Moderates believe that under
the influence of Modernisation India will emerge as a
Nation. They believed Britishers as a source of moder-
nisation. They looked at __British rule as blessing in disguise__.
Moderates didn't want to antogonise the British. Hence,
they adopted mild constitutional methods like Prayers,
Petitions and Protest. They believed that the task of Indian
National Congress is to develope awareness & that is why
congress session were organised annually at different places.

It was the efforts of moderates/early leaders that there was
a growth of Nationalist consciousness in the country.
Aurobindo Ghosh entered into Indian politics when the
foundations of Nationalism were already laid. Aurobindo
realised that National movement requires a __bold theory__ of
Nationalism. He could understand the __pulse of the nation.__
