● Peasants Movements
Peasantry - It represent the poor - subsistence farmers,
landless labourers & Bonded labourers,
The History of peasant movement in India is much older
than the History of National Movement. one of the most
well known peasant revolt, was the Indigo revolt of
1860.
Causes of Peasant movement -
(i) Britishers have changed the pattern of land use.
(ii) Britishers have forced change in land relations.
(iii) British policies of land settlement has resulted into
the loss of land & concentration of ownership in the
hands of zamindars.
