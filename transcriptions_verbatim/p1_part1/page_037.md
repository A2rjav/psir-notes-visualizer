Aurobindo Ghosh tried to find the answer of the
question as to how we can achieve freedom in a
manner that is not against the wider interest of
the society. He was against the capitalist idea of
Freedom. He found it too mechanical. According to him,
if liberty become a device to satisfy our natural impul-
ses. without taking into consideration the natural impul
se of others. It will result into chaos. He also does not
support the socialist concept of freedom as it results
into oppressive state system. According to him, there sho-
uld be Harmony between Individual, Nation & Humanity
the Gospel of Humanity is based on the triangle of
Liberty, Equality & Fraternity. According to Aurobindo Gh-
osh, the longing for freedom is lodged so deep in the
human heart that thousands of arguements are powe-
less to uproot it. According to him, ~~there is~~ goal of huma-
n life is to attain freedom. Freedom consists in understand
ing the reality. Freedom is freedom from Ignorance.
Freedom means the realisation of the universal spirit
He gives the concept of Sat + chit + Ananda. The real
freedom lies in the understanding of God It is possi-
ble when we achieve the state of supermind. Supermi-
nd is possible through the Integral Yog. Integral yoga
combine Dharma Yoga, Raj Yoga, Hath Yoga, Gyan Yog,
Bhakti yog, Karma Yoga.
