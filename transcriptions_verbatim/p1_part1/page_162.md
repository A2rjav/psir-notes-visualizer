The prominent philosophical ideas on which Jana-
Sangh/BJP is based are.
(i) Cultural Nationalism
(ii) Gandhian socialism.
(iii) Integral Humanism- based on the values of Sanatan
Dharma. Given by Pandit Dindayal Upadyay. Integral
Humanism is a philosophy which integrates mind soul
and body. It also has cosmopolitan approach based on
Indian values. According to them, India should adopt
western science but not western way of life. They
are inspired by the modernisation of Japan. They are
critics of Nehruvian Modernisation. Western ideas are
not the product of Indian History & Hence they do not
have relevance in Indian context.
(iv) Hindutva. It denotes the idea of India as a Hindu
Nation.

When BJP was formed the leaders have given the
idea of positive secularism. Positive Secularism
means party is committed to protect the religious
rights of all communities.
(v) National Integration.

Rise of BJP
(i) In 1984 BJP got 2 seats & 7.4% vote share
(ii) In 1989 BJP got 88 seats & 11.56% vote share.
(iii) In 1991 BJP got 120 seats & 20.95% vote share.
(iv) In 1996 BJP got 161 seats & 20.80% vote share
(v) In 1998 NDA got 182 seats & 25% vote share
(vi) In 1999 NDA got 183 seats & 23.07% vote share.
