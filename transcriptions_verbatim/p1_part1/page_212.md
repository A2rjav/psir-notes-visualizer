* Changes introduced by 101 amendment act:
1) It introduced a new article ~~art 268~~ 246 A
It grants parliament and state legislature power to make laws
w.r.t. GST. For the first time a concurrent power of a tax
has been given. Parliament's exclusive power to make law on
subject on union list will be subjected to art 246 A.
2) Changes in art 268~~A~~
For the time being stamp duty is left as it is but
the provision for excise duty is eliminated.
3) 268 A: has been ~~omitted~~ submerged
4) Added 269 A: adds the the tax with respect to the services supplies
in interstate trade and commerce and import & export.
5) 270 is left as it is
