__Kautilya's Ideas on Foreign Policy-__
The focus of Arthashastra is on acquiring land.
According to Chanakya, King should aspire to become
Chakravarty Samrat. He calls King as Vijigishu. means
one who aspires for victory. War is Kshatriya Dharma.
After coronation King has to start expansion. Hindu
      राजाभिषेक
texts mention various rituals like Ashwamedh Yagya.
Yagya means sacrifice. (Rajsuya Yagya)

-> Kautilya gives following Siddhant's:
(i) __Four Fold Policy__
Sam, Dam, Danda, Bhed.
(Peace, Economic Diplomacy, Force, Spies).

(ii) __Shadgunya Niti (Six Fold Policy)__
Sandhi - Treaty
Vigraha - Break the Sandhi / Treaty.
Aasan - Stationing of ~~fourth~~ troop near the territory
        of Enemy.
Yana - Mobilisation of forces, military exercises.
Samashrya - Join hands with those who have similar
            objectives.
Dvaidhibhava - Dual policy.

(iii) __Mandal Siddhant-__
Mandal Siddhant is a theory of Geo strategy. It tells us
about the strategy of war. Mandal comprise of 12 kings.
12 kings multiplied by 7 elements of sovereignty means
84 elements.
Vijigishu has to take into consideration the 84 elements
before starting the war.
