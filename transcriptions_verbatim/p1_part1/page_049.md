⑧ Gandhi's Concept of Rights and Duties.
Gandhi's conception of Rights & Duties is based on the
philosophy of Geeta. Geeta emphasised on Nishkama
Karma. According to Gandhi we should focus on our
duties. If we focus on our duties we automatically get
the reward. Rights are present in duties in embryonic
form. When we perform our duties we automatically
get the rights. Rights cannot be independent of duty.

→ Gandhi on Women-
According to Gandhi 'Seeta & Droupadi should be the
ideal of women in India. Seeta & Droupadi represent
moral force or soul force. Seeta is a symbol of moral
strength. It was the moral strength of Seeta that Ravan
could not touch Seeta.

→ Gandhi on Education-
Gandhi gave the concept of 'Nayee Taalim'. According
to Gandhi we should learn from the book of Life
rather than learning ^about Life from books. Gandhi believe
that the education should be a life long process. He su-
ggested 'Earning while learning'. Person should be able
to finance his own education rather than being depen-
dent on others. Hence Gandhi suggested/emphasised on
vocational education. Gandhi favoured teaching Home
Science, Nursing, Forestry.
