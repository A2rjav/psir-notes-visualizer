Assessment by Bipin chandra-
Though Moderates did not carry Mass struggle but
they carried struggle at Ideological level. They presented
the critic of colonialism. They laid the foundation of
the most spectacular mass movement. According
to Ranade, the memorials are not addressed to the
British govt. they were addressed to Indians.
According to Gokhale, we are at such a stage when
our achievements are bound to be small & our failu-
res too frequent. We must contend ourselves by
serving our country by our failure. It is through these
failures that the strength will grow.

● DIRECT ACTIONS

① Extremists phase
the term Extremism & militant Nationalism is used to
denote the young leaders Lal, Bal, Pal.
why they were called as Extremists-
They appeared more militant or extremists in their
Ideas when compared with the early Nationalists who
appeared moderates.
→ Comparison between Moderates & Extremists-
With the exception of Aurobindo Ghosh who talked
about complete freedom, rest of the leaders were
asking for dominian status. The only difference was
the pace. The young leaders were satisfied with the
extremely slow pace of the political reforms
