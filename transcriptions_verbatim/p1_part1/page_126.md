UNIFORM CIVIL CODE

-> UCC and its necessity.
Q What is uniform civil code?
Uniform Civil code is a western concept which emerg-
ed in Europe in Modern Times. Europe has witness-
ed 30 years of war where religion was one of the
primary source of conflict. Hence, European experien-
ce has resulted into the recognition of the necessity
that the role of religion should be confined to the
personal sphere & that is why the European model
is based on strict seperation of personal & political,
Religion and state. UCC is based on the idea of Uni-
versal Citizenship.

UCC not necessarily common civil code. More than
common code for all communities it asserts supre-
macy of the state w.r.t the law. It permits state
to make laws governing different aspects of the life
of the citizen excluding freedom of conscience.
