conventional view is represented by Ashutosh Varshney

outbursts. Paul Brass has challenged above approach &
held that riots in India are well planned organised
events. According to Paul Brass, complete 'Riot machi-
nery exists'. Environment is never free from communal
violence. He gives 3 stages -

① Preparatory stage - He calls riots as 'staged Dramas'.

② Precipitation stage - In this stage there is a role of con-
version specialists. It is the stage when violence
actually errupts. Most sensitive periods are near
