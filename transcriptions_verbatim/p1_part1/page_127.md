UCC is based on the monistic theory of the law.
According to which law is command of sovereign.
It becomes common code because all laws have to be
brought in allignment with the constitutional principles
constitution being the fundamental law.
Status of laws in India.
India has combined supremacy of constitution with
legal pluralism. There is a common civil code, common
criminal code & only in the matters of personal
laws there exists different personal laws. In above
context the principle of multi-cultural citizenship has be
adopted.
(i) Britishers have codified personal laws governing
christians & parsi's.
(ii) After Independent Parliament has codified Hindu
personal laws which are at present applicable for
Hindu's Jains & Buddhism.
(iii) Anand Marriage Act governs the personal laws for
Sikh community.
(iv) Only Muslim personal laws remain outside the pur-
view of the state. In case of Muslims, Muslim Per-
sonal law sharyat Application Act 1937 applies.
The act prohibits the intervention of the state in the
matters of personal laws.
