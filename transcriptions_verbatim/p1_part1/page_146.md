Paul Brass
In his book 'Ethnic groups & the state talks about
the struggle at 3 levels.
(i) Struggle within groups.
(ii) Competition b/w ethnic groups
(iii) Competition b/w ethnic groups & the state.

Solutions of Ethnicity-
(i) According to Sarkaria & Punchhi commission, Democra-
tic Decentralisation is the best way to adress
secessionist trend.
(ii) Atul kohli in his article' can democracies accomodates
ethnic movements' suggest that democracy is a cause
of problem as well as part of the solution.
Introduction of Democracy in prismatic societies create
sense of insecurity among traditional elites who go
for mobilisation ~~all~~ on the basis of ethnic identity to
ensure their priviledged position. However, only demo-
cracy offers the solution. He appreciates the way Pandit
Nehru has tackled Linguistic Nationalism. According to
him, India has lot of Democracy but not right type
of Democracy. We need not less Democracy but more
democracy.
(iii) Ashutosh Varshney
In his article - ' Ethnic Conflict & Civic life?
suggest the necessity of vigorous civic engagement
among different ethnic groups.
