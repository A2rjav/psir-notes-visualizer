Co-operative federalism is based on interlocking, interdependence
and overlapping concepts. Union and state are not
water tight compartment. Centre is dependant on state and state is
dependent on centre. Union govt. is act as elder brother. Co-op
federalism is adapted where
1) Sessionist trend exists.
2) Regional imbalance exists.

2) If USA is coming together model, India is holding together
model. State came together to form federation in USA &
in case of India highly centralised system of adm. has been
converted into federal model for
1] Administrative convenience
2] To meet regional aspiration.

3) USA is an example of symmetrical federalism and India
is an example of asymmetrical federation.

4) USA is known as centrifugal model, because flow of power
is towards the periphery of state. India is known as
centripetal model, because flow of power is towards the
centre.

5) USA is known as legislative federalism as residuary power
lies with state govt. whereas India is known as executive
model because union govt. is dependent of state govt
for implementation of its laws and policies.

__Q. To what extent it is appropriate to call India quasi-federal?__

K.C. Wheare called India quasi-federal. According to K.C. Wheare
Indian constitution is federal in form, but unitary in spirit, Indian
constitution has prominent unitary feature and subsidary federal
feature. Arguments to which suggests that India is quasi-federal:

1) Division of power is tilted towards union
2) Residuary power lies with union government.
3) Emergency power
4) Art 3
5) All India Services
6) Integrated judiciary
7) Governor appointed and removed by cent union
8) Single citizenship
9) Finance commission
10) CEC
11) CAG
