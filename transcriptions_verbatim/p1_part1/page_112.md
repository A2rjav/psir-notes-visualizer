Analysis of India as a secular state-
One of the earliest work on India as a secular state
is of D.E. Smith. in his book 'India - a secular state'
published in 1962.
D.E. Smith provides 3 features of a secular state:
① Freedom of Religion
② Citizenship & Right to equality.
③ Seperation of state & Religion.
According to him. India has 1st two features but not
the last feature. D.E. Smith's explaination of the
India as a secular state with few features & not all
features has been criticised by Mark Gallanter.
According to him. It is wrong to evaluate Indian
model from the perspective of American model.
India has its own unique model.
