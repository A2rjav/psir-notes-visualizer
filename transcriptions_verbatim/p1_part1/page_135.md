According to Rudolph & Rudolph the role of caste in
politics has resulted into 'modernisation of tradition'
and traditionalisation of Modernity'. Modernisation of
tradition means secularisation of caste. Traditionalisati
on of modernity means democracy acquiring traditional
features of caste & religion.
In their book 'Pursuit of Lakshmi' they have mentioned
the increasing role of caste since Green Revolution. Green
Revolution has strengthened the position of intermediate
castes or OBCs. They refer OBCs as Bullock capitalists
Presently the politics in all states is dominated by OBCs.
