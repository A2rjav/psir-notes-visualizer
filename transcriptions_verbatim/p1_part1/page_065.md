According to Ambedkar there is a lack of clarity in Gandhi
over varna & caste. Gandhi's view is textual (Bookish).
Varna is textual whereas caste is contextual. Varna syste
is an ideal but its practide form is caste system. Varna
system is based on worth but caste system is based on Birt
Varna system is division of labour caste system is divi-
sion of labourers. Varna is the textual view. Gandhi
himself is an example. Gandhi is not observing his own
Varna dharma.

(ii) Ambedkar was critical of Gandhi using the term Harijan
for untouchables. Harijan is a misleading term. Untouchables
are not those who are loved by God rather untouchables
form the most exploited section of Hindu society. Insteac
of Harijan Ambedkar has used the term Dalits or the
depressed sections of the society.

(iii) Ambedkar was critic of Gandhi's Harijan Sevak Sangh.
Gandhi has not accepted the proposal of Ambedkar to
include the members of Dalit community in the mana-
gement of Harijan Sevak Sangh. Gandhi held that the obje-
ctive of Harijan Sevak Sangh is that the members of
upper caste should serve dalits should work in the interes
of dalits. Ambedkar compared Harijan sevak sangh with
Mythological 'Putana'. Harijan Sevak Sangh is not to help
dalits but to distract them from the reality so that
they are continued to be expoited.
