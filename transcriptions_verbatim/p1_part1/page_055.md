(iii) Ethics
What is Ethics?
Ethics are principles governing our social life.
According to him origin of ethics is not in scriptures or
book of religion. Origin of ethics is in 'the reason of man
During man's struggle for survival whatever man found is
rational, desirable, it became the basis of ethics or
social norms.

Radical Humanism suggest to reject outdated customs and
traditions. It suggest to rationalise the principles governing
our life.

Radical Humanism is materialistic. Radical Humanism can be
considered as existentialism. M.N. Roy is a source of inspira-
tion for rationalists.

M.N. Roy has formed a party to contest elections to promote
the ideology of Radical Humanism. Later on he ended the
party became the critic of party politics. He held that parties
go for immoral politics. Hence he started the movement
known as Radical Humanism. M.N. Roy wanted to establish
Radical Democracy rather than parliamentary democracy.
M.N. Roy also presented people's plan as a blue print of
future economic organisation for India. He favoured indus
trialisation, modernisation. However, he wanted industries
to be owned by people. He believe that Parliamentary
democracy results into deligation of power hence he supp-
orted grass root democracy.

He called himself as Humanist & distanced himself from
crude materialism & determinism of orthodox marxism.
