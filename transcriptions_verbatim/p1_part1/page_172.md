__Changing Socio-Economic profile of legislators.__

What is the advantage of studying of profile?

(i) Parliament is the mirror of society.
When we study profile we can understand the nature
of our society.

(ii) Studying profile also helps in understanding the direc-
tion of the political developement/change.

__Profile of Parliamentarians in India.__
It is to be noted that the profile of parliamentari-
ans & the members of state legislature do not di-
ffer in any significant manner. Similarly the profile
of members of LS & that of RS do not differ.
In case of India, It is believed that upper House
represent the assembly of elders. It is said that
lower house represent Peter Drunk & upper hou-
se represent Peter Sober.

Shanker & Rodriques in their study on Indian Parlia-
ment has classified the evolution of Indian Parlia-
ment in 8 phases on the basis of profile of parlia-
mentarians:

(1) __Phase I - uptill 1967__
(i) Parliament was dominated by Brahmins.
(ii) Women minorities & OBCs were under represented.
(iii) In terms of Profession lawyers formed the biggest
group & the 2^(nd) group was of agriculturalists.
(iv) Most of the members were educated from foreign
universities including the members from scheduled
Caste communities.
