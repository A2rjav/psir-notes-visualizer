Achin Vanaik, is critical of the arguements of Neo-
Gandhians & suggest that religion based public mora-
lity will be bangerous. The root cause of communi-
lism is religion itself. Hence, secularism is needed.

Akeel Bilgrami suggests secularism should be the
product of dialogue. He suggests bottom up approach
rather than imposed from above.

Gurpreet Mahajan, she suggest secularism should
ensure that no one should suffer or indisdadvantaged
because of belonging to a particular religion.

Romila Thapar, suggests that we require to bring
secularism out of its ~~tattered~~ ^pale shadow found in Indian
Constitution & we should follow some bold mo-
del of secularism.

Above debate show that there is dissatisfaction not just
theory but practice of secularism in India.
Charles Tailor in Rajiv Bhargav's edited book secularism
and its critics has mentioned that secularism is a
necessity for a democratic life of a religiously diverse
society.
