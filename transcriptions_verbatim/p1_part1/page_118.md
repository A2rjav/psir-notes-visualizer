● Origin of communalism in India-
what is communalism?
the use of Religion for political purposes is called as
communalism.
Causes of communalism / Explaination of communa-
lism in India-
there 4 schools of thought.
① Essentialists- Louis Dumont (scholar).
According to Essentialists arguements Hindu's & Muslim's
are 2 antagonistic communities. The reconcillia
of interests — is not possible & hence co-existence
is not possible.
② Institutionalists - Asghar Ali Engineer (scholar)
According to him if policies of the state w.r.t the
distribution of resources is based on casteor religion
communalism is bound to emerge.
③ Instrumentalists - Bipin chandra (scholar)
Elites use religion as an instrument for political purpose.
④ Social constructivists-
According to social constructivists, there may be no real
cause of conflict but communities develops some percep-
tions about each other, mental maps & do not verify
their perceptions.
