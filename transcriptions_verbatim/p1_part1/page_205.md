Pandit Nehru assured that healthy convention will develop and
eminent person of integrity will be appt. Nehru also proposed that
such persons will be appointed who don't have active political
background in recent past. Person from same state can't be
appt as governor

Actual practice:
    Healthy convention have not be followed. Rejected ~~polit~~
politician & retired administrator including judges have been
appt. without cooling of period..

Suggestion:
    Since healthy convention have not work it becomes necessary
to put these qualification in black & white in the consti

Manner of appt.
    Art 155 governor shall be appointed by warrant by president
under his hand & seal. ~~Since~~ Since the appt of governor in hands of
~~comp~~ union, union enjoys lot of bargaining power.

Raja Mannar committee set up by Govt of Tamil Nadu,
had suggested that, the provision should be made by
which consultation with CM of state be made, mandatory
before the appointment. If CM is not comfortable, such
person should not be appointed.

    Punchhi commission has recommended that, the platform
of interstate council can be utilised to select the name of
person and to create a panel. Governor can be automatically
appointed from names mentioned in panel.

System of removal - Art 156

Governor holds office during the pleasure of the president
Since, governor is entirely on the pleasure of the union
govt., Union govt. has lot of bargaining power. It has been
suggested that, governor should be provided security of tenure
as in case of president Punchhi commission has suggested.
governor can be given an opportunity to be heard before
his removal.
