What does Radical imply?
Radical means man in the original sense.

According to M.N. Roy, none of the existing ideologies liberate
man in true sense. If fascism suppresses man in the name
of Nation, Marxism suppresses man in the name of class.

Radical Humanism talks about the cosmopolitan union of
truly liberated persons.

According to M.N. Roy, Radical Humanism is a philosophy of
social life which is based on the philosophy of nature.
M.N. Roy aims to create a new society where ~~a~~ man can
achieve freedom in real sense. Society which is based on
scientific way of life. According to M.N. Roy there are 3
components of Radical Humanism -
(i) Freedom
(ii) Reason
(iii) Ethics.

(i) __Freedom__
According to M.N. Roy Freedom means freedom from all
sorts of fear & insecurities.
(ii) __Reason__
According to M.N. Roy every person is rational, though some
may appear irrational. It is the presence of reason in
man which can become the basis for Human solidarity.
According to him, Reason is not spiritualistic or metaphysical
Reason is materialistic in origin.
How?
Reason developed as a result of man's struggle for survival.
Man's struggle for existence is still continuing & can be the
basis of rationality.
