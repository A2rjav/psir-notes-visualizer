1) His approach is ethnocentric. He treats US model as an
ideal type and evaluate Indian model on the basis of US
model.
2) His approach is legalistic and static. Federalism is a dynamic
concept. There can be big difference in text and context. Neither
Indian federation nor US federation is static. Over the period
of time balance of power in USA has shifted towards
federal govt. Formal US president Roosewelt coined the
term 'new federalism'

USA federalism.
1) American civil war.
2) Increasing role of USA in international politics
3) Doctrine of implied power applied by ~~US security force~~ SC.
4) US government adopting welfare approach.

Similarly since the breakdown of congress system there has
been gradual increase in powers of state governments in India.
The bargaining power of state govt has increased further
since coalition politics has become the regular feature of
Indian political system.
