Introduction-
It is said that in India people do not cast their vote
they vote their caste. According to M.N. Srinivas caste
is present among Indians even at sub conscious level.
According to christophe Jeffrelot caste forms the mosaic
of Indian politics.
According to Rajni Kothari, In India it is not just caste
has influenced politics but politics has also influenced
caste.
What is caste?
The term caste comes from Portugese word casta
which is the Europeans translation of Indian term Jati
One of the earliest study on the caste system is of
Louis Damont. He described caste system as the uniqui
system of social stratification found in India based
on the principles of Purity and Pollution. He describe it
as a rigid system based on Birth which does not permit
social mobility.
M.N. Shrinivas has challenged Dumont's understanding
on saste. According to M.N. Shrinivas Indian system of
caste & the western system of class are not entirely
opposites. Class system in affect is as rigid as caste.
