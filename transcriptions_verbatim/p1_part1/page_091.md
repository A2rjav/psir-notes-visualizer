$\rightarrow$ __Socialist Perspective__-
Socialist Perspective was influenced by Russian
Revolution. Revolutionaries like Bhagat Singh, Congress
leader like Nehru, Subhashchandra Bose have intro-
duced socialism in Congress. Some radical congress-
men known as Young turks like JP Narayan,
Acharya Narendra dev & Minno Masani lead the
foundation of Congress socialist party in 1934.
They didn't opposed congress they only wanted to
radicalise the political programme of congress.
They were also influenced by Gandhi.

$\rightarrow$ __Radical Humanist Perspective__.
M.N. Roy considered congress as a Bourgeoise Party &
Gandhi as a Bourgeoise leader.
M.N. Roy has given the philosophy of Radical Huma-
nism. According to him, Nationalism suppresses
Man, there is a need to build cosmopolitan union
of liberated persons.
