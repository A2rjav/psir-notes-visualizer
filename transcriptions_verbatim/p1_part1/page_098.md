SC in Keshvanand Bharti case has settled the matter
that it accepts that the constitution is given by the
people to themselves. Court accept the written words
Factual correctness cannot be questioned. As preamble
mentions that the constitution is given to us by our-
selves we have to accept it as correct. Court further
suggested that any one knowing the composition of
the assembly will never challenge the fact that mem-
ber did not represent the voice of the people of India.

Indian constitution is an organic document. It has
been evolving formally by the process of amend-
ments & informally by the interpretations of judici-
ary. We cannot imagine the existence of India as a
Nation without the corner stone of the Nation.

Some facts associated with constituent assembly.
(i) In 1922 Gandhi held that Swaraj will not be the free
gift of the British. Swaraj will be the expression of
Indians.
(ii) In 1934, Congress working committee adopted a resolu-
tion that constituent assembly will be elected on the
basis of universal Adult franchise.
