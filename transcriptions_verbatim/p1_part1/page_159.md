In Western countries coalitions have been formed to
run the govt. but in India negative coalitions have
been formed with the sole objective of keeping
someone out of power rather than running the
govt. Eg. Janata Party coalition was anti- Indira
coalition where socialists & rightiest combined toge-
ther.
Since coalition is a power sharing arrangement
political parties would like to have Minimum part-
ners or in the words of Riker, 'Minimum winning
coalition'. However, in India there are 'oversized
coalitions because any faction may leave the party
anytime.
There are some Indian innovations also like National
Agenda for Governance or common minimum progra-
The coalition culture of India comes nearest to the
coalition culture of Italy.
Is coalition desirable?
There have been many negative consequences of
Coalition Politics in India Eg. Growth of money & muscle
power, Growth of corruption, defection, decline of
parliament, politisisation of post of speaker, weak
ening of institution of PM, Policy Paralysis, Judicia-
Activism, Presidential Activism.
Hence there has been the debate on the desirabili-
ty of coalition politics.
