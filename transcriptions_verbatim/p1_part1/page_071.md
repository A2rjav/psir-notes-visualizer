Why ~~consti~~ Early Nationalists adopted Constitutional Method
① Ideological Reasons-
Early Nationalists were influenced by liberalism. They consi-
dered British rule as blessing in disguise. They held British
rule as divine intervation to eradicate the evils of the
misrule of the past. Ranade thanked British for intro-
ducing for introducing rule of law. Surendranath
Banerjee appealed to introduce institutions that are
truely British in character so that Indians can rejoice
in permanent union with British. Dadabhai Nauroji
held that British are justice loving people. Indians have
to communicate their demand in a reasonable mann-
er. Ranade held that British rule is source of inspira-
tion hope , confidence & we are fortunate that we
are under their rule than any other imperialist power.
