Assessment of Non-co-operation Movement

① Movement was failure in terms of the promises made
by Congress and Gandhi. Still Gandhi got what he wanted.

ⓐ He wanted to test the capacity of masses.
ⓑ He wanted to establish that Congress does not represent
the microscopic minority. Masses response to the call of
congress. including Muslims. M
Movement acquired multi-class character.
There was some visible success also in the form of
Burning of foreign clothes & Boycott of the visit of
Prince of Wales.

Gandhi knew that swaraj was not possible in a year.
His promise was just to motivate the masses.
