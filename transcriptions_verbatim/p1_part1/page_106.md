In western world secularism emerged as a result of
the experience of the religious wars. Machiavelli sugg-
ested seperation b/w church & state. Within christianity
there has been a internal reform movement by which
a sphere of God has been made limited to the perso-
nal sphere. Hence, scholars like T. N. Madan suggest
that it is wrong to impose western secularism on
people in South Asia where civil society has not been
secularised. In societies where religion shaped all sphe-
res of life.

Secularism remained a highly contested oncept not just
in India but even in western countries. __Rajiv Bhargav__
has given 3 models of secularism:

① extreme
Ultra substantive Model. (FRANCE)
Model is found in the countries where secularism is
based on the substantive values like autonomy, human
dignity, freedom of choice.

② Ultra Procedural Model. (USA)
Ultra Procedural implies that State adopts strictly
neutral approach as far as the religion is concerned
State & Bureaucracy is based on complete rationality.

③ Contextual Model. (IND)
It means politically negotiated model. He calls Indian
model as a contextual model. Indian model is not
based on any single overarching idea.
