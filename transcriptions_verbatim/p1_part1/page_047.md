What is true civilisation according to Gandhi

True civilisation does not teach man How to fullfill his
wants rather how to minimise his wants. True civilisa-
tion makes a person a better Human being and not
animal.

Why Gandhi criticised Modern Civilisation ?
The Critic of Modern Civilisation was Gandhis attempt
to establish counter hegemony. It was Gandhis war of
position. Britishers have legitimised their imperialism
in the name of civilising mission or white man's burden.
Gandhi challenged civilising mission because Britishers
~~themself~~ do not know the meaning of civilisation.
Indians need not to take any lessons from them.

⑥ Gandhi's Theory of State
Gandhi was influenced by Leo Tolstoy. Leo Tolstoy in his
book 'Kingdom of god within you' has held that Man
can control himself and there is no need for external
agency like state. Gandhi called state as 'Soulless Machine'
According to Gandhi, state is not compatible with the
principles of Non-violence even the minimal state require
some element of coersive force. State cannot understand
Ahimsa because it does not have soul. According to Gan-
dhi presence of state reminds him of his weakness.
Presence of state is not compatible with the concept of
Swaraj. Hence, Gandhi proposed democratic decentralisa-
tion. Gandhi's Ramrajya is a stateless society.
