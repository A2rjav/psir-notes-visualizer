The term federation comes from Latin world Foedus
which means contract. Federation is government by contract.
U.S. is the 1st federation. Federation is described by taking
USA as reference point. USA is known as coming together
model where India is known as holding together. In USA
diffn state combine to form federal govt. Constitution is
contract document. Constitution determine power & functions
of 2 diffn govt. To ensure that they don't enchroach in each
others sphere, their exist and independent judiciary. Thus
3 essential component of federation include written const.,
division pow of power and function, independent judiciary.

Division of power does not mean delegation/
devolution of power. The source of power of subnational
government is the constitutional itself unlike unitary form of
govt where source of power is centre. Sub national govt. g have
original source of power. They are not subordinate to national
govt they are full fledged govt in their own context. Hence,
federalism is also known as dual governance. Person is
under 2 sets of government. Federation differs from
confederation as in federation unit lose their their external
sovereignty. Hence, in all federation in India division of
power does not apply w.r.t. international treaty and
convention, Art 253 as differentiated by US SC. federation
is indestructible union.
