● Party System -
  Party system does not depend on no. of parties
  but no. of parties having systemic relevance.
  Eg. India have multiple parties even at the time of
  independence but till 4ᵗʰ General Election in 1967
  Indian Party System used to be known as one party
  dominant system/ congress system.

● Distinctive Features of Indian Parties -
  According to Rajni Kothari, Political parties in India have
  some western features as well as some non-western
  features. Non-western features include, lack of internal
  democracy & lack of seperation between party and
  Government.
