Sir. Syed Ahmed Khan came under criticism from the
orthodox section in the Muslim community. He was also
controversial because of his advice to the Muslims for
not joining Indian National Congress.

There is a transition in the thoughts in Sir Syed Ahmed
Khan. Initially he was an advocate of Hindu Muslim
unity. He even adviced Muslims not to eat beef taking
into account the sentiments of Hindu neighbours.

However, there is a change in his ideas after the language
controversy in Awadh. Urdu in Persian script was the
official language of the province. There was a demand to
make Hindi in Devnagri script as official language. He
became convinced that neither Hindus will agree to wha
Muslims want, nor Muslims will agree to what Hindus
want. He believed that differences are going to increase
and it is better that they stay as seperate community.
Thus Syed Ahmed Khan als known as the originator of
two nation theory which resulted into the partition of
subcontinent.

Sir Syed Ahmed Khan wanted to get British support for
promotion of the Education among Muslims. In his book
Asbab - i - bagawate hind (causes of revolt of 1857)
He has clarified that the real cause of revolt of 1857
was not the conspiracy by muslims by rather the wrong
policies of British administration. In his article titled 'the
loyal Mohammaden's of India' He has given assurance
about the loyalty of muslims towards the British. He was
success ful of getting the support of ~~this~~ Britishers which
has led to the establishment of Mohammaden Anglo
oriented college which later on became Aligarh University
