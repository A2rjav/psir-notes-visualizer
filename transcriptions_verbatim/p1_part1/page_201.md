__SC. observation on art 356 in S.R.Bommai case.__
Federalism and secularism are the integral components of
basic structure. The purpose of inclusion of art 356 is to
preserve rather than subverd it. In art 356 there is latent
capacity to deprive 2 basic feature of constitution. Hence, it
is necessary that use of art 356 should be with caution and
only when non-use will result into the harm to national
interest. Not to be used, where other remedies are possible.
Frequent elections undermine the faith in democracy it is
the obligation of president to preserve const & any misuse
will bring havoc. SC held that sow a ~~so are~~ wind, reap ~~rip~~ a whirl wind

__Other areas of disputes .__
__All India Services (315-323)__
CM has very limited powers w.r.t members of all India
services. They primarily act as an agent of union. There have been
instances where union govt. has reinstated the official suspended
by CM. SC has directed to constitute civil services board to
deal with disciplinary matter related to members of all India
services.
