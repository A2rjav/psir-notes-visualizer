Civil Disobedience Movement (1930-31)
Nature of Civil Disobedience-
Non-co-operation was directed against the economic
base of the raj. Civil disobedience was direct
challenge to the authority of the govt. Non-co-opera-
tion was mild. It was not co-operating with the
govt. Civil Disobedience was more offensive it was
opposition of laws made by the govt.
How Gandhi described civil-Disobedience-
He was influenced by Thoreau who described Civil
Disobedience as peaceful protest against unjust laws
by civil society.
Reasons-
Seven years have already been passed & Gandhi was
looking for the new movement. He found atmosphe-
re favourable as it was surcharged with Anti-British
feeling because of Simon Commission, Death of Lala
lajpatray & economic depression. Gandhi put forward
11 point demands:
ⓘ Abolition of salt Tax.
ⓘⓘ Total Prohibition on liquor.
ⓘⓘⓘ Reduction of land revenue by half.
ⓘⓥ Reduction of Military Expenditure by half.
ⓥ Reduction in the salary of higher officials.
ⓥⓘ Hike in the tariff of the imported clothes.
ⓥⓘⓘ Release of political prisoners.
ⓥⓘⓘⓘ Abolition of CID
ⓘⓧ License to use firearms in self defense.
ⓧ Rationalisation of Ruppee-Pound sterling ratio.
ⓧⓘ coastal tariff regulation bill.
