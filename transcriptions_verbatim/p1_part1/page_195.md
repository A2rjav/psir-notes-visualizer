__Is award subjected to Judicial review?__
    It is not subjected to judicial review; but SC can entertain the question w.r.t the working of tribunal.

__What is the basis of SC judgement?__
    SC has supervisory jurisdiction over working of all tribunal and courts except army courts within territory of India. Recently in Dec 2016 on Kavery water issue SC held that it has unfettered power to hear appeal arising from the judgement of tribunal. It has clarified that the dispute only restrict SC from entertaining original complaint and suit on its own; but not its power to hear appeal against the tribunal judgement. As per above judgement now the awards also comes under the scope of judicial review.
