● Non-Co-operation Movement (1920-22)
Significance -
1st truely Gandhian all India movement.
causes-
(i) Suppression of Civil Liberties in the name of curbin
terrorist violence. (Rowlette Act).
(ii) Extreme paid with Jalian wala Bagh Tragedy & the
way British parliament & British public supported
General Dyre.
○ Dissatisfaction with the report of Hunter Commission
○ Economic disstressed because of 1st world war.
○ Khilafat movement which Gandhi saw as an opportu-
nity to bring muslims in the national movement though
Gandhi was criticised for communalise otherwise secu-
lar movement.
○ People were looking for action.

Strategy of, Non-co-operation Movement-
Nagpur session of 1920 approved the programme of
extra-constitutional mass struggle lead by Gandhi
How Gandhi described Non-cooperation
Right to refuse co-operation with the ruler who
misrules.
Goal of Non-co-operation Movement-
Attainment of swaraj within a year.
Programme of Non-co-operation-
(i) Surrender of titles & honours.
(ii) Boycott of govt. schools, courts, colleges & foreign clothes
(iii) No tax campaign though it was optional, left on provincic
leaderships.
