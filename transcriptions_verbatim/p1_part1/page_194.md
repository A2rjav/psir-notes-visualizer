In case of time of award amendment came in 2002
which held that tribunal has to give award in 3 years and
an exceptional situation 5 years. 2002 amendement has plugged
one loophole; but led left other loophole. Once award is given
parties can seek clarification in specific time. However there is
no timeframe within which tribunal has to come up with
clarification. Act mandate that central govt should maintain a
data bank for speedy action. No effort has been made to
make deta bank. Present govt. has announced that it will
create a body with task of maintaining data rainfall,
water availability, etc. Present govt. has also announced
decision to constitute permanent tribunal and its benches
to avoid unnecessary delay. Present govt has also announced
resolution of despute resolution committee to ave for political

\* __Composition of tribunal:__
At present union govt. can ~~represent~~ ^request chief justice of
India. CJI can constitute tribunal comprising 3 judges
One judge has to be one of SC. The other judges can be
either from SC or HC. In the permanent tribunal which
is proposed a retired judge of SC will be acting as a
chairperson. Besides the 3 judges, tribunal also has to
access or to help judges in assessment.

Punchhi commission has recommended the intro. of
multi-disciplinary tribunal comprising the members of
civil society like social scientist, environmentalist, panchayat
etc The award of tribunal is to be treated at par
with decree or order of SC. and it is the duty of
union government to get the award implemented.
Tribunal has the power of civil court.

Earlier it was not clear whether tribunal can give
interrim judgement. Now with the intervention of SC
in case of Kavery water, it is clear that tribunal can give
interim award. Whether it is an e interim award or final
