Evolution of Party System In India -
Phase I - uptill 1967
This phase is known as the phase of one party dominant
system, In the words of Morris Jones. One party Domi-
nant system differs from one party system
as found in socialist countries. In socialist countries.
one party & one ideology exist & but in one party
dominant system many parties can exist with diff-
erent ideologies. But other parties will not have any
relevance.
Prof Rajni Kothari ~~k~~called Indian party system during
this phase as congress system.
What are the characteristic features of Congress system
Congress was the Hegemonic party, a rainbow coalition
or Grand Coalition. Congress enjoyed Hegemonic status
because of its ^role^ ~~loan~~ in national movement & because
of its ideology which could accomodate different sections
into a strategic alliance.
Rajni Kothari calls congress as 'Party of consensus'
He calls other parties as 'Parties of pressure'
What is parties of pressure?
With the exception of communist party in Kerala,
other parties were not in a position to form the govt.
even at the state level. Hence their status was like
pressure groups.
In the words of Rajni Kothari they were working on
the margins which means their presence was mar-
ginal or peripheral,
