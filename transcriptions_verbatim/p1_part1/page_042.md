According to Gandhi,
① In passive resistance, not using violence may be
determined by the conditions which are not conducive
for the use of violence. In Indian context Aurobindo
Ghosh proposed passive resistance. He was not against
the use of violence. For Gandhi non-violence was an
article of faith and not shaped by conditions.
② In passive resistance person may have enimity towards
whom passive resistance is employed. However, Satyagra-
hi has no enemity or ill feeling towards anyone.
Satyagrahi is against the evil and not against the evil
doer. If fight is Satyagraha the means is going to be
non-violence and the essence is feeling of love & ability to
make sacrifices.
③ According to Gandhi, Satyagraha is not the weapon of
weak. It is a weapon of strong. It is a weapon of those
who are morally strong and not just physically strong.
④ According to Gandhi, ~~ten~~ If there is a choice between
violence and cowardice. He will choose violence.
