- __Capitalist Encirclement__-

Stalin proposed revolution in one country. Whereas Lenin
favoured expansion of revolution.
Why?
Revolution in Russia will not be secured if incircled by
capitalist countries.
Stalin wanted to avoid the conflicts with western world
so he gave socialism in one country.
Trotsky was contemporary of stalin who challenged stalin's
idea in his book 'Revolution Betrayed' & has proposed
permanent revolution. Like lenin he was also fearful of
capitalist encirclement.
Just like capitalist Encirclement theory exist in commu-
nist Domino theory exist in capitalism. Against Soviet
Expansionism USA, proposed containment of communism.
Domino's theory came w.r.t South East Asia It suggested
that if one country will fall to communism other will
also fall.
