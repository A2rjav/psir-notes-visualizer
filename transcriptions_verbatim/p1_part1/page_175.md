• Electoral Behaviour of People in India -

Electoral Behaviour studies have been the major area of
research among the behavioural political scientists.
Such studies help political parties to shape their policies

According to Milan Vaishnav of Karnegi Foundation
it is extremely difficult task to study the voting beha-
viour of Indians because of the size & diversity.

Kenneth Arrow, has given impossibility theorem this
suggest that it is difficult to determine preferences
when consumer/voter has more than 3 choices.

Mukulika Banerjee has conducted Ethnographic survey
to understand the voting behaviour of Indians.
Her studies are presented in her book "Why India
votes" one of the basic question of the research
was why people in India bother to vote & specially
poors when such a massive voting does not bring
any qualitative change in governance. The results of
the research show following observations:

Different people vote for different reasons: In gene-
ral people vote because power inversion takes place
It is the only day when people think that power
has shifted from the hands of politicians into their
hands.-> Some people vote because they think that it
is better to choose & diss miss the person who gove-
