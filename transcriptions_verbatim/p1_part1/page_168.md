It has realised that without entering the system it is
not possible to change the system. AAP believes in
Swaraj or Grass root democracy. AAP believes in
bottom up approach & rejects high command culture.
AAP stands for Intra-party democracy, members have
Right to recall the members of executive council,
no two members of the same family will contest
election nor will join executive council. AAP wants
to focus on developments & aims to take Indian poli-
tics away from caste & religion.
