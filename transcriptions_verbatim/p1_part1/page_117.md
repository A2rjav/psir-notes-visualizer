Role of Religion in Politics-
Religion has been present in politics since beginning.
The presence of religion in politics is also a universal
phenomena. Since the end of cold war there has been the
phenomenal rise in the role of religion around the world.
Clash of ideologies has been replaced with clash of civilisa-
tion there has been the growth of ethnic conflicts around
the world. Though the History of modern India has never
been free from the influence of communalism But there
has been the phenominal rise in communalism in India
since 1990s. Since 1990s Mandal & kamandal have become
the major idioms of Indian politics. Thomas Blom Hansen
in his book 'the saffron wave' has explained the rise of
Hindutva & BJP's position as a symbol of growing influe-
nce of religion in politics.
What type of politics is the Politics of Religion?
Politics of Religion is considered as the politics of identity
Features of the Politics of Identity?
(i) Politics of identity is not the politics of reason but poli-
tics of passions.
(ii) Politics of Identity is necessarily violent. Once emotions
are raised they become uncontrollable and it culminates
into violence.
(iii) Politics of Identity is unethical & Anti-National.
(iv) It is unethical because Persons having political ambitions
play with the sentiments of the people.
(v) It is anti-national because it is always exclusivist in nature
(vi) Politics of Identity is Anti-development. It distract the prio-
rities and attentions.
