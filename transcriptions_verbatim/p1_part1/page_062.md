Why untouchability was enforced?
So that they continue to serve the other castes. It is
because of untouchability that even when untouchables
take up any other professions, members of other caste
do not take up their services. It forced them to conti-
nue with the traditional jobs. Since all castes were
benefited, so all castes started practicing untouchablity.
How to end untouchability?
According to Ambedkar, remaining Hindu, it is not possi-
ble to end untouchability or caste system. Only by ~~having~~
leaving Hinduism it is possible for untouchables to lead
a life of dignity. Only when we put dynamite on Veda's
and Manusmriti Annihilation of caste is possible. He
gives the examples of Bhakti saints & earlier social refo-
rmers who tried to end untouchability but could not
end. According to him, even Gandhi will not be success-
ful. Gandhi is a political leader. He is not a religious
or spiritual leader. People will respond to his call in
political sphere. They will not respond his call in
social sphere. According to Ambedkar, Hindu's are not
bad people but they are extremely religious people.
and the problem lies in Hinduism. Ambedkar held that
it was not in his choice to be born as Hindu. But it
is in his power ~~know~~ not to die as Hindu. on 14th
November 1956 he converted in Buddhism in a grand
ceremony. He held that by disowning the religion of my
birth i am reborn. I reject Hinduism, I reject the
religion which discriminates between man & man.
and which treats me inferior.
