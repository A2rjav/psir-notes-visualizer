Phase III- communalism during Indira Gandhi
During the time of Indira Gandhi we see the wor-
st consequence of the use of religion which led to
the terrorism in Punjab. During the time of Nehru
communalism was limited to urban areas but now
it has started spreading to rural areas against the
politics of Akali's Congress started supporting the
Dalit's in Punjab. the politics of religion culmina-
ted into the tragedy of the operation of Blue star.
Assassination of Indira Gandhi followed by Anti-
Sikh Riots. Primarily in North India violence continued
for 8 consecutive days in capital itself and justice
is still not served.

Phase IV - Communalism during Rajiv Gandhi

brings a law nullifying the progressive judgement of SC.
in shah banu case. Appeasement of Hindus made BJP
insecure. BJP started Ram Mandir issue.
