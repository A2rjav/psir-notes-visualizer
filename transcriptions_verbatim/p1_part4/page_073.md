● __Importance of the state__
  The most imp. concept of Political Theory is state.
  It is said that Political science begins & ends with
  the state. There was a time in Political Science
  when the focus was shifted from study of state
  as an institution to the study of politics as a process
  Scholars like Theda skocopol called for bringing the
  State back in. She suggested to maintain the centrality
  of state in Political analysis.

● __What is the idea of the state?__
  State is an abstract concept & Govt is the concrete
  manifestation of the state. State represent authority
  Authority is needed so that people can lead collective
  life.
● __Evolution of state__
  In the western world we see the following forms
  of the state:
  (i) Earliest form was of city state.
  (ii) Roman Empire.
  (iii) Nation state.
  In Europe we have also seen the growth of supra-nati-
  onal institutions i.e. European union, still the dominant
  form of state around the world is Nation state. Nation
  state is an European institution which emerged in Mode
  rn times but became the universal Institution as a result
  of colonialism & Imperialism.
● __What are the Elements of the Nation state?__
  Nation state comprise of four essential elements-
  (i) Territory
  (ii) Population
  (iii) Government
  (iv) sovereignty
