POLITICAL IDEOLOGIES

IDEALISM

● Exponents of Idealism -
  Socrates, Plato, Aristotle, Rousseau, Hegel, I. Kant
  T.H. Green.                                   [Imanuel Kant
● For idealists Man is social/moral by Nature.
  For idealists society is prior to Man.
  For idealists state is prior to Man.
  For idealists society & state is Natural/organic.
  Idealists do not give importance to the Rights they give
  Importance to duties.
  In idealism we see the developement of two schools.
  (i) __Hegelian school (German Tradition)__ -
      German idealists gave primacy to state.
  (ii) Kantian school
      Primacy to society not state.
  (iii) British School (oxford Idealists)

  Political idealism may result into the justification of -
  (i) collectivism, (ii) Totalitarianism.

  __Medieval Times__
  In medieval times the Theory of the Divine Rights of the
  kings was promint.
  The Theory of Divine Rights led to the justification of
  state absolutism on the basis of religion.

  Both Idealism & Divine Rights Theory justified state
  absolutism but on different grounds. Idealism on the
  ground of reason/logic & Divine rights on the ground
  of religion.
