● __Samuel P. Huntington and His concept of__
  __clash of Civilisations__
  According to S.P. Huntington, the end of History
  thesis does not represent the reality of post
  cold war world order. He has predicted the clash
  of civilisation. Throughout cold war world was
  devided between 2 different ideological camps i.e.
  communism and capitalism. Ideological differences
  can get resolved but cultural & religious differences
  are difficult to resolve. In his book 'clash of Civili-
  sation' he mentions 8 civilisations-
① Chinese
② Japanese
③ Hindu Civilisation
④ Orthodox Civilisation i.e. Russian
⑤ Islamic Civilisation
⑥ Western Civilisation
⑦ African
⑧ Latin American
