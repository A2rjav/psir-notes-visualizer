terms, they believe that the best way to resolve
disagreements on the issues of moral conflicts is

each other equal. They go for public reasoning.

can develop overarching consensus. Overarching con-
sensus is different from Modus Vivendi.

that will lead to peace & stability will depend on
overarching consensus. However, communitarians con-

to the political conception of justice & they are skeptical
about the possibility of overarching consensus.
