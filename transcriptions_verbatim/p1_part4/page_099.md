__Purpose of the Theories of the Power-__
Theories of power suggests location of power.

① __Elitist Theory of Power__
Oldest theory.
According to elitist theory, power lies with the elites.
Who are elites?
Human population can be devided into-
ⓐ Elites
ⓑ Masses.
Elite is a special class. ie. Not a ordinary.
There are special qualities which make a person elite
According to Plato & Aristotle, Reason & Courage are the
qualities of the ruling class or masters.
According to Machiavelli, Prince has to be Fox & Lion.
→ Elitist Theory of Power has been given by Italian scholar
~~Pareto~~ Pareto.
Salient Points of Pareto's theory
__Book__- 'Mind & Society.'
According to Pareto, the History of mankind is nothing
but graveyard of Aristocracy.
In every society there have been two types of elites
ie. Foxes & Lions. According to Pareto, History is cyclical
in nature & power flows in a closed loop. There is no
trickle down effect of power. This theory is known as
theory of circulation of elites. Elitist theory of power is
the basis for elitist theory of democracy.
According to Elitist Theory of Democracy, power with
Masses is a Myth. Masses do not have capacity to hold
power or exercise power. The only role masses can play
is the election of elites. It is sufficient for democracy.
