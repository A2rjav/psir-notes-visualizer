__Thomas Kuhn__

__Book__- The structure of scientific Revolution
Given the concept of paradigms (Framework)

According to Thomas Kuhn even scientific theories
are Paradigms. Paradigm is a conceptual framework.
Scientist also work with a paradigm. Paradigms also
change. For some time existing paradigm may be able
to answer most of the question. However, there are
many questions which existing paradigm may not be
able to answer. when no. of such questions increase
existing paradigm comes into crisis. when existing
paradigm unable to answer majority quesⁿ old para. is rejected
& new paradigm comes into existence. Thus the concep
of paradigm suggests that there is no finality in the
knowledge.

-> Post Modernism challenges the basic premises of Modernity
(i) Foundationalism
(ii) universalism.

(i) foundationalism is based on the philosophy of science
that there are certain fundamental principles which can
be understood also. Eg. There are some fundamentals of
human nature.

(ii) universalism suggest that there are few things which
are universally applicable or which should be univer-
sally applicable. Eg. from the perspective of western
countries there are some universal rights which
should be available to all human being. Universalism
is inspired by Newton's universal law of Gravity.
