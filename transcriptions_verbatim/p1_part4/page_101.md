$\rightarrow$ __C Wright Mills__
He has given the concept of power elite. In context
of USA. According to him, power in USA lies with the
small section of elites whom he call as power elites.
In USA Federal politicians, corporate (Big) Houses, Top
officials of Military form power elites. All decisions in
USA are taken by power elites in their own interest.
According to C Wright Mills, the decision to drop AutomIc
bomb was taken by the power elites.
US was not always lead by power elites in the begi-
nning USA was more democratic. However, because of
internal crisis like civil war & increasing role of USA
in international politics the importance of these
classes have increased.
__Who are power elites?__
In every society there may be some key institutions or
organisations. Persons holding top positions in these
institutions or organisations are power elites.
