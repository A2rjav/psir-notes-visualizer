differently.
According to Walzer, Complex Equality means No citizens
standing in one sphere or with regard to one social
good can be undercut by his standing in some other
sphere with regard to some other good.
Eg. Person X may be choosen over person Y for political
office, the two become unequals in sphere of politics.
However, this inequality will not be inequality in general
so long person X does not take the advantage of his
office
