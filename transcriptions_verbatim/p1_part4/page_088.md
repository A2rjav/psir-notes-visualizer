__Concept of Managerial Revolution__
Managerial Revolution as a concept has been advanced to
show the changed nature of capitalism. Managerial revolu-
tion suggests the disconnection between the owners of
capital & descision makers. Capitalists are no more desci-
sion makers. Decision making has shifted from capitalist
class to managerial class.
Managerial class is a open class. Anyone can join the managerial
class. Even the persons coming from the working class families
can be the managers. In industry decisions are taken by ma-
nagers now. The nexus between money & Descion making has
been broken. The norms in the industry has changed.
