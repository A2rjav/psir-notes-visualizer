● __History of the term Ideology__
The term ideology was coined by French scholar
Count d Tracy.
● __What was its purpose?__
He was contemporary of Nepollan Bonapart.
This was the time of enlightenment & scientific
revolution.
Tracy wanted to establish a school for the scientific study
of ideas. The purpose was understanding all ideas on
scientific basis Hence, he coined the term ideology.
Ideology denoted systematic/scientific study of Ideas.
However, Ideology got politicised. Science of Ideas
could not come into existence. Tracy & his supporters
came to be known as Republicans.
