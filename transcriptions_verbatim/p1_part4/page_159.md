● Introduction -
  Justice is the most important concern of political
  philosophy since the time of plato. In political philo-
  sophy Justice is not limited to the legal system.
  Political philosophers have talked about justice as principle
  of Ethics. The most important idea in Justice is the
  Idea of Distributive justice.

  John Rawls is considered as the greatest philosopher
  of 20ᵗʰ century. Rawls position in contemporary poli-
  tical philosophy is at par with plato in classical political
  philosophy. The credit for revival of Political philosophy
  is attributed to John Rawls. His book 'A Theory of Justice'
  Published in 1971 marks the beginning of the __revival__
  __of political theory (philosophy).__

● Works of John Rawls -
① In 1958 his Article - 'Justice as Fairness' was published.
② In 1971 came his 'Theory of Justice.'
③ In 1993 Rawls book - 'Political Liberalism' was published.
④ 'The Laws of the people' published in 1999 where Rawls
  has responded to the arguements & questions raised by
  Cosmopolitan scholars like Thomas Pogee & Charles Beitz.
