what are the arguements for justification of Federal Authority
- Laski's arguement is since society is federal so authority
should also be federal.
How society is federal
Man has multiple needs. One organisaⁿ cannot fullfill
all his needs Hence man has created various associations.
All such associations like family, kinship group, professional
org., lot groups, together constitute the society. State alone
cannot perform all the functions. These organisations should
also have some amount of freedom & authority, Only state
should not have authority of obligaⁿ of man. Other org. also
deserve the obligation.
Since society has changed, Hence monistic concept appears
anachronistic. Hence, pluralistic theory is a better theory
of sovereignty. It is also more realistic view of the sove-
reignty of the state.

"State is a keystone of social Architecture."      — Laski
Laski is a pluralist. Laski does not support the monistic
theory of sovereignty of the state. According to Laski, since
society is federal authority should also be federal. Pluralism
is not anarchism. There is an inconsistency in the approach
of pluralists towards the state. They donot favour abolishing
a state but at the same time they don't want to give excessive
power to state Regarding pluralist it is said that they want
to keep the cake & have too. Inconsistency comes because
of the unique role state has. It is true that state is one
of the association like other associations. But state plays
the role of equilibrium maker among the competing interest.
