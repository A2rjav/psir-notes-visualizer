• __Basic assumptions in socialism -__

Core value of socialism is Social & Economic Equality.
Because the origin of socialism is in context of criticism
of capitalism.
Laissez Faire capitalism has resulted into increase in
inequality & exploitation.
Socialists propose that Industrial society can be based
on better principles than that of capitalism.
They suggest to base society on principles of socialism.

• __Conception of Human Nature (By socialists) :-__
According to them, man is social by Nature.
According to socialists ~~Man~~ No man is an Island
each man is a part of continent.

• __~~The~~ Socialist concept of Society :-__
Socialists reject agregative view of society &
Believe that society is Natural, Organic entity.

• __Socialist concept of Rights -__
Socialists believe in Social & Economic Rights.

• __Socialist concept of liberty__
For socialists, liberty is a Bourgeoise concept. Instead
of liberty they use the term Freedom. Real freedom
is freedom from necessities.

• __Socialists concept of Equality__
① Social & Economic Equality
② Equality of outcomes
③ Absolute Equality
