[Margin: Question on Human Rights ->]
Q. Implementation of Human Rights is regarded
as matter of changing the conduct of states. comment.
Human Rights is a most powerful Idea of our times.
Because Human Rights can bring lot of change in the quality
of life. It provides us scope for freedom from expoitan,
& wants & hunger. If we adhere to HR a better world is
Possible. Though there is a realisation of the potentials &
promises yet Human Rights has reduced to another discourse.
There are many practical & conceptual issues w.r.t. the
implementation of Human Rights. Any Attempt to imple-
ment Human Rights. any demand for Human Rights becomes
political & conflictual. Problem exists because there is a
lack of consensus of meaning of Human & what basic
rights should constitute the ~~universal regime~~. There is a
need that all those who are the stake holders ie. govts.
of developed countries, civil society (HR-NGOs) of develo-
ped countries, States in Third World countries & civil
society should take honest efforts to depolitise Human
Rights. Only when International community will start giving
priority to right over their geopolitical interest we can
build the legitimacy of Global Human Rights regime.
Similarly Human Rights Associations (Advocacy groups of
the western countries working in non-western countries
have to adopt more neutral approach. most of the states
from non-western countries suffer from insurgencies,
Successionist threat. Human Right groups only emphasise
on the rights of law breakers, criminals, non-state actors.
Same level of activism is not shown for the state
authorities. It does not mean that states in develo-
ping countries need not to adopt zero tolerence
approach towards the violation of Human Rights,
