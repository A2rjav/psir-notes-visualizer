ROBERT NOSICK -Noble laureate.

- Anarchy, State and Utopia - Nosick's Book
- Nosick is a critic of John Rolls.
- Nosick does not support welfare functions.
According to Nosick,
"Minimal state is inspiring & right."
- He revives the idea of Night watchman state. The
  only role of the state is to see that property is
  rightfully acquired/ transferred. State have no role
  in distributive justice. He also considers progressive
  taxation as bonded labour.
Minimum state is inspiring because it motivates
person to work hard. It is unethical if state force
man to work against his wishes. Nosick is also
regarded as semi-Anarchist. He believes that even
security services ~~to~~ can be provided by private sector.
