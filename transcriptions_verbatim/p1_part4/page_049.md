__Mao's concept of Revolution -__
In Marx there is only one revolution & that to again:
mode of production. Whereas in Maoism there is a
concept of permanent Revolution & Revolutions at both
ideological & material level.

Mao has given the concept of Guerilla Warfare.-
Guerilla Warfare should be the strategy of poors.
Mao was also a strategist. In his Red Book he discuss
the strategy of Revolution. According to him, people
should 1st try to capture the periphery like Rat
Nibbles. He held that people/masses should attack when
enemy is resting, they should withdraw when enemy
~~army~~ attacks.

According to Mao, Power flows through the barrel of Gun.
It means the only way people can come to power is
through violence.

__Campare Mao with Gandhi -__
Both Mao & Gandhi were contemporaries. Both have been
successful mass leaders. Influence of both go beyond
their own countries. Both fought against common enemy /
evil ie. Imperialism.
However, the two differ in terms of strategy, If Gandhi
considered violence as an Article of faith & suggested
non-violent method as a method of struggle for masses.
Mao believed in violence. Mao held that power comes
through the barrel of gun. If Gandhi suggested the tech-
nique of Satyagraha, Mao suggested the technique of
Guerilla warfare for masses.

Though both are successful but Gandhism is morally superior
to Maoism. Violence does not result into any solution.
Violence give rise to violence. According to Gandhi, violence
belongs to the Animal & Eye for Eye will make the world
blind.
