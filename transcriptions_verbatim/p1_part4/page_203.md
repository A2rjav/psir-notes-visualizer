Stage II
In medieval times Political science became the
Sub discipline of Religious studies or scholastics.
Stage III
It was Machiavelli who made politics independent of
religions and ethics. He was the 1st person to establi-
sh autonomy of politics as a discipline. He held that
for politics we have to go for emperical observation
and understanding of History rather than Ethics &
Religion.
Gradually Political Science became a specialised disciplin
where the study of state (Govt. & Constitutions) became
the core area of study. It has been held that Political
science begins & ends with the state.
Stage IV - After 2nd World war
Uptill 2nd WW Europe was the place for the growth
of the political ideas, philosophy, after 2nd WW the
growth of discipline has been in USA. American Politi-
cal scientiest under APSA (American Political Science
Association) started advocating that Political Science
should be made pure science. In USA a movement
which is also known as Revolution developed that is
Behaviouralism. Behaviouralists introduce scientific
techniques in Political Science to make Political Science
a pure science.
Stage V - from 1960's onwards.
Rise of post Behaviouralism.
It was realised that if attempt is made to make politi-
cal science a science it will not only compromise the
subject matter it will also compromise the relevance.
