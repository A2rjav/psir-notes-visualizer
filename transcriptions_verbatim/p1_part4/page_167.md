• What are the 3 principles of Theory of Justice as
Fairness?
(i) Liberty principle
(ii) Equality of opportunity.
(iii) Difference Principle. - working in the interest of the least
advantaged section of the society.

→ __Lexical Order__
~~It in~~ a definite order or sequence.
Principles of Justice are in a particular sequence of
priority. Liberty has priority over Equality of opportunity &
difference principle.
Equality of opportunity will have priority over difference
principle.
What is the basis of Lexical Order ? -
Rawls give Maximin principle. Since persons are ration
al they would prefer a system which will give Maximum
opportunity or freedom to develope their talents or advan-
tages. They will also ensure that the system is such which
ensure their well being in case they happen to be the least
advantaged. System should be such which minimise the
disadvantages. Rational person will choose the alternative
whose worst outcome is better than the worst outcome
of any other alternative eg. If person chooses Liberty as the
only principle & he happens to be the least advantaged the
worst outcome will be worse than the outcome if he had
choose dignity as the 1st principle.
