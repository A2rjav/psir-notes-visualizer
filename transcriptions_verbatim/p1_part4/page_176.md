• __Criticisms of Rawls Principles of Justice__
(1) Maximum Liberty (Equal) to all.
(i) __Communitarian critics -__
ⓐ According to communitarian's, Rawls has not given enough
   importance to the community. They also do not support the
   arguement that liberty ie. freedom to make choice should
   be above all other values. It is not necessary that the life
   of a person exercising his freedom of choice is superior to
   the life of person not exercising his freedom of choice.
   Suppose a person exercise a freedom of choice to marry
   multiple time, it does not mean that a life of a person who
   marry only for once is not as satisfying.
ⓑ communitarians also suggest that we are not free to choose
   our ends. On the other hand, end is already given. We disco-
   ver our ends rather than choosing them.
   Rawls priority to liberty is based on the conception of Ato-
   mistic man, whereas man is situated self.
   Rawls himself could not be free from the preferences of
   his own society. His preference for liberty is because of
   his society ie. US culture.
(ii) __Social liberals critics__
ⓐ __Amartya Sen__
   In his essay, 'Inequality Re-examined' Amartya Sen appre-
   ciate Rawls but he suggest that freedom without capacity
   carry no meaning. Even Martha Nussbaum also criticised
   Rawls for overlooking the capacity approach.
(iii) __Feminist scholars__
ⓐ __Carole Gilligan__
   She suggest that instead of liberty, 'Ethics of care'
   should top the priority.
