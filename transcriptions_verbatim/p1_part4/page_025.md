Q. Do you think liberalism has won the battle of
     ideologies?
-> Liberalism has been one of the longest surviving
   ideology in the west. Liberalism has got challenges
   from different ideologies at different times.
   Earliest challenge came from conservatives / Rightist.
   Since, October Revolution in Russia in 1917, till
   1991 disintegration of ~~soc~~ USSR. Hegemony of
   Liberalism has been challenged by socialism -
   communism. During inter war period liberalism
   faced another challenge in the form of fascism.
   Fascism is considered as extreme conservatism.
   In ~~se~~ WW II liberalism defeated Fascism but could
   not defeat communism. In 1991 scholars like
   Francis Fukuyama proclaimed 'Liberalism as the
   End of History'. He held that we are not just wit-
   nessing the end of history, we are witnessing the
   highest point of man's ideological evolution. History
   has proved that there is not better idea than that
   of liberalism. However, Liberalism has never been
   free from the challenges. In terms of philosophy, libe-
   ralism is been challenged by multi-culturalism,
   communitarianism, post modernism. In terms of
   politics Liberalism is challenged by Islamic-funda-
   mentalism, Putinism, confucianism. It is too
   early to say that liberalism has won historic
   battle of ~~liberalism~~ ideology. As there is world wide revival
   of Right & Left. Neo liberal world order is facing
   crisis, Brexit is one such indication.
