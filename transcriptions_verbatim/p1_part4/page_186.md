__Amartya Sen on Rawls Theory__
(1) He appreciates Rawls. for giving the criticism of Utilita-
rianism. He appreciates because Rawls make fairness as
the basis of Justice. He also appreciates because everyone
should get equal freedom. However he disagree with
Rawls on following:
(i) He does not support the necessity of any universal idea
of Justice. We can never arrive at an idea of Justice
which can satisfy all. He explains it through the story
of 3 children.
(ii) He also does not believe that Justice is a product of pro-
cedures. According to Amartya Sen, ~~Naya~~ Nyaya is
superior to Niti.
(iii) He was inspired by Buddha. Buddha was trying to eli-
minate human sufferings. The purpose of policy
should be to eliminate human suffering which he
calls as realisation focused approach.
(iv) He calls his methodology as social choice which is supe-
rior than rational choice. Rational choice is based on
abstract persons making decisions in idealised con-
ditions. Social choice is real persons making choices
in practical situations.
