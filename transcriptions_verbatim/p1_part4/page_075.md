-> __Views of Laski on Monistic Theory__ -

"It would be of lasting benefit to the discipline
of political science. If the entire concept of sove-
reignty of state is expunged out of the discipline."

sovereignty is considered as the most important concept
in Modern Political theory. Modern Nation state is the
defining form of political organisation in modern times.
Sovereignty is the most imp. element of Modern Nation
state. Hobbes is recognised as the 1st modern thinker
because he gave the 1st complete theory of sovereignty of a
state. He clearly established that on a particular terri-
tory there can be only one sovereign. Man has only
two options either to live under the state or anarchy.
Hobbes theory is known as Monistic Theory which is
further developed by John Austin who listed the chara-
cteristic of sovereignty that it is indivisible, inalienable
permanent characteristic of the state.

Laski is a critic of Monistic Theory. Laski is an inter-
nationalist who believed that Monistic Theory of sove-
reignty is threat to a international peace which has
become a necessity. He believes that international
organisations & law have to be strengthened to avoid
world wars. |
