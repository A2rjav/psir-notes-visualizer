• __Rawls Principles of Justice -__
(i) Each person is to have an equal right to the most extensive
   liberty compatible with similar liberty for others.
   (Maximum equal liberty).
(ii) Social & economic inequalities are to be arranged so that they
    are both -
    ⓐ Reasonably expected to be ~~to~~ everyone's advantage.
    ⓑ Attached to positions & offices open to all.
• __Lexical order__ of principles of justice
(1) Liberty
(2) ~~To be~~ 2b - Equality of opportunity.
(3) 2a - Difference Principle
• __Explanation of Principles of Justice -__
(i) 1st principle suggest that persons will have freedom to pursue
    their rational plans. Liberty will result into inequalities.
    Inequalities ~~are natural bec~~ will emerge because people will
    differ in terms of talent. However, natural facts are not just
    or unjust. It is how the institutions deal with these natural
    facts. We can create such institutions which minimise the
    disadvantages & maximise the advantages. Inequalities are
    justified only when -
    ⓐ Inequalities are the outcomes of equality of opportunity.
       Every person should have opportunity to pursue his talent.
       (Merit Principle)
    ⓑ Inequalities are justified only when inequalities are made
       useful for the least advantaged section of the society.
    Above principles are fair because they are providing opportu-
    nity to pursue one's talent (desert). The person who happens to
    be the most advantaged understand that his achievements are
    not just because of his talents. He has certain advantages over
    the others. Those who are in the least advantageous position
    will also find it fair because -
    - everyone had liberty.
    - everyone had equality of opportunity.
    - their interest is taken care of.
