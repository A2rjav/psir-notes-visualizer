As a philosophy communitarianism emerged as a critique
of libertarianism (liberalism). Some of the prominent
communitarians are Alas Dair MacIntyre, Michael Sandal,
Charles Taylor, Michael Walzer.

__Debate between Communitarians & Libertarianism__

(i) __Concept of Self (Human Nature)-__
According to communitarians, the liberal discription of Man
as a Atomistic Man is not a reality. Man is never an unen-
cumbered self. Man is embedded self or situated self.

According to liberals, self is prior to end. But according to
communitarians self is not prior to end rather constituted
by end.
Self is prior to end -> It is a liberal arguement. They suggest that
man should have freedom to decide his end or goal. or concep-
tion of good/right also. On the other hand communitarians
suggest that man is situated self. We cannot dittach ourself
from our community. Good is common. Good is shared. We
cannot know good alone. We discover rather than choose.
In We live in time, space & culture. It is difficult for man to
come out of his community. Communitarians talk about pra-
cticle reasoning or self discovery rather than abstract reason-
ing.

(ii) __Concept of Community -__
Liberalism/Liberals do not give importance to community.
For liberals community is nothing except the aggregate
of Individuals. Liberals believe that person can ~~live~~ leave
his community. Liberals also believe that there is nothing
called common good. Common good is sum of individual
goods.
