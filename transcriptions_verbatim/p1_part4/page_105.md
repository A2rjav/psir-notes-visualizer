-> what is Genealogy?
According to Foucault, Genealogy is the study of
present History. Genealogy is understanding knowledge
power connection.

Foucault's theory of power is given ~~in~~ his book-
'Discipline & Punish.' In order to understand his
theory of power we have to understand following
concepts of Foucault.
(a) __Discourse__
Discourse is the way of telling the reality rather than
telling reality. Every age has its own discourse.
E.g. In Medieval time it was the discourse of the
christianity which shaped our understanding of reality.
In Modern times it is the discourse of science which
shapes our perception of reality. Science has become
highest discourse. According to Foucault, fight is never
for the absolute truth. It is impossible to know the
ultimate truth. Instead of ultimate truth he mentions
'regimes of truth'. In his books like Madness & Civilisa-
tion, Birth of a Clinic, Discipline & Punish, History of
Sexuality he has applied archaeology & Geanealogy.
Discourses shapes the norms & morality. When we diff-
erentiate b/w a mad & Normal person it is not that
we know what is madness & what is normal. Rather
it is the way madness is explained to us.
