-> Will kymlicka has given following reasons for the
rise of identity consciousness:
(1) __Demographic changes:__ and Higher scale of Immigration
(2) Growing consciousness about human rights.
(3) The neglect of minorities & minorities becoming
assertive.
(4) __Securitisation of Ethnic relations.__

As situations are changing the existing models of
tolerations as practiced in France & USA are coming
under challenge. Western countries like Canada in 1970's,
Australia in 80's have publically declared the adoption
of multi-culturalism as policy. EU also adheres to
multi-cultural approach. One of the earliest consti-
tutions adopting Multi-culturism values is Indian
constitution.
