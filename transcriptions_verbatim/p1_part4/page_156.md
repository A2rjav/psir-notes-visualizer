Marxist scholars suggest that Democracies in Western
countries is a Democracy only for the small section
of the rich class. According to Ralph Miliband even
welfare state remains the instrument of the rich
Class.
Macpherson who himself is a critical liberal suggests
that western democracies are Inegalitarian.

There is also a debate between scholars like Lee kuan Yew
& Amartya Sen over the relationship between demo-
cracy & developement & whether democracy is suita-
ble for Asian societies. Lee kuan Yew suggest that
Democracy is a western obsession & does not match
to Asian values. Amartya Sen suggests that Democracy
is 'Universal value'. He gives 3 values of democracy.
① __Intrinsic Value__ - means democracy gives sense of
   empowerment.
② __Institutional value__ - related to good governance.
③ __Educative Value__ - it makes citizens conscious & respo-
   nsible.

At present there is a debate over the weaknesses of
Representative model. We are witnessing the rise of
Majoritarianism & decline of deliberative democracy,
for scholars like Habermas there is a need for
strengthening public sphere. Similarly Armartya sen
supports public reasoning. It is believed that we
have to make democracy participative & deliberative,
without which we cannot achieve the goals of sustain-
able developement. There are scholars who also mention
about the paradoxes of democracy. eg. Larry Diamond.
