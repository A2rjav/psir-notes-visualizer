HEADING: FEMINISM
---
Feminism is a Meta-Ideology. or Umbrella ideology.
There are many schools of Feminism like liberals,
socialists, Radical feminism, Post-colonial and
post feminism. The common threat connecting all
feminist is the concern towards the exploitation of
women & an attempt to end exploitation. Feminism
is both ~~a~~ A philosophy & A movement. As a move-
ment Feminism developed in the form of 3 waves.

① __1st wave__ - 1st wave started at the time of French
Revolution.
Marry Wollstonecraft is called as Mother of Feminism.
In 1787 she wrote an essay - 'Thoughts on education
of daughters'. In 1792 she wrote 'vindication of
the Rights of Women.' In this book she has questioned
the exclusion of women from citizenship rights &
duties. She has also presented the charter of Rights
of women include with the declaration of Rights of
Men.
Earliest form of feminism is known as Liberal Femi-
nism. The earliest demand for women movement
was equal rights for women & par with men. The
most important right for which feminists were active
was for right to vote & that is why feminist move-
ments were called as Suffragate Movement.
The Seneca Falls convention in USA in 1848 was the 1st
convention to formally demand Right to vote for
women in USA. Suffragates were successful in
getting Right to vote for women in liberal countries.
