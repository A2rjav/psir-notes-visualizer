● What is Democracy?
Democracy can be understood in two ways:
(i) Narrow sense/Term -
    Democracy is considered as form of Government.
(ii) Broader sense/ Term -
    In Broader sense Democracy is way of life.
    Democracy is a issue of culture.

● __What are the components of Democratic culture?__
(1) Respect for Human Dignity
(2) Respecting Individuality
(3) Culture of Rights, Toleration, Rationalism. \
