• Relationship between Liberty, Equality & Justice.
Liberty, equality & Justice are the prominent values
in the Normative Political Theory. They are overlapping
values. The relationship between Liberty, Equality &
Justice can be understood only with reference to a
particular school of thought. Different schools of tho-
ught not only disagree on their relationship, they also
disagree on the meanings of these concepts.
① Classical Liberals/ Neo liberals / Libertarians -
Classical liberals look at liberty as absence of inter-
ference by the state & society. According to Hayek,
liberty is absence of coercion. This view of Liberty is
now known as Negative liberty. Liberals support the
idea of equality before law & equality of opportunity.
However for liberals attempt to establish social & eco-
nomic equality by the state reduces liberty. Hence,
liberty is Anti-thetical to the idea of social & economic
equality. According to Nozick's Theory of Entitlement, it
would be injustice if state interferes in person's right
to property for the sake of justice.
② Marxists
Marxists call liberal idea of liberty as false conscious-
ness. The so called liberty in western capitalist society is
not liberty but Alienation. The liberal idea of equality
is formal & procedural & far from being substantive.
Instead of Liberty & Social & Economic Equality being
Anti-thetical they are complementary. However, Marxists
make a difference between liberty & freedom. Accord-
ing to Marxists freedom is freedom from necessities.
Only under communist mode of production man can
achieve perfect freedom. Thus social & Economic
equalities are the pre-conditions for freedom &
Justice.
