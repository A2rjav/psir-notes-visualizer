Lenin's book- Imperialism the highest stage of capitalism.

Karl Marx has confined his analysis of capitalism in
domestic context. He has not discussed the external
aspects of capitalism. It was Lenin who analysed
~~that~~ external aspects & Lenin's theory became the basis
for Marxist theory of International politics (Dependa-
ncy school).
                                                 ↓
                                             colonialism.
Lenin has exploded the myth that imperialism is white
men's burden. It is nothing but the highest stage of
capitalism. He has given 3 stages of capitalism:
① Mercantile capitalism
② Industrial capitalism.
③ Finance capitalism

He has analysed 1st world war & held that it was
nothing but the capitalists war. Lenin even
thought of supporting the nationalists parties
fighting against imperialism. Lenin was not against
nationalism in the way Karl Marx was. Lenin even
thought that communist revolution can start against
capitalism in colonies.
