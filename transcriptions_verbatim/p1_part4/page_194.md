In Rawls theory people are behind Thick veil of Igno-
rance but in Dworkin they are behind thin veil of
Ignorance. They know their talents, advantages, disad-
vantages, but do not know the actual value of their
talents. It can be decided only by Auction. Value cannot
be decided by the person. Value depends on how much
market is willing to pay. In the end of the Auction it
may happen that persons talent has the least demand
in the market. Hence, people will go for keeping some
clampshell as a Insurance. Hence a common pool can
be created. Dworkin Proposes Envy Test that distribu-
tion is fair which does not result into the feeling of
Envy. when a person is satisfied with his own bundle
then feeling jealous for the bundle of the others.
Dworkin gives the concept of option luck as well as
Brute luck. Insurance can compensate both Auction
and Brute luck. Dworkin suggest that common pool
can be used for the benefit of those who suffer
from Brute luck. Suppose a person suffers from some
physical disability he will have to keep some extra
clampshell to compensate for the disadvantages.
Hence he cannot enter into the Auction with the same
level of equality. Such person should be given extra
clampshell to compensate for Brute luck. According to
Dworkin Libertarian arguement that people are res-
ponsible & should be held responsible for the out-
comes of their choices does not apply. According to
Dworkin people are not responsible, should not be
held responsible for the unchoosen circumstances in
which they make choices. Hence he suggest Endow-
ment sensitive auction ie. giving extra clampshells.
to compensate disadvantages.
