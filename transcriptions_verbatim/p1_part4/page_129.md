● __Democracy and Developement__

Q. Explain with reference to the developement in India
and china. The Relationship between Democracy
and Developement. Do you think democracy is a
pre-condition for developement or obstacle for deve-
lopement.
The relationship between the Democracy & Developement
has been a matter of debate. There is a major debate
b/w Lee kuan Yew and Amartya sen.
→ __'Lee Theisis'__-
According to Lee kuan Yew India remains, 'A Nation
of Unfullfilled Greatness'. The reason is India has
choosen the wrong path. According to him, Democracy
is not conducive for developement. Democracy results
into Indiscipline. slow decision making process.
Democracy is western obsession. Democracy does not
suit to Asean societies. Asean societies require, 'Asean
values'. Asean values like discipline / duty, respect to
Authority & social solidarity is more conducive.
According to Lee kuan yew India took the wrong way
ie. socialists way. China also took the socialist way
but china could change its approach.
