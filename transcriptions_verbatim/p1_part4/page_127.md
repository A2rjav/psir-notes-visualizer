We are living in 'the Age of Democracy'. Samuel P.
Huntington, has explained the expansion of Democra-
cy around the globe in the form of 3 waves of
Democracy.

* __1st Wave__
1st wave started in 19th century. In the 1st wave most
of the countries of western world become democratic

-> __1st Reverse wave__
In the 1st half of 20th century there has been a reverse
wave of democracy when many European countries
like Germany, Italy, Portugal, Spain came under Dicta-
torship.

* __2nd Wave__
2nd wave started after 2nd World War & most of the
countries of the colonial world became Democratic.

-> __2nd Reverse Wave__
2nd Reverse wave started very soon & by 70's with the
exception of few countries like India, Sri Lanka etc.
Democracy collapsed.

* __3rd Wave__
3rd wave started with the collapse of communism in
Eastern Europe. Democracy became Global phenomeno-
with the exception of Arab world. American political
scholars gave the theory of 'Arab Exceptionalism.'
which means that democracy is not compatible with
Islam. Arab Exceptionalism was a discourse to justify.
the western support to the Gulf Monarchies & Dictator
ships in Egypt & Tunisia. Arab Spring challenges the
western discourse of Arab Exceptionalism. Arab Spring
could have become the 4th wave. However Arab Spring
turned into Arab Summer because of the geopolitical
objectives of western power.
