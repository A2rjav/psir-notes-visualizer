③ ● __Concept of Citizenship and Equality__ -
Feminists are against the concept of universal citizenship
Universal citizenship means equal treatment for all.
Feminists like Iris Marion Young support the idea of
differentiated citizenship. They want differential treat-
ment or positive discrimination by the state in favour
of Women.

④ __Concept of Equality__ -
Feminists support differentiated Equality rather than
simple equality. Simple equality implies colour blindness.
State need to differentiated between men & women make
special provisions in favour of women. Hence according
to feminists, "By differentiating Equality we equalise the
differences."

● __Comparison between Liberal Feminism & Radical Feminism__
Feminism is a meta ideology & there are many schools of
Feminism. Feminism developed in the form of waves
1st wave starting from French Revolution till 1960s was
dominated by Liberal feminists. Since 1960. we see the
second wave known as Radical Feminism.

Radical feminists do not regard Liberal feminism as a
true feminist discourse because it is just an applicatio
of liberal concepts in context of women. Radical femini
sm emerge from the specific experience faced by
women because they live in gendered society. Promi-
nent ~~like~~ Radical feminist like Simon de Beauvoir and
Betty Friedan and others have tried to analyse patri-
archy. If liberal feminism demanded civil & political
rights Radical feminism aimed at ending the patriarchy
