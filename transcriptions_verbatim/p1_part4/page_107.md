In his book Madness & Civilisation. He has explained
how the system of segregation started between Normal
& Mad person. The persons who have not been useful
for capitalism were considered as mad.

Foucault has given special focus on the __disciplinary power__
He has analysed the disciplinary institutions, their origin
and purpose. The institutions he ~~s~~ has studied include Aim
Asylum, schools & prison. Discipline is enforced to the
system of examination, rewards & punishment.

Foucault has suggested the concept of 'Economy of Power'.
In modern times mass society has come into existence.
In order to control such a large section of society.
state adopts the method of discipline which Foucault
calls as Governmentality. Governmentality is achieved by
Homogenisation & through disciplinary institutions.
It is for the purpose of the convinience of the state,
population is classified into males & females, national
and foreigners. Through discipline people learn how to
control their body mind & soul. He gives the concept of
Biopower. i.e, power controlling human bodies.

Foucault has applied the concept of __Panopticon__, semi-circu
lar design of prison suggested by Bentham. The idea of
Panopticon is to ensure self regulation. Instead of keeping
policeman for every criminal. modern prisons have centra
surveillance tower. Prisoners automatically discipline them
selve thinking that somebody is watching their actions
Similarly society is also like a panopticon. we discipline
ourselves we keep our body & mind in control because
we feel that infinite eyes of society are keeping watch
on us.
