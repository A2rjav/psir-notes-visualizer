Legal Constitutional Approach has been applied by the
political scholars in a big way when political science
was understood as the study of state. The approach was
employed primarily in the branch of comparative
govt. and politics. These scholars have studied the provi-
sions of the constitution. Legal Constitutional Approach
is very close to Legal Institutional Approach. In Legal
Institutional Approach focus has been on the study of
institutions like parliament, judiciary, its origin, evolution,
challenges etc.
Present status
Legal Institutional Approach has been the dominant
approach till 2nd world war. However, with the emergence
of Behavioural Approach, legal approach suffered from
decline. Behaviouralists suggested to ~~politi~~ study
political behaviour, ~~study~~ politics as ~~study~~ ~~rather~~
process rather than the study of institutions
