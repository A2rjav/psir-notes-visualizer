Modern Nation State is a territorial entity. It is based
on the idea of centralised govt. One of the earliest
philosophers theorising Nation state include Thomas
Hobbes. Hobbes has clearly established that in a parti-
cular territory, on the population living in the territory
the ultimate power will be of the state. Max Weber
described state as an institution which has monopoly
over the use of coersive power in a particular territory.

● __Concept of Sovereignty__ -
  Refer previous chapters.

● __Theories of sovereignty__ -
(1) __Monistic Theory of sovereignty__ -
    __supporters__ - Hobbes, John Austin
    Monistic Theory suggest that in a particular territory
    there is only one sovereign & sovereignty is concentrated
    at one place i.e. state
    According to Monistic Theory, sovereignty is-
    (i) Permanent characteristic of state it is inalienable
        and indivisible
→ __Criticism of Monistic Theory__ -
    Monistic theory has been criticised for following reasons-
    (i) It is a legalistic view rather than political view.
        The approach of law is textual, whereas political analysis
        is contextual. There can be a big gap between theory and
        practice. In reality sovereignty is always a compromised
        concept. No state can exercise sovereignty in ideal sence.
        Externally the choice of state is limited by the powers
        of other actors & its own powers. Internally sovereignty
        will be limited by other associations, peoples customs
        and traditions, no state can impose its will on the people
        without consent of the people. Hence monistic soverei-
        gnty is a legal fiction
