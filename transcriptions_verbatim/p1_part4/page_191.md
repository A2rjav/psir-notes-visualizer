According to Dworkin, Equality is 'Sovereign Virtue'.
It means equality will trump over all other virtues.
Equality is the idea of modern times as our conscious-
ness is growing our understanding of equality is
expanding becoming deeper. Equality is closely asso-
ciated to Human Progress. Human Progress can be
defined as expansion of Freedom & Equalities.
Equality is linked to the concept of Dignity. Equality
gives the sense of well being.

● Types of Equalities -
① Liberal Idea -
Equality before law.
Equality of opportunity.

② Marxist View -
For Marxists the above to ideas represent formal proce-
dural view of equality. It is not substantive idea.
Real equality is possible only in communist society.
Where private ownership does not exist, classes do not
exists. Every person works according to his ability & gets
according to his needs. Hence Marxists are not satisfied
with Equality of opportunity, they want equality of
outcome.
