● Affirmative Action
What is Affirmative Action?
Affirmative Action is positive Discrimination in favour
of the weaker section of society. Affirmative Action
should not be treated as reverse discrimination. It
should not be considered as discrimination against
someone, rather it is a discrimination in favour of
someone.
The idea of Affirmative Action is based on the assump-
tion that in every society there can be certain sections
who have been historically disadvantaged like Blacks
in USA or Dalits in India. It is assumed that they
cannot come at par with those sections of the socie-
ty who are considered as the forward section on
their own. Hence, external catalists. are required.
Countries may follow different type of Affirmative Action
policies. Reservation or Quota's is one of the strongest
option for affirmative action.

permanent basis for the same group it defeats the
purpose. They are enabling policies which should ena-
ble the members of backward classes to come at
par with the forward sections in a given time frame.
