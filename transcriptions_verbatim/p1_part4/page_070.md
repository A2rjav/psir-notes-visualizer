● __Short Note - Feminisation of Poverty__
The term feminisation of poverty was coined by Diana
~~B~~ Pierce in 1978. Feminisaⁿ of poverty suggest that
women form the majority of the poor population in
the world. The phenomena of feminisation of poverty
has increased since neo-liberal globalisation has
expanded. Datas suggest that even in countries like
USA the largest no. of homeless population constitute
of women. Similarly, china has poor record with
respect to feminisation of poverty. Feminisation of
poverty is more acute in developing countries The root
cause of it is patriarchal structure of the society.
Women suffer from multiple deprivations because of
their sex. Since they suffer from deprivation in terms
of educational empowerment, Nutritional empower-
ment, violence against women that they suffer more.
Phenomena of feminisaⁿ of poverty has increased
because of rolling back of the state. The most
adverse impact of withdrawal of subsidies has
been on women.
