-> __What is real man according to Multi-Culturialist__
We cannot isolate or abstract man out of his
culture or identity. It
According to Bhikhu Parekh,
(i) Man is Culturally embedded we cannot seperate man
from his culture. It is impracticable to accept that man
will forget his culture when he comes in public sphere.
Not only it is not realistic it is unfair. It is not possi-
ble to respect a person but not respecting his
culture. If we extend a rights toa person but do not
extend recognition to the culture, our approach is
impracticable.

(ii) Liberals do not give necessary recognition to the cul-
ture. Culture provides meanings to our understanding.
Culture acts like a glass through which we understa-
nd the reality.

(iii) Every culture is Hybrid & plural consciously or uncon-
sciously cultures interact with each other. There is no
culture which can be considered as entirely pure.
Liberals try to impose culture of majority on the
minority in the name of universalism & rationalism.

(iv) Just like Biodiversity is good for ecology or EVS
Similarly cultural diversity is also good. Each culture
is rational, we can learn & enrich our culture by
recognising different cultures.

-> __Basic assumptions of Multi-culturists__
Multi-culturialists share assumptions with
communitarians and cultural relativists.
Like communitarians multi-culturialists reject
universalism & talk about particularism.
Like communitarians Multi-culturialists support
politics of identity & politics of community.
