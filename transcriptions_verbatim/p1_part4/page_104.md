● __Introduction__
Foucault is considered as one of the greatest intellectual of
our times. Foucault was not only an intellectual, he was
also an activist. Foucault was initially associated with the
left movement. He was professor of Psychology & Philoso-
phy. Foucault ~~&~~ has studies Linguistics, Economics & other
disciplines also. Foucault was influenced by Nietzsche.
Nietzsche was critic of enlightenment. Enlightenment has
not resulted into freedom of man. Earlier man was
controlled by religion. Now man is controlled by science.
Nietzsche held that God is dead. According to Nietzsche,
nobody can claim to tell the truth, theories are just
perceptions. We require to understand the things from
multiple point of views.
~~Foucault~~ He has given the concept of the will to power
(Nietzsche). Will to power leads to will to knowledge
as knowledge is power.
Foucault was influenced by Nietzsche's book titled,
'Genealogy of Morals'. Nietzsche has analysed the
origin of Morality.

● __Methodology of Foucault -__
Foucault called his methodology as, archaeology and
Genealogy.
→ What is Archaeology -
According to Foucault, it is a specific method to study
history in terms of the evolution of knowledge system.
