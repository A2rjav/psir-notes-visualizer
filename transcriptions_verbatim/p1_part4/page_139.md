(I) __Majoritarian Representation__
ⓐ __Plurality system__-
__Example__- Elections for Lok Sabha & Legislative Assembly in India.
Plurality system is least representative it results into wastage of votes.
ⓑ __Second Ballot__
__Example__- Presidential election in France.
Winning candidate requires absolute majority.
Absolute Majority means 50% plus 1 of total votes.
Why called as second Ballot-
(i) In case no candidate secures absolute majority in the 1st round of elections only top two candidates are retained.
