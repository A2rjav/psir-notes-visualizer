__Criticism of End of Ideology__
End of Ideology thelsis could not get wide acceptance
socialist scholars never accepted the end of ideology
thesis. They considered the end of ideology is nothing
but the concealed defence of the western way of
life. Among the critics of end of ideology include -
(i) C. Wright Mills
According to him it is wrong to think that western socie-
ties have established the equilibrium between the rich
and the poor.
(ii) C.B. Macpherson
He held that western societies are hardly Egalitarian.
Hence the importance of politics has not lost.
(iii) Alasdair MacIntyre
He held that end of Ideology is itself an ideology.
