• __Marxist Analysis__
There are 2 schools of thought in Marxist Analysis:
(1) __Instrumentalists / Dependency school__ -
__Scholars__ - A.G.Frank, Celso Furtado, Raul Prebish,
Samir Amin, Imanuel Wallerstein.

Dependency school is developed by the scholars of
Third World countries. Dependency school is influenced
by Lenin. Dependency school Theorists have analysed
the phenomena of Neo-colonialism. Dependency school
has analysed the Nature of state in Africa & Latin America.
Some of the basic concepts of Dependency school are:
(i) Colonialism has ended only in formal sense.
(ii) Neo-colonialism & Neo-imperialism is continuing.
(iii) Colonialism is a phenomena associated with capitalism.
capitalism has become world system. ~~state~~
(iv) States can be devided into core states & peripheries.
(v) Since the origin of capitalism core & peripheries
have got interlinked.
(vi) Prosperity at the core has its roots in the poverty &
the peripheri.
(vii) There is an unequal exchange, which results into uneven
developement. The developement of core & the develope-
ment of underdevelopement at peripheries.
Those countries which are more closely associated with
the core countries, there situation is worse.
According to these scholars the states in these countries
are puppet states or Clientlist regime (Like Saudi Arabia,
Bahren) The states in these countries are the instru-
ments of Metro politan Bourgeoise.
