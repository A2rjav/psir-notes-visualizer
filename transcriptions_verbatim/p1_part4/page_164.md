Continued with Justice...
Utilitarian theory of justice is the dominant theory in
most of the western countries. Rawls aims to build
alternative theory of justice in the tradition of libera-
lism.
What Historical circumstances were prevailing at the
time of Rawl ?
During his time there were many social movements &
protest going on in US society Hence he felt that the
existing theory of justice is not sufficient & we need
to build new theory of justice.
what type of new theory of justice?
The theory which is acceptable to all the theory which
all sections of the society find reasonable. Thus Rawls
ambition is to build such theory of justice which is ratio-
nal & which can be considered as an ultimate theory of
justice & which can be conducive for social stability.
How Rawls describe his theory ?
Rawls describe his theory as 'Deontological Theory'.
What is Deontological Theory?
Utilitarianism is an example of ontological or Teleological
Theory. Hence, utilitarianism is called as end state theory.
According to utilitarianism, action is justified so long it
results into the maximisation of Happiness. Rawls Theory
of Justice is Deontological because everyone accept justice
as fairness as a principle. They adhere to it whatever
may be the consequence.
