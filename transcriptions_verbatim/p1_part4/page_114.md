Max Weber made the distinction b/w Naked power
and Authority. The use of power by the state is
considered legitimate by the people. Hence, he
suggests that state represent Authority. He has given
3 ideal types of Authority. The dominant type of
Authority will vary from society to society.
The concept of legitimacy is closely associated with
the concept of authority. Because Authority is legi-
timate power. States continue because states enjoy
legitimacy in the eyes of the people. According to
Max Weber the relationship can be explained through
the equation:
  Authority = Power + Legitimacy.

● __Indicators of legitimacy__
(i) Law obidience among the citizens.
(ii) Respecting national symbols, Anthem, Flag etc.
(iii) Payment of Taxes
(iv) Social Discipline.
(v) Lesser no. of Crimes, protests
Citizens observing National Festivals

● __Methods of Building legitimacy__
(i) Maintainance of law & order.
(ii) Maintainance of Rule of law
(iii) Freedom of Press
(iv) Independence of Judiciary
(v) Social welfare schemes
(vi) Regular elections
(vii) Propaganda.
