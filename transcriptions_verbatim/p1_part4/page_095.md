Structural school / Theory of over Developed state -
The Theory of over Developed state is given by Pakistani
Scholar Hamza Alawi
Though theory is about Nature of state in Pakistan But
it is also applicable in case of other S. Asian States Eg.
India, Bangladesh
According to Hamza Alawi , The Nature of the state in
Pakistan needs more deeper analysis than the instrume-
ntalists approach . It will be inappropriate if we suggest
that the State in Pakistan is just an Instrument of the
capitalists class of core countries.
Hamza Alawi , has applied the structuralists / relative
autonomy theory in case of Pakistan. even relative auto-
nomy theory needs modification.
Salient points of his theory of overdeveloped state -
It is wrong to apply the same concepts for analysis of
the nature of the state in case of post-colonial societies,
which is applied in case of western countries.
why?
There is difference in Historical conditions.
(i) State in western countries is an instrument of the capita
list class.
(ii) state in post colonial countries is not an instrument of th
indegenous capitalist class. state actually mediates among
the 3 competing classes.
(a) Metro-politan Bourgeoise
(b) Indegenous Bourgeoise
(c) Indegenous Feudal class.
