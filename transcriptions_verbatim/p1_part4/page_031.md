(1) __Will Kymlicka__
__Book - Multi-Cultural citizenship - A Liberal Theory of Minority Rights (1995)__
Will kymlicka suggests that Multi-Culture is the
natural & inevitable evolution of Liberalism.
He believes that only liberals can ~~be~~ adopt Multi-
Culturialism. Will kymlicka suggest that govts.
should give special rights to the minorities. He gives
3 category of rights:
(i) Special Representation Rights.
(ii) Self Governance Rights - If the ethnic community
is concentrated in a particular geographical area.
(iii) Poly Ethnic Rights - It means minorities should be
given special cultural rights. He suggests govts. to
grant exemptions e.g. Muslim girls should be allowed
to wear scarfs in a school, muslims can be given
Holidays on Friday, Sikhs can be allowed to wear
turban.*
