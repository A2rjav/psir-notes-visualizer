What is Reasonable pluralism?
In liberal countries, persons follows various compre-
hensive doctrines.
Example of comprehensive doctrine.
Liberalism - as philosophy of life, christianity, Islam,
Judaism*
Reason of stability in these societies?
According to Rawls, the reason is democratic political
culture
What are the features of Democratic Political culture?
(i) value of equal citizenship.
(ii) People are willing to offer fair terms to each other.
(iii) People stay together for right reason rather than
compulsion or bargaining.
- Rawls uses the term overlapping consensus.

According to Rawls, overlapping consensus is not
Modus vivendi.
What is Modus vivendi?
Modus vivendi is a bargain guided by self interest.
overlapping consensus is based on moral conviction
of fairness.
What is overlapping consensus?
Reasonable & Moral people living in democratic political
culture are able to develop overlapping consensus on
'Political conception of Justice'. for particular reasons.
What is Political conception of Justice
Political conception of Justice is based on the principle of
Justice as fairness with some ~~[illegible]~~ literal modification.
According to Rawls, Political conception of Justice is
'free standing idea'.
