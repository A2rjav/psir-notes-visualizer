Q. Write a short note on Liberal concept of Tolerism.
   or Toleration.
-> Along with liberty, Toleration is the value of liberalism.
The term Toleration comes from the latin word
Tolerare' which means to bear or endure. The idea
of Toleration is necessary for realisation of the
liberal idea of liberty. Liberty can be enjoyed
only in Tolerent society. Toleration is considered as
the 'substantive Heart of Liberalism'. Toleration is
a deliberate exercise of self restrain towards the
things or behaviours which we strongly disaprove &
had not been tolerent could have used force. Toleran
is not about things with respect to which we are
indifferent, it is not just acceptance of diversity. Tole-
ration is accepting the things which we otherwise
hate. Toleration is considered as personal virtue, social
virtue as well as political virtue.
-> __Context of Toleration debate.__
It emerged in the context of the issue of the
managing diversity. One of the earliest can view on
toleration in Liberalism is associated with J. Locke.
In his letter consernig Toleration, J. Locke suggest that
state should give freedom of conscience & state
should not dictate matters of religion.
- J.S. Mill also gives the idea of toleration when he
  proposes harm principle. The only basis for inter-
  vention in the freedom of man is when his actions
  result into the concrete harm.
- John Rawls suggests that Toleration is a feature of
  liberal societies because people in Liberal societies
  can continue to possess different comprehensive
  doctrines (Christianity, Judaism, liberalism, Islamism)
