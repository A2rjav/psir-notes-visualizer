[Margin: ②] __Participatory Democracy -__

It means Direct Democracy.
It also means, Increasing participation of people in
Governance. Participatory Democracy also means Demo-
cratic Decentralisation.
$\rightarrow$ Why participatory Democracy?
① Good governance is not possible without participation
of people. Participation insures transferency & accoun-
tability. Participation has educative value. When people
participate in Democracy they understand governance,
they develop responsibilities, they can also understand
the problems in Governance. ~~Representative~~ ^participatory^ Democracy
should be the next logical step after Representative
Democracy.
Various studies show (Agenda 21) adopted at Rio summit
adopted in 1992. Various studies show the relationship
b/w Democracy & Sustainable Developement.
2^nd ARC report recomment 'Citizen centric Governance'
__Methods of Participatory Democracy -__
① Referendum
② Initiative
③ Recall
④ Right to Information
⑤ Citizens charters, Social Auditing
⑥ Watchdog bodies like Human Rights commissions, Lokpal.
⑦ Democratic Decentralisation (Devolution of powers
to Local Governments)
