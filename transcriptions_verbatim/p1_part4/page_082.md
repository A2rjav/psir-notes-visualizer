(1) __Origin of the state -__
There are two theories of the origin of state :
(a) Social contract Theory
(b) Evolutionary theory

(a) __Social Contract Theory -__
Significance - Social contract Theory implies that a state is
not created by God rather the product of the free will
of Man. For liberals state is an institution of utility.
