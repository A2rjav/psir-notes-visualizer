__Example__
We can give the example of Germany under Hitler.
In Germany both capitalists as well as workers were
organised. This allowed state to gain autonomy. State
gained primacy or leadership. It is also true that ~~at~~
ultimately fasist state protected the interest of proper-
tied class.
It may also happen that more than one propertied
class exist e.g. Feudal class as well as capitalist class as
in 19th century France. Since none of the class is able to
dominate the economic structure state gains relative
autonomy.
Even Gramsci has also given similar concept. Gramsci
has given the concept of 'Integral State' the component
of integral states are civil society or political society or
state. Because of the civil society state appears autonomous
However when civil society fails state comes for the rescue
of the capitalist class. Structural Marxists like Althusser,
Nicole Poulanzas also support relative autonomy theory.
According to Althusser, state in capitalist society possess
two type of operaters:
(i) Ideological state operateres (Institutions of Civil society)
(ii) Coersive state operaters (Police & Military)

Relative Autonomy theory is also known as structuralist
theory. In structural Marxism importance is given even to
the elements of super structures. Marx has adopted structu-
rialist approach in 18th Brumaire. Structuralism was
developed by Gramsci & that is why he is known as Theori-
tician on a super structure. Gramsci has held that super-
structure is not just reflection of base rather structure
in itself. According to him, there are two layers in
superstructures.
