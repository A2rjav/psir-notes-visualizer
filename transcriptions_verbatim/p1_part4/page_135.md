• __Short Note - Polyarchy__
Polyarchy is a concept given by a Robert Dahl. Polyarchy
is a more realistic view of Liberal Democracies.
Liberal Democracies are not oligarchies nor they are
ideal democracies. Polyarchy is 'Approximate Demo-
cracy'.
According to Robert Dahl, Poliarchies have following
features:
① Rule of law
② Fundamental Rights
③ Free and Fair election
④ Freedom of Press
⑤ Freedom to form association
⑥ Right to ~~dis~~ decent
⑦ Independent judiciary
