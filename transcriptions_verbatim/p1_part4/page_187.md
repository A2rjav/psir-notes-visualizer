__Nozick's Theory of Justice -__
Nozick calls it as Entitlement Theory of Justice.
Nozick gives libertarian view of justice. He does not
support progressive taxation. He support Nightwatch
man / minimal state. State should not attempt to
remove injustice. Historical injustice cannot be stre-
tched too far in the present times. State has only
following powers w.r.t. Right to Property:
(i) State should see that property is rightfully owned,
transferred or inherited.
(ii) There is one situation in which state can intervene te.
in Right to Property when Right to Property can put
the life of many in danger. He gives a example of
village where there is a single well. Well is the only
source of the water. Life of villagers are depend on
the water from the well. If the owner of the well
deprieves others from taking water state can use
force & make water available for others.
