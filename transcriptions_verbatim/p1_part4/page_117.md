__End of Ideology -__
End of Ideology is a concept given by some western
scholars in 1960s and 1970s. The period coincides with
the period of the 'Detente'.
Detente symbolised relaxation of tensions between the
two super powers. Some western scholars claimed that
the age of ideology is over. Ideological battles carry no
importance. One of the prominent exponent of End of
Ideology thelsis was Daniel Bell. He has written a book
titled 'End of Ideology' where he ~~he~~ has claimed that we are
living in an age where politics is at the backsit & economics
is in the front. The prime question of our times is developeme
nt. In both worlds East & West (East-USSR) similar type of
Bureaucratic state & technocratic societies have come into
existence. Hence, ideological struggles do not have much
relevance. In case of western countries welfare state has
resolved the conflict b/w labour & capital.
