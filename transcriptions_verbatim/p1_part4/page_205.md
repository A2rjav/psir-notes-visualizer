However, there has always been a big question mark
on the nature of Political theories as scientific to
quote Maitland - when i see a question paper with
the title Political Science, i am more troubled with the
title than the questions.

It is to be noted that today it is acknowledged that
even scientific theories are paradigms.

Since the emergence of post modernism it has been
acknowledged that all theories are discourses. According
to Foucault there is a knowledge power connection. Even
scientific theories are not free from the phenomena
of power. Post modernism has established that theo-
ries are interpretations. In contemporary times Poli-
tical science is considered as 'Interpretative Discipline'

[Margin: Q] • Utility of Political Science as Discipline-
"Political theory is quite simply, man's attempt to
consciously understand and solve the problems of
his group life and organisations. It is a disciplined
investigation of political problems. It not only show
what political practice is but also what it means.
In showing what a practice means, or what it ought
to mean, political theory can alter what it is."
