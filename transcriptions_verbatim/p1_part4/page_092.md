$\rightarrow$ __Theories of post colonial states__
Post colonial states have been studied by Liberal perspective
as well as Marxist perspective.
__Liberal Perspective__
__Approach of Liberal scholars__
(i) They have applied political developement & political modernisa-
    tion approach.
    Contribution of F.W. Riggs (Father of deve. Admin")
    In case of Third World countries Riggs has preferred the
    term 'Prismatic societies'
    __Prismatic Society__ -
    Prismatic society means societies in transition societies
    moving from the underdeveloped stage to developed stage.
    we can call it as developing societies.
    According to him, Prismatic society reflects following feautures-
(i) __Formalism__ - It means big gap in theory & practice.
(ii) __Polynormatimism__ - co existence of rational & irrational
    norms of policy making.
(iii) __Poly communalism__ - Entire country is not one community
    rather there are different communities. Communities co-
    exists but are suspicious about each other.
(iv) __Economic system__ - Bazar-canteen model
(v) __Administrative system__ - SALA model. In administration we
    see the combination of rational rules as well as family &
    kinship norms
(vi) __Functional overlapping__ - There is no functional specialisation,
    functions overlap e.g in many countries army is involved in
    civilian Administration.
