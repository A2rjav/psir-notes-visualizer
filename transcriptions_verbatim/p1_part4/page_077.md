__What is philosophical justification of pluralism :-__
According to the supporters of pluralism. Universe is
multi-dimensional. Man is also the part of Universe.
Man is also multi-dimensional. There are multiple needs
of Man. One association cannot fullfill all the needs of
Man. Hence, one association cannot claim the monoply
over the obligation of Man. In order to fullfill his needs
man has created many associations. State is only one
such association. Since other associations also fullfill the
needs of Man they should also have authority & man
should be obliged towards them also.
-> __What is Pluralist view of sovereignty?__
Sovereignty should be devided among different organisa-
tions. We can give the example of European Union where
sovereignty is not monopolised by any state. In many area
sovereignty has been shifted out of the state into the
supra-national bodies. Like member countries of Euro
zone do not have sovereignty on their currency and Mone
tary policy. Decision making has been shifted to Euro-
pean Central Bank.
-> __criticism of Pluralistic view of sovereignty__
Pluralistic theory of sovereignty is a source of confusion.
why?
Pluralists are not anarchists. Pluralists do not want to give
authority to the state But at the same time do not end the
state. The type of functions state performs results into
the superiority of state over other institutions. Regarding
Pluralists it is said that they want to ~~cake~~ keep the cake
and eat it too.
