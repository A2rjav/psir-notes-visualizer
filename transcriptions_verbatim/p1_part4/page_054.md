__Basic assumptions of Fascism -__
Basic assumptions of Fascism can be drawn by observation
about the type of social political system that was establi-
shed in Italy & Germany during inter-war period.
Though Nascism & Fascism were not the only two
examples. There were other examples also like Japan
under Prince Fuminaro, Portugal under Salazar, Peroni-
sm in Argentina. All these can be considered as variants
of Totalitarianism. The basic difference in Fascism &
Nascism was that Fascism gave primacy to the state &
Nascism talked about race.
Fascism core value was unity & solidarity. Fascism can be
considered as ultra-nationalism. Hitler made race as the
basis of nationalism. Race became the basis for the justi-
fication of Hitlers Imperialist policies known as lenben-
sarum.

__Human Nature__
Fascism is reaction against rationalism. Fascism suggest
that human nature is guided by passions & not by
reason. Fascists prefer politics of passions. Insightment
of passions rather than appeal to reason. When passions
are insighted they become uncontrollable & hence politics
of passions necessarily culminates into violence.

Fascist leaders believe in the concept of inequality. They
make difference between elites & masses. Masses to be
controlled by elites. Leader has the central position.
Fascism demands absolute obidience towards the leader.
Leader is super man.

__Society__
Fascism give importance to society. Fascism called itself
as 'Creative Destruction'. They were not satisfied with the
values of enlightenment. Enlightenment promoted ration-
alism, individualism, questioning of customs & traditions.
Fascism wants to destroy society & culture which was the
result of enlightenment. They do not support the concept
of atomistic man seperated from his culture & society.
They want to recreate the society. where people are
not isolated rather discover themself in culture, tradition.
