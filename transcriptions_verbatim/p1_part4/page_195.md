③ Amartya Sen's Theory of Equality-

He suggest to make people equal not just in resources
but in capabilities. Amartya sen gives two concepts:

① capability - capability is the ability to do what
   one wants. Capability is absence of deprivation.

② Functionings - Functionings is 'being & doing'. Functioning
   is doing what one wants to do, it is a source of
   well being.
   Eg: If we give two children the bicycles one knows cycling
   and the other does not know, they cannot have equal
   level of functioning.
   He compares hunger & fasting. Hunger represent
   deprivation as hunger cannot be choice whereas
   fasting can be a choice & itself a source of functiong.

Hence he suggest state to adopt capability approach.
Poverty is not just the absence of income it is presence
of deprivation. Resource equality is equality of means
whereas equality of capability is a equality of ends.
The ends being functioning.

capability approach is better because it takes into
account the diversity. Different people may have
different types of deprivation. Eg. In Indian society
the nature of deprivation which a poor girl child
may suffer are going to be the different from the
deprivation which poor male child will suffer. Capability
approach focus on Human developement. In Capapibility
approach people are not means of economic growth
rather ends of economic growth. Thus he defines
developement as freedom.
