• Exponents of Participatory Democracy -
(i) Rousseau Only with the participation of people that
    General will can be filtered.
(ii) Founding fathers of US constitution like George
    washington was critic of party politics.
(iii) Gandhi, M.N. Roy, Jay Prakash Narayan.
(iv) J.S. Mill For him Direct Democracy is an Ideal
    type & representative govt. is second best.
(v) Benjamin Barber participatory democracy is necessa-
    ry for addressing the problems of corruption.

Among the supporters of Participatory Democracy,
there are large no. of scholars with leftist orientation
This model of participatory democracy is known as
Radical Democracy.
Radical Democracy emphasise on the role of social
movements & civil society protests. Among the promi-
nent scholars we can refer to Habermas belonging to
critical school
→ Habermas -
Habermas has given the concept of public sphere.
He has also given the concept of "Ideal speech situation
and communicative action.
What is Public sphere?
It means people coming together entering into the
discussions & debates, identifying their problems,
thinking about solutions. According to Habermas, the
real strength of Democracy in western countries comes
from Public Sphere. It is not | parliament or Congress
rather in coffee houses that Democratic culture comes
into existence.
