● __Short Note- Veil of Ignorance__.
(i) __Introduction__
Veil of Ignorance is a thought experiement applied by
Rawls while giving the principles of Justice.
(ii) __Why Experiement?__
Because he wants to derive the Theory of justice which
appears Rational & Fair by all.
(iii) __Conditions__
~~Veil of~~ Veil of Ignorance is a concept used in Original position.
In original position persons have come together to determine
the principles related to the distribution of primary goods.
They dont know particular facts they know only general facts.
Behind the veil of Ignorance persons arrive at the 3 principles
of Justice which forms the basic principles of Rawls Theory
of Justice as fairness.
(iv) __Significance__
Veil of Ignorance ensure that the decisions are purely rational.
(v) __Criticism__
Dworkin suggests that there is no point putting people behind
the veil of Ignorance because we cannot impose those
principles of justice which they have agreed behind the ~~a~~
veil of Ignorance.
Dworkin in his book 'Sovereign Virtue' suggests the concept
of thin veil of ignorance. rather than thick ~~a~~ Veil of Ignorance
proposed by Rawls.
According to communitarians, 'it is difficult to consider that
man can ever be in a original position.
