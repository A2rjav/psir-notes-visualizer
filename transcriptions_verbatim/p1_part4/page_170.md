thinks that Justice is the outcome of Just institution
based on some abstract idea of rationality.
Rawls is criticised by Feminists, Marxists, Libertarians,
Communitarians as well as social liberals.
● __Statements of Rawls:__
① "Justice is the 1st virtue of social Institutions, as truth
is of system of thought. A Theory however eligant
and economical must be rejected or revised if it is
untrue, like wise laws & institutions no matter how
efficient & well arranged must be abolished if they
are unjust. __Each person possess inviolability__ ^inviolability Founded on
the principles of justice that even the welfare of a
society as whole cannot override. For this reason justice
denies that the loss of freedom for some is made
right by greater good shared by others. It does not
allow that the sacrifices imposed on few are out-
weighed by the larger sum of advantages enjoy by
many. Therefore in a just society the liberties of equal
citizenship are taken as settled, the rise secured by
justice are not subject to political bargaining or to
calculus of social interest...... __Being 1st virtues of Human
activities, truth & justice are uncompromising.__"
② "Right is prior to good."
③ "The Natural distribution is niether just nor unjust,
it is not unjust that a person born into society at
some particular position. These are simply natural
fact. What is just & unjust is the way institutions
deal with these facts."
