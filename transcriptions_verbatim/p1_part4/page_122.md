HEADING: Difference in Modernism & Post Modernism-
---
According to Modernity, universe is in order, there is a
structure in universe. According to Post Modernists,
universe is in chaos. there is no structure & universe is
expanding. Hence, we cannot give fixed meanings.

__Difference in Modernism & Post Modernism__-

(i) According to Modernists there is an objective reality
which can be known objectively.
According to Post Modernists knowledge is subjective.
The basis of knowledge is not observation. But
experience.

(ii) Modernity has constructed meta. narratives.
whereas post - modernism supports local narratives or
multiple narratives.
What is Meta Narrative?
The term Meta Narrative was coined by Lyotard.
According to Lyotard, meta. narrative is a theory that tries to
give a totalising comprehensive explaination through
various events & various spheres of life.

(iii) Modernity believes in the authority of science.
Post Modernism does not believe in the authority of any
mode of knowledge.

(iv) Modernity stands for universalism.
whereas Post Modernism believes in diversity & feudality.

(v) Modernity prescribes hierarchy, order & centralism.
Post Modernism prescribes anarchy, dispersal of power,
localisation & Radical democracy.

(vi) Modernity thinks that objectivity is required, subjectivity
~~is~~ is bad.
Post modernism thinks subjectivity has to be respected
& objectivity is not possible.
