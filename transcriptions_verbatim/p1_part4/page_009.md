What Marxists say to this concept of Equality?
It is a procedural Equality. It is the equality for
the name sake (seek)*
Equality According to Marxists, real equality is
social and economic.

Classical Liberal Principle of Justice -
Principle of Justice is Merit. Reward should be in
accordance to the Merit.
What is the concept of Merit? What is the difference
between Merit and talent?
Merit, is a social concept. Different societies gives diff-
erent importance to different talent.

Relationship Between Liberty and Equality?
Liberty here means socio-eco.
According to them If efforts are made to bring
equality in social & Economic terms it will reduce
liberty. Thus equality is anti-thetical to Liberty.
According to them, Absolute equality is injustice.
