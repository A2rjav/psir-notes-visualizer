• __Orthodox Marxism__
They believe that real Marxism is the ideas of Mature
Marx. They give importance to the works like
communist Manifesto. Persons like Agels , Lenin
Projected Karl Marx as a God. and his predictions
are bound to be a real.
Orthodox Marxist consider Marxism as a Science . .
They consider it as a scientific explaination of History.
The most important principle of Marxism is a Histori-
cal Materialism.
