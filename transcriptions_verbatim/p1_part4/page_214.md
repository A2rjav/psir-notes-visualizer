Why Behaviourialism?
Behaviourialism started after 1st world war but in a
big way after 2nd world war. It was promoted
by Americal Political Science Association. There are
intellectual as well as Historical reasons behind the
emergence of Behaviourialism.
Historical Reasons-
The discipline of Political science was in the state of
decline. David Easton suggested decline of political
theory whereas Robert Dahal declared the death of
Political theory. They have blamed Historicists & tradi-
tionalists like Sabine & Dunning for the dealine of poli-
tical theory. They held that these scholars have engaged
themselves studiying old ideas; They are not producing
any theory which can deal with contemporary problems.
Why this realisation?
After 2nd world war academic conferences were called
so that scholars can give suggestions on future recons-
truction & development. In these conferences scholars
belonging to different spheres were invited but poli-
tical scientists were not invited.
Why?
Other disciplines have refined their methodologies,
they have adopted scientific methods They could pro-
duce reliable & varifiable theories.
