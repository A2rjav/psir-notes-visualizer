● Social Liberalism / Liberal Egalitarianism -

of social liberals differ from modern Liberals.
Modern Liberals suggested state intervention in eco-
nomy. State establishing PSOs providing employ-
ment. Social Liberalism is based on the concept
of partnership among pvt. sector, state & civil
society. Pvt. sector should play the responsibility
of promoting growth. State should act as facilita-
tor & empowered civil society ensure trasperency
& accountability. Social liberals suggests state to invt.
in capacity building. some of the prominent social
