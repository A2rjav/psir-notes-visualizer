__How to define Socialism?__
It is difficult to define socialism because socialism
is like a Hat that has lost its shape because
everyone wears it.

__Why everyone wears it?__
It is politically useful specially in those countries
where large section of society is poor & Where
poors have right to vote.

__Why it is said that it has lost its shape?__
Reason- Endless varieties of socialism.
There are country specific models, person specific
models, there are methods which supports peaceful
route to socialism, some supports violent route
to socialism.

Though it is like a Hat which has lost its shape but
still we can recognise the Hat.
