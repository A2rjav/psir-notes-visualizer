__Salient points of Foucault's analysis of power__.-
① Foucault's analysis of power is considered as post stru-
ctural view of power though he never accepted that
he is structuralist or Post structuralist. Instead of
Macro view of power Foucault give micro view of
power. He is not limiting the understanding power to
particular institutions or agents. He disagrees with
Hobbes who gives the sovereign view of power i.e,
power lies with the state. He also disagrees with the
Marxist view of power, according to which those who
control the eco. structure holds the power in the society.
According to these theories power is unidirectional.
According to Foucault power is multi-directional. It goes
in all directions & comes from anywhere. Power is flowing
throughout the society. in the form of capillaries or net-
work like organisations.
② There is a connection b/w knowledge & power. Knowledge
represent the discourse. The dominant discourse shapes
the universe of power. According to him, there is no
knowledge without power. Similarly power cannot be
exercised without knowledge (Discourse).
→ What does it mean to say that there is no knowledge
without power?
Knowledge is a discourse. The discourse which gets the supp-
ort of majority becomes the dominant discourse or the
regime of truth. Every discourse represent the power of
someone. Eg. the discourse of sexuality, criminality,
Madness is determined by the dominant view. To deter-
mine what is normal-abnormal it is the power of
[Margin: psychiatrist] doctor, psychiartist that works. Similarly he also
suggest that power cannot be exercised without kno-
