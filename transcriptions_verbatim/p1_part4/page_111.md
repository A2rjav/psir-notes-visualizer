(7) Max Weber's / Weberian Concept of Power-
Max Weber is a Modernist. Max Weber is a critic of
Karl Marx. When Karl Marx suggests that state represent
force. Max Weber suggest that state represent authority
Like Hobbes Max Weber also suggest that in a particular
territory only state has monopoly over the use of instru-
ments of coersion. which means only state has authority
to use force.
What is Authority?
Authority is legitimate power. or power plus legiti-
macy is Authority. In different societies the nature of
authority may vary. Max Weber has given 3 Ideal
types of Authorities.
What is Ideal type?
Ideal type means models.
(i) Traditional Authority-
In some societies traditions & customs becomes the basis
of authority eg. the authority of queen in Britain is based
on customs & traditions.
(ii) Charismatic Authority -
In some societies Charisma or personality can be the
basis of Authority. Eg. Authority of Gandhi.
Normally Charismatic authority is a feature of traditional
societies or societies in crisis.
(iii) Legal Rational Authority -
The Authority which is the product of legal rational
principles or rules eg. In Modern societies the authority
of public servants can be considered as legal rational.
