__Joshua Cohen__
Joshua Cohen has given 5 elements of Deliberative
Democracy:
(i) It is an independent & ongoing association of people
with expected continuity.
(ii) People accept that Deliberation is the deciding factor
in formulation of laws & policies.
(iii) Non-coersive environment when each person recognise
the capacity of the other to speak.
(iv) There is a respect for pluralism of values.
(v) Deliberation is a source of legitimacy.
→ Cohen has also given following advantages:
(i) Best way to deal with disagreements.
(ii) Best way to secure the legitimacy of collective action.
(iii) Encourage public perspective on public issues.
(iv) Better policies
(v) Public education.
(vi) Developement of trust
(vii) A way to know our genuine preferences.
(viii) Strengthening democracy.
(ix) It enlarge our understanding of issue.
(x) It makes democracy consociational.
(xi) Deliberative demo. is centered on Human dignity. It gives
priority to debate over choices, discussion over voting.

Consociational - acting with consensus.
