⦿ __concept of Power__

Power is the central concept of political science.
However, like all concepts power remains contested
concept. Different scholars have looked at power in a
different way.
● __conventional view of Power-__
In conventional sense power is considered as domination.
According to Robert Dahl, Power is a relational concept.
Power is the ability to get things done by the others
even against their wish. Power is considered as coercive
by nature. N
● __Non-conventional view of Power-__
Hannah Arendt has given non-conventional view of
political power. According to H. Arendt, Political power
does not mean coersion it rather means empower-
ment.
-> Steven Lukes give 3 dimensional view of power:
① Power in formal sense. (Office) (Max Webers view-weberian)
② Power as agenda setting (Marxist view)
③ Power as thought control process. (Gramscian view)
