“Right is prior to Good.”
Above statement is given by Rawls as a criticism of utilitarian
theory of Justice. Utilitarianism has been the dominant
theory of Justice in most of the western societies. including
USA. Rawls has witnessed social protest. During his time he
has witnessed feminist, Black right movements. Hence he is
convinced that there is a need for alternative conception of
Justice. conception of justice should be such which appears
fair to all. only when principle of justice is considered as fair
there will be peace & stability in the society which is necessary
for the pursuit of rational plans.

Within liberalism there are two traditions of ethics. First is
Utilitarian represented by Bentham. Second is Kantian Principle
which is based on the ideal of Human Dignity & Moral Individualism
Rawls supports Kantian Tradition. According to Emanuel kant,
Each person is end in itself. No person ought to be treated as
means to an end. In similar way Rawls also suggest that each
person possess an inviolability founded on the principle of justice
that even the welfare of entire society cannot override. Rawls call
his theory as Deontological. Justice as a fairness has its intrinsic
value. It cannot be compromised for the sake of larger sum of
advantages enjoyed by many. It cannot be compromised to poli-
tical bargaining or calculus of social interest. __It is a settled princi-__
__ple. Being the 1st virtue it cannot be compromised.__

Rawlsian idea is superior to utilitarian idea of justice.

“Each person has an inviolability founded on principles
of Justice.”
