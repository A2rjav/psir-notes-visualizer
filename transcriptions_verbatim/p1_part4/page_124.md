● __Post Modernists Criticism of Ideology -__
For post modernists ideology is also a discourse or Meta-
Narrative. Though totalising ideologies like Marxism
claims to tell the ultimate reality & Marx claimed histo-
rical materialism as science. but from the post modernists
point of view science is also a discourse. Knowledge is
not free from its function of power. If Marxism aims
to establish the power of working classes feminists
discourse ~~e~~ is to establish the power of women. For
Post Modernists objectivity is neither possible nor
desirable.

● __Short Note on Post-colonialism.__
Post colonialism is influenced by post modernism. Post
colonialism questions the hegemony of the west which
is not just limited ~~to~~ in the economic & political sphere
rather which is also prevailant in cultural sphere inclu-
ding academics. Post colonialism challenge 'Euro Centrism'
in Academics. Post colonialists want that the perspective
of the people living in post colonial societies should
also be heard. Post colonialism is influenced by Foucault's
Theory of Knowledge & power. According to Post colonialists
the way the western world described the east is not
free from the phenomena of power. ~~As~~
Edward Said is known as Father of Post colonialism.
Edward Said has given the concept of orientalism.
What is orientalism?
orientalism explains how west has described the east
(or orient ✓). when west describes east the objective
is to establish the hegemony of west on East.
Edward said has given the example ~~to~~ of how western
world has given the interpretation of Islam. It is not
telling the truth about Islam rather constructing the
truth.
