(b) __Historical Approach.__
• __Exponents -__
(i) machiavelli
(ii) Savine
(iii) Laski
(iv) Dunning.
There has always been the perception that politics is
closely related to History. It is held that politics is
present History & History is past politics. Similarly it is
said that History is a root, politics is a fruit.
According to Laski, Every thinker is the child of his times
and no political idea is ever intelligible. Save in the
context of time.
According to machiavelli, History is the best guide to under-
stand politics. He did not want that philosophy should be
the basis to understand politics. The study of History has
~~st~~ dominated Political studies for long. International
relations for long has been studied as 'Diplomatic History'.
According to Savine, Historical Approach serves all the
three conditions of good approach. 1st condition is
factual, 2nd Explainatory, 3^rd Evaluative.
(what happed, what should happen, what would have happ-
ened).
though Historical Approach has been popular because of its
simplicity there are some limitations of Historical Appro-
ach.
(i) History is Extremely vast all that has happened in history
may not be useful. It becomes a challenging task to iden-
tify relevant data.
(ii) there are many important political ideas which have
never existed in History yet important for the student
of political science like theory of philosopher King or
idea of communism.
