(d) __Emperical Approach.__
__Compare Emperical Approach with Normative.__
Emperical Approach is as old as Normative Approach.
Normative Approach cannot be completely indepen-
dent of Emperical Approach. Eg. Plato has given the
theory of philosophical king only after observation
of situations in Athens.
Aristotle has applied Emperical Approach in his
classification of constitution & theory of revolution.
Machiavelli also recommends emperical observation
along with Historical Approach.
Emperical Approach focus on the study of facts by
the method of observation. According to John Locke,
mind is tabularasa (clear slate/board). It means
nothing is imprairted on our mind and soul.
According to Locke, observation and experience is the
source of knowledge. In order to understand chara-
cteristics. of emperical approach we can compare
Emperical Approach with Normative Approach.
