• Legitimacy
Q Describe the meaning of legitimacy, what are the methods
employed by the state to gain legitimacy. What is a
legitimation crisis.
—> What is legitimacy?
Legitimacy reflect the consent of the governed. That
exercise of power which is considered right by the
people will be considered as Legitimate.
Difference in legitimacy & legality
Legality means as per law. Law can be the basis of
authority but untill & unless there is a consent of the
people law cannot be enforced.
__Importance of legitimacy -__
According to Roussou even the strongest man is never
strong enough untill he converts his strength into right &
his power into authority.
Hence, legitimacy is a pre-condition for Governibility.
__Debate on legitimacy__
There is a debate on the legitimacy of the state bet-
ween Marxist and Liberals. Liberals assert that State
represent the use of legitimate force. On the other
hand Marxist challenge the legitimacy of the state
Marxist call state as a class instrument. Marxist call
for revolution against the state. According to Liberals
the fact that the state is continuing is an indicator that
state represent the will of the people.
