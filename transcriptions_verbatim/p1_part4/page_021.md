__Hayek on Social Justice__
"Social Justice is Mirage" - Hayek
- It is unachievable. Justice is a virtue of a person &
  not of a state. In the name of Justice state
  increases its power. State takes money from the
  productive sector, money does not reach to targeted
  section. It give rise to parallel economy as it is pocketed
  by politicians & civil servants.
- According to Hayek progressive taxation is a bonded
  labour.
- Instead of state providing justice, state should promo
  te charity.
- If a person is poor, the fault does not lie with
  the rich. Poverty is a bad luck. Hence, for removal of
  poverty coersion of rich is not justifiable.
- Hayek does not accept concept of +ve freedom/higher
  freedom. According to him freedom in simple term is
  Absence of Coersion.
