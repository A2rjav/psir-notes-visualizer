FASCISM

Q. Critically analyse the nature of Fascism as a political
ideology.
Q. Analyse the factors which gives rise to Fascism.
Q. Do you think Fascism has ended with the end of
2nd world war? Should we always be concerned about
possibility of rise of Fascism.

Fascism is not regarded as political ideology. ~~It is regard-~~
~~-ed as Propaganda.~~ It is true that none of the ideology
tells truth. However, there is no truth at all in Fascism.
Fascism is considered as Hodge-Podge of ideas. Fascism
as an ideology came after fascist states got established.
These states appointed scholars to write philosophy of
Fascism. Eg. Moussolini appointed Gentile Geovanni to write
the philosophy of Fascism. Moussolini himself wrote
the philosophy of Fascism. Fascism is not a systematic
school of thought. Fascist scholars have taken ideas from
different philosophers to justify their actions.
