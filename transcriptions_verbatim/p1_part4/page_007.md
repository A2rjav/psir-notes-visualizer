• Classical Liberalism-

Every ideology has its social base.
classical liberalism is considered as the ideology of
emerging Bourgeoise class. At that time middle class
liberals defended the Rights of Man against the
state. They claimed Liberty of Man from the Autho-
rity.
central value of Liberalism
central value of liberalism is liberty.
Liberal (classical) view of Man.
Man is Individualistic by Nature.
Man is Rational. Rational Man in liberalism is the
Man who knows how to protect his self interest.
They believe man is utilitarian by Nature.
They believe man is materialistic by Nature.
Their view on society -
Society is a creation of Man. society is not organic in
Nature. Liberal view of society is called contractual view.
According to Liberals there is nothing called collective
Interest. Society is aggregate of Individuals.
Classical liberal view of society is also known as Market
Model of society. Society is market place where man goes
for satisfaction of his interests.
