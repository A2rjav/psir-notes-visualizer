MAO

- Mao was the first person to apply
  communist principles in purely Asiatic
  situations & in purely fuedal society.

- Maoism is a pragmatic & flexible form of communism.
  Maoism is not against capitalism. Maoism is also not
  against Nationalism. Maoism is against -
  ① Fuedalism
  ② Imperialism

- Mao made some fundamental changes in the theory of
  communism. Mao accepted all principles of Marxism like
  Historical materialism, Dialectics, Class struggle, Revolution
  but tried to modify Marxism to suit Chinese situation.
  China was international colony, colonial powers have esta-
  blished their influence in different zones. China was also
  fuedal society with long history of peasants revolts.
  Mao himself came from peasant family. Marx does not
  consider peasantry as the revolutionary class, Lenin
  brought peasantry in the revolutionary class, but in
  subordinate position to working classes. Mao has given
  central position to the ~~working classes in~~ Revolution.

● __Dialectics__
  Antagonastic ~~dialetics~~ dialectics means reconciliation is
  not possible. According to Mao, people have antago-
  nistic contradictions with fuedal class and foreign Bour-
  geoise, Imperialists.

  Non Antagonistic means contradiction which can be
  resolved Eg. in case of china Mao held no contradiction
