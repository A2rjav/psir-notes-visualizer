[Margin: ③] __3^rd Wave -__
The 3^rd wave of Feminism has started from 1990s.
1990s saw the emergence of post Feminism. The
prominent scholar of post Feminism is Susane
Faludi. She writes the back lash       against feminism.
Post feminists do not support the radical Feminists
arguement that women are subordinate sex. They
suggest that women should celebrate womenhood.
They do not support the Feminine Myths concept.
They rather emphasise that the qualities of women
are superior than men & they need to be celebrated.
Today feminism has been internally differentiated. There
are many schools of Feminism. Eg. Black Feminism.

[Margin: ⓘ] __Black Feminism__
__Scholar - Bell Hooks -__
According to her the problems of Black women
differ from white women.

[Margin: ⓘⓘ] __Post Colonial Feminism__
__Scholar - Chandra Talpade Mohanty__
According to Post Colonial Feminists the problems of
women in post colonial societies differ from the
western world. They are under dual burden. 1^st of
Patriarchy & 2^nd of colonialism & neo-colonialism.

[Margin: ⓘⓘⓘ] __Eco Feminism__
__Scholar - Vandana Shiva__
According to Eco Feminists men have exploited both
women as well as nature.
The nature of women is closer to nature.
Women are the worst sufferers of environmental
degradation.
since the life of women is very close to nature, there
cannot be any sustainable developement without
giving centrality to women.
