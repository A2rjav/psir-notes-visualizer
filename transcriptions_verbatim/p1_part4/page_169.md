● __How Rawls look at Justice?__
(i) Justice as the 1st virtue of social institution.
Which social institutions?
Political Institutions, Economic & other social Institutions
Which principle of Justice?
Justice as fairness.
__Note__- Communitarians like Michael Walzer in his book 'Sphere
of Justice' suggest that for different spheres there should be
different principles of Justice.
Rawls want to give an absolutely rational idea of Justice
which can be accepted by all if they look at these principles
from being purely rational point of view.
Communitarians suggest that it is not possible for the
people belonging to different culture to agree the one idea
of Justice.
Rawls think that just institutions can create just societies.
