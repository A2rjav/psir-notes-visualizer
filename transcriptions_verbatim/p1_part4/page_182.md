• __Sandal's Theory Of Justice :-__
  __Book - 'Liberalism & Limits of Justice'__
  Sandal has criticised rowl's theory of justice on two major
  grounds. (i) Rawls conception of self.
            (ii) Rawls conception of society.
(i) __Rawls conception of self.__
  According to Sandal, man is embedded self. Man is never
  in original position. Original position is an abstract idea.
  People are not abstract individuals making choices, they
  are moral agents (members of society) located in time &
  space, embedded in network of relations. For Rawls self
  is prior to end. But according to sandal self is constitu-
  ted by end. People take decisions in real life on the basis
  of what they consider as right. However, it is never a free
  choice. Hence, the concept of justice can be understood only
  within the framework of community & not independent of it.

(ii) __Rawls conception of society.__
  Rawls should have taken much fuller view of community.
  concept of Rights cannot be detached from communities
  culture & tradition. It is not possible to adopt value neutra-
  lity. It is not understandable as to why we should not affirm
  our identities in public sphere.
