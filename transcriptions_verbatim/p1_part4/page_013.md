"Human consciousness postulates liberty.
 Liberty involves rights.
 Rights demand state."
                                - T.H. Green.

In above statements T.H.Green have sumed up entire
philosophy of positive liberalism.
In the 1st statement  T. H. Green like all liberals show
the importance of liberty. It is natural for human
beings to demand liberty. without liberty human
existence will be mere animal existence. Liberty is
imp. for the developement of a personality of a
person.
In the 2nd statement he shows the importance of
society. Man can enjoy liberty only when society
acknowledges his liberty as his right. For T.H.Green
rights have moral orgin.
In the 3rd statement we see positive liberals view on
state. State is not originator of a right. State is a
guarantor of a right. Like all liberals state remains
an instrument for the welfare of man. He is not
compromising individual for the sake of the state.
If classical liberals sacrifies equality & dignity for the
sake of liberty & utility, if socialists sacrifies liberty
for the sake of equality & if idealists sacrifice man
for the sake of the state positive liberals try to
achieve equilibrium among competing values & conte-
sted claims of individual, state & society.
