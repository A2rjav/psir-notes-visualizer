● __Existentiatioism & Satre__ -
Existentialism has its roots in France.
The concept of Freedom goes beyond the concept of
Freedom given by the liberals. In his famous book
'Being and Nothingness' Satre mentions that Existen-
ce is Prior to Essense. Existence means Person
in its original sense whereas the Essense is the
identity which others give. According to satre, Existen-
ce has a priority over essense. Person should have
freedom to determine his own identity. According to
Satre, the tradition of Essentialism has its roots in
religions. All religions are Teleological. Religions su-
ggest that Man is created by God. God wants man
to fullfill certain Goals & if these goals are not full-
filled God punishes man. It appears as if Man is
Robot, remotely controlled by God. It treats man
as an object rather than subject with consciousness
on its own. Satre held that belief in God is a bad
faith & belief in oneself is Good faith.

gender & which is a matter of disadvantage to the
other gender.
