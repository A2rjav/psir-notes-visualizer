because welfare state is based on 2 contradictory
principles:
ⓘ Socialism in politics
ⓘ Capitalism in Economics.

To avoid revolution states in west extended right to
vote to poors. In order to get votes from the poors
political parties have to make promises to
address poverty. To full fill promises they take
money out of economy. The graph of expectations
keep on rising. There is a limit to which economy
can finance social security programs. ~~By~~ Beyond a
limit industry cannot finance. Hence, the only option
left is rolling back of the state. When a state rolls
back social security it is bound to suffer from the
crisis of legitimacy. Even welfare state is not the
solution for the contradictions in capitalism.
