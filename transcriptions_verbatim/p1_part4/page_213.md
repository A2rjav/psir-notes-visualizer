(i) Normative Approach means study of values (Liberty,
Justice, Equality).
Imperical Approach means study of observable facts
(ii) Normative Approach ~~means~~ is prescriptive whereas
Imperical Approach is Discriptive
(iii) Normative Approach deals with what ought to be
whereas Imperical Approach deals with what is.
(iv) Normative Approach is ~~future oriented~~ (change oriented)
whereas Imperical Approach is status quoist (static)
(v) The criteria of Normative Approach is right & wrong
whereas the criteria of imperical approach is true
or false.

Imperical Approach has led to the evolution of scientific
approach. Though imperical Approach in its is not scientific
Approach. For any theory to be qualified as scientific
theory it has to pass through the rigourous methodo-
logy adopted by science.

__Modern Approach/scientific Approach/ Behavioural__
What is behavioural approach?
Movement in political science to introduce scientific metho
dology is known as Behavioural movement. However,
when methodology changes method as well as scope
of discipline also change. Hence Behaviourilism even
changes basic assumptions about Human nature & politics.
It is for this reason Behaviourialism is also known as
revolution.
