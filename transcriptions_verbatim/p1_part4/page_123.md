● __Prominent Thinkers of Post Modernism-__
① __Foucault__
Foucault's concept of Discourse

② __Lyotard__
He is first person to explicitely mention the term post
Modernity in his book 'The Post Modern condition'.
He described post Modernism as incredulity towards
Meta-narratives. Lyotard supports local narratives.
According to him, we are living in an age where meta-
narratives have lost their power to convince. He suggest
to focus on diversity, local contexts & multiple points of
views.
③ __Derrida__
Derrida has given the concept of De-construction.
De-construction can be considered as an approach to
understand meanings. According to Derrida, a particular
text may have multiple meanings. Human language is not
so developed that all what a person wants to communi-
cate, he can communicate. It is also not possible that
the other person recieves exactly what is being commu-
nicated. Hence there can be multiple interpretations of the
text. He suggests double reading.
