• __Betty Friedman__
__Book- Feminine Mystique__
She has also exploded various myths associated with
women. She has interviewed white women belong-
ing to well of family in USA. She wanted to know
whether they were satisfied with their life. Most of
the women responded that they were not satisfied
with their life & they feel that something is missing.
However they were not able to determine the root
cause of problem. Betty Friedan suggest the pro-
blem as problem of no name. She define that the
problem is Patriarchy. According to her, it is not
enough that women have right to vote it is also not
enough that they enjoy social & economic rights. So
long patriarchy continues it is not possible that wo-
men will be able to gain true satisfaction from their
lifes. Betty Friedan also blames society, media for
developing myths relating to women. She has critici-
sed women magazines potray career women leading
unsatisfied life & housewife leading a happy life.
She also suggested that the myth is propagative
that a women should marry at a particular age after
age they loose their attraction.
This forces women to leave their careers & higher
studies which re-enforce the subordinate status of
women.
