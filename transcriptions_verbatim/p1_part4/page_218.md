__Nature of Post Behaviouralism.__
Is post Behaviouralism nearer to traditional Approach
or Behavioural Approach.
Post Behaviouralism is not closer to traditionalism.
It is not a 'counter revolution' against behaviouralism.
It shares basic assumptions with behaviouralism. It
carries behaviouralism forward. If traditionalism is
thesis, Behaviouralism is Anti-thesis & Post-Behavi-
ouralism is synthesis.

__Intellectual Influences on Post Behaviouralism.__
(i) Karl Popper has given the concept of Falsification.
What is Falsification?
Falsification is the criteria of scientific theory in social
sciences. In social sciences it is not possible to produce
such theories which can be verified through experiment
in laboratory. In social sciences it is enough if theory is
open for critical investigation. From the perspective of
Falsification, Marxism is determinism.

(ii) Thomas koon suggested that there is no finality in
knowledge even scientists works with conceptual
framework or paradigm. He also suggested that
the importance of theory is not in how precise it is
but how useful it is in management of crisis or
problem solving.
