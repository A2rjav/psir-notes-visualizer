(i) __Theory of legal Rights-__
Utilitarian Theory.
__Scholar- Bentham.__
According to Bentham, Rights are properly the creations
of law. According to him, Natural Rights are non sense,
& nonsense upon stilts.
According to him, Natural rights is a terrorist language &
Natural rights results into Anarchy.
Bentham supports Hobbesian Tradition. According to
Hobbes man can not enjoy rights in the state of Nature.
In the state of Nature might is Right. ony when state
comes into existence, man can enjoy rights. Hence, the
real source of rights is state. Man has ~~no~~ liberty only
where law is silent. Similarly, Bentham in his book
'Anarchical fallacies' criticised the theory of natural rights.
Asserting rights against the state will result into chaos
in the society. For Bentham the real source of rights is
state. Rights against a state, right against the wishes
of state carry no meaning.
(ii) __Conservative Theory__
__Scholar- Edmond Burke__
According to Edmond Burke, Natural rights are meta-
physical abstractions. It means there is no existence
of Natural Rights. According to conservatives the real
source of rights is society's customs & traditons.
No one can enjoy rights untill & unless they are not
according to the customs & traditions.
