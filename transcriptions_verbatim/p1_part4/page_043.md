non western societies there is greater importance
to collectivity.

middle east where many political parties like
Baathiest party in Iraq & Syria.

Neo-liberalism. Marx was proclaimed as a God
that has failed.

of acceptance as it was before 1990s. Not any country
will go entirely for socialist politics or socialist eco-
nomy. However, it is believed that socialism should
not be considered as Dustbin of History.
