__Difference in End of Ideology & End of History__

End of History is the revival of End of Ideology theisis.
Both of these concepts aim to justify the liberal way of
life. However End of History is more assertive in
claiming the superiority of liberalism rather than in com
parison to end of ideology.
- According to End of Ideology thesis Ideological struggle
carry no meaning. But according to End of History libera-
lism is end ^point of man's ideological evolution.

● __Post Modernist Critic of Ideology__
● __What is Post Modernism?__
Post Modernism can be understood as-
(i) Movement.
(ii) A school of Thought

-> As a Movement post modernism is against the negative
consequences of Modernity.
-> As a school of thought post modernism challenge some
basic assumptions of Modernity.

Post Modernism first emerged in the field of Art,
literature & later on in other disciplines. Nietzsche
is known as Father of post Modernism.
Why?
(i) Nietzsche has challenged enlightenment & claimed
that enlightenment has made man free.
