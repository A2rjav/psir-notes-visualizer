__Instrumentalist Perspective -__
• "State is an Executive committee of the Bourgeoise class"
- Karl Marx
→ ① __Introduction__
Nature of State From Marxist Perspective
② __Context__
Marx does not subscribe to the Liberal view that state
is a neutral.
on the contrary state is the executive committee of Bour-
geoise class.
③ __Meaning of Executive committee__
Executive committee has no autonomy to make decisions.
It is simply to implement the agenda of Bourgeoise class.
④ __The basis of the arguement__
Marx theory of Historical materialism.
⑤ __conclusion__
Though state is the core concept of Political science yet
all concepts in politics are contested concepts. The nature
of state remains a matter of debate. For idealists, state
is a source of virtue, for classical liberals, state is a
necessary evil, for anarchist state is a unnecessary evil,
for feminists, state is a institution of Patriarchy.
There is an internal debate between Marxism itself.
For instrumentalists state is a instrument but for other school
of thought state does enjoy relative autonomy.

• "State represent irreconcilability of class Antagonisms."
- Lenin
→ ① __Introduction__
(i) This is Marxist view of the state.
(ii) Lenin is orthodox marxist & according to Lenin Historical
materialism class struggle revolution are fundamental
principle of Marxism.
② __what Lenin says__
like Karl Marx, Lenin also believes that state is not neutral
rather state is class institution for the instrument of
the dominant class.
