Any attempt by the state to bring equality results
into the reduction of liberty. Hence, classical liberals/
& libertarians wants to limit the state to the role of
Night watchman.
Marxist scholars believe that substantive equality ie.
Social & Economic Equality is a pre-condition for
freedom. They define freedom as Freedom from necessi-
ties. Social liberals like Rawls consider Liberty has a
priority over equality. He prescribes a lexical order in
his principles of Justice. He makes liberty as a conditio
to adress a concerns of the least advantaged section
of the society. Amartya Sen suggest the inter-dependenc
between empowerment & developement. According to
A.sen Empowerment of people results into the deve-
lopement. Thus political liberty ie. Democracy can be
the means for developement. Even the constitution of
India recognises relationship b/w political & Social-
Economic Democracy. In Part III we have enforceable
Fundamental Rights & in part IV social & eco   rights
Though rights in part IV are not automatically enforcea
ble yet they are soppose to be fundamental in the
governance of the country. The members of constituent
assembly recognised the value of liberty & hence the
rights have to be given automatic enforcement.
According to, Greenville Austin, Political Revolution was
as means to achieve social & economic revolution.

__Note-__ According to A.sen, when people are empowered
they make demand for public action.
