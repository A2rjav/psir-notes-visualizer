• MacPherson's Theory of Democracy-
MacPherson has studied all philosophical & emperical
models. If empirical models overlook the normative
aspect of Democracy, classical model overlooks the
emperical dimensions. MacPherson has combined Empe-
rical & Normative Dimensions.

→ __Salient features of MacPherson's Theory__
① Liberalism cannot claim monopoly over democracy.
② There can be other models of Democracy
③ There can be socialist model of democracy subject
   to the condition, single party has intra-party demo-
   cracy.
④ He even includes one party dominant model as found
   in many Third World countries. Subject to the condition
   that party enjoys majority support.
⑤ According to MacPherson there are many weaknesses
   in the model of western democracies. Western demo-
   cracies in their present form are not ideal. They are
   not egalitarian.
⑥ According to MacPherson, Democracy should result
   into the empowerment. There are two dimensions
   of power.
   ⓐ __Extractive Power__ - It is coersive in nature.
      Forcing someone to do which it he could not
      otherwise do.
   ⓑ __Developemental Power__ - It means developement
      of capacities.

Egalitarian- believing in the principle that all people are equal &
             deserve equal rights & opportunities.
