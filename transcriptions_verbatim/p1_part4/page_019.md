● Neo Liberalism (1970's to 1990's) -

> What is Neo liberalism?
In simplest term Neo liberalism is market funda-
mentalism.
Neo liberalism in one way the revival of laissez
faire state. But it is more inhuman & oriented
towards market than classical Liberals.
classical liberals suggested ~~for~~ Market as a principle
in economics. But Neo- Liberals have expanded the
Market Principles to 'all aspects of life. They proje-
cted Market as God.
Neo- Liberalism is least ~~de~~liberal and most funda-
mentalism as fards market is concerned.
