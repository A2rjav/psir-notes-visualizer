Political Science started as a part of Political
philosophy. Political science has been closer to
Political philosophy. Traditional political theories deal
with Normative issues like Rights, Liberty, Equality,
Justice.
Traditional Theories came under the crisis because
Traditional theories were normative and Arm chair
theories. Scholars like Lord Bryce called for Emperi-
cal studies. He called for facts, facts and facts.
Behavioural Movement aimed at studying facts and at
producing such theories which are value neutral to
make political science pure science.
Post Behaviouralism ended fact value dichotomy.
It held that there is no need to make Political science
a pure science. Political science is a policy science or
applied science. They also suggested to incorporate values
and not to treat techniques as an end in itself.
Behavioural / Post Behavioural scholars like Robert Dahal,
held that, Fact and values are not completely Dichoto-
mous / seperate. In fact there is a continuity between
them. Facts become values & values becomes facts.
