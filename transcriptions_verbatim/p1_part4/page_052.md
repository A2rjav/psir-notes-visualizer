[Margin: ②] __Structural School / Structurialism -__
Marx was structurialist. According to Marx, Economic
Structure is the basic structure. Marx is criticised as
Economic determinist. Because according to Marx,
Economic factor is the only factor behind all events.
Gramsci has further modified structurialism and has
also given importance to Superstructure.
Althusser have further developed structurialism.
suggest that Economic factor is over determined in Marx.
He gives the concept of Multiple structures. History can
start from any structure. He gives the example of Russian
Revolution which had not started from economic structure
rather political structure.
