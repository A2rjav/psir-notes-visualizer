④ __Syndicalism__
It is a French variety of Socialism.
Syndicalism supports violent. It is nearest to the
ideas of Karl Marx. Besides Karl Marx they are also
influenced by George Sorel the author of Reflections
on violence.
Techniques proposed- They propose strike , lockouts,
Slow work.
According to them there is no need of political state
only economic state is enough.
__Difference in Guild & Syndicate__
- Guilds represent different economic groups like farmers
  traders, consumers, workers.
- Syndicates are just working class organisations.
Syndicate society will be confederation of syndicates.
Entire power will be in the hands of workers.

(ii) Popularity of Socialism.
Since the victory of communism in Russia till the end of
cold war socialism was the major ideological challenge to
liberalism. World was devided into two blocks:
ⓐ First World - The capitalist world
ⓑ Second World- Socialist- Communist world.
Besides 1st & 2nd world there was group of nations
with colonial past & aiming to pursue the policy of
Non-alignment which came to be known as 3rd world.
There was bigger influence of socialism than capitalism.
