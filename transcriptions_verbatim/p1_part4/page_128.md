* __Globalisation and Democracy__
Globalisation has increased the no. of demo-
cracies. However, it does not mean that Globalisation is
Democratic. No. of Democracies have increased but
world order has not become Democratic.

Joseph Stiglitz has pointed towards the 'Democratic
Deficits' of the institutions of Global governance.
According to him, to make Globalisation work we
have to address the democratic deficit.
David Held has given COSMOPOLITAN model of
Democracy. [Margin: विश्वनागरिक]

-> __Context of the Theory__-
[Margin: बदलवून टाकणे]
Globalisation has undermined the authority of nation
state. So far the govt. in the nation state is the
highest level of Democratically elected govt. with
the exception of EU which has established democra-
tically elected European Parliament, no other region
has such institution. Hence, the policies are taken by
the Bureaucracy & people do not have say in the
formulation of policies.

-> David Held has proposed ~~a~~ cosmopolitan model.
we have to have democracy at the Global level.
He has proposed long term and short term measures.

-> In the long term we have to think about Global govt.
and Global Parliament.

-> In short term we need to go for following reforms-
(1) Reform of UN Security council to make it more
Democratic.
