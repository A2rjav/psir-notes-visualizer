> __Basic assumptions of positive Liberalism.__
  Core value - Liberty.
  However they have redefined the concept of Liberty.
  Negative liberty became positive. It was held that
  when state intervenes it does not reduce Liberty
  rather inhance liberty. Through Liberty is capacity.
  [Diagram: arrow pointing to "Through" labelled "+ve liberals"]
> __Their concept of Man/Human Nature:-__
  Progressive view of man/ developemental view of man
  For them Man is moral & ethical.
> __Their concept of society__
  Society is important, real & cannot be considered just
  an aggregate of Individuals.
> __Their concept of state__
  State is an Institution of virtue. State is necessary
  for good life.
> __Their concept of Justice__
  They believe that liberty can be balance with equality.
  To bring the balance there is a need for affirmative
  action or positive discrimination in favour of weak to
  establish level playing field in the society.
  Positive liberals takes inspiration from Imanuel kant.
  I. Kant has given the concept of Human Dignity.
  Positive liberals also give importance to dignity along
  with liberty.
