__Models of Democracy__

① __Representative Democracy__ -
Original form of Democracy is 'Direct Democracy.
With the growth of population & the rise of modern
complex societies representative democracy becomes
Inevitable.
J.S. Mill has considered representative democracy
as the 2nd best form of govt. However, he was
concerned about representative democracy beco-
ming tyranny of majority. Hence he proposed various
reforms. One of the challenge of representative demo-
cracy is how to ensure the representativeness.
Hence, scholars have suggested models of representa-
tion & improvements in electoral systems. There are
two major models of representation.

ⓐ __Deligate Model__ -
__Scholar__ - Bentham, John Locke.
According to them, representative should not have
freedom to take independent actions. They should
stick to the mandate given by voters.

ⓑ __Enlightened Model__ -
-> __Scholar__ - J.S. Mill
Representatives should have autonomy because they
have more experience of politics than ordinary
persons
-> ~~Ed~~ Edmond Burke
Representatives do not represent narrow constitue-
ncy. Parliament is not a place for advancement
of local interest. It is an assembly on nation &
National perspective should shape the choices.
