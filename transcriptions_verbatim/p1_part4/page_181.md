(iii) __Concept of Justice.__
According to Liberals there can be a universal principle
of justice. Justice is a product of reason in man. Liberal
concept of Justice is based on the concept of Atomistic
man. If Liberals take universalistic view of justice, commu-
nitari~~ans~~ take particularistic view of justice. The sense
of justice will vary from society to society. The idea of
justice cannot be independent of time space & culture.

(iv) __Concept of State__
According to Liberals state should adopt value neutrality.
State should be indifferent towards different ways of life.
There should be seperation between personal & political.
Liberals propose universal citizenship. Liberals relive on
the idea of Nationalism to achieve solidarity.
According to communitarians, state ~~st~~ need not be neutral or
indifferent. State should promote such values which stren-
gthens social solidarity. State should not expect that person
will become so rational to forget his communities, customs &
traditions. State should adopt cultural relativist perspective
State should not attempt to achieve homogenisation for
social solidarity. Justice is not universalising. Justice is an
art of differentiation. According to them, there is no need to
keep religion out of public sphere because that is not
possible. It is better if we recognise it & give space in
public policies. Communitarians supports consociational
democracy rather than Majoritarian democracy.
