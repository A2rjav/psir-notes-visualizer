(i) __Textual Approach.__
    It is a literal interpretation of text. Since it is literal
    interpretation meaning does not change.
(ii) __Contextual Approach.__
    It is interpreting text according to the context.
    It includes taking into consideration the context
    of the author and the context of the leader. Since it is
    contextual meanings keep on changing.

According to Contextualists, we cannot understand the
meaning of text without understanding the text.
Eg. We cannot understand why Hobbes justify absolute
authority of the state without understanding the situa-
tions of his time.
According to Textual Approach if we want to understand
the text there is no need to understand the context
we read ~~so text~~ again & again we can grasp the
meaning.
According to Contextualists no amount of readings will
be sufficient to understand the text.
According to contextualists what is understood as the
Ultimate explaination is also a interpretation.

According to the supporters of Textual Approach there is
no point studying texts if meanings are contextual.

Gadamar suggests that we live in effective History. and
there should be fusion of Horizons.

Tully suggest that it is necessary to understand the right
context. Eg. The right context of Locke can help us to under-
stand that his advocacy of Right to property was for the
protection of religious dissenters.
