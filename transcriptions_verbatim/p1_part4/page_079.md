Q. Analysise the impact of Globalisation on sovereignty
   Of the state.
-> __Define Globalisation__
Globalisation is understood as borderless world. Globali-
sation means world becoming Global village.
Globalisation started in a more prominent way since
the disintegration of USSR or since the end of cold war
-> __What is sovereignty?__
Sovereignty is the essential element of the modern natio
state. Modern Nation state is a territorial entity. Modern natio
state emerged out of the Roman Empire. In case of Modern
Nation state Nation became the basis of statehood. Treaty
of westphalia of 1648 gives 1st formal recognition to the
idea of Nation state. As described in the theories of scholars
like Hobbes on a set of people living in a particular terri-
tory, authority of the state shall be supreme. Sovereignty of stat
has two dimensions ie. Internal & External.
Since 1990s we are witnessing the proliferation of Global
conventions, regional, international supra-national institu-
tions. There are Global challenges. It is beyond the capacity
of any single nation state to deal with these challenges on its
own. Hence Nations are expected to act in concert with each
other. Globalisation expects the state to go for more respon
sible exercise of sovereignty. Sovereignty is always a diluted
concept. Globalisation has diluted it further. However, the
impact is not been same for all countries. The sovereignty o
powerful countries is less compromised that the weaker coun-
tries. Regarding the impact of Globalisation on state & sovereign
there is a difference of opinions among scholars
