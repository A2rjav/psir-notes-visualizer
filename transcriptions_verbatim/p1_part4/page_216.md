Post Behaviourialism -
Time- 1960s
Why Post Behaviourialism?
Political science was once again in the state of decline.
This was the time when there were many protests
taking place in USA. However, works of political scholars
had no relevance in addressing these issues.
Why?
Political scholars neglected normative issues. They
dealt with only such issues where it was possible to
employ scientific technique. Hence, Political works were
primarily in the field of electoral behaviour. Political
Scholars compromise the scope & relevance of the subject.

David Easton who has given 8 intellectual foundation
Stones has given another lecture at American Political
Science Association introducing post Behaviourialism.
The title of lecture was 'Credo of Relevance. He cri-
ticised political scholars seating in ivory towers, perfe-
cting their techniques but compromising the subject
matter & the relevance. He called for creative theory.
What is creative Theory?
It is guided by -
(i) action
(ii) relevance.
