→ Nancy Fraser
Nancy Fraser Suggest that marginalised sections of
Society should form their own public sphere, for which
she has coined the term counter public.
→ Jacques Ranciere -
He reconceptualises the concept of Political to include
those who have been at the margins. Participation is
the only way to end domination & exploitation.
Other Radical scholars include Judith Butler, Chantal
Mouffle, Ernst Laclau.
→ According to Amarty Sen, Participatory Democracy,
requires capacity building & conducive environment.
→ In case of India, we can say that 73rd C.A. Act
failed to achieve its objective because govt. has not taken
enough steps for capacity buiding of people.
73rd Amendment Act provides for Reservation for
weaker section. Reservation alone does not ensure
participation.
