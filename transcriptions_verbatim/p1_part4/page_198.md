③ Social Liberals / positive liberals.
Social liberals like T.H. Green, Laski view Liberty &
Equality as complementary. T.H. Green takes the view
of positive Liberty in terms of capacity.
T.H. Green and Laski support intervention of state for
the regulation of Market. Laski justifies social &
economic rights & welfare state.
In contemporary times scholars like Rawls has incorpo-
rated the ② principles of Liberty & Equality in his
theory of Justice as Fairness. Amartya Sen also defi-
nes developement in terms of Freedom & freedom
in terms of expansion of capabilities.
Thus the nature of relationship remains a matter of
debate among the prominent schools.
Indian constitution reflects the approach of positive
liberals.
To conclude we can mention value pluralism given by
Issaih Berlin who held that there can be multiple
values, values are incommensurable.
