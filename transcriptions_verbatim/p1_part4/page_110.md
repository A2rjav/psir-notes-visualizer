$\rightarrow$ Foucault has mentioned ~~free~~ ^3 mechanisms
of social control-
(i) __Hierarchical observation__
It means observing people.
(ii) __Normalising Judgement__
It means punishment of non-conformity
(iii) __Examination__
It means testing persons quality & ability w.r.t. the
societies social norms & values.
__Gaze__
Person keeps watch on himself.
