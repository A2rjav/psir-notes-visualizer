● What is Justice?
According to Rawls, Justice is fairness. Justice means
fair to all those who have been advantageous & those are
in disadvantage. The Theories of Justice are based on
the following principle..
(i) Desert - means what one deserves on the basis of
          his quality
(ii) merit - from the perspective of society ie what
          society values.
(iii) Need -
Rawls combine all the 3 principles in his Theory of Justice
● Context of Theory.
Criticism of Utilitarian theory of Justice.
“ Each person Possess inviolability founded on the
Principles of Justice that even the welfare of whole
society cannot overright the loss of Freedom of some
for the greater good of many.” -Rawls.
Rawls believe that the Dignity of every person has to
be respected. Like Imanuel Kant who held that no one
Ought to be the means for the other person. Rawls also
Suggest that the dignity of a person cannot be compro-
mised for the sake of greatest happiness of greatest
number.
