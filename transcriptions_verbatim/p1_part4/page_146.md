__Amartya Sen__
Amartya sen supports the idea of 'public reasoning.'
without public reasoning sound policies cannot emerge.
Public reasoning suggests public debate. In his book
'uncertain glory' he suggest that without Public debate,
we cannot push governments to take actions. According
to him the reason for the neglect of public Health is
that Heath has been missing from public debate.

Public Reasoning is a mode of deliberation. It is a way
of justifying particular position by use of reasoning.
Public reasoning provides set of arguements which others
can accept. Public reasoning is a condition for well order-
ed society. According to Amartya Sen, following conditions
are required:
(i) The Idea of Public Reasoning must exist.
(ii) Public sphere should exist.
(iii) People should have capacity.
