• Features of communist party -
① communist party is different from trade onions.
communist party to be a secret society it has to
work secretely.
why ?
Its programme is to bring revolution so no state
will allow.
Trade unions are part of capitalist system.
Trade unions can develope only, trade union consciousness
and not revolutionary consciousness.
② communist party will differ from Bourgeoise parties in
their organisation. Bourgeoise Parties are Hierarchical .
Theire power is concentrated in the hands of leadership.
③ Communist Party will be based on the principles of
Democratic centralism. communist party is a pyramidical
organisation broad at the base & narrow at the top.
The smallest unit is called as cell. It represents the people
at the grass root level. People at grass root level will
decide the main programme of the party. It will be
④ communicated to the top leadership. At the top will be
the central committee. Central committee will decide
peoples demand , in practicle programme of action. Once
decided programme has to be implemented at all levels.
In this sense there will be centralisation.
