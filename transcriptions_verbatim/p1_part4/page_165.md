• __Rawls Methodology-__
Rawls revives the tradition of social contract. Social contract
will ensure that the Principles of Justice are the outcomes
of rational dialogue or deliberation rather than imposed
from outside. Social contract will also result into the emerge-
nce of consensus on the foundational principles govern-
ing society.
• __concept of Original Position-__
Original Position is like the concept of state of Nature in
classical social contract ~~theories~~. It is a situation before
contract.
→ __Features of the original Position-__
① Persons have come together to enter into the contract.
what type of persons?
Rawls does not look at persons as Hobbesian Individuals
He rather suggests moral individuals. in kantian sense.
Persons have inherent sense of right & wrong. They are
the persons who understand that cooperation is benefi-
cial.
why? They are rational, they have certain goals in their
lifes. which they cannot pursue if there is no peace in
the society. In the words of J. Rawls every person has a
rational plan in his life.
② Persons are assembled for the distribution of primary
goods.
What are primary goods?
He mentions five primary goods.
ⓘ Liberty
(ii) Rights
(iii) Income
(iv) Wealth
(v) Dignity.
