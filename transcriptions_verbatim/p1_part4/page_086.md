__Relative Autonomy Vs Instrumentalist__
Instrumentalist theory gives no autonomy
to the state, state has no freedom of action. whereas the
2ⁿᵈ theory suggest that in some situations state gains
some amount of autonomy that state appears neutral.
Unlike liberals Marxists do not believe that state is
completely neutral. It is only relatively neutral.
what does relatively neutral mean?
State may appear neutral b/w conflicting interest (Proper-
tied class and poors but ultimately state comes for the
protection of rich. In crisis situation state will support the
rich. E.g. If we apply relative autonomy theory with
~~st~~ respect to state in USA despite the fact that big Banks
were responsible for subprime crisis which has created
hardships for large section of the people, state came to
the rescue of the bankers bail out the banks. On the other
hand state has used police force on the people in occupied
wall street movement.
Why state becomes relatively Autonomous?
It all depends on the historical conditions prevailing.
In his books 18ᵗʰ Brumaire of Lois Bonaparte he has
explained the situation of 'Bonapartism'.
What is Bonapartism?
Those states are Bonapartist states where executive is very
strong. It may happen that different social groups are
equally strong so that a particular group is unable to
dominate the basic structure. In such situations state beco-
mes relatively autonomous. both groups may compete for
the support of the state.
