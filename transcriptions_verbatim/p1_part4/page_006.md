Idealists are also called as Rationalists.
Why?
Because they believe that idea is real & idea can be
understood only through the use of reason.

● __Modern Times__
  Modern Age in Europe started as a result of move-
  ment like Renaissance, Reformation, Enlighten-
  ment. These movements established that man is
  rational. If man is rational man can be given rights.
  and liberty.
  Liberalism stands for rights of man, it challenged
  the rights of Kings. Liberalism challenges authority.
  Liberalism has challenged customs & traditions.
  Liberalism is not just a political ideology, it's a way
  of life in the west. It is a defining culture of west.
  Western Europe & North America together constitute
  the liberal world. Outside West India is considered
  as the Island of Liberalism.
  Till 1991 world has witnessed the clash of ideologies. i.e.,
  Liberalism Vs Communism.
  Since Sept. 11, 2001 scholars are talking about the
  clash of civilisation. Primary clash is between western
  civilisation & Islamic fundamentalism.
  In 1991 Liberal scholars claimed that Liberalism has
  won the Historic Battle of Ideologies.
  Francis Fukuyama proclaimed Liberalism as end
  of History. However, It is too early to suggest that
  Liberalism is end of History.
  Since, 2008 Liberalism is in crisis. The major challenge
  to Liberalism comes from ideologies like communita-
  rianism, multi-culturialism, Islamic fundamentalism.
