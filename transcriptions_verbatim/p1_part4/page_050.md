__NEO MARXISM__

In neo-marxism there are two schools both are influ-
enced by Gramsci.
① __Critical School / Frankford School__
Frankford School was founded in Germany in 1920s. But
later on shifted to the University of Columbia. Because of
Hitlers policies.
• __Prominent scholars -__
(i) Eric Fromm
(ii) Max Horkheimer
(iii) Theodore Adorno
(iv) Herbert Marcuse

• __Source of Influence__
(i) Karl Marx
(ii) Hegel
(iii) Sigmond Friud

• __Why it is known as critical school?__
Critical school is in dialectics with-
(i) capitalism.
(ii) Rationalism. (Enlightenment)
(iii) Orthodox Marxism.

They were critical of Soviet Marxism. They were also
critical of Fascism.

__What is their concern?__
Their concern is emancipation. They believe that the real
objective of K. Marx was ~~Emancip~~ation of man giving
him freedom in real sense. They object the view that the
real concern of Marx was class struggle & revolution as
projected by Soviet Marxists.
