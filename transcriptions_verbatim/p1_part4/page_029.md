-> __What is Multi-culturialism?__
Multi-culturialism is an approach or ideology
representing the interest of the non-dominant sections
of the society. It may include minorities, immigrants
refugees, or any non-dominant group

-> __Why Multi-Culturialism?__
The traditional liberal idea of managing diversity
is proving inadequate.

-> __Why this debate is prominent debate?__
World is becoming smaller & people are becoming
more conscious about their rights. Our idea of
Justice & fairness is becoming more enriched.

-> __What is the basic difference between traditional__
__Liberals & Multi-Culturialists?__
Traditional liberals take the view of man which
can be called as atomistic man (independent man).
The concept of Atomistic Man is being criticised as
'Abstract Individualism.' Abstract Individualism
does not give the picture of the real man.
