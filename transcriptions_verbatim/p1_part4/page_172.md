He was dissatisfied with the utilitarian Principles of
Justice which have been the dominant principles of

(iii) Influences
Rawls is influenced by the concept of Human Dignity
given by Emanuel Kant. Thus Rawls suggest that within
Liberalism there is superior tradition that is tradition
of Moral Individualism.

(iv) Describe Rawls Ideas:
According to Rawls justice is the 1st virtue of social insti-
tutions as the truth is the 1st ~~virtue~~ virtue of systems of
thought. If society is not based on the principles of justice
it will neither have peace nor stability. The theory of justice
has to be based on the idea of Justice as fairness. The
idea of justice as fairness is a deontological perspective.
It is based on the belief that human dignity has to be
the fundamental ideal behind any theory of justice. Accordi-
ng to Rawls, Each person has inviolability founded on Princi-
ples of justice. Human Dignity is the ultimate principle which
cannot be violated. Any ided of justice has to incorporate
~~to~~ the idea of Human Dignity. Human Dignity is a settled
principle it cannot be subjected to any bargain or social
calculus. Hence he suggests ~~that~~ that the loss of freedom
for some cannot be justified for the greater good of others.

__Note -__ His theory is deontological. In his theory of justice
Human Dignity as a principle can not be compromised or
overlooked. The idea is to explain Human Dignity is essen-
tial component of any idea of justice.
