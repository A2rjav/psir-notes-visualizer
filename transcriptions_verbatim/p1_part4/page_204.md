Post Behaviourists held that it is niether possible
nor desirable that Political Science becomes a
pure science. though Political science can be a
‘Applied Science’.
In contemporary times in the western world specially
in Europe some new perspective like post modernism,
critical school developed. The scholars of third world
countries have developed the school of post coloni-
alism.
Today Political science is a vibrant discipline with
expanding frontiers. It’s scope & methodologies
are being expanded. Today Political science not only
includes the study of state it also includes the study
of family (Personal is Political) it also includes Envir-
ronment. Many hybrid disciplines like political socio-
logy, political economy, Bio politics have also come
into existence.
• Nature of the Discipline-
Nature of political science remains a matter of debate.
According to one school of thought the nature of
Political science is philosophical. They suggest that Poli-
tical theories are normative theories. They suggest
that political scholars should take up normative issues &
the ~~better~~ method to tell if the philosophy or theory
is by philosophical approach.
on the other hand there have been a scholars who
wanted that Political Science should be distanced
from philosophy. Political Science should be made
pure science. These scholars (Behaviouralism) assumed
that it is possible to introduce scientific techniques
& to produce non-normative theories.
