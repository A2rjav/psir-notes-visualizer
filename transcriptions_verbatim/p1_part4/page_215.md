Features of Behavioural Approach
David Easton in his lecture to Americal Political Science
Association has given '8 intellectual foundation
Stones of Behaviourialism'.
What are these features?.
(i) Regularities - it means just there are observable
regularities in the natural world similarly there are
observable regularities in human behaviour. Hence we
can make scientific theory.
(ii) Systematisation- like ~~smresearch~~ in Natural sciences
there should be systematisation in social sciences. It
means there should be connection between the hypo-
thesis and data collection.
(iii) Verification - Our Hypothesis should be capable of
verification through facts.
(iv) Techniques- They suggested to incorporate scientific
techniques ie. Mathematical & statistical techniques.
(v) Measurement - Results should be expressed in quantifia-
ble manner.
(vi) Integration - Adopt interdisciplinary approach. Howe-
ver we should not bring our theory near to History
philosophy & law rather we should bring our theory
near to scientific disciplines like psychology, sociolo-
gy & economics.
(vii) Value Neutrality - Political scholar should not expre-
ss his value preferences
(viii) Pure Science - when we will observe about methods
Political science will become pure science.
