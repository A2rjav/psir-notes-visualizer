__Deliberative Democracy__
__Idea__ - Deliberation means Dialogue, Debate, Disscussion
There is no need to add the term deliberative to
democracy. The real meaning of Democracy is Delibe-
rative only. We have forgotten the essense of Democracy
In persent time Democracy is considered as Game of
Numbers. or in the words of J.S. Mill Tyranny of
majority Democracy is not about the number but abou
the arguement. According to J.S. Mill, If all but one has
a difference of opinion, it would not be right on the
part of Majority to silence that single voice. In recent
times scholars like Habermas, David Held, Joshua Cohen
Amy Gutman, Seyla Benhabib are the exponents of Deli-
berative Democracy. One of the ~~earliest~~ exponent of Deli
berative Democracy is Pericles the Athenian statesman.
According to him, Deliberation is never a stumbling rock,
it is indispensible, preliminary to any ~~wise~~ action.
