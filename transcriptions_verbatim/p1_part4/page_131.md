-> Amartya sen.
According to Amartya sen, Democracy is a pre-condition
for developement. Economic growth alone cannot meet
the parameter for developement. It is not the in
nature of govt. in china i.e. the main factor behind
better record of china in poverty eradication, Health
& Education. It has been the conscious choice of
elites to invest in Health & Education. So that china
can take up the opportunities generated by Globa-
lisation. It can be a coincidence that democracies
do not have good record of developement. As such
democracy is always conducive for developement.
He gives the example of kerala. The Govt. of Kerala
has invested in Health & Education. Investment in
Health & Education has created capabilities. Kerala
has done better in poverty reduction & achieving
gender equality. However, he admits that enough
investment has not been made in higher edu-
cation. Enough opportunities have not been created
in context of Income & wealth. He has also ~~he~~
analysed Gujrat Model. GJ Model has weaknesses
on social front. While GJ has created physical infra
structure but GJ does not have good educational
opportunities, GJ have lower Life expectancy, Gender
inequalities & poor health care system.
