__T.H. GREEN__

__Green's view on Liberty:-__
"Liberty is the capacity of doing something worth doing"
"An Individual is free only when he has capacity to
full fill the law of his being."
T.H. Green is the 1^st person to define liberty in terms of
capacity. In ~~neg~~ classical liberalism liberty is been
defined as absence of interference by the state.
However, this concept of Liberty led to the exploitation
of the masses. Classical liberalism came under criticism
for its inhuman nature. It was Mill who tried to re-
difine utility. But mill could not give the idea of +ve
liberty. It was oxford Idealists, T.H. Green who gave
concept of +ve liberty. +ve liberals are inspired by
Imanuel kant who talks about human dignity.
According to T.H. Green Liberty is something worth doing
which is appriate for humans. Liberty cannot be against
human dignity. He also suggests that when state intervenes
for capacity building of poors, it should not be treated as
limiting the liberty. There is a difference in Rights and
priviledges. Rights have to be universally available ~~only~~ If
only few can enjoy rights i.e. Priviledge.
