End of History-
End of History Thesis is associated with the ideas of
Fransis Fukuyama. 1st published in 1989 as an essay
and ~~to~~ later on in 1992 as a book with the title
'End of History & the last Man.'
What does End of History Thesis suggests?
End of History does not mean, end of the events in
History. End of History means, 'the end points of Man's
ideological evolution.'
What is the implication?
Liberalism or liberal democracy is the ultimate form
of govt. which can be concieved by Human being.
End of History on one hand establish the supremacy
of liberalism & on the other hand establish the
supremacy of the west. It is a claim that libera-
lism has won the historic battle of ideologies.
Fukuyama's concept of End of History is the appli-
cation of Hegel's & Marx's explination of End
of History. However, neither German state was End
of History nor Soviet Union. End of History concept is
more influenced by the ideas of Alexandre kojeve
He predicted that history will lead towards the esta-
blishment of universal & Homogenous state.
