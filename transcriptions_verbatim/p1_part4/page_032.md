__Bhikhu Parekh__
__Book - Rethinking Multi-culturialism.__
Though he shares some basic assumptions with
W. kymlicka about the importance of culture & the
need to give special rights he opposes W. kymlicka
on two grounds:
(i) There cannot be any justification to make a differen-
ce between National Minorities, Immigrants and
refugees. We cannot say that for immigrants &
refugees their culture does not matter.
(ii) He does not agree with the view that the multi-
culturialism is a feature of Liberal societies only.
He believes that liberals have inherent sense of supe-
riority. Bhikhu Parekh suggests, 'Dialogue between
civilisation' so that we can arrive at some basic
minimum values which will be acceptable to all.
