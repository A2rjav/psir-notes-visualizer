Next developement in the concept of Ideas is attri-
buted to Karl Marx. Karl Marx made distinction
b/w Ideology & science. He claimed Marxism as
science & other schools of thoughts as Ideologies.
He held that ideas & ideologies result into false
Consciousness.
It was lenin who held that working classes also
need their Ideology. He held that communism as an
Ideology of the working class.
Another Marxist scholar who has analysed the nature
of Ideologies is Gramsci. Gramsci gave the concept of
Hegemony. Gramsci held that liberalism is a Hege-
mony & communism is a counter Hegemony.

Liberal scholars like Karl Popper started calling Marx
as an enemy of open society. He called Marxism as
an Ideology & Liberalism as a open school of thou-
ght.
Hannah Arendt held that Ideology & terror are the two
instruments of the Totalitarian states. She has put
Fascism and Stalinism as an examples of Totalitarianism.

Above discussion show that quite contrary to the opinion
of Tracy the term ideology has got politicised.
Scholars made distinction between Ideology & science.
They called their own thinking as scientific & the think-
ing of opponents as Ideological. Later on post modernist
scholar like Foucault gave the term discourse for know-
ledge system. Foucault even suggests that science is
also a discourse.
