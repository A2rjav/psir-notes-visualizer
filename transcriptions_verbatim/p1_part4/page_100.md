→ __Robert Michels__
__Book__ - 'Political Parties'
Robert Michels have given 'Iron law of Oligarchy'.
Whether a country is democratic or communist power will
always get concentrated in the hands of small section
of elites. Hence, oligarchy is the Iron law.
Robert Michels analyse the nature of socialist political
parties. He found that there is no difference in the work-
ing of socialist parties & Bourgeoise/liberal parties. In
both parties power gets concentrated.
