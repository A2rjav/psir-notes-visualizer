● __Short Note - Original Position__.
(i) Concept used by Rawls in his Theory of Justice.
(ii) Rawls has adopted the social contract tradition to give
his Theory of justice.
(iii) Features of Original Position.
Moral Individuals rather than Hobbesian Individuals:
They are disinterested persons (mutually).
They are assembled for ~~the~~ determining the principles
of primary goods. i.e. Liberty, Equality, Income, wealth,
Dignity.
He proposes a thought experiement known as Veil of Ignorance
(iv) Features of veil of Ignorance
- They do not know particular facts about themselves as well
as society.
- They have some general idea of economics, psychology.
- They arrive at the 3 principles of Justice.
- Libery - Equality of opportunity - Difference principles
(v) __Conclusion.__
To conclude we can say that the real purpose of the concept
of original position is to suggest that we can make rational
policies only when we can eliminate our own personal prefe-
rences. Similarly we should evaluate laws & policies from the
original position otherwise our understanding will be biased.
