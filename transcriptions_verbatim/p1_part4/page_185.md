Political conception of Justice suggest that people believe
that the best way to resolve the conflictual issues is
through the right procedures which include deliberation
in legislatures as well as access to the judicial system.
Thus, Rawls limit liberalism to the political sphere. He is
not using liberalism as a comprehensive doctrine but as
political liberalism. Thus Rawls admit that one principle of
Justice cannot be applied to all spheres of life, one princip
of Justice will not appear reasonable to all. Political libe-
ralism is a characteristic of societies with democratic poli-
tical culture.
