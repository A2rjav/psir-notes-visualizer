② __pluralist Theory of Power -__
__Scholar - Robert Dahal__
In his study titled 'Who governs', he has challenged the
observation of C. Wright Mills with respect to Democracy
in USA. It is wrong to say that USA is an oligarchy.
On the other hand, USA is polyarchy.
__what is Polyarchy?__
Robert Dahal has conducted field surveys to find out whether
ordinary people in USA enjoy power. He found that
ordinary people do enjoy power but not as an individual
but as the member of the particular association or
interest group. There is very active interest group poli-
tics in USA these int groups lobby for protection of the
interest of their members. Hence, it is wrong to say
that the distribution of power in USA is too narrow
in USA. According to him, as ordinary persons in USA
do enjoy power in day today affairs of their life.
Decisions to use automic bomb is not regular day to
day decision. Thus USA is a polyarchy rather than Oly-
archy. Power is not in the hands of few nor in the
hands of all but in the hands of many.
__Deformed Polyarchy__
Robert Dahal has modified his views earlier he
believed that all ~~poe~~ groups in USA enjoy almost equal
powers but later on he admitted that all pressure groups
do not have equal powers. Corporate groups have more
power than other. Thus polyarchy is deformed or tilted
towards the corporate group.
