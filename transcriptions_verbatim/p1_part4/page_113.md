Q. Examine the relationship b/w power, Authority and
legitimacy.
Power is the central concept of politics. Behaviouralists
have explained the politics as study of shaping & sharing
of power. Though power is a central concept yet it
remains a contested concept. Power, authority & legitimacy
explain different dimensions of power.
__Power__- there are two broad ways in which power is
understood ie. in conventional sense power is considered
as Domination ie Ability toget things done by others.
Hannah Arendt has given non-conventional view of
power when she respites power not in terms coercion
but empowerment acting in concert.
In political science the Analysis of power is traditionally
associated with the Political power exercised by the state
though H. Arendt talks about the power excised by the
civil society & Michel Foucault talks about power in
terms of discourses. The main debate is b/w the liberals
and Marxists where liberals claim that the use of power
by the state is legitimate whereas Marxist claim that the
use of power by the state is illegitimate it is a coersion
by the state in the interest of the dominant class.
