like David Easton criticised Historicists like savine and
Dunning responsible for the decline of political theory.

What is contextual Approach?
Contextual Approach is a approach for studying texts.
Eg. Machiavelli's 'The Prince' or Hobbes's 'Leviathan'.
It is influenced by the developements in the field of the
interpretation of religious texts like Bible or Quran.
There are two approaches for the study of text:
(i) Textual Approach.
(ii) Contextual Approach.
