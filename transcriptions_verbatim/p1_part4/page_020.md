In 1970s it was introduced in Britain then in USA
& by 1990s Neo- liberalism expanded throughout
the Globe. It was introduced as , shock Therapy in
Russia and other former communist countries. It was
introduced as structural adjustment programs in India.
and other Third World countries. The conclusion of
Uruguay - rounds of talks led to the establishment
of WTO in 1995.
Principles of Neo liberalism can be understood from the
ten points washington consensus proposed by John
williamson.

(i) Reduction in Fiscal Deficit.
(ii) Pro-Industry tax reforms.
(iii) No Indiscriminate subsidies only targeted pro-growth
subsidies.
(iv) Market determined interest rates.
(v) Competitive Exchange rates to promote exports.
(vi) Trade liberalisation. by reducing tariff and non-
tariff barriers.
(vii) Promotion of FDI
(viii) Disinvestment of PSUs
(ix) Deregulation
(x) Protection to Right to Property.
