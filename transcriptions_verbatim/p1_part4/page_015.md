Short Note on Legitimation Crisis-
It is a concept given by Neo-Marxist scholar Haber-
mas in context of welfare state.

What is Marx position on the state-
According to Marx state is instrument of a capitalist
class & use force on workers in the interest of
capitalist class. In the words of K. Marx, state is the
executive committee of the Bourgeoise class. Workers
cannot expect justice from the state.

In response to socialist attack & crisis in capitalism the
nature of state changed in the western country.
Western countries adopted welfare state. Welfare
state takes many initiatives to provide social secu-
rity to the underprivileged class. In this way
Western countries could avoid working class revolution

According to Habermas welfare state only postpone the
crisis it cannot end the crisis. Even welfare state is
bound to face the crisis of Legitimacy.

Why crisis of Legitimacy?
Indications of crisis of the legitimacy
Increasing public protest, Decreasing ability of a state
to govern eg. Peoples protest in Greece after the govt.
of Greece introduced austerity measures (withdrawal
to come out of sovereign debt crisis.
