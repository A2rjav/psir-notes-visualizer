[Margin: American] Ralph Miliband Analysis of Post capitalist state.
Book- The state in capitalist society published in 1969.
Ralph Miliband has shown that the Nature of state has
not changed despite the fact that workers have Right to vote
According to him, managerial revolution is a myth. In western
countries economic power is still concentrated in the hands
of the capitalist class. Though managerial class is an open
class but the chances of sons & daughters of working classes
joining the managerial class is extremely rare. Untill & unless
they are extremely talented it is not possible for them to
Join. Management education is unaffordable.
Ralph Miliband has also analysed the Bureaucracy & Judiciary
in these countries. Even Bureaucracy & Judiciary remains
elitist & it is hardly representative thus according to him
there is no real change Economic power remains concen-
trated. The class which holds economic power also holds the
political power. Hence, state continues to be the instrument of
rich/capitalist class.
Nicole Poulanzas
Nicole Poulanzas does not agree with the instrumentalists
view rather support the structuralist/ relative autonomy
theory. According to him state in western liberal societies
can be considered as an instrument of a particular class.

Realistic.] Introduction of universal Adult franchise has changed the
nature of the state. In these countries competitive party
system & electoral system exists. In order to come to power
political parties have to get support from the large section
of the society. Hence there programs cannot be exclusive
to a particular class. This makes state neutral. In order to
remain in power party has to take care of the interests
of the different classes.
