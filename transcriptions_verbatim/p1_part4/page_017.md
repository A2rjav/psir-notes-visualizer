__Introduction__ -
Laski was source of Inspiration for Pandit Nehru.
Laski was positive Liberal with socialist leanings.
We attribute Fabian socialism to the ideas of Laski.
Nehru's socialistic pattern of Indian society is
influenced by Laski.
Laski has given the ~~g~~ elaborate set of social and
economic rights to be provided by the state
Part IV of Indian constitution which mentions many
social & economics rights are influenced by Laski.

"Every state is known by the rights it Maintain."

"Rights are those conditions of life without which no
man can seek in general to be at ~~his~~ its best."

"~~If~~ The social order which is not based on claims of
person is based on the sand."

"Rights are claims but not empty of duties."

"Either state should control the capital or capitalists
will control the state."

__Approach of Laski__
We see transition is Laski's ideas. Initially Laski was
advocate of liberty. His famous book is grammer
of Liberty. Later on he got inspired by the achieve-
ments of USSR. However, he became disappointed
because soviet system denied liberty. He came back to
Liberalism but remained liberal with socialist lean-
ings. Laski wanted state to take up the capacity
building tasks, intervene in industry on behalf of work-
ers for regulation of working hours, wages, providing
benefits like maternity reliefs, participation of work-
ers in management.
