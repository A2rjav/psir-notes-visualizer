② __Pluralist Theory of sovereignty__ -
__scholars__ - Laski, MacIver
-> __what is pluralist theory of sovereignty__ -
According to pluralists there are multiple centres of
authority rather than the single centre of authority.
According to Monists all power in a particular territory
is concentrated in the state. Whereas according to plura-
lists power is distributed among various associations.
According to Pluralists, state is also an association.
