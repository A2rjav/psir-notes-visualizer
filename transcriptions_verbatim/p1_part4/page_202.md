● __Evolution of Political science as discipline.__
Political science is one of the oldest disciplines. But
at the same time one of the most dynamic & vibrant
discipline.
Political science originated in Ancient Greece. The
term political science comes from the Greek word
Polis which means city state. The systematic study
of the city state, its institutions, processes became
the area of study. Aristotle who is known as the
Father of Political science, called Political science a
master science.
According to Barker, Political science is a master
science. Architectonic in its character. From which
all other practical sciences take cue means directions.
Stage I
It started in Ancient Greece. It started as the Branch
of Ethics or Philosophy. Scholars like Plato held that
Ethics & Politics cannot be seperated. The question of
good life cannot be seperated from the question of
good political life.
