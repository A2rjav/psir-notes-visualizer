According to former PM Manmohan Singh, India-
Africa partnership is a genuine "two ways
street".

Present PM Narendra Modi, suggest that India-
Africa partnership go beyond strategic considera-
tions. It is a relationship with strong emotional linka-
ge. The relationship is based on History, Culture, comme-
rce & kinship. India's approach towards Africa cannot
be transactional or utilitarian. According to him, "from
Gujrat to Durban we find common spirit, the spirit
of vasudhaiv Kutumbkam. & the spirit of ubuntu."

According to PM Modi, India is focusing on energy secu-
rity. maritime security & food security.
