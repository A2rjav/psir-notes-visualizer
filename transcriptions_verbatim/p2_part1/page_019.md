What are controversial issues in India's civil
nuclear liability Act?

(i) Section 4 - Suppliers liability
Which type of liability?
Strict and no fault liability.
No fault liability means supplier cannot give any excuse.
The only exception is in case of civil war or armed
attack by hostile state.

(ii) Section 6 - Total compensation.
Maximum amount is fixed ie. 300 million SDR
SDR - Special Drawing Rights.
1 SDR = 87 ₹   ∴ ₹ 2610 crore
Maximum liability of operator is only ₹ 1500 crore (NPCIL)
Liability of Govt of India is 1100 ₹ crores.

(iii) Section 17 - ~~Suppliers~~ ^operator's^ right to re course from supplier
After operator has failed the compensation, operator
can seek the amount from supplier.

(iv) Section 17 (A) - suppliers liability will apply only when
it is explicitly mentioned in the contract.

[Margin: USA + objecⁿ]
(v) Section 17 (B) - Right to recourse even when it is not
explicitly mentioned in the contract. ~~Supplier~~ ^operator^ can
seek recourse in case of nuclear accident because of
patent or latent defects in the material, equipments &
even in the services provided. It includes the responsi-
bility of supplier & his employee. USA has objection on
sec. 17(B). According to USA it is not as per internati-
onal norm. On the other hand according to India it is
as per international norm set up by 'convention on
supplementary compensation'.
