Ministry of External Affairs has published its stand
on south china sea:
India supports freedom of Navigation, over fight &
unimpeaded commerce based on internation laws
(UNCLOS). State should resolve the dispute through
peaceful means without threat or use of force.
State should act with self restrain. Then sea lines
of communication passing through south china sea
are critical for peace & stability. India urges all
parties to show utmost ~~set~~ respect on UNCLOS.

Why India is interested in south china Sea?
Since, 1991 India has developed huge economic
interest in the region in India's trade & investment
growing in the region. South China sea is also
having oil & Gas. India intends to work with con-
tries like Vietnam for exploration of oil & gas.

What is the importance of South China Sea?
It is 1.4 mn sq miles water body in Pacific ocean
Starting from Singapore & malacca straight in
south and stretches till taiwan in North.
It touches the boundary of China, Taiwan, viet-
nam, Malaysia, Phillipines & Northern Indonesia.
It is the busiest International sea link the total
traffic is 3 times more than the traffic passes
through suez canal. & five times more than the
traffic which passes through Panama canal.
