-> Reasons-
(i) Multi-lateralism is always favourable to weaker
countries because they cannot get what they want
in bilateral negotiations.
(ii) WTO in principle accept differential treatment.
(iii) In WTO decisions are taken by consensus, every
country has veto power. Even the smallest country can
stop agreement if it considers agreement as counter
productive.
(iv) WTO provides an opportunity for capacity building of
developing countries.
(v) WTO ensures that they get the widest possible market

In practice-
WTO policies have not benefited developing countries
in general. One of the worst consequence of WTO
policies is in agriculture sector. Not only it has result-
ed into stagnation in Agricultural growth. It has also
impacted food security.

In theory, every country has veto power, but in real
practice developed countries have adopted various
techniques so that countries of south fall in their
line. (Green Room Diplomacy).

WTO's dispute settlement body-
Uptill now none of the African countries have utilised
the platform. The entire mechanism is extremely costly.
