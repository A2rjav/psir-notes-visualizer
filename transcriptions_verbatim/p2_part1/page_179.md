NAFTA should not be compared with regional orga-
nisations like EU or ASEAN. It is purely a trading
block. It does not have any supra-national featu-

NAFTA is in controversy because of US president
Trump is against Multi-lateral Trade Agreements.
However, it is to be noted that NAFTA in reality
is bilateral agreement. US has agreements with
Mexico & Canada seperately. Present President of US
is extremely critical of Mexico as well as Canada
for following unfair trade practices. He aims to
renegotiate NAFTA treaty.
