$\rightarrow$ Other areas of dispute.

① Investment Treaty
A Bilateral Investment Treaty (BIT) is due for long.
In 2012 USA has brought new model investment
Treaty. In 2015, India has brought New model Invest-
ment Treaty. There is a big gap b/w Indian Model &
American Model.

what are the differences?
Indian Model is called as enterprise based whereas
US model is Asset Based.
As per Indian Model company should have substantial
physical presence in India & should be employing subs-
tantial number of Indian workforce.

USA Model is Asset based which wants protection in
investments in shares, derivatives. USA also wants to
include protection for investment in Intellectual Prop-
erty Rights.

② Most Favoured Nation Status
Indian Model does not have MFN status. India wants
to deal each country individually.
