● BRICS

Is BRICS a challenge to US Hegemony?

What is BRICS?
BRICS is an idea which is still evolving. The acronym
BRIC was coined by Jim O neil an economist asso-
ciated with Goldman Sachs.
These countries were considered as emerging market.
Means showing fastest rate of economic growth.

It has been predicted that by the year 2050, the
total GDP of these countries will surpass the total GDP
of G7 countries.

BRICS gradually started evolving as a geopolitical plat-
form. 1st BRIC summit took place at Yekateringberg,
Russia in 2009. BRICS countries started taking geo-poli-
tical issues also. BRICS agenda is evolving. They take up
issues like crisis in middle east, reforming the insti-
tutions of Global Governance special IMF, WB, South -
South cooperation, developement partnership with Latin
America & Africa.

Importance of BRICS
(i) BRICS represent a huge market.
(ii) Raw Materials, Natural Resources, skilled manpower,
(iii) Russia & china are members of UNSC India is aspiring
member even Barzil. They also represent major military
power.
