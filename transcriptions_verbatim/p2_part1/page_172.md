-> Is there anything new in Act East?
Majority of the scholars feel that there is nothing
new in substantial terms it is just a rebranding
However, According to C Raja Mohan It is more
than re-branding. the speed, scale, & space with
which we are engaging was never seen before.
According to Rajiv Bhatia, a former diplomat there
are five new features:
(i) Tangible Actions, concrete results
(ii) Geographical space has expanded from Mangolia
to the pacific islands like Fiji.
(iii) Focus from South East Asia to North East Asia
considering china's rise.
(iv) Earlier expressions were not as bold in defence secu-
rity & strategic terms.
(v) India is more confident in protecting its rights in
