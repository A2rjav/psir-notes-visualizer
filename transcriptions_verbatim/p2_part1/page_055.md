Border Defence Co-operation Agreement, Dec. 2013

Occassion - Manmohan singh's visit to china.

Context - In recent years there have been many
Occassion when countries have blamed each other
for incursion. Incursions happen because boundaries
are not demarcated. Hence. to handle incursions
this agreement was important.

$\rightarrow$ Features:
(i) Regular dialogue b/w defence forces at all levels.
(ii) Establishment of hot lines neither side will use
~~use~~ force or threat of force if military personnel
comes in face to face situation both will maximise
restraint.
(iii) No tailing of each others forces in case of any
misunderstanding seek clarification.

$\rightarrow$ Emerging areas of dispute.
(1) Water Dispute
water dispute is also linked to Tibet.

What is importance of Tibet?
(i) Source of all major Asian rivers.
(ii) It is expected that it has oil gas & other mineral
reserves.
(iii) Rich forest wealth.
(iv) Geostrategic location at the tri-junction of south Asia,
centra Asia & East Asia.

What is water issue?
As per $12^\text{th}$ FYP of china, china will be constructing
around 30 dams on Brahmaputra (Tsang Po).
