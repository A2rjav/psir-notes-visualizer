● International court of Justice. (ICJ)
Only Body of UN which is directly inhe-
rited from league of Nations.
It is used to be called as Permanent court of ~~Juris-~~
~~diction.~~ (PCJ). Justice.
→ Composition:
15 Judges, 1/3rd retire every 3 year,
Term of Judge- 9 years, eligible for re-election,
elected by UN General Assembly & UNSC from among
the names proposed by member states. Idea is to give
representation to all civilisations & legal system. ~~It deals~~
with the matter of fact or interpretation of any treaty
does not entertain dispute of political nature.
→ Jurisdiction:
(i) Contentious Jurisdiction-
Since ~~Normal~~ International law is voluntary ICJ can
take up matters only with the consent of parties to dispu
te.
(ii) Advisory Jurisdiction-
It can give advice to all UN bodies including UNSC &
General assembly.

If countries want they can adopt compulsory jurisdiction
