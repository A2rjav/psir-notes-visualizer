US policy of Hegemony::
To establish its Hegemony USA aims to contain any
Potential challenger.
What USA has done to china?
(1) Entered into alliance with chinese neighbours.
(2) created domestic issues?

-> Taiwan - since 1972 USA has acknowledged Taiwan
as a part of china, USA is following one china policy.
(shanghai communique, 1972)
In 1979 USA has passed Taiwan Relations Act. by which
USA is committed to ensure that Taiwan's integration
with china should not be by force.
USA has deployed Missiles in Taiwan as a part of its
commitment. Present chinese president is committed to
end 'centuries of Humiliation'.

-> Tibet.
Since beginning USA is involved in Tibet. US congress
has passed Tibet Policy Act 2002. According to which
it has appointed special co-ordinator for Tibetan issue
USA is committed to protect culture, linguistic & religious
heritage of Tibet. USA is supposed to promote the
dialogue b/w china & Tibet.

US has been critical of the Human Rights violation of
