-> What is EU?
EU is primarily a trading block of 28 European
Nations. Though it is a trading block yet mem-
bers do have common foreign & security policies.

-> History of India- EU Relations-
India is one of the earliest country entering into
diplomatic relations with EU in 1950s itself.
After adoption of new economic policy : India &
EU entered into joint political statement in 1993
which takes the relations beyond trade & econo-
mics. In the year 2000 they started summit &
since 2004 they are strategic partners in 2005
they have adopted Joint Action Plan to carry
forward strategic partnership. Areas of strategic
partnership include Political, Cultural, economic,
strategic dimensions. EU has emerged India's
largest trading partner. EU is also the source of FDI
However India recieves just 1% of EU's FDI.
India's share is less than 1/10th of china. Since
EU is primarily a trading block the most impo-
rtant factor that can make relations strategic
is earliest conclusion of Free Trade agreement.
Since, 2007 they have conducted multiple rounds
of talk. In 2016, PM Modi adopted 'agenda 2020'
