-> Examples of co-operation-
(i) India approached USA in 1962 war USA agreed to
send the help but by that time china had declared
unilateral cease-fire.
(ii) In 1965 war with Pakistan USA had suspended eco-
nomic & military aid to both the countries & did not
support Pakistan despite Pakistan was US ally.
(iii) USA helped India to overcome the crisis of food secu-
rity. USA also helped India in Green Revolution.

During this phase India continued to maintain Anti-
American rhetoric. India kept on insisting on Non-
alignment. India was extremely critical of US invol-
vement in Vietnam war. Ultimately USA left India
for more pragmatic countries (china & Pakistan).
In South Asia developed US-Pakistan-china Axis.

-> Phase II- From 1970s to 1990s
phase of only conflict & no co-operation.
(i) India entered into treaty of Peace & friendship with
USSR.
(ii) A new axis developed India- USSR- Vietnam.
(iii) Both USA and china had threatened India in 1971 war
(iv) USA has build apathy against India after Pokhran I

Throughout the phase the ~~focus~~ of USA's south Asia
policy was on nuclear non-proliferation. India was
in opposite camp, Indian economy was closed economy.
Hence, only consideration was nuclear proliferation.
