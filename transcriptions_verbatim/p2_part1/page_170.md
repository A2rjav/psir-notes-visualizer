Geographical proximity, cultural & Historical links.
-> What is look East policy?
Look East is an evolving concept.
(1) Look East - Phase I
Under Narsimharao Govt. Focus was on economic
diplomacy & ASEAN as priority region.
(2) Look East - Phase II
under vajpayee Govt.
- Look East started gaining strategic dimension
along with economic.
- Focus shifted beyond ASEAN & to go for improve-
ment in the relations even at Bilateral level.
- Vajpayee's policy had domestic dimension also
known as Look East through North East.
- North East to be integrated with Asia's most dyna-
mic region.
- Govt. of India entered into defence partnerships with
countries like Vietnam, Malaysia.

● India's Act East Policy
Act East Policy was announced at India. ASEAN
Summit in Myanmar in 2014. Minister of External
Affairs suggested that the focus of Act East shall be
on 3 C's - culture, commerce & connectivity.
Ministry of External Affairs has published notification
on Act east on 23^rd Dec. 2015 in Press Info Bureau
(PIB).
