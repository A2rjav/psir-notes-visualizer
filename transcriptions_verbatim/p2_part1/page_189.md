Pakistan. It seems every PM wants to have its
legacy on India's Pakistan Policy. India's Pakistan
Policy is often criticised as having lack of consi-
stency, It is believed that India has a posture &
not the policy.
For any sound policy, it is necessary to have the
clarity of goals & understanding of the country
with which we are dealing. Pandit Nehru never
considered Pakistan can be a serious challenge. He
expected that within few years Pakistan will come
back to India as a state Pakistan cannot sustain.
However, It is wrong if we think Pakistan is a weak
state. Even in contemporary India many analysts
think that Pakistan is on the verge of collapse.
Pakistan is no match to India.

Antony Lieven, in his recent book on Pakistan
'Pakistan a hard country' show that Pakistan as a
State ~~a~~ has a strong foundation. Pakistan has one of
the most professional armed forces, Bureaucracy.
Pakistan's economy is vigilient.
Ian Bremmar calls pakistan 'Pivot state'.
Andrew Korybko has called Pakistan 'the zipper state
of Eurasian integration'.
Pakistan is a pivot state because of its location. It is
located on the borders of west Asia & central Asia.
It also touches south Asia & East Asia.
