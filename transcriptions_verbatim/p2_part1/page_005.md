• India - USA Relations

-> Official Status:
(i) Strategic Partners.
(ii) Since, 2010 we are having institutionalised strategic
dialogue which has been changed into institutionalised
strategic & commercial dialogue. The five pillars of strate-
gic partnership are.
(a) Strategic issues.
(b) Energy & climate change
(c) Science & Technology
(d) Health & Innovation
(e) Education & developement.
How leaders of these countries explain the relation?
(i) Former PM Vajpayee called USA as India's natural allie
(ii) Former PM Manmohan Singh called usa as Natural
Partner.
(iii) Present PM N.Modi calls USA as India's natural global
partner.
(iv) Former US President obama called India US partnership
as the most defining partnership of 21st century.
(v) Former US Secretary of Defense Ashton carter
called India as USA's indispensible partner
(vi) former US secretary of state Hillary Clinton held that
USA is making 'strategic bet on India'
(vii) Present US President D. Trump so far has not made
any such comment which can be called as Anti-India.
Indian PM is the 5th world leader with whom US President
made a call & appreciated.
