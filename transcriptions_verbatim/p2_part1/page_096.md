NAM is the largest platform of developing countries
outside UN general assembly. In the words of former
PM of India Mrs. Indira Gandhi, NAM is largest peace
movement. It stands for South-South co-operation.

__Historical context of NAM__
It originated in the context of cold war when world
was devided b/w the two military blocks. It is an
attempt by the newly independent countries to stay
away from military alliances, determine their own
priorities, focus on developement & become a stabi-
lising force by promoting disarmament. It is a brain
child of prominent leaders of the developing world
like Pandit Nehru of India, President Sukarno of
Indonesia, Marshall Tito of Yugoslaviya, Abdul Nasser
of Egypt.
