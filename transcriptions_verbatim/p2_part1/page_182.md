-> SAARC objectives:-
Peace & prosperity in South Asia.

-> SAARC Principles:-
(i) Non-interference in domestic affairs.
(ii) SAARC will not be used to discuss bilateral disputes
or conflictual matters
(iii) Each country will have veto power & SAARC deci-
sion will be taken by consensus.
