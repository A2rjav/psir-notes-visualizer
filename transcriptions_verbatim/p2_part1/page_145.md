Now, countries of North want to roll back the
compulsory licensing provision. They want strict
norms. They want that compulsory licensing should
be limited to the domestic market & should not be
used for exporting. African countries have a serious
concern because most of them do not have infra-
structure & expertise.
Besides IPR, geographical indicators have also been
an area of conflict. Western countries in general &
US companies in particular have been quick to regi-
ster for geographical indicators. India developed disp-
ute with USA on Basmati Rice, Neem, Haldi.
There is also a big gap b/w North & South w.r.t.
investment policies. The most contentious issues are
dispute settlement, domestic requirement etc.

__where WTO is moving ?__

There is a scepticism towards the future of multi-
lateral trade talks for various reasons.
(i) Policies of USA
whether obama Administration or Trump Administration,
both tried to undermine WTO. Obama Administration
launched Trans-Pacific Partnership (TPP), Trans-Atlantic
[Margin: Trade &] Investment Partnership (TTIP) b/w US & EU. In case
these mega trade blocks come into existence it will under-
mine WTO.
Trump policies are openly protectionist.
(ii) Emergence of various regional trading blocks.
Trading blocks are protectionists. Trading blocks also go
against principle of non-discrimination as member coun-
tries gives preference to members of block.
