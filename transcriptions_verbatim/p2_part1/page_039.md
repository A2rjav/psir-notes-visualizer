even today Chinese dependence on USA is many ti-

has become junior partner of china. The reason for
closeness b/w Russia & china is not the closeness b/c
India & USA the real reason is western policies
of isolating Russia. Which have pushed Russia
towards china. China is the biggest beneficiary of
the rift b/w west & Russia. At present the alliance is
tactical but it may soon become strategic because
both countries are fast increasing their interdepen-
