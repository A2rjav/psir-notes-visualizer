$\rightarrow$ Initiatives taken by Modi Govt.
(i) Upgraded strategic partnership with light like
minded countries.
(ii) Initiated Forum of India Pacific Island Co-operation
(iii) Adopted India-ASEAN plan of action for 2016-20
India will be participating in all the 3 pillars of
ASEAN community.
(iv) Invited countries to participate in International
Solar alliance & Make in India initiative. Present
Govt is trying to complete India led connectivity
projects at a much higher speed. Govt is giving
lot of focus on physical connectivity as well as di-
gital connectivity.
(v) PM is also giving renewed attention towards the
domestic dimension. PM has coined a new term
for North East ie. NEZ (North east to. become
National Economic zone). He visited Assam, Nagala-
nd, Manipur & Tripura.
(vi) However, considering the security environment in
North East there are many obstacles in realisation
in the vision of Look East trough North East.
(vii) India ASEAN FTA in services have been given
effect since 2015.
