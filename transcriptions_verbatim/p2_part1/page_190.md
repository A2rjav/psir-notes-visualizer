Pakistan will never be diplomatically isolated as
India thinks. At Present Pakistan has good relati-
ons with all powers. Pakistan is controlled by the
Army of Pakistan which is known as deep state.
It is always easy for countries like USA & china to
deal with Army rather than the civilian govt. Hence,
it doesn't seem that Army is going to loose its signifi-
cance. So long Army is in power there is hardly
any hope for India for normalisation of relations.
Pakistan's Army's legitimacy in country's political system
is because of projecting India as a threat. It is be-
lieved that even when we resolve the kashmir issue
relations will not improve for two reasons.
(i) Even Kashmir is not just a territory or a source
of water. Kashmir is a matter of two mutually exclu-
sive identities. Identity of India as a secular state &
Identity of Pakistan justifying seperate homeland for
minorities of the subcontinent.
(ii) The attitude of Army of Pakistan. Some scholars often
suggest that India should talk with Rawalpindi ra-
ther than Islamabad. However, such arguements
are based on unrealistic view of Pakistan's army.
It is also suggested that it is better for India if
Army directly rules the country. It would be possible
for India to have direct dialogue at official level.
