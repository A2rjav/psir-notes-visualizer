● Non Agricultural market Access (NAMA).

— Main group is NAMA 11
-> Countries of North
North has already liberalised in terms of tariff
reduction. North expects countries of south to reduce
tariffs.

Countries of South considers customs duties as a source
for financing developement. Associated with NAMA
is trade facilitation agreement in goods. Developing
countries view is that on one hand their revenue
collection is declining on the other hand they have to
make additional expenditure on Building infrastructure.

According to developing countries, developed countries
have reduced tariff barriers yet they impose Non-
tariff barriers. Hence, it is difficult to expand the
export. There has been disagreement on the formula.
As a part of July package in 2005, Hong kong. countries
have agreed for Swiss formula for tariff reduction.
