__Manmohan Doctrine__-
Manmohan Singh govt. also aimed at normalisa-
tion of relations. There was significant relations
improvement in relations. Main theme of M
doctrine was economy diplomacy.

Manmohan Singh govt. was working on following lines
(i) Promotion of trade
(ii) People to people contact.
(iii) Informal Manmohan Singh-Musharraf formula on
Kashmir
$\rightarrow$ De-militarisation of public spaces in Kashmir.
$\rightarrow$ Making LoC a soft border.
Govt. was also close to the ressolution of Sir creek
issue & Siachin issue

However since 2011, relations started deteriorating.
Violence (cease-fire) became prominent. As there
was a pressure on Pakistan after Mumbai terror
attack.
In India it was realised that India becomes more
vulnerable if govt. takes a step for talk. Either it
results into violence at LOC or terrorist attack.

Modi's Policies.
Modi as announced neighbourhood 1st policy,
invited all SAARC leaders. However, neighbourhood
1st policy in context of Pakistan is not working
rather it will not work considering the vested interest.
