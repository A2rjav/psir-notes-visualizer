● Evaluation of UN
Evaluation of UN has to be seen in context of UN Goals
and achievements.

→ Goals and objectives of UN-
The objectives are given in Preamble of UN charter-
(i) To save succeeding generations from the scourge of
war.
(ii) To re-affirm faith in Fundamental Rights & Human dignity.
(iii) strengthen conditions under which justice prevails &
respect of International law is maintained.
(iv) Promote social progress & better standard of life in
the interest of larger freedom.

→ overall Assessment-
UN performance has not been satisfactory in any of
the core objectives. However it is been said that 'Had
UN not been there situation would have been worse?
