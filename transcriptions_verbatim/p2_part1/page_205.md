What is the urgency to solve?
As per UNCLOS all water disputes should be resolved
by 2009 after which UN can declare any such body
as international & both the countries will be the
loosers. Sir creek issue was one of the issue in compo-
site dialogue & was considered as doable issue.

What is the dispute?
Goes back to British time. There was dispute b/w the
state of sind & state of Kutchcha. 1914 map. to
resolve the dispute 1914 map was produced. Pakist-
an supports the map. As per 1914 map Sir creek comes
under Pakistan's territory ie. sind's territory. However,
1914 map is contested because it has too. contradi-
ctory provisions. First is paragraph 9 - According to
paragraph 9 boundary lies east of the creek. this line
is known as Green line.
Second is paragraph 10 - water body to be devided
from the middle as per Thalweg principles.
