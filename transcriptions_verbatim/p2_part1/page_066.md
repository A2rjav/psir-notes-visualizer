India has changed its look East Policy to Act East
policy. Basic difference in look east & act east is
the primary focus of look east is economic
whereas that of Act East is strategic. In look
east the focus was on South East Asia. Act East
expanse to Asia Pacific Region./Indo Pacific ocean.
Since 2004 India is consistently releasing its mari-
time doctrine the title of 2009 doctrine is -
'Freedom of seas'. The title of 2015 doctrine is -
'Ensuring safe seas'. India has mentioned freedom
of seas as a key National priority. India has assu-
med the role of 'Net security Provider' British India was
a Net security provider in Indian ocean.

Net security provider-
country which provides security to other countries
also & Net security means from all types of threats
i.e. conventional & non-conventional.
