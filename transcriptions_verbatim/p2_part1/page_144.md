-> Concept of positive list.

-> Negotiations are happening on 4 pillars or modes:
Mode I - those areas where product is consumed in
importing country. India has interest in mode I

Mode II - Eg. consumption Abroad Eg. Tourism.
developed countries are interested in Mode II

Mode III - establishing commercial presence like
Insurance / Banking sector. It is a priority of developed
countries.

Mode IV - Movement of natural persons. India is
agressive about mode IV. Initially developing countries
were relectant but developing countries opened mode II
& III sufficiently. However, developed countries are pro-
tective. India has proposed trade facilitation Agree-
ment in service sector. This will result into the ease
of movement. It will bring predictability in country's
visa policies. India is also planning to take visa policy
of Trump Administration to WTO's dispute settlement
body.

● IPR issues-
For the 1st time IPR issue were included in trade
talks in Dunkel Draft. 1995 agreements proved
counter productive for developing countries especially in
Health sector. It has created crisis of public health
specially in African countries. Ultimately under Doha
round of talks countries of north were forced to accept
compulsory licensing in the interest of public health.
