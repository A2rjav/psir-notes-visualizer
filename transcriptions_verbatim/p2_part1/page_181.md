Regional organisation of South Asian countries came
into existence in 1985. Brain child of Bangladesh
President Zia ur Rehman.

-> Why he proposed SAARC?

Till Afghanistan was not inducted in SAARC, none-
of the South Asian countries were linked to each
other territorially. though Maldives & Sri Lanka have
maritime boundary. Since mid 70s India's relations
with all neighbours deteriorated. India was considered
as Bullying Big Brother. Hence to challenge India's
regional Hegemony Zia ur Rehman proposed platform
where smaller neighbours can come together to
balance India.
