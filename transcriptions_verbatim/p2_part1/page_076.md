● Evolution of India - Japan Relations -

Before independence
① Civilisational linkages with Japan. There has always
been a warmth in the relations at the level of
civil society. At 1914 Ravindranath Tagore acknoweled-
ged a warmth people in Japan had towards India.
Japanese accept that it is not possible to under-
stand Japanese culture without understanding India's
impact. Japan helped subhashchandra Bose in raising
Indian National Army.

After Independence- till 1974
Japan appreciated the stand of justice Radha Binod
Pal who alone gave dissenting judgement in war crime
tribunal constituted against Japan in 1948. India
was one of the 1st contries to enter into peace treaty
with Japan in 1950 after 2nd world War. India became
highest recipient of Japan's official developement
assistance.

According to David melon , relations b/w India & Japan
during this phase was not as warm as they should
be. He calls state of relations as. "Tepid"

Why?
Though there was no animosity yet India & Japan
were in opposite camps. Japan was ally of USA.
whereas India was critical of USA & was in the league
of USSR. Japan & Russia till date do not have nor-
mal relations & technically they remained at war.
