① Gareth Evans - former Foreign minister of Australia.
No other organisation embodies as many dreams yet
so many frustations as UN. Security Council has been
the prisoner of Great power manouvering, General
Assembly as a theatre of empty rhetoric, economic
& social council has become dysfunctional & becoming
increasingly irrelevant, Secretariat is a host of ineffic-
ent individuals.

what is the relevance of UN?
though there is a dissatisfaction yet there is a recogni-
tion that we need more UN rather than less UN as
suggested by former UN secretory general Ban-ki-moon

We are living in an age where no country including the
single super power is capable to address the challenges
on its own. Regarding UN it is said that had UN not
been there we would have created it. Global govern-
ance has become a necessity today.

Can UN work?
that is the biggest question mark. UN remained paraly-
sed throughout the cold war because of east west
conflict. End of cold war generated optimism but very
soon world got devided once again. We can say that
the more world has changed the more it has remained
the same.
