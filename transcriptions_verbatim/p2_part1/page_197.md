* Gilgit Baltistan Issue-
Gilgit Baltistan is a part of Indian state of Jammu &
kashmir. Considering the strategic location Britishers
had taken it on lease from Maharaja of Kashmir.
At the time of independence British officers raised
Pakistan flag. However, since then Pakistan conside-
red it as its part. The ethnic composition in the
region is different from valley as well as the regio-
ns in Pakistan. People are followers of shia religion
& have greater linkages with Tribes in Central Asia.
Gilgit Baltistan has no constitutional (vague) status
in Pakistan. Pakistan has exploited the region but has
denied any benefits. After popular protest Pakistan
has established Northern Area Legislative Council.
However in 2009 it has renamed the institution as
Gilgit Baltistan and has made legislative council
into legislative assembly. Locals do not have any
rights. Non-locals Panjabis & Pashtuns have been
the ruling elites.

Indian PM has also raised Baltistan issue.

After India raising Human Rights issue Pakistan govt
started considering to make Gilgit Baltistan as 5^th
province. Pakistani govt's decision has been opposed
not only by Govt of India but also seperatists in
Kashmir.
Why?
They feel that if Pakistan will declare Gilgit Baltistan
as a state, it will become easier for the ruling party
in India to end Article 370 & to end the temporary
status of Kashmir.
