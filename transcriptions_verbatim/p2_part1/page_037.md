● India Russia Energy Partnership.
Russia has world's largest Natural Gas reserves,
2nd largest coal reserves, 10th largest oil reserves.
Russia India energy partnership theoritically appears
to be most attractive but because of Geographical
reasons there is a limited feasibility. At the max
Indian companies can go for investment in oil & gas
sector or India can have swap deals. Recently,
Russia- China have entered into agreement for constru-
ction of pipelines Eg. Power of Siberia pipeline,
East Siberia, Pacific Ocean Pipeline; Altai Pipeline.
Indian companies have lost bid to get stakes in
Sakhalin II. We got stakes in Sakhalin I but it is not
an attractive investment in terms of cost. India is
trying to get stakes in Sakhalin III in Siberia & Yamal
Gas field in Arctic. There is a scope for energy
partnership b/w Russia & Pakistan. Russia is constru-
cting a pipeline b/w Karachi & Lahore.
