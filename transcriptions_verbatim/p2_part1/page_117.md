Non- Alignment as a Foreign Policy

Q. What is the relevance of Non-alignment as
a foreign policy option?

What is Non-alignment?
India's contribution to the theory of International
Politics & the practice.
Initially there was lack of clarity.
USA considered Non-alignment as immoral & India's
opportunism. Soviet's were more suspicious and
Stalin held that those who are not with us are
against us. So
Scholars of International Politics compare Non-align-
ment with USA's foreign policy of isolationism.
The features of Non-alignment as a foreign policy
became clear from time to time through Pandit
Nehru's speeches. Nehru rejected that non-alignment
as a isolationism. He asserted that India will be
taking active role in international affairs.
Non-alignment is neither indifference, nor equit
distance like Nehruvian secularism. Non-alignment
is 'principled Distance'. India will take principled
stand on the basis of Right & wrong rather than
any prior commitments.
