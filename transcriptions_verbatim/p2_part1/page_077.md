India's closed economy was hardly attractive for
Japan. Japan's stand was not pro-India in
1971 war. Japan was extremely critical of India's
Nuclear Test in 1974 & temporarily halted offic-
ial developement assistance.

From 1974 to 1985
Relations remained "Tepid" though Japan resumed
Official developement assistance. Japanese companies
started investment in automobile sector. Cold War
prevented any strategic convergence b/w India &
Japan. Even at present Japan is not in perfect
convergence with India as far as Pakistan is
concerned.

From 1985 onwards-
Rajiv Gandhi visited Japan in 1985. India was also
taking small steps towards liberalisation. Japan
~~But~~ Rajiv Gandhi became the 1st Indian PM to
adress Japanese diet. His speech remains the gui-
ding framework of all govts. in India. In the words
of Rajiv Gandhi, relationship b/w India & Japan are
not limited to the market place. let us rediscover
ourselves in the Hearts and Minds of our people.
Relationship is not just for mutual benefits, it is in
the "greatest interest of Humanity."
