less arranged marriage'. There is nothing strategic
in India-EU relations. Because India & EU show a
big gap on strategic issues. Eg. India's position on
Russia's annexation of Crimea is completely opposite
to EU's position. Similarly Russia is the biggest geo-
strategic challenge to EU whereas Pakistan is biggest
geo strategic challenge to India (China also). European
countries have good relations with china as well as
Pakistan. EU's position w.r.t Human Rights violation
in Kashmir is not in sink with India's position.
India EU' has conflictual interest at WTO in terms
of Intellectual Property Rights, Agricultural subsidies,
they also do not have convergence on climate chan-
ge issue. India- EU strategic partnership is based
on some weak ideas like:
(i) multi- Polar world order.
(ii) strengthening multi-lateralism.
(iii) counter terrorism.

However when these issues come in concrete situa-
tions divergences are more.
