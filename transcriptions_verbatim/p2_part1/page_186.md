● BIMSTEC

BIMSTEC was first established as bist in 1997.
original members were Bangladesh, India, Sri
lanka, Thailand. Towards the end of 1997 Myan-
mar also joined. In the year 2004 Nepal & Bhu-
tan joined & since then organisation is known as
BIMSTEC

-> significance -
Organisation has strategic & huge economic releva-
nce.
(i) It can be considered as SAARC minus Pakistan.
Negating Pakistan & bringing like minded country
Thailand in place of Pakistan.
(ii) BIMSTEC is a ~~breach~~ bridge b/w South Asia &
South East Asia.
(iii) the focus of BIMSTEC has been quite similar to
that of Mekong Ganga promoting people to peo-
ple contact, connectivity, trade & investment.
