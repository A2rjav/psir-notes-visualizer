* Indus Water Treaty -
According to Brahma Chellany, Indus water treaty
is perhaps the only water agreement where the
greater share is for the lower riperian state &
lower riperian state enjoys veto power. As per the
treaty Pakistan will get the water of 3 western
rivers ie. Jhelum, Chenab & Indus & India will get
the water of 3 eastern rivers ie. Ravi, Beas, Sutlej.
However, the total water of Indus system in west-
ern rivers is four times more. 80% of water is in
western rivers & 20% in eastern rivers. As upper
riperian state India has right to use the water of
western rivers for non-consumptive purposes.
-> Veto power of Pakistan-
India can carry with the projects only with the consent
of Pakistan.
-> What if Pakistan objects?
(i) Treaty establishes permanent Indus water commission
to resolve the matter.
(ii) If matter cannot be resolved at the level of commissi-
on countries can go for mediation by neutral experts.
(iii) If matter is not resolved then countries can go for
arbitration.
-> Importance of water for Pakistan.
It is vital for Pakistan's irrigation. More than 80% of
Pakistan's irrigation depends on the water it recie-
ves. Pakistan has one of the largest canal system
in world.
