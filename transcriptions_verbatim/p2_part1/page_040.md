(ii) Russia Pakistan Relations.
Ian Bremmar calls Pakistan the Pivot state of
International Politics. Because of its location and
being epicentre of terrorism. Russian experts
like Andrew korybko associated with Govt sponsor-
red think tank. calls Pakistan as zipper state
of Eurasian integration (connective state). This show
the importance Russia is giving to Pakistan.

Russia's flirtations with Pakistan are not new. Even
till 1970s Russia tried to bring Pakistan to its
side. Russia Pak. military exchange is also not new.
Today Russia is coming to Pakistan because Russia
is isolated. Russia looks for new friends & new
markets. Pakistan is important to address Russian secu-
rity concern arising out of extremism. Pakistan can,
also be a market to compensate losses Russia is
having in India in addition to having with china.
Pakistan has a reason to show closeness with Russia
it not only give access to high technology defence
equipments it also allows to play Russia card against
USA. It can get economic & military aid from USA
It will also weaken India's confidence as so far
Russia is the only all weather friend of India. It is
to be noted that Geostrategic scenario is changing.
No country will overlook Pakistan. Pakistan has good
relations with all P5 countries with the exception
of India it has good relations with all major powers
including Iran & Saudi Arabia.
