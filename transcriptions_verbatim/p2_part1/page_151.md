⦿ World Bank.

→ World Bank comprise of 5 institutions.
(i) IBRD
(ii) International Developement Assistance
(iii) IFC - International Finance corporation.
(iv) MIGA - Multilateral Investment Guarantee Agency
(v) ICSID - International centre for settlement of
Investment Disputes.
