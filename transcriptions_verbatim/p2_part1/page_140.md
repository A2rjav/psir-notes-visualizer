Farmers have been exposed to the fluctuations of
the prices of Agricultural commodities in International
market. Since developing countries have joined WTO
the no. of incidents of farmers suicides have increa-
sed. WTO policies have affected food security in
many ways. Many farmers shifted from food crops
to cash crops. there is restriction on PDS system.

__$\rightarrow$ Market Access -__
It means lowering tariffs & removing non-tariff
barriers. Western countries continue to impose non-
tariff barriers (sanitory & phyto-sanitory measures).
In market access there is a major dispute b/w India
and USA. India is defensive, USA is offensive. They
have failed to arrive at agreement over Special
Safeguard Mechanism (SSM) & Special Products (SP).
- countries can trigger SSM in case of import search
  to protect domestic producers. There is a disagreeme-
  nt as to when it can be done. For India small amount
  of surge is enough. But for USA it should be substantial
  amount.
SP $\rightarrow$ Every country can exclude some products from liberalisa-
  tion if the product has some linkage with any special
  programme like Rural Developement, Employment. etc.
  According to USA maximum no. of products to 5-7.
  India wants to keep more & more products.
