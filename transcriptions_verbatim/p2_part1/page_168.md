__Significance of ASEAN for India.__

Look East/ Act east is one of the most significant
initiative of Govt of India. ASEAN is the
gateway for India's integration in Asia Pacific.

ASEAN is the nucleus in the region. ASEAN is a major
force for building security architecture in the region

Initially India overlooked ASEAN considering it as pro-
us block. With the adoption of new economic policy,
disappointing situation in South Asia, India had to look
towards extended neighbourhood. ASEAN was an obvious
choice considering Asia Pacific Emerging as the
centre of gravity of world economy.

In 1992, India initiated its integration with ASEAN

India became sectoral Dialogue partner. Same year
Pakistan also became sectoral Dialogue Partner. Pakistan
is a still sectoral dialogue partner but India have
moved ahead.

1996 - within 4 yrs. India became full dialogue
partner.

2002 - India became summit level partner.

2012 - India-ASEAN became strategic partners.
