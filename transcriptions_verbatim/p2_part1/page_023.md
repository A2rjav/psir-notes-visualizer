-> Section 84 - compulsory licensing.
compulsory licensing for a patented medicine can
be given after 3 years of patent in following con-
ditions:
(a) In the interest of public health.
(b) If medicine is overpriced.
(c) In case patented medicine is not worked in India.
Means it has not been introduced on commercial scale
USA considers section 84 is detrimental to the interest
of US companies, not only India manufactures generic
drugs for domestic consumption but also exports
outside.
Recently Bayer company has lost the legal battle
against NATCO of Hyderabad. NATCO has manufactu-
red SORANIB generic replacement of NEXAVER.
According to SC, company has already recovered the
cost in the very 1st year. The cost of NEXAVER is ₹
₹ 2,80,000 for one month. Whereas the cost of generic
drug is ₹ 8,800.
(vii) Totalisation issue.
Indian professionals working even ~~for~~ short durations have
to contribute to US Social Security Fund. However they
do not get benefit of it. Presently the amount is
$ 28 bn. India wants this funds to be channelised
back into Indian Provident Fund system. USA has tota-
lisation agreement with many countries but has not
done such agreement with India so far.
