__What is required?__
USA should treat china respectfully, engagement but
not containment. US needs to ensure that china's
rise is peaceful.

$\rightarrow$ __Is china a real threat to US Hegemony?__
$1^{\text{st}}$ School of thought - Lee kuan Yew
china's rise is exceptional. No other country has expanded
comprehensive national power in such a short time
like china. He reminds of Nepolean who held that
china will astonish the world when it will come out
of slumber. To support Lee kuan Yew's arguement
we can say that the size of chinese economy will
surpass ~~US~~ economy by 2030. china has already started
building sino-centric world through the initiatives like
AIIB, OBOR.

$2^{\text{nd}}$ school of thought -
There is no near threat to US Hegemony.
$\rightarrow$ china cannot challenge US Hegemony because It does
not have peaceful periphery.
$\rightarrow$ china is passing through pressure cooker syndrome
(internal issues).
$\rightarrow$ china cannot compete with USA in terms of technology
$\rightarrow$ china is nothing but world's factory.
NO comparison b/w USA's soft power & china's soft
power.
