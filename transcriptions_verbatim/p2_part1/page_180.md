• America's grievances against Canada :-
(i) There are grievances against Canada's protectionist
policy in Dairy sector.
(ii) Grievances against export of softwood from canada
to USA.

It is difficult to say how much adverse impact has been
on USA's economy. So far NAFTA has been a win-
win situation for all. Mexico's economic situation is
improved a lot. USA has also benefited because of
Rules of origin under NAFTA. US consumers bene-
fit from the cheaper Mexican export specially groce-
ries. Oil import from Mexico & Canada has kept oil
prices in USA low & provided hedge against uncer-
tainties of the middle east. It increases competi-
tiveness of all the 3. Together they can compete
China & EU in a much better way.

→ Why Trump administration is against NAFTA?
It seems the real determinant was domestic poli-
tics. Opposition to NAFTA in elections could get
Trump the support of working & lower middle
classes who have lost the job. It also consolida-
ted position of republicans in states like california
which had suffered more because of relocation
of business. Trump has been more critical to Mexico.
He not only suggest to create tariff wall but also
concrete wall & suggest Mexico to pay for the wall.
