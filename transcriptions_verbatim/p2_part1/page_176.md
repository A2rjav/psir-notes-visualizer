● Asia Pacific Economic Co-operation (APEC).
APEC is an example of open regionalism. It
means minimum institutionalisation & country's
take up voluntary pledged according to their
capacity.
APEC was established in 1989. It comprise of
21 member economies from Japan to Peru.
All major Asian economies except India is part of
APEC. It is the only platform where people's Repu-
blic of china & Taiwan both are represented that
is why it uses the term member economies rath-
er than member states.
Purpose -
Formulate such rules which can create a free trade
area in Asia Pacific. The idea is to facilitate seam-
less trade & investment. It also promotes techni-
cal co-operation, business facilitation & capacity
building. the idea is -
(i) to remove impediments to trade at border.
(ii) To promote supply chain across border.
(iii) To improve business environment beyond border.
