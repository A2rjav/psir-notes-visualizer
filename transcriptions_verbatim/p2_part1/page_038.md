HEADING: Nuclear Energy Partnership.

● Nuclear Energy Partnership.
Nuclear Energy is the most strategic Area. India &
Russia have also adopted strategic vision in nuclear
energy. Russia is expected to build 12 new rea-
ctors. They have already concluded agreement on
Kudankulam phase 5 & 6. India & Russia have
entered into an Agreement for joint uranium
mining. Russia will be providing technology to
handle nuclear waste & manage nuclear safety.
They will be working together to produce Nuclear
Reactors to be sold to the 3rd world countries.

● Economic Partnership.
Most disappointing aspect of the relations. In pre-
sent age no relations can be carried without strong
economic inter depende. Despite many actions
bilateral trade is below potential. India Russia
bilateral trade is around 11 bn $ whereas Russia
china trade is above 100 bn $. Similarly India US
trade's above 100 bn $. There are some structural
problems in Russia:
(i) Poor law & order situation
(ii) Insufficient banking infrastructure.
(iii) Protectionist Policies
(iv) Strict visa regime.
(v) Russia's tension's with western countries.
(vi) India's trade with western countries.
