Q. What are India's Arguements against IMF & what
type of reforms are required in IMF.

● What is the purpose of IMF ?
It is a pillar of liberal international Economic order.
IMF provides loans to the countries facing BOP crisis.
the other functions of IMF include - Surveillance of
currency fluctuation or Macro-economic stability.

Now, IMF has also started providing long term loans
to the developing countries to adress the poverty.
-> IMF has following facilities -
(i) Stand by arrangement.
For short term crisis.

(ii) Extended Fund Facility-
Some countries may not be able to repay the debt
without re-structuring their economies. Hence, IMF
provides long term loan.

(iii) Supplementary Resource Facility.
Special facility in case there is a sudden flight of capital.

(iv) Poverty & Growth Facility.
Funds are given to low income countries for poverty
reduction programme.

(v) European Financial stabilisation Facility-
Recently created to adress sovereign debt crisis in Europe.
