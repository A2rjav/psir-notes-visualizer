What are the grievances of developing countries ?

IMF impacts the sovereignty.
Why?
When countries approach for funds IMF imposes
conditionalities. Conditionalities are such that they
force countries to go for such economic reforms
which developed countries want.

What are other grievances of developing countries ?
there is a concept of weighted voting in IMF. Country's
Vote share depends on country's quota or subscription.

What is the basis of quota?
When any country join IMF (Present strength 180)
it is assigned quota in proportion to the relative weight
in global economy.

(i) GDP of the country.
(ii) openness to trade
(iii) Volume of current A/c transaction.
(iv) Level of official reserves.

Even if country wants to increase the subscription so
that its voting share increases. Country cannot do
on its own. For any decision on substantive matters
85% of the total votes are required. Developed coun-
tries (G7) alone have a share of more than 50%.
USA's share is around 17%. It means nothing can ha-
ppen without USA's consent.

Western countries impose conditionalities even for increa-
sing subscription. Country has to open its economy.
