The focus of Look East Policy has been to take
the advantage of Geographical proximity, histori-

MGC is not just an economic initiative it is also a
strategic initiative. It aims to recover its lost gro-
und in the natural sphere of India's domination.
India has lost ground to china. China has much ear-
lier established a platform known as greater
Mekong region. In MGC, India & thailand will be
working together. The initiative formally was lau-
nched at Vientianne, the capital of Laos in the year
2000. Mekong & Ganga are civilisational rivers. There
