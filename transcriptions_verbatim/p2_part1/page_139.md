Agriculture is most protected & most politicised in
both North and south. there is a big difference in the
attitude of the countries of south & North. Agriculture
is the back bone of the economies in south. Agriculture
is also a source for employment for large section of
population. Agriculture is not just a source of liveli-
hood it is a way of life. Developing countries cannot
think about Agriculture just from commercial perspe-
ctive. In countries of North just 4% of the population
is dependent on Agriculture. whereas around 70% of
population in countries of south is dependent on Agri-
culture. The share of Agriculture in GDP of countries of
North is 3% whereas in South it is around 30%.
In case of India it is 14%. The countries of North
have technological superiority they are placed in favo-
urable climatic conditions. In developing countries most
of the producers are subsistence farmers whereas in
developed countries they are doing farming on comme-
rcial scale. Developed countries continued to provide
huge domestic subsidies to their farmers. When the
farmers of developing countries are exposed to inter-
national competition they are unable to compete.
