(vii) . UN secretariat.
[Margin: (5)] It requires right sizing of Bureaucracy insuring
transparency & accountability. Presently there is a
dominance of western countries in UN Bureaucracy.
There is a need of equitable distribution.

(viii) Funding
UN is ill-equipped to full fill its obligations. On one
hand resources are limited on the other hand coun-
tries do not ~~fail pay~~ their membership dues on-time.
USA itself has been a frequent defaulter. It has been
suggested that UN should have some independent source
of revenue Eg. It can be authorised to impose tobin
Tax. (tax on international financial transaction).

(ix) UN Peace keeping.
countries are loosing interest in UN Peace keeping.
as peace keeping operations are becoming more & more
dangerous. There are disputes b/w the troop contributing
countries & funds contributing countries. UN does not
have stand by forces. It has to depend on member cou-
ntries.

Above areas of reforms are urgent. Otherwise UN
will not be able to full fill its obligation.
What are the prospects?
