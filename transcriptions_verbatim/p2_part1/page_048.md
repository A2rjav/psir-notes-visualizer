__What is the Border Dispute?__

India & china have been civilisational states rather
than territorial states like European states. Between
these two civilisations there used to be transition
zone. Once British empire got established they tried
to fix India's territories. In the west they made
Durand line a boundary b/w India & Afghanistan.
With china they wanted to settle the boundary Hence
they called a conference in Shimla in 1914. In this
conference they invited the representatives of the
Chinese emperor, representatives of Tibet.

__What was the relationship b/w Tibet & china.__
Whenever Chinese central authority was strong Tibet
was under china & whenever authority was weak
Tibetans used to claim autonomy. In shimla confere-
nce Britishers accepted Chinese sovereignty over Tibet.
However they ~~forced~~ china to accept the autonomy
of outer Tibet. They accepted complete sovereignty
of china over inner Tibet. They also got special
trading rights in Tibet. In this conference Chinese
representative left in a middle & british entered
into an agreement with representatives of Tibet.
