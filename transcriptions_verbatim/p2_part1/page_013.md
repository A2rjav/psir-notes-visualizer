→ phase __III__ : since the end of cold war
① In 1989, during Gulf War, India for the first time
allowed refueling of US war plains, despite Iraq had
close relations with India.

② Disintegration of USSR not only impacted India's
economy but also its strategic environment.
India was forced to look west.

According to Ashley Tellis the current India-US
strategic partnership is based on the background
of the changing environment in Asia. USA came
to support India in context of rising China to
maintain the Balance of Power.

The opening of Indian economy was another very
strong incentive for US engagement.

Despite economic & strategic imperative one of the
major concern of Clinton Administration was to join
India , NPT & CTBT.
India & US relations deteriorated after Pokhran II
USA has again imposed sanctions.
