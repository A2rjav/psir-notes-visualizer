challenges EU is facing-
Today EU is facing huge challenges internally as well
as externally. Regional integration at such a level will
never be a challenge free task. As EU expands, the
process of integration comes under challenge. EU has
never been as harmonious as it appears to be. There
are many fault lines.
(i) Europhobic nations vs. Europhilic Nations.
France Germany Belgium, Netherlands have been Euro-
philic countries. They created EU with the primary
objective of ensuring peace. On the other hand coun-
tries like Britain & Denmark have been Europhobic
nations. They joined organisation to take the benefits.
In comparison to EU countries there is greater strate-
gic conversions with USA. Britain & Denmark have
not joined all EU treaties. They have gone for 'opt
out clauses'.
(ii) Core countries vs Peripheral countries.
Britain, France, Germany, Austria, Finland, Sweden
are known as strong economies. Whereas, Southern
countries like Greece, Portugal, Spain do not have
strong economic performance. The countries of Eastern
Europe added further complications. In order to main-
tain the equal standards of life advanced economies
have to pay for the benefit of peripheral economies.
Hence, people in core countries are often not in
favour of further enlargement because it increase
their burden.
