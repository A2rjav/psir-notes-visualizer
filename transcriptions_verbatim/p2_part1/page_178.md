● NAFTA

NAFTA is the 1st & so far most ambitious regional
trading agreement. It came into existence in 1994.
Initially CUFTA (Canada US FTA) came into existence
in 1989. Later on Mexico was included. It is also the
1st trading agreement b/w developed & developing
countries. NAFTA represent a combine GDP of around
$ 20 tn. Population of 444.1 mn out of which US
population is 304.1 mn, Mexico, 106.8 mn & canada
33.3 mn...

→ Features of the Agreement-
(i) Elimination of Tariffs.
(ii) MFN status
(iii) Members cannot give better deal to the investors
from Non- NAFTA countries.
(iv) Govts. have to give preference to NAFTA countries
in case of Govt. procurement.

→ Rules of origin.
Significant percentage of the exported item must originate
in the importing country. Eg. Mexico supplying manufactu-
