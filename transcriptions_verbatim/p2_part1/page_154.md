④ 1993 - Maastricht Treaty
   Agreement to establish common currency.
   Since then organisation is known as EU i.e. Moneta-
   ry union.

   EU countries wanted to create a single constitution
   However, proposal was rejected in referendums
   in Ireland, Denmark & France.

⑤ 2007 - Treaty of Lisbon
   So far this ti is the last treaty & it has taken EU
   integration to a new level. Lisbon treaty has extended
   qualified veto over most of the issues. (majority of
   the countries representing majority of population.
   Lisbon treaty has also increased the powers of Euro-
   pean Parliament. It has strengthened EU identity. It has
   established EU's common foreign & security policy.
   It provides for the post of high representative for the
   purpose. It has also introduced EU charter of Human
   Rights.

• EU Institutions -
  ⓘ European council highest body known as EU summits
     where EU Heads of the state meets. They meet
     several times in a year.

  ⓘ Council of European union (Ministerial council).

  ⓘ European Commission
     It is a body representing EU. It is also an executive
     body to implement EU decisions.
