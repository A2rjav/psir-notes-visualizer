African countries are even closing their offices in

The negative consequences of WTO policies have been
realised within few years of WTO's existence.
Hence, there was widespread protest against WTO. It
became difficult to hold WTO meetings. Ultimately,
developed countries were forced to adopt Doha Deve-
lopement Agenda.

What is Doha Developement Agenda?
New round of talks. It was agreed that developement
rather than trade will be priority. Doha Developement
Agenda includes wide range of issues of both deve-
loped & Developing countries. It was accepted that
agreements will be accepted in the form of single
undertaking.

What has been the approach of developed countries?
They have been trying that Doha Developement Agenda
collapses & new rounds of talks start. For the 1st
time in Nairobi meeting Declaration, it was mentioned
that some member countries think that Doha deve-
lopement agenda has lost its relevance.

Difference in WTO & GATT
GATT was just covering trade in goods. WTO covers
many new issues such as trade in Agriculture, services,
IPRs, Investment.

WTO agreements are evolving & the level of liberalisation
in each sector vary.
