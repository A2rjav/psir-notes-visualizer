* __Gujral Doctrine.__
  Gujral as a Foreign Minister & later on as a Prime
  Minister had invested lot of energy in improving
  relations with neighbours including Pakistan. Gujral
  doctrine aimed at establishing peaceful periphery.
-> Features of Gujral Doctrine-
   Panchsheel Principles + 2 additional principles.
   (i) India will go for unilateral consensus* (Elder brother
       & not big brother).
   (ii) India will give unilateral concession subject to condi-
        tion, neighbour should not be allowed their terri-
        tories to be used for carrying terrorist operations
        in India.
-> Outcome:
   Immediate impact-
   (i) Improvement in relations with all neighbours.
   (ii) Mahakali Treaty with Nepal.
   (iii) Ganga water sharing agreement with Bangladesh.
   (iv) Visa liberalisation with Pakistan for patients, students.

* Vajpayee Govt.
  Vajpayee held that we cannot change our neighbo-
  urs though we can change our friend. We can cha-
  nge history but we cannot change Geography.
  However, Vajpayee govt. took decision to go nuclear.
  India- Pak. relations deteriorated.
  India's decision legitimized Pakistan's nuclear weapon.
  India & Pak. entered into arms race.
