HEADING: What is to be done?
---
What is to be done?   (3)
(i) Abolish UN.
(ii) Reform UN

No point abolishing. It becomes necessary to reform
UN.
What are the prospects of reform?
very dim prospects. only recently UN has celebrated
the 70th anniversery. UN General Assembly failed to
develope consensus on the agenda of reforms.

What reforms are needed?
Reforms which enhance the efficiency, effectiveness
representativeness, credibility & legitimacy.

What reforms are required?
(i) Reform ON Principles.
Art. 2(7) does not permit UN intervention in the
domestic affairs. UN principles are based on the prin-
ciples of West phalian world order. Post cold war situ-
ation requires greater intervention in domestic affairs.
UN intervenes on the basis of UN General assembly
ressolution. on the basis of responsibility to protect.

(ii) UN charter still carries the reference of 'enemy states'
the reference of enemy states has become anachro-
nistic. Today Germany, Japan are the contenders of
the permanent seat in UNSC.

(iii) General Assembly.
Structural changes are not required yet there is a need
for better co-ordination b/w General assembly & UNSC.
